Visit the National Campus

Pro Shop & Racquet Bar

July 10, 2017
Share

The USTA National Campus Pro Shop features the newest products from adidas, Wilson, HEAD, Babolat and more. The Pro Shop is stocked with a wide assortment of National Campus merchandise such as T-shirts, caps, sweatshirts and novelty items. Powered by Fromuth Tennis, a nationally recognized distributor, this brand new showroom is open every day. The Pro Shop is a great shopping experience for fan and player alike.

Hours of Operation

Mondays-Friday: 8:00 a.m. - 8:00 p.m.

Saturday & Sunday: 8:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m.*

*Hours subject to change 

Racquet Bar

Racquet Bar is located just outside of the Pro Shop in the Welcome Center. Pull up a bar stool and meet our team of tournament-level racquet technicians working behind five sleek new Wilson Baiardo stringing machines. Drop off your racquet and you will be pleasantly surprised at the quality of customization and speed of service. It is the quickest way to improve your game.

Prices

Stringing: $20 per frame

Matching: $25 per frame

Gripping: $5 per frame

Bumper Kits: $10 per frame

Racquet Demos: $10 for 3 days; $25 for 1 month

* all demo fees get credited to the purchase of your racquet at the conclusion of the program.

Consultation: By Appointment
