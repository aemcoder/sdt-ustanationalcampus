2026 BNP Paribas World Team Cup

The USTA National Campus is hosting the 2026 BNP Paribas World Team Cup from September 16-19th. You're invited to experience the action up close for FREE!

Learn More

Celebrating 50 Years of Wheelchair Tennis

This year, which marks the 50th anniversary of the sport’s creation - sees the prestigious competition return to the USA, where it was first staged in 1985. You're invited to experience the action up close for FREE! Join us for a week of elite wheelchair competition and fun-filled activities!

An Opening Ceremony will kick off the tournament on Tuesday, September 15 at 4:00 PM on Championship Court 1, featuring a Parade of Nations and anthem performance.
