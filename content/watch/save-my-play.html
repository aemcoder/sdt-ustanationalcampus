Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record my match?

Scan the on-court or rack card QR code with your smartphone and tap the red record button. No app download or signup required.

If AI editing is enabled on my recording, can I still view the full, unedited match?

Yes. All AI recordings also include the full, unedited footage in addition to the match with gaps removed and automatic highlights generated.

Can I access my recording if I forgot to record my match?

Yes. Please email support@savemyplay.com and provide: court surface, court #, match start time, and match end time.

How long does it take to receive my recording?

Once your match finishes recording, it typically takes a few hours to process the video. Guests can expect to receive the email with their recording by the end of the day.

What is the cost to purchase my recording?

Recordings can be purchased for $19.99 per reservation.
