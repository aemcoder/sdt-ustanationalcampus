Dynamic Warm-Up

The dynamic warm-up is an essential component to any pre-practice or pre-competition routine. The dynamic warm-up should increase body temperature, actively stretch muscles, include a shoulder warm-up, and incorporate foundational movement patterns and coordination drills.
