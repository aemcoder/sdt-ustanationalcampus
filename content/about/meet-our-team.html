Meet Our Team

style
pad-small, col-10-xs

At the USTA National Campus, our team of dedicated coaches believes that what we do is more than just a game. Our goal is for every participant that walks through our doors to feel like they are embarking on an incredible journey with unlimited possibilities both on and off the court. Our role as coaches is to guide you through the practice and play options that we offer and ensure you are getting the best experience possible.

​

All members of our coaching staff are Safeplay approved and background checked. They receive ongoing continuing education training as well as regular staff training and development opportunities.  Our coaches come from all parts of the world and bring a diversity of experience and wealth of knowledge to the court.
