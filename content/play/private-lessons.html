Frequently Asked Questions

Does the price of the lesson include the court time?

Yes, absolutely! All of our lesson fees are all-inclusive. You do not need to book or pay for court time separately. We handle the reservation and cost of the court for the duration of your scheduled lesson

Do I need to form my own group for Semi-Private or Group lessons?

Yes, both our Semi-Private (2-3 students) and Group Instruction (4+ students) options require a pre-existing group.

I don't have a group, but I'd like to join a small group lesson. What are my options?

If you are interested in a small group setting but do not currently have a group, please visit our Programs page for a list of available clinics, camps, and skill-level-based classes. 

How far in advance can I book a lesson?

Lesson requests may not be made sooner than two weeks (14 days) prior to the desired lesson date.

What is the difference between a standard Group Lesson and a Private Group Camp?

The difference is the instructor-to-student ratio. Standard Group Lessons are taught by a single instructor (pro) and typically accommodate up to 4 students. Private Group Camps are defined as any large group that requires more than one instructor (pro) to maintain quality instruction and a good student-to-proportion ratio. These are custom-designed, larger sessions.

Will my card be charged immediately when I book?

No, your card is used as a hold only and will not be charged at the time of booking. The card will only be charged the day of the lesson, or if the lesson is canceled, or if there is a no-show.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations must be received at least 24 hours before the lesson to avoid being charged for the full value of the lesson. This policy also applies to lessons booked less than 24 hours before the scheduled time. Requests for lessons may not be made sooner than 2 weeks before the lesson date. Group lessons requiring more than one pro are considered a private group camp.
