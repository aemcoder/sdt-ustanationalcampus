INSTRUCTIONAL

Padel 101

Learn the game or brush up your skills if it has been a while since you last played. Drop-in reservations open 1-week prior to the class date. Level: New Padel player, no experience.

Padel 102

Continue advancing your skills! Players will need to have completed Padel 101 or be evaluated by coach. Players should know the fundamentals and be able to utilize the walls. Drop-in reservations open 1-week prior to the class date. Level: New Padel player, limited experience.

Innovation Clinic

The "Innovation Clinic" leverages the advanced Volley ball machine to isolate and perfect your padel strokes. Players of all levels will benefit from this tight focus on technical precision. Drill down on footwork, racket prep, and contact points to build a better padel game.

PRACTICE TO PLAY

Practice to Play (Novice/Intermediate)

This session is for Novice & Intermediate players with match-play experience. The ideal environment for players to develop new skills, improve tactical awareness, and gain confidence in live match situations, Practice to Play combines 45 minutes of targeted drills and 45 minutes of guided match play. Must be approved to join.

Women's Practice to Play (Novice/Intermediate)

Our "Women's Padel" clinic offers a fun, welcoming space exclusively for women to learn the basics and sharpen their skills. Master the fundamentals, practice core techniques, and learn match strategies with supportive coaching. It is the perfect blend of skill-building, fitness, and community fun.

SOCIAL PLAY

Padel Community Drop-In (Free)

We offer opportunities each week where players can enjoy free padel while meeting other players in the community. Players should either have completed Padel 101 or have previous padel experience in order to play. Players who register must be able to serve, rally, and score. Players must register in advance and bring their own racquet (balls are provided). The coach will be leading and facilitating play, however this is not an instructional session. Ages 18+.

Sundays | 7:30 a.m. - 9:00 a.m.

Padel Novice Open Play (101)

This is for novice players who can execute basic strokes but have little match play experience. This social play session will introduce players to Padel through the guidance and support of a National Campus pro.

Padel Intermediate Open Play (102)

This is for intermediate level players who have match play experience. This social play session is an opportunity to come out and meet other players, play social padel, and have some fun!

Padel High-Intermediate Open Play

This is for high intermediate/advanced level players who have tournament experience. Displays a balance of aggressive and/or defensive positioning and shots from all parts of the court. This social play session is an opportunity to come out and meet other players, play social padel, and have some fun! Must be approved to join.

COMPETITION

Americanos

Join our Americano 1-Day Tournament, a spirited competition tailored for novice and intermediate players. Registered individuals will experience a dynamic rotation of partners across multiple rounds. This unique structure identifies the top-performing player based on their total cumulative point tally.

Upcoming: August Americano | August 16, 9:00 a.m. - 11:00 a.m.

USPA Padel tournaments

Compete in official USPA-sanctioned tournaments, including USPA 100, 250, and 500 levels. These multi-day events feature division-based draws for men and women across various skill levels, offering a professional setting to earn World Padel Ranking points and test your skills. A World Padel Rating account is required for registration.

Frequently Asked Questions

I’m new to padel. Are there programs for beginners?

Absolutely! Our Padel 101 program is designed specifically for new players. It’s a great way to learn the fundamentals in a fun and supportive environment!

Where do classes take place?

All padel programs are held on Padel courts.

Can I drop in for a class or do I need to register for a full session?

We offer flexible options! You can make a daily drop-in reservation (space permitting), or save by registering for the full session. Drop-ins are encouraged to be reserved at least 24 hours in advance.

How do I know which class level is right for me?

New players to the USTA National Campus can sign up for a free 30-minute evaluation with one of our teaching pros to assess their level and provide level-based program recommendations.
