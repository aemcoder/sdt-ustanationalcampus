Instructional

Tennis 101

Learn the game or brush up your skills if it has been a while since you last played. Level: New tennis player, no experience.

Tennis 102

A continuation of Tennis 101 for those players who can serve, rally, and score. This hour will focus on the basics of court positioning, shot placement, and doubles strategies. brush up your skills if it has been a while since you last played. Level: Recreational, no match experience.

Singles Training

Combine strokes and tactics in these lively game-based sessions. Practice singles situations with active play. Professional/Coach will feed balls and review technique and shot selection. Level: NTRP 2.5-4.0+

Doubles Training

Combine strokes and tactics in these lively game-based sessions. Practice doubles situations with active play. Professional/Coach will feed balls and review technique and shot selection. Level: NTRP 2.5-4.0+

Serve & Return

Work on your serve and return mechanics, technique, and strategy. 30 minutes of serves, 30 minutes of returns with a ball machine, and 30 minutes of serve/return game-based activities. Level: NTRP 2.5-4.0

Innovation Clinic

Pro-led activities that highlight and explore a stroke from a tactical or technical perspective. Pro-led exercises will be structured so that material and activities can be useful for all levels. Ball machines and other tennis technologies will be used. Level: NTRP 3.0+

Fitness

Novice Cardio

Designed for new or returning adult players, enjoy a heart-pumping workout, and hit balls continuously. Improve your fitness and your tennis simultaneously. Level: Encouraged to have taken Tennis 101. NTRP 2.0-2.5

Cardio Move

Enjoy a heart-pumping workout and hit balls continuously. Improve your fitness and your tennis simultaneously. Level: NTRP 2.5-4.0

Singles Live Ball

A fast-paced and energetic 90 minutes of pro-led singles style point play. The serve, return, and “downtime” in between points have been removed to amp up the energy and fun! Join us for live-ball play and continual focus on moving from defense to offense. Level: NTRP 3.5+

Doubles Live Ball

A fast-paced and energetic 90 minutes of pro-led doubles style point play. The serve, return, and “downtime” in between points have been removed to amp up the energy and fun! Join us for live-ball play and continual focus on moving from defense to offense. Level: NTRP 3.5+

Advanced Drill Session

An hour of intense drilling with other advanced players. Approval from the Head Professional is required to join. Level: NTRP 4.5+

Social Play

Red Ball Tennis Community Drop-In (Free)

Red Ball Tennis is a FUN adult social experience designed to get new players on a court and have them playing the game in 10 minutes! Played on our renovated Nemours Family Zone courts with modified equipment (provided) and organized by National Campus teaching pros, Red Ball Tennis requires no experience and guarantees to have you playing tennis within minutes! Played on a 36-foot court and 23-inch racquets. Ages: 18+

Love To Play

Playing the game is just as important for your development as practice! Love to Play is for novice players who can execute basic strokes but have little match play experience. This social play singles and/or doubles session will introduce players to the written and unwritten rules of tennis through the guidance and support of a National Campus pro. It is a great way to meet new friends and put your weekly new skills from class to the test! Coach approval is required to participate. Level: NTRP 2.0-2.5

Singles Match Play

Come out and meet other players, play social tennis, and have some fun! One of our pros will help connect you and periodically mix up the pairings for the duration of the event. Level: NTRP 3.0-4.0

Doubles Match Play

Come out and meet other players, play social tennis, and have some fun! One of our pros will help connect you and periodically mix up the pairings for the duration of the event. Level: NTRP 3.0-4.0

Doubles Match Play (4.5+)

Come out and meet other players, play social tennis, and have some fun! One of our pros will help connect you and periodically mix up the pairings for the duration of the event. Approval from the Head Professional is required to join. Level: NTRP 4.5+

Frequently Asked Questions

I’m new to tennis. Are there programs for beginners?

Absolutely! Our Let's Try Tennis program and Tennis 101 program are both designed specifically for new players. Learn the fundamentals with our session-based Tennis 101 program, or in 3 weeks with our Let's Try Tennis program!

Are there any high-energy or fitness-focused options?

Yes! Our Novice Cardio, Cardio Move, and Live Ball programs offer non-stop movement and are perfect if you want a great workout while improving your game

Where do the classes take place?

Most adult programs are held on our Har-Tru (clay) courts. However, due to occasional event conflicts, some sessions may be moved to our state-of-the-art Plexicushion courts.

Can I drop in for a class or do I need to register for the full session?

We offer flexible options! You can make a daily drop-in reservation (space permitting), or save by registering for the full session. Drop-ins are encouraged to be reserved at least 24 hours in advance.

What’s the benefit of the Social Play programs?

It’s a great way to enjoy casual match play and grow your game by competing. One of our pros will help coordinate and rotate pairings throughout the session to keep things fresh and fun.

How do I know which class level is right for me?

Many programs are listed by NTRP level (e.g., 3.0). Click here for help determining your NTRP rating and finding the right fit for your skill level.

New players to the USTA National Campus can also sign up for a free 30-minute evaluation with one of our teaching pros to assess their level and provide level-based program recommendations.
