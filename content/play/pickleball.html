Adult Clinics

Pickleball 101

This is for players who have never played pickleball. It is an introduction to the rules, how to serve, return and score and to get familiar with court position. This class is a drop-in and can be taken multiple times. Players should be able to dink, serve, return and understand the scoring before moving to the next level. After completing this level players will be able to join open play.

​

Pickleball 102

This class is the next step after moving up from Pickleball 101. The focus is on building consistency with shots in the No Volley Zone, transition area, 3rd shot drop, and serve/return. This class will also introduce lobs and overheads. Players moving on will be able to sustain dinking rallys, become more consistent with transition shots, and develop more control placing the serve and return of serve. This class is available as a session or a drop-in.

​

Practice to Play (Intermediate Level)

Our practice to play is for intermediate (3.0-3.99) level players who have match-play experience. This social play session offers players an opportunity to sharpen their skills. The session includes 45 minutes of drills and 45 minutes of match play.

​

Pickleball Skills & Drills (Intermediate 3.0+)

This class is for intermediate players. This clinic will primarily focus on player development around game-based situations with a focus on specialty shots, formations, and strategies that are level appropriate. Players will work on developing spins on more shots and different doubles strategies. Sign up by the session or reserve a drop-in for individual sessions.

Junior Clinics

Junior Pickleball Beginner Clinic

Ages 9-13. This instructional program is designed for players who are new to the game or looking to improve the consistency of their strokes. The focus will be on play-based drills centering on No Volley Zone, transition area, and serve and return to develop proper stroke habits.

​

Junior Pickleball Club (Starting Fall 2026)

Ages 9-13 This program is for players who have completed and advanced from the Junior beginner pickleball class or for players that have played the game before and know how to score. Players will continue to work on technique and consistency with groundstrokes, serve and return, as well as hitting in the non-volley zone. Will also continue to work on moving forward, work on the transition from the back of the court toward the non-volley zone and paddle positioning.

Junior Pickleball Intermediate Clinic

Ages 9-13. For players who are participating in tournaments, this instructional program focuses on play-based drills to improve their doubles game and to increase opportunities for singles play. A designation is required for this program.

DUPR PLAY

DUPR Match Play

Great opportunity to come out and work on your DUPR rating. This will be a coach-led activity. The player will be placed at the beginning based on their rating. Games to 11 win by 1. Players will partner with each other on their court and have 40 minutes to complete all three games. Scores are written down and then added up

Frequently Asked Questions

I’m new to pickleball. Are there programs for beginners?

Yes, our Pickleball 101 class is the perfect place to start!

Where do classes take place?

All pickleball programs are held on our Nemours Family Zone courts.

Can I drop in for a class or do I need to register for a full session?

We offer flexible options! You can make a daily drop-in reservation (space permitting), or save by registering for the full session. Drop-ins are encouraged to be reserved at least 24 hours in advance.

How do I know which class level is right for me?

New players to the USTA National Campus can sign up for a free 30-minute evaluation with one of our teaching pros to assess their level and provide level-based program recommendations.

What is the difference between Community Drop in and Level based play?

Community drop in is for free and all levels are able to play. Our level based play is only for certain levels and there is a coach that oversees play
