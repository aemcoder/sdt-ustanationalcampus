BOOK YOUR FREE30

Discover Tennis On Us!

Central Florida residents new to our programs are invited to enjoy a complimentary 30-minute session with one of our certified teaching pros. Whether you're picking up a racquet for the first time or returning to the game, this one-on-one experience is designed to get you started with confidence.

During your session, you'll hit and rally while getting tips and personalized recommendations for classes and programs that match your goals. If you’re looking for gear, they can point you in the right direction at our Pro Shop and help you register for what’s next. For kids, the coach will connect with parents to walk through the best pathway for development and future success. All ages and abilities are welcome.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive a response?

A representative will contact you to schedule your session within 24-48 hours.

Do I need to have an account to book my session?

You must have a free CourtReserve account in order to schedule a Free 30. Click here to create your account.

What racquet sports are available for Free30s?

Free30s are available for all three racquet sports (tennis, pickleball, and padel).

Is this offer available to players of all ages?

Yes, all Central Florida residents new to the USTA National Campus can take advantage of this offer regardless of age.

Do I need to bring any equipment to my session?

Equipment can be provided, if needed.
