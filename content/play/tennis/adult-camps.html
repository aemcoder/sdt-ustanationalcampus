Our adult Pickleball camps are perfect for individuals and groups, or come for a fun team-building weekend and take your game to the next level. Featuring over 12 hours of instruction over 3 days, our Pickleball camps are the perfect place to work on your strokes and grow in your knowledge of the game. Players will work hard but also have fun under the direction of our expert coaching team!

Each 3-day camp will focus on three key areas of development. First, "Owning the Zone" will cover consistency and control within the Non-Volley Zone (NVZ), with an emphasis on directional decisions, movement, and balance. Second, "Decision to Transition" will guide players on how to move in and through the transition area, with a strategic focus on making the right call on the third shot. Finally, "Starting Your Play Off Right" will strengthen the serve, return, and the critical next shot, helping players set the tone from the very beginning of each point.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does my fee include?

Your on-court time will consist of tactically focused drills with each camp run by a Senior pro lead supported by the Adult Camp delivery team. Our adult camps include a welcome gift, complimentary demo racquets courtesy of the Racquet Bar, access to our showers and locker rooms, and guest speakers during lunch to help maximize your off-court time.

Who will run my camp?

Every session is led by a Senior member of the National Campus teaching staff and supported by the hand-selected Adult Camp delivery team trained in the curriculum. We rotate the pros to give you a well-rounded experience. Every coach is well-trained in the camp philosophy and curriculum to maximize your on-court experience here at the USTA National Campus.

What will I do on court?

Players will be placed in groups based on level, no larger than 4 per court. Our adult camps follow a pre-planned schedule. If a player needs to excuse themselves for a portion of the camp for any reason, the time missed cannot be able to be made up. Pairings are made by the lead pro based on level and are subject to change during and between each session.

What is expected of me physically?

Go at your pace: Florida heat and humidity can be challenging for some athletes; however, National Campus adult camps are paced with breaks in between each activity and demos before every activity. If there is ever a need, we have athletic trainers on-site to respond to any medical need.

What if I want to play more?

There are endless options for additional learning and fun. Feel free to drop into a Cardio Tennis class or one of our weekly socials if you are interested in playing outside of your camp activities. If you would like some extra one-on-one time with a National Campus pro, you can book a private lesson. Additionally, all camp attendees can play on-site for free during the duration of the camp. Simply stop by the Welcome Center desk to book any of the above options.

Can I bring my family with me?

Yes! They can also drop into morning and evening programming.

What amenities are available at the facility?

Guests can enjoy access to the Net Post Grill, water fountains, vending machines, and locker rooms (please note: towels are not provided). The campus is designed for comfort and convenience while you’re on site.

What type of court surface are camps held on?

Camps are typically held on Har-Tru green clay courts. If weather or tournament scheduling requires, camps may be moved to our Plexicushion hard courts to ensure your experience continues without interruption.

Cancellation Policy

  • Standard Camps: Notices of cancellation given 61 days or more before the camp start date may be given a full refund of the total registration fee. Between 60 and 15 days before the camp start date may be given a refund of the total registration fee minus 20%. Notices given between 14 days and 4 days before the camp start date may be given a refund of the total registration fee minus 50%. Notices given 3 days or less before the camp start date are not eligible for a refund.

  • Tennis & Wellness and Private Camps: Notices of cancellation given 61 days or more before the camp start date may be given a full refund of the total registration fee.
