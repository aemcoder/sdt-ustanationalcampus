Red Ball Tennis Community Drop-In

DROP-IN

Tennis Made Easy!

Looking for a fun way to get active and connect with other adult players? Red Ball Tennis is the perfect fit! Designed for adults of all levels, from those picking up a racquet for the first time to experienced players, we offer a welcoming community where you can learn, improve, and share your love of tennis in a relaxed and supportive atmosphere.

​

Our free community drop-in is a FUN, adult social experience that brings new players to the courts and has them rallying within minutes! Played on the renovated Nemours Family Zone and led by National Campus teaching pros, players use modified equipment (provided) and enjoy a stress-free, low-impact way to dive into the game—no experience required!
