Who, Why and What We Are

The USTA is a progressive and diverse not-for-profit organization whose volunteers, professional staff, and financial resources support a single mission: growing tennis to inspire healthier people and communities everywhere.

Located at Lake Nona in Orlando, FL, this groundbreaking facility is designed to enhance the sport at every level and create an unparalleled playing, training, coaching, and educational experience for recreational players, competitive players, coaches, and spectators. More than 100 international, national, and local events and tournaments are scheduled at the facility in 2017.

Player Development

The USTA National Campus is home to USTA Player Development National Training Headquarters. The Lake Nona-based training center is one of three USTA Player Development Traning Centers in the country.

Player Development works with players at many levels of the pathway from red, orange, and green ball Early Development Campers, promising young juniors, and aspiring professionals in Collegiate Tennis, all the way to the Top American prospects.
