Rising American tennis players like Bjorn Fratangelo, Mackenzie McDonald, and Jared Hiltzik all train at the USTA National Campus when they’re not traveling across the globe to compete. See where some of these resident Home of American Tennis players are competing this week.

Where in the World are the Stars from the USTA #NationalCampus

Mackie McDonald finishes up the Asian swing and heads to Europe.

Bjorn Fratangelo takes his first title this year at a 100K USTA Pro Circuit event in Fairfield, CA.

Ellie Halbauer looks to take the title in the USTA Pro Circuit 25K event in Florence, SC.

Mackie McDonald travel to Antwerp, Belgium to compete in the European Open ATP 250.

Jared Hiltzik takes the title at the Texas Tamale Houston Cup.

Chris Herman wins the Chicago Classic ITF Futures event.
