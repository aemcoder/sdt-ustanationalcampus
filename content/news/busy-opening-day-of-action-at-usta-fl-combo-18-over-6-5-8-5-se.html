Busy Opening Day of Action

Saturday marked a busy opening day of match play at the USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships. All squads entered into the event contested two dual matches over the course of the day.

A dual match consists of three courts of doubles matchups; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

The men’s 6.5 title appears to be going to either Broward (Lauderdale Tennis Club) or Marion (Country Club of Ocala) after both went 2-0 on the day. Broward holds a slight advantage in the standings thanks to five court victories compared to Marion’s four, but the victor of their head-to-head match Sunday will have the ultimate inside track. However, Orange/Seminole/Lake (Tuscawilla Country Club – Winter Springs) remains in the hunt after going 1-1 to open the weekend and could also play spoiler on day two.

In the women’s 6.5 competition, Orange/Seminole/Lake (USTA National Campus) clinched Flight 2 with a perfect 2-0 effort on the strength of going 6-for-6 in their individual matchups while not dropping a single set. They will likely face the winner of Broward (Weston Tennis Center) and Brevard (Viera Regional Park), the two undefeated squads in Flight 1 that are set to square off Sunday.

Both flights were highly-competitive Saturday in the men’s 8.5 division. Unbeaten Broward (Weston Hills Country Club) and Brevard (Titan Tennis Center – Melbourne) vaulted to the top of the standings in Flight 1 while 1-1 Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) will be looking to play their way back into the mix. Flight 2 currently belongs to Hillsborough (River Hills Country Club – Valrico), but with a trio of crews at 1-1 overall heading into Sunday, very little has been decided to this point.

Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) is one dual win away from booking their spot in the women’s 8.5 championship match thanks to claiming two victories as the lone unscathed team in Flight 1. Additionally, Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club) and Escambia (Roger Scott Tennis Center – Pensacola) will clash on Sunday with Flight 2 on the line.

The men’s 6.5 and women’s 8.5 champions will be determined Sunday while the women’s 6.5 and men’s 8.5 titles will be played for Monday morning.
