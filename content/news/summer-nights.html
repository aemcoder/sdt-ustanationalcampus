Summer Nights at the USTA National Campus!

Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday nights

July 10-August 9

Featuring: Half price courts (all day)

  • $10 large cheese pizza after 3pm
  • $4 Craft beer/wine after 3pm
  • $3 ice cream

Tuesdays: Family Night:

6:00pm –7:15pm / $25 per family; Tennis in the Nemours Family Zone for both adults and children! Additional activities include face painting, live music, games, and more!

Wednesdays – Summer Series Team Tennis Challenge:

6:00pm – 7:15pm / $10 per child; A series of team tennis challenges for players 10 and under in the Nemours Family Zone.  

Thursday – Rock n’ Roll Doubles:

6:00pm - 7:30pm / $10 per person; Open to all skill levels. This adult social play will be directed and supervised by our campus pro staff.
