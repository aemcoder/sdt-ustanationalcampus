Rain Extends Opening Day

Less than ideal weather greeted the 2020 USTA Florida 18 & Over Men’s 4.0 & Women’s 2.5/3.0/4.5 Sectional Championships on Saturday. Even still, all scheduled matches were completed by the end of the day.

Teams are competing in dual matches consisting of three matchups in total – one singles and two doubles; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Four squads got through Saturday without a loss in the men’s 4.0 division, putting them in solid position to advance to Monday’s semifinals. In Flight 1, either Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) or Broward (Parkland Tennis Center) will move on, as the two currently sit atop the standings at 2-0. Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) lead the way in Flights 2 and 3, respectively; with five court victories, Hillsborough is almost assured a place in the final four, while Marion has more work to do in the only flight featuring a double round-robin format.

Things also began to take shape for the 3.0 women after day one of the event. Undefeated Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) and Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park – Casselberry) could decide Flight 1 when they square off, but 1-1 Brevard (Rockledge Community) remains in the mix as well. Duval (OakLeaf Tennis – Orange Park) holds the advantage in Flight 2 with a pair of impressive wins and Miami-Dade (Kendall Tennis Training Center – Miami) is the only crew left unscathed playing out of Flight 3.

The squads making up the women’s 4.5 competition will play one last round within their respective flights Sunday morning before the divisional final is contested in the afternoon. Flight 1 will go to the winner between Escambia (Pensacola Country Club) and Miami-Dade (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center – Miami) after both went 2-0 over the course of Saturday. Meanwhile, Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) is the lone unbeaten in Flight 2, needing only to defeat second-place Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) to book a spot in the championship.

Sunday’s matches are slated to begin at 7:30 a.m. It will mark the first day of play for the women’s 2.5 division. For more information on the 2020 USTA FL 18 & Over Men’s 4.0 & Women’s 2.5/3.0/4.5 Sectional Championships, click here.
