The first day of the 2018 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships concluded Saturday evening at the USTA National Campus, as all teams competed in round robin dual matches.

Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) and Broward (City of Oakland Park) are just one victory away from advancing to the men’s 6.5 championship. Marion went 2-0 in the three-team Flight 1, needing only to defeat Escambia (Independent) on Sunday. Broward is in prime position in Flight 2 as they are not only the lone unbeaten remaining, but also will take on 0-2 Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) in their final dual.

Five squads got through Saturday without a loss and have their sights set on reaching the semifinals in the women’s 6.5 division. Just like on the men’s side, Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) has the inside track in Flight 1, while Escambia (Independent) and SMD (Alper JCC) sit atop the standings in Flight 3 and 4, respectively. Perhaps the most intriguing dual of the day from the division will be Hillsborough (FishHawk Ranch) taking on Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) after both went 2-0 in Flight 2 on the opening day of action.

For the men’s 8.5 competition, Escambia (Independent) owns a 2-0 record in Flight 1 and is a win over IRMSL (Vero Beach Tennis and Fitness Club) from securing their spot in the championship semifinals. Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) holds the top spot in Flight 2 after an undefeated Saturday with Sarasota (Payne Park) and Brevard (The Palmer Inn and Tennis Club) hoping to still be in the mix at 1-1 overall. The teams in Flight 3 played just once on the day, with SMD (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center) and Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center) prevailing but Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA) and Leon (Foreastmeadows Park and Athletic Center) very much alive going into Sunday.

 The women’s 8.5 division has three clear flight leaders with the remaining teams battling for the wild card spot. SMD (Coral Oaks Tennis Club) took down both 1-1 teams for firm grasp of Flight 1; Sarasota (Longwood Athletic Club) and Escambia (Independent) turned in dominant performances in Flight 2 and 3, respectively, en route to going 2-0 on the day.
