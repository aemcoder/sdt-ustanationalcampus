It’s been fun in the sun all summer long at the USTA National Campus. Check-out some highlights from the summer in Orlando, FL through photos from the USTA National Campus. From innovation PlaySight clinics and youth summer camp, to players of all ages and levels competing for national titles, there's a way for everyone to experience tennis like never before at the Home of American Tennis.

Summer Highlights from the USTA National Campus.

#USTennis players prepare for the US Open at the Home of American Tennis.

Competitors from across the sunshine state head to the USTA National Campus to battle for state titles.

Tennis players from across the globe travel to the USTA National Campus to work on their game.

Juniors players of all levels and ages battled it out at the Home of American Tennis for top titles.

USTA President Katrina Adams connects with players from all sections at the ATA National Championships.

Players learn more about their games during PlaySight Innovation Clinics.

Top NJTL players from across the country travel to Orlando to play for a National Clay Championships wildcard.

Rainy days at the USTA National Campus call for showing your artistic side by drawing your best tennis court.

Top players from across Florida play for the state's top titles in Bobby Curtis singles.

ATA National Championships brought hundreds of players from ages 10 to 80 to play in Orlando.
