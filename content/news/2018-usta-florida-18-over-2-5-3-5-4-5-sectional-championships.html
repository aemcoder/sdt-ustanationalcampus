2018 USTA FL 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships

Sunshine State Squads Back in Action

August 17, 2018

style
article-hero

For the second consecutive weekend, USTA Florida League Section Championships action is set to fill up the courts of the USTA National Campus. This time it will be teams at the 2.5, 3.5 and 4.5 levels doing battle Aug. 18-20. First matches are set to begin at 7:30 a.m. Saturday morning.

The 2.5 women and 3.5 women will be competing on hard courts while the two men’s divisions plus the 4.5 women will be on clay. All round robin dual matches are slated to be contested on Saturday and Sunday with divisional semifinals and finals scheduled for Monday.

Each dual is composed of five individual matchups – two singles and three doubles. In order to claim victory, a team must win at least three of those five courts.

With the 2.5 women’s competition consisting of just two flights, the top finisher in each flight automatically advances to Monday’s championship round. Both 3.5 divisions feature four flights, meaning the flight winners will secure a place in the semifinals, where it will be Flight 1 versus Flight 2 and Flight 3 clashing with Flight 4. Coming up short in a flight at the 4.5 level might not spell the end for teams, as the second-place finisher with the most individual court wins will earn a wild card to join the three flight champions in the semifinals.

Teams taking home titles at last year’s sectional championships included the 2.5 women representing Duval (Florida Yacht Club), who finished 4-1 overall along with Hillsborough (Westchase); the decisive number was 12, which was the number of individual victories they earned, compared to only 10 for Hillsborough. In the 3.5 competitions, Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) claimed the women’s crown with a 4-1 win over SMD (Crandon Park) while the Orange/Seminole (Fort Gatlin Recreation Center) men got past Brevard (Fee Avenue) by a slim 3-2 margin. For the 4.5 championships duals, Orange/Seminole/Lake (Sanlando Park) defeated Broward (Weston Hills CC) 3-2 in women’s action and SMD (Doral Park) bested Duval (Independent) by the same score line on the men’s side.
