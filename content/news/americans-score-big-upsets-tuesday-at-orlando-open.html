Six More Americans into Round Two

November 17, 2020

Two seeded players were knocked out of the opening round, both at the hands of American competitors, Tuesday at the 2020 Orlando Open presented by Nemours, an ATP Challenger 80 event. Those victories were two of six secured by Americans in singles action, placing a total of 10 into the second round.

Unseeded Bjorn Fratangelo managed to win half of the return points he played versus No. 3 seed Daniel Elahi Galan of Colombia. That resulted in Fratangelo breaking serve four times, while dropping serve just once, en route to a 6-3, 6-3 triumph. Meanwhile, Nick Chappell made the most of receiving a lucky loser spot in the main draw, stunning seventh-seeded Ramkumar Ramanathan (India) 6-3, 6-4 in just over one hour on court.

Brandon Nakashima proved too tough for Roberto Cid Subervi of the Dominican Republic, coming through 6-1, 6-3, while No. 2 Denis Kudla held off a strong challenge from lucky loser Kevin King (USA) 6-4, 6-3. Additionally, eighth-seeded Mackenzie McDonald overwhelmed Russian Teymuraz Gabashvili 6-2, 6-1 and qualifier Alexander Ritschard prevailed 6-4, 6-3 over Ulises Blanch in an all-American battle.

No. 1 Thiago Monteiro (Brazil) took the court for the first time this week, defeating American qualifier Stefan Kozlov 6-1, 6-3, who suffered an injury in the second game of the match. Fourth seed Prajnesh Gunneswaran of India was nearly pushed to three sets by lucky loser Sadio Doumbia (France) before winning 6-3, 7-5. Sweden’s Elias Ymer also earned a tightly-contested 7-5, 7-6(3) victory against teenager Chun-hsin Tseng (Taiwan).

In the doubles draw, three matches were on the schedule for Tuesday, but only one was actually played, with Peter Polansky (Canada) and Dmitry Popko (Kazakhstan) rallying past American duo Christopher Eubanks and Nakashima 3-6, 6-2, 10-6. Tseng and countrymen Tung-Lin Wu received a walkover from the American pairing of Thai-Son Kwiatkowski and Ritschard; Treat Huey (Philippines) and Martin Redlicki (USA) also advanced via walkover from Aziz Dougaz (Tunisia) and Mohamed Safwat (Egypt).

Day five is slated to get underway at 10 a.m., featuring the rest of the doubles opening round matchups along with singles round of 16 action.
