Our 2021 Spring Break Programs are now open for registration!

Our Spring Break programs will feature professional coaching and training combined with the perfect blend of competition, camaraderie and fun!

Programs will be offered daily March 15th - 19th in both the Nemour's Family Zone and Youth Development areas. 

Program pricing and schedules are as follows:

NEMOURS FAMILY ZONE:

  • 10:00 a.m. - 12:00 p.m.
  • $40.00 per day
  • Programs offered in both Red Ball and Orange Ball 
  YOUTH DEVELOPMENT:
  • 9:30 a.m. - 12:30 p.m.
  • $60.00 per day
  • Programs offered in Campus Youth 1, Campus Youth 2, Campus Youth Varsity, and Campus Youth Performance
  *In the event of rain and courts become unplayable, programs will be canceled and players will not be able to stay on campus.      CLICK HERE to register for 2021 Spring Break Programs.
