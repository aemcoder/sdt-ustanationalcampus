USTA FL Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships

Rain Forces Play Indoors

January 23, 2021

style
article-hero

Consistent rain forced most match play indoors Saturday at the USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships. 

Teams are competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Flight play concluded Saturday evening for the 7.0 division, as four squads successfully moved on to the semifinal stage. Alachua (Westside Park Recreation Department – Gainesville) had already clinched Flight 1 before taking the court for their final match by virtue of recording three sweeps; they were beaten by Polk (Beerman Family Tennis Center – Lakeland) in the last dual of the day but still finished with 10 court victories and 21 sets to their credit. Pinellas (Seminole Lake Family Tennis) and Orange/Seminole/Lake (Red Bug Lake Park – Casselberry) will enter Sunday without a loss. Pinellas battled their way to a 3-0 record in Flight 2, winning all three matches two courts to one, while Orange/Seminole/Lake cruised through Flight 3 with eight individual matchups secured.

In the semifinals, Orange/Seminole/Lake is set to clash with wild card Broward (Weston Tennis Center) – a squad which actually finished with more court victories than Pinellas in Flight 2 – and Alachua will be across the net from Pinellas.

Only one round was completed in the 9.0 division, where Broward (Brian Piccolo Tennis Center – Hollywood) and Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville) continued to pull away from the rest of the field. With both sitting at 3-0, Broward holds a slight edge over fellow undefeated Alachua thanks to owning eight individual matchups compared to just seven for Alachua. Sarasota (Independent) is still in the hunt, as they are currently third at 2-1 overall, with the other three crews having already been eliminated.

Action continues Sunday beginning at 7:30 a.m. with the 7.0 semifinals. For more information on the USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships, click here.
