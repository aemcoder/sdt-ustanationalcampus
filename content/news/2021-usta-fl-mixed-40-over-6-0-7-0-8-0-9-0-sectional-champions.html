USTA FL Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/7.0/8.0/9.0 Sectional Championships

Busy Summer of League Play Kicks Off

June 24, 2021

style
article-hero

A full summer of USTA Florida adult league events at the USTA National Campus kicks off this weekend with the Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/7.0/8.0/9.0 Sectional Championships. The 6.0 and 8.0 divisions will get underway Friday, June 25, while the 7.0 and 9.0 competitions are slated to start Saturday, June 26.

Teams will be competing in dual matches consisting of three doubles matchups; in order to claim the overall dual victory, a team must win at least two of those three courts.

The 6.0 competition is the smallest on the weekend, with only seven squads entered; four have been placed into Flight 1 and the remaining three make up Flight 2. The pair of flight winners will advance to Sunday championship dual, which will take to the courts at 8 a.m.

For the 8.0 division, 12 teams have been equally divided into three flights. Their round-robin matches will take place over the course of Friday and Saturday, resulting in the trio of flight champions along with the top runner-up moving on to Sunday’s knockout stages.

Boasting a combined 17 crews in its four flights, the 7.0 competition is by far the largest of the event. Two busy days of flight play on Saturday and Sunday will give way to the four flight winners securing a place in the divisional semifinals; those duals are scheduled for 7:30 a.m. Monday morning with the final to follow at approximately 10:45 a.m.

The 10-team 9.0 division will help keep the courts full on Saturday and Sunday, as the crews jockey for position within the two flights. Every dual will be hotly contested, as just the two first-place finishers will earn the right to compete for the team title Monday morning at 8 a.m.

Defending champions include the 6.0 Volusia (Florida Tennis Center – Daytona Beach) team, the 7.0 Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) squad, the 8.0 Broward (Weston Tennis Center) crew, and the 9.0 Pinellas (Innisbrook Resort – Palm Harbor) team.
