Team USA Wins Junior Fed Cup

Andrew Robinson | October 1, 2019

style
article-hero

Team USA won its third consecutive Junior Fed Cup title, and perhaps making it sweeter, the team did so on home soil at the USTA National Campus.

Team USA defeated Czech Republic 2-1 in the final match, with the doubles team of Connie Ma and Robin Montgomery clinching the title in the decisive doubles match with a 6-2, 7-5 victory.

Katrina Scott was also a member of the team, which was captained by Jamea Jackson.

The American boys fell short in Junior Davis Cup, falling to Japan 2-1 after losing its decisive doubles match. The team consisted of Martin Damm, Toby Kodat and Dali Blanch and was captained by Philippe Oudshoorn.

Team USA was into the Junior Davis Cup final after facing seven match points in elimination matches in the quarterfinals and semifinals.

Junior Davis and Fed Cup will return to the USTA National Campus through 2021.
