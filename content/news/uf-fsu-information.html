MARCH 10 FAN INFORMATION

Brian Ormiston | March 8, 2017

style
article-hero

The USTA National Campus will be at the center of college tennis on March 10. For fans who are making plans to enjoy College MatchDay and the rivalry between Florida and Florida State, here are some key things to know before arriving on campus.

Fans are encouraged to arrive early to enjoy the festivities as well as secure seats or standing-room locations. The USTA National Campus is expecting a full stadium to enjoy an incredible atmosphere showcasing college tennis at the highest level.

First Serve

-First serve at the collegiate complex is at 6 p.m.

Festivities

-Before every College MatchDay at 4 p.m., fans will be treated to live music, games, food, school-inspired drinks and much more.

-A free kids’ clinic will take place at the collegiate complex starting at 4:45 p.m.

-On March 10, the UCF men and Tulsa also will be playing at the collegiate complex at 6 p.m. This match will take place on the north side (courts 7-12) while UF-FSU will be on the south side (courts 1-6).

Admission

-Admission is free.

Parking

-Parking is free.

-Valet parking is available for $5 in front of the Welcome Center.

-Overflow parking will be on Wellspring Dr., which leads up to the USTA National Campus.

Seating

-Standing-room only is available on the viewing deck that overlooks all courts at the collegiate complex.

-Overflow seating will be available outside of Court 6. To access this seating, take the stairs up to the viewing deck, walk to the end of the deck and take the stairs down.

Weather

-As of March 8, the forecast for March 10 is a high of 86 degrees, low of 62 degrees with a 20 percent chance of precipitation.

Contact the USTA National Campus

-(407) 675-2500

-10000 USTA Blvd., Orlando, FL 32827

-The USTA National Campus entrance is on Wellspring Dr., off Lake Nona Blvd.

TV

-All matches air on ESPN3 through WatchESPN and the ESPN app.
