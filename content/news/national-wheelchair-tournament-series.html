National Wheelchair Tournament Series

Off to Successful Start

Dan Pyser | September 1, 2017

style
article-hero

The 2017 Wheelchair Tournament Series launched at the USTA National Campus last Saturday. Ten more similarly structured tournaments – one day, round robin doubles competition – will occur across the country in the coming months. The tournaments are designed to encourage novice players to participate in tournament play by promoting a social, competitive environment.

Last weekend’s event featured two divisions: Rollers and Wheelies. The Rollers were made up of a combination between Open and A players while the Wheelies division featured players that normally compete in the B and C divisions. John Becker was the winner of the Rollers, while the Wheelies had a tie for the top spot between Donald Shapiro and Signe Dietrichson. Players competed in a total of five rounds of matches, with the total number of games won added up at the end to determine the final winner. For photos from the event, click here.
