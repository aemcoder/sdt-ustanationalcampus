Wheelchair World Team Cup Americas Qualifications

January 29, 2018

For the first time in the history of the event, the USTA welcomes the BNP Paribas Wheelchair World Team Cup Americas Qualifications to the USTA National Campus. Play gets underway on Tuesday, Jan. 30 and runs through Friday, Feb. 2. Each day’s schedule will feature squads going head-to-head in dual matches.

A total of three men’s and two women’s teams from North and South America will be in action, with their sights focused on advancing to the BNP World Team Cup later this year. The nations of Chile, Colombia, Ecuador and United States will be represented on the men's side while Argentina and Colombia will do battle in women's dual action. Each team match will consist of two singles and one doubles matchup featuring three tiebreak sets and regular scoring. 

The Team USA contingent will be made up of Casey Ratzlaff, Chris Herman and Todd Hanover.

In last year’s Americas Qualifications, the Brazil men defeated Team USA in the finals while the Brazil women claimed the title over Argentina.

The National Campus will serve as one of four host sites for the regional qualifying competitions for the ITF’s flagship wheelchair tennis team event. The African Qualifications will be held in Nairobi, Kenya, on Feb. 15-18; the Asian Qualifications will take place in Colombo, Sri Lanka, on Feb. 22-25; and the European Qualifications are set for Vilamoura, Portugal, April 11-15.

The winners of the men’s and women’s events at each of the four regional qualifiers will earn a spot at the 2018 BNP World Team Cup, which will be hosted by the KNLTB in Apeldoorn, Netherlands, May 28 through June 3. Men’s, women’s and quad competitions, as well as a junior event supported by the Cruyff Foundation, will be contested in Apeldoorn.
