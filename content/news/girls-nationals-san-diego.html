The USTA Girls' 16s / 18s Nationals in San Diego is our NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP - the most important girls amateur tournament in the US.  The honor roll of past champions includes many the sport's greatest players (Chris Evert, Tracy Austin, Jennifer Capriati, Lindsay Davenport), and past participants include all of the current crop of top American women on the WTA Tour.  

The 18 & Under singles and doubles champions receive Wildcards into the US Open next month. Broadcasting this National Junior Championship LIVE on Tennis Channel is unprecedented.  

The schedule is as follows:

Saturday, August 12 from 2:30 PM - 8:30 PM ET (11:30 - 5:30 PM PT)  

*immediately following the Rogers Cup from Canada.

Saturday's telecast will include the Girls' 16s singles final and both 18s semifinals.

Sunday, August 13 from 4:00 - 6:00 PM ET (1:00 - 3:00 PM PT)  

* immediately following the Rogers Cup and Western & Southern first round

Sunday's match will feature the Girls' 18s singles final.  

Commentators are scheduled to be Chanda Rubin and Christen Bartelt.
