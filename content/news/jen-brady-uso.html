Orlando Residents

Earn First US Open Wins

Dan Pyser | September 1, 2017

style
article-hero

The US Open main draw began Monday at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing, N.Y. Orlando residents Bjorn Fratangelo and Jennifer Brady both earned their first-career US Open victories on Tuesday. Fratangelo topped former world No. 14 Ivo Karlovic in his third main draw appearance before falling in the second round to seeded Adrian Mannarino on Thursday. Brady, making her US Open main draw debut, kept her perfect record alive, advancing to the third round Thursday after dismantling 23rd-seeded Barbora Strycova 6-1, 6-1 in less than an hour.

Also advancing to the third round on Thursday was 15th-seeded Madison Keys, who cruised to a straight-sets victory. For photos of the Orlando residents who train at the USTA National Campus competing in New York, click here.

Orlando residents Tommy Paul, Frances Tiafoe and CiCi Bellis all made appearances in this year’s main draw. Tiafoe was part of one of the highlights from the opening round, falling to 19-time Grand Slam champion Roger Federer in a thrilling five-set match in Arthur Ashe Stadiun. The 19-year-old pushed the third-seeded Federer to the brink after winning the first set and forcing a fifth with impressive play that earned praise from one of the game’s all-time greats.
