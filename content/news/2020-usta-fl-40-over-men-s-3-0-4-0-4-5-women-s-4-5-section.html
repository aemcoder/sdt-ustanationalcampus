2020 USTA FL 40 & Over 3.0/4.0/4.5+ Sectional Championships

Matches Begin Friday Morning

October 29, 2020

style
article-hero

A busy fall of Sunshine State league play at the USTA National Campus continues this weekend with the 2020 USTA FL 40 & Over Men’s 3.0/4.0/4.5+ & Women’s 4.5+ Sectional Championships. Four more crews will take home team titles throughout the four-day event that kicks off the morning of Friday, Oct. 30 and wraps up Monday, Nov. 2.

Teams will be representing regions from across the state, squaring off in dual matches made up of three courts – one line of singles and two lines of doubles. In order to claim the dual victory, a team must win at least two of those courts.

The men’s 4.0 competition will kick off the event Friday morning, as the 13 teams making up the field will each take part in two duals. The round robin portion of the division will be played out over the course of Friday and Saturday, with the trio of flight winners plus the top second-place finisher advancing to Sunday’s knockout stages.

Getting underway Saturday afternoon and crowning its champion Sunday afternoon, the men’s 3.0 division features only four squads – Alachua, Broward, Duval, and Marion. They will play each other just once, making the weekend a true round robin playoff to determine the sectional champion.

Both men’s and women’s 4.5+ competitions will also take to the courts starting Saturday. The two divisions are set up in similar fashions, with the teams being divided into two flights to begin the weekend and only the flight winners earning the right to battle for the title.
