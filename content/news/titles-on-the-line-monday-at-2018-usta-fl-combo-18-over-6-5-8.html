Titles on the Line Monday

December 2, 2018

A total of four team titles will be up for grabs on Monday at the 2018 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. The duals for the 6.5 divisional finals have been set while the 8.5 action will feature both semifinal and final rounds.

In the men’s 6.5 division, the competition has been whittled down to the final, where Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) and Broward (City of Oakland Park) are set to square off. Marion did drop their final dual in Flight 1, but the squad had already clinched a place in the championship round prior to taking the court. On the other hand, Broward enters Monday having swept through Flight 2 on the strength of dropping just one individual matchup and two sets.

Marion (Fort King Tennis Center), Hillsborough (FishHawk Ranch), Escambia (Independent), and SMD (Alper JCC) navigated their way through flight play unscathed at 3-0 to move on to the 6.5 women’s semifinals. Those matchups pitted Marion against Hillsborough and Escambia versus SMD.

Both duals ended in 2-1 results with Hillsborough and Escambia prevailing. For Hillsborough, Jackie Caouette and team captain Krissy Heybroek cruised 6-1, 6-0 at the top spot, while Shannon Deck and Kim Ortmeier held on 7-6, 6-1 on court two. Marion avoided the sweep with a 3-6, 6-4, 1-0 comeback at line three. Escambia collected wins in the contests at the second and third positions to defeat SMD. Lisa Robertson and Alexandra Mauro took the only court that finished up in straight sets (6-1, 6-4). That was coupled with Jennifer Jackson and Tara Baril getting through 6-3, 3-6, 1-0.

In the men’s 8.5 division, Escambia (Independent) was the lone squad to fight through the first two days of play without losing a dual to win Flight 1. Sarasota (Payne Park) and Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA) each needed the tiebreaking procedure to help them claim Flight 2 and 3, respectively. Rounding out the final four was SMD (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center), which earned the wild card slot by finishing second in Flight 3. Monday’s duals will see Escambia across the net from Duval and Sarasota battling SMD.

Escambia (Independent) will also be the heavy favorite in the women’s 8.5 semifinals on Monday thanks to a comprehensive effort in Flight 3, posting a 4-0 record thanks to winning 11 of the 12 courts they contested. Their semifinal opponent will be SMD (Coral Oaks Tennis Club), the undefeated representative from Flight 1. Also surviving round robin play was Sarasota (Longwood Athletic Center), the top finisher in Flight 2, and wild card entrant Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Center), the flight runner-up with the best winning percentage in individual matchups.
