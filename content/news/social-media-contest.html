USTA National Campus Social MVP Contest Official Rules

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN.

EACH ENTRANT MUST BE AT LEAST FOURTEEN (14) YEARS OF AGE AS OF THE ENTRY DATE OF THE SWEEPSTAKES. IF YOU ARE A MINOR, ASK YOUR PARENT OR LEGAL GUARDIAN FOR PERMISSION AND TO HELP YOU ENTER.

 

EACH ENTRANT (AND EACH MINOR ENTRANT’S PARENT OR LEGAL GUARDIAN ON BEHALF OR HIMSELF/HERSELF AND ON THE MINOR’S BEHALF) AGREES TO THESE OFFICIAL RULES.

The object of the USTA National Campus Social MVP Contest (the "Contest") is to submit an original picture or video capturing your time at the USTA National Campus (the “Submission”). The Submission must be an original picture or video. All eligible Submissions will be reviewed by a panel of judges selected by the United States Tennis Association Incorporated (the “Judges”) who will select the most compelling Submission in accordance with these Official Rules and based upon criteria set forth in the Judging section below.

 

ENTRY PERIOD. The Contest entry period begins on September 20, 2018, at 12:01 AM Eastern Time (“ET”) and ends on September 22, 2018, at 11:59 AM ET (the “Entry Period”).

ELIGIBILITY.  The Contest is open only to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia, who are at least 14 years of age or older. Eligible minors must obtain their parents or legal guardian’s permission prior to participating.  Do not enter this Contest if you are not a legal resident of and located in the United States or the District of Columbia at the time of entry. Void outside the 50 United States, the District of Columbia and where prohibited by law. Employees of the United States Tennis Association Incorporated (“USTA”), Employees of the USTA Sectional Associations, and their respective subsidiaries, affiliates, parent companies, sales representatives, agents, advertising and promotion agencies or any other agencies or entities involved with this Contest and members of their respective families (parents, siblings, children, and spouse and their respective spouses) and/or those residing in the same household (whether related or not) of each of the above are not eligible to enter or win.  By participating in this Contest, participants (and each minor participant’s parent or legal guardian on behalf of himself/herself and on the minor’s behalf) agree to be bound by these Official Rules. The USTA reserves the right to disqualify from this Contest any person it believes has intentionally violated these Rules.

HOW TO ENTER.  To enter, participants must: 1) follow the @USTA account on Instagram, Twitter, and/or Facebook; and  2) post a picture or 30 seconds or less video from your event at the USTA National Campus with the hashtag  #NationalCampus.  All entries must be received by 11:59:59 a.m. ET on September 22, 2018.  By fully completing and submitting all required information you will be automatically entered in the Contest and be a “Participant.”  Limit one entry per person/per household.  All entries become the sole property of the USTA.  Entries will be declared made by the authorized account holder of the e-mail address submitted at the time of entry.  “Authorized account holder” is defined as the natural person who is assigned to the registration e-mail address by an Internet access provider, on-line service provider, or other organization (such as a business, educational institution, etc.) that is responsible for assigning e-mail addresses for the domain associated with the submitted e-mail address.  The authorized account holder may be required to provide proof of identity.  Any other attempted form of entry is prohibited; no automatic, programmed, robotic or similar means of entry are permitted.

Submission Requirements:  Participants must send their Submission as directed during the Contest entry process.  Submissions must be an original work (owned) by Participant and that does not violate the intellectual property of another. Submissions must not contain material that is inappropriate, indecent, obscene, sexually explicit, hateful, tortious, defamatory, slanderous or libelous. Submissions cannot contain images inconsistent with the positive image and goodwill to which the USTA wishes to associate. Compliance with the foregoing requirements shall be determined by USTA in its sole discretion and USTA may, in its sole discretion, remove or discard any Submission.

Online entry only. No other method of entry will be accepted.  Participants are solely responsible for Internet connectivity, software and/or hardware that may be required in order to create and/or submit any Submission.

All entries must include a valid social media account for the Participant (or participant’s parent or legal guardian).  All information submitted by Participants is subject to, and will be treated in a manner consistent with, the USTA Terms of Use accessible at https://www.usta.com/en/home/about-usta/who-we-are/national/usta-terms-of-use.html and Privacy Policy accessible at https://www.usta.com/en/home/about-usta/who-we-are/national/usta-privacy-policy.html. Participants must fully complete and submit all non-optional data requested to be eligible.  Incomplete and/or inaccurate entries are void.  In case of multiple entries received from any person or email address or household, only one entry received from such person or account is eligible to win.  By participating and completing a submission Participants (and each minor participant’s parent or legal guardian on behalf of himself/herself and on the minor’s behalf) indicate their agreement with these Official Rules. Submissions that do not comply with the Submission Requirements, the Content Restrictions or otherwise with these Official Rules will be void, eliminated and not eligible for consideration.

JUDGING. The Judges will review all eligible Submissions based on the following criteria: i) Originality (25%); ii) Creativity (25%); iii) Adherence to Theme (25%); and iv) Artistic Impression (25%). On or about September 22, 2018, the most compelling Submission as determined by the Judges will be the winning Submission. In the event of a tie, the Creativity score will be used as the tiebreaker. Sponsor may, in its sole discretion, extend the submission period or elect not to determine a winner of the Contest.
