Orlando Clay Championships Level 7 and Orange Level 1

Play Concludes with Eight Champions Crowned

February 25, 2018

style
article-hero

The USTA Florida Orlando Clay Championships Level 7 and Orange Level 1 concluded on Sunday, crowning a total of eight champions on the day. Showcasing play in all age divisions, this intermediate-level event gave juniors who already have experience with tournament play the opportunity to earn ranking points.

Only three No. 1 seeds prevailed in their respective draws – Arthur Radulescu (boys’ 16s), Madison Molock (girls’ 12s) and Brenda Yoris (girls’ 14s). Molock was the most dominant player on the weekend, giving away a mere total of five games across her three matches while winning an impressive five sets by a 6-0 margin. Yoris responded emphatically to being on the brink of elimination in her opening match, posting a pair of straight-set victories on Sunday to clinch the title. Radulescu battled through his semifinal encounter with Dylan Cass 6-0, 4-6, 10-8 before holding off Harrison Michalowski 7-5, 6-2 in the final.

In the boys’ 12s championship contest, the draw’s top two seeds went head-to-head. Neither No. 1 seed Jae Centonze or No. 2 seed Hayden Scragg had surrendered a set in reaching the final, and Scragg was able to maintain that level, cruising past Centonze 6-2, 6-0. Boys’ 14s top seed Edmund Bennett appeared in top form ahead of his final round encounter with second seed Tyler Leaird, losing only two games in six sets. Bennett recorded his fifth 6-0 set of the tournament to take the first set, but Leaird bounced back to steal the second by a 6-2 margin and held on to claim an extended match tiebreak, 11 points to nine. Boys’ 18s No. 1 seed Nicholas Olomu also advanced to the championship, only to be upended by Daniel Blackman 7-5, 7-5.

The championship match of the girls’ 16s draw pitted No. 2 seed Logan Voeks against third seed Juanita Cabrera. Voeks had won all of her previous matches in straight sets while Cabrera outlasted unseeded Meadow Garcia in the semifinals, 10-7 in a decisive match tiebreak. Voeks would prevail in two tightly-contested sets, both by the margin of 6-4, for the title. The only unseeded final on Sunday, Valentina Serino came through with a 6-1, 6-2 defeat of Demme Kraut to claim the girls’ 18s crown.
