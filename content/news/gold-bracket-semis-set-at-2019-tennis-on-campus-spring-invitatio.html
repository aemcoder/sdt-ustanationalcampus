Gold Bracket Semis Set

March 30, 2019

style
article-hero

Bracket play kicked off on Saturday at the 2019 Tennis on Campus Spring Invitational, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Two rounds in each of the five brackets were contested throughout the second day of the event.

All dual matches are contested using the World TeamTennis (WTT) format, consisting of five no-ad sets – men’s singles, men’s doubles, women’s singles, women’s doubles, and mixed doubles; mixed doubles is always played last.

In the Gold Bracket, the four teams battling their way to the semifinal stage are Boston University, Cal Poly, Georgia, and Pittsburgh. Georgia turned in a pair of comprehensive efforts on the day, first cruising by Rowan 25-8 before powering past UC Davis 30-8 in the quarterfinals. The opponent for the Bulldogs will be Cal Poly, who opened Saturday with a 29-18 victory over Minnesota and followed that up with a tight 26-19 win against Cornell.

Pittsburgh barely survived the round of 16, besting UCF by the slim margin of 26-21. They took on Wash U in the final eight, securing a 28-19 triumph. Meanwhile, Boston University dominated Arkansas 30-14 in their morning contest but were pushed to the limit by UCLA in the quarterfinals, eventually prevailing 26-22.

The remaining brackets will see their teams take to the courts for just one match tomorrow. Harvard and MIT will square off in the Silver Bracket final, while the Bronze Bracket is down to Nevada and Washington State. Either Portland or Wisconsin will be crowned the winner of the Copper Bracket, and the Titanium Bracket features a final matchup between College of Charleston and Winona State.

Bracket play action continues at 8 a.m. on Saturday, including the Gold Bracket semifinals; the championship match will be contested at approximately noon.
