A weekend after weather heavily impacted play for the 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional, Saturday provided a steamy but beautiful opening day of play for the USTA Florida 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships.

In the women’s 2.5 division, Orange/Seminole (Isleworth) is just one dual victory away from advancing to Monday’s championship round while Duval (Deerwood) has already secured its spot. In the three-team Flight 1, Orange/Seminole needs only to defeat Hillsborough (Arbor Greene) in Sunday’s rematch to move on. Meanwhile, Duval producing a pair of sweeps on the day coupled with the three other squads in Flight 2 coming up with two individual victories each has mathematically clinched the flight for Duval before a ball is struck Sunday.

For the 3.5 women’s competition, Flight 2 remains a toss-up going into the second day of play thanks to Brevard (Titan Tennis Center), Escambia, Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) and Duval (Julington Creek Plantation) all sitting at 1-1. Brevard holds the slight advantage with six court victories compared to five for Escambia and Hillsborough along with four for Duval. In Flight 1, Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) went 2-0 Saturday to claim the inside track on a semifinal berth. The performance of the day came in Flight 3, where not only did Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) go undefeated but did so without the loss of a single set. Volusia (Florida Tennis Center) looks in good position in Flight 4 as the lone unbeaten, but a trio of squads at 1-1 will be looking make a move Sunday.

Looking at where things stand in the men’s 3.5 division, Flight 1 will come down to Sunday’s dual between Broward (City of Oakland Park) and Orange/Seminole (National Campus); both produced 2-0 efforts on Saturday, with Broward taking all 10 individual matchups and Orange/Seminole winning nine. Flights 2, 3 and 4 all tell a similar story – one undefeated team needing just to take care of its final round robin dual Sunday to move into the semifinals. Duval (Eagle Harbor) in Flight 3 and Collier (Longshore Lake) in Flight 4 will be taking on the last place team in those flights, putting them in line for advancement. Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) will be up against Hillsborough (Hunters Green), which despite a 1-1 record ended the day with the same amount of court victories and sets lost as Marion.

A total of four teams are just a dual win away from a spot in the women’s 4.5 semifinals. Hillsborough (Cheval Athletic Club) will survive to play on Monday with a victory over Broward (Weston Hills) or a Broward loss to Leon in Flight 1. Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club) and Orange/Seminole (Sanlando) will battle on Sunday with the top spot in Flight 2 on the line. A dominant opening day effort from Duval (Glen Kernan) as all but locked up Flight 3. All teams will be fighting to win every single game they can with a wild card berth into the semifinals still to be claimed.

Duval (Independent) and Broward (City of Fort Lauderdale) look solid to move on from Flight 1 and Flight 2, respectively, and into the men’s 4.5 semifinals. Duval prevailed on eight courts and lost six sets; Broward was a winner on nine total courts while dropping only four sets. A few more scenarios could play out in Flight 3, where both Escambia and Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex) are 2-0 with two duals remaining for each. Sarasota (Longwood) is also in the hunt after a 1-1 day Saturday, and even if they are unable to claim the top position in the flight, an undefeated Sunday could secure them the semifinal wild card.
