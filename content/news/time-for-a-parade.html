The Florida Citrus Parade in downtown Orlando is always a major event in the Sunshine State, and the USTA National Campus was well represented on Dec. 29.

Witnessed by thousands in person and even more from around the world on television or online, the Florida Citrus Parade is one of the largest sports-affiliated parades in the nation according to WESH. It coincides with the Russell Athletic Bowl and the Buffalo Wild Wings Citrus Bowl at Camping World Stadium.

US Fed Cup Captain Kathy Rinaldi joined the USTA's float which featured a miniature tennis court. She was joined by USTA National Promotions Manager Marilyn Sherman as they hit a few balls on the parade route.
