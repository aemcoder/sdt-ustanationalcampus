Starting at 8 a.m. ET on Saturday, Dec. 31, players will be allowed to Book a Court for the first time at the USTA National Campus. As a thank you to those booking courts for Jan. 2-8, the USTA is providing an introductory offer with players receiving 50% off the published court fees.

The National Campus opens to the public on Monday, Jan. 2, at 7:30 a.m., with open play beginning at 8 a.m. Court reservations throughout the year can be made 48 hours in advance at USTANationalCampus.com.

When booking online for Jan. 2-8, the price will automatically record the discount at check out.
