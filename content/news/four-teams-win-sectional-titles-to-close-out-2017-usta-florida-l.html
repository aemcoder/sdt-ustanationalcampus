USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships

Four Teams Win Sectional Titles on Sunday

December 10, 2017

style
article-hero

The 2017 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships, held at the USTA National Campus, wrapped up on Sunday as four teams won sectional crowns. With the conclusion of the tournament, the 2017 USTA Florida League season comes to a close.

The women’s 5.5 division featured only a championship match on the day, in which Duval took on Leon. Duval raced through the matchup at the top doubles spot 6-2, 6-1 but Leon claimed the second position 6-4, 6-4. That left line three as the decider, where Duval earned the opening set 6-3 and hung on to prevail in the match tiebreak after losing the second frame 6-4.

In men’s 7.5 action, both Polk and SMD survived the semifinals with 2-1 triumphs to move on to the final. All three courts between Polk and Marion ended in straight sets, as Polk took the first and third spots to advance. SMD was pushed to the limit by Orange/Seminole, even after winning the top position 6-0, 6-1. Orange/Seminole outlasted SMD on court two, 6-4, 3-6, 1-0, but it wasn’t enough; SMD came through 7-5, 4-6, 1-0 on line three.

The Polk versus SMD dual was a hotly contested one. Polk managed to secure court one by a 6-3, 6-4 margin and was in good position after winning opening sets at the second and third spots as well. SMD rallied back to split sets in both contests, but Polk finally put the match out of reach by winning the match tiebreak on court two.

Broward and Orange/Seminole took very different paths to reach the championship round in the women’s 7.5 competition. In one semifinal, Broward responded with its back against the wall to defeat Duval 2-1. Broward eased through the matchup at line three, 6-2, 6-2 but was down a set on the other two lines. Duval evened the score with a 3-6, 6-4, 1-0 victory at the top spot, but in a contest that could have gone either way, Broward rallied 6-7, 7-5, 1-0. In contrasting fashion, Orange/Seminole swept Marion 3-0 without dropping a set. Only one of the six sets was closer than 6-4.

In the championship, Orange/Seminole proved to be the dominant squad once again, earning the team title with a 2-1 result over Broward. None of the three matchups were extended to match tiebreaks, with Orange/Seminole producing a 6-2, 6-3 win on court one and a 7-5, 6-3 triumph at the third spot.   

The lone match of the day in women’s 9.5 play, Pinellas defeated Alachua 2-1 for the sectional crown. Alachua got off to an impressive start, sprinting to victory on court one 6-1, 6-2. Alachua staked an early lead on line two as well before Pinellas roared back in the second set, 6-0, and kept the momentum to prevail in the match tiebreak. On court three, Alachua was on the brink of defeat after losing the opening set 6-2 but hung tough to win the second in a tiebreak. With everything on the line in the match tiebreak, it was Pinellas coming up with the goods.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
