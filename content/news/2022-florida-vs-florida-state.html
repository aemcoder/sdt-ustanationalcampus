Gators Sweep College MatchDay

February 5, 2022

style
article-hero

After a year hiatus, College MatchDay returned to the USTA National Campus on Feb. 5 in front of an energetic crowd that had to endure over an hour rain delay. In one of the biggest rivalries in college sports though, the rain did not damper spirits. When the weather cleared, Florida pulled out identical 4-1 victories for both the men and the women against Florida State.
