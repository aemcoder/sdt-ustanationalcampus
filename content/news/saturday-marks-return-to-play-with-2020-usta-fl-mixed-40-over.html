Saturday Marks Return to Play

September 19, 2020

style
article-hero

Tournament play is officially back at the USTA National Campus, as a warm and sunny September Saturday welcomed participants to the opening day of the 2020 USTA FL Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships.

Volusia (Florida Tennis Center – Daytona Beach) sits atop the lone division in the 6.0 competition at the end of day one thanks to sweeping a pair of duals; overall, their players dropped only one set and 33 games. Hillsborough (Ridge at Wiregrass Ranch – Wesley Chapel) also closed out Saturday with a 2-0 record, setting up what is likely to be the decisive matchup on Sunday when they clash with Volusia. Also still in the running is Duval (Deercreek Country Club – Jacksonville), which went 1-1 and will need some help to bring home the divisional title.

A trio of squads put themselves in prime position to advance to the 8.0 semifinals while the remaining teams in the field may have to set their sights on securing the lone wild card slot. IRMSL and Broward vaulted to the top of Flights 2 and 3, respectively, by claiming five of the six courts their players contested to post a record of 2-0. Additionally, Volusia is the lone unbeaten in Flight 1 thanks to earning four individual matchups. In the wild card race, Pinellas from Flight 2 currently holds the inside position.

The 6.0 champion will be crowned Sunday afternoon while the winner on the 8.0 side will be determined Monday afternoon.

Action continues at 7:30 a.m. Sunday morning. For more information on the 2020 USTA FL Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships, click here.
