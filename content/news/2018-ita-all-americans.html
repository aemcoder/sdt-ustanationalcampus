Americans claim ITA All-American trophies

Whittle, Wolf Rise to the Top

Brian Ormiston | October 10, 2018

style
article-hero

Gonzaga’s Sophie Whittle (Nipomo, Calif.) delivered a stunning victory in California at the Riviera ITA Women’s All-American Championship on Oct. 7. One day later, in Oklahoma, Ohio State’s JJ Wolf (Cincinnati) captured the crown of the Saint Francis Health System ITA Men’s All-American Championship on Oct. 8.

By winning their respective ITA All-American titles, Whittle and Wolf both secured spots on the 2019 USTA Collegiate National Team. Team members receive grant money to play USTA Pro Circuit events throughout the summer, as well as coaching and training from USTA Player Development staff. Previous NCAA student-athletes who were a part of the team include James Blake, Jennifer Brady, Bob Bryan, Mike Bryan, Irina Falconi, Nicole Gibbs, John Isner, Steve Johnson and Mackenzie McDonald.

Whittle came out of qualifying, winning three matches, highlighted by a thrilling three-set win in her final qualifying contest, to earn her spot in the main draw. En route to the finals, she came from behind twice and also defeated Duke’s Kelly Chen, a WTA Top-900 American, in straight sets. Whittle squeaked out a semifinal victory over the fifth-seeded Fernanda Contreras, from Vanderbilt, then surprised Duke’s Maria Mateas to claim the title, 6-4, 5-7, 6-3. Mateas was ranked in the WTA’s Top 300 this past summer and is ranked No. 1 in the ITA Top 10 Freshmen/Newcomers chart for 2018-19.

Wolf entered the draw as a seeded player, only surrendering a combined 10 games in his first three matches. He edged fellow American Alex Lebedev of Notre Dame in the quarterfinals, 6-4, 7-6(2), and topped American Emil Reinberg of Georgia in the semifinals, 6-0, 7-6(4). Wolf continued to roll in the title match, getting past Mississippi State’s Nuno Borges, 6-0, 7-6(3).
