The first day of the 2019 USTA Florida Combo 50 & Over Sectional Championships got off to a delayed start, and therefore a much later than anticipated finish, on Saturday at the USTA National Campus due to heavy rainfall overnight.

Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) has all but locked up the men’s 6.5 sectional title after defeating Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) twice on Saturday. Both matches were one-sided, as Marion won five of the six courts and 11 of the 13 sets played.

On the 6.5 women’s side, Flight 1 will come down to the contest between Escambia (Independent) and SMD (Salvadore Park – Coral Gables) while Hillsborough (Arbor Greene Club – Tampa) is just one more victory away from claiming Flight 2. In the lone flight featuring five teams, Pinellas (Seminole Lake Family Tennis) and IRMSL (Harbor Ridge – Palm City) sit at 2-0 in Flight 3 while Sarasota (The Venetian Golf & River Club – North Venice) is still in the mix at 1-1 overall.

In the men’s 7.5 competition, unbeaten Sarasota (Lakewood Ranch Athletic) and Collier (Vineyards Golf & Country Club – Naples) look to be on a collision course in Flight 1 with a spot in the championship dual on the line; however, Pinellas (Treasure Island Tennis Club) could either play spoiler or finish atop the standings themselves after splitting their matches on day one. Flight 2 is in the firm grasp of Hillsborough (Temple Terrace Recreation Department), the lone team to go 2-0 in the flight on Saturday.

Looking at the three flights of the women’s 7.5 division, only Flight 1 has two squads remaining undefeated, as Brevard (Rockledge) and Collier (Greater Marco Family YMCA – Marco Island) are both 2-0 overall; the winner of their meeting will move on to the semifinals with the loser still in the running for the wild card slot. Flight 2 will go to Escambia (Independent) should they earn a win in their lone dual on Sunday, while the same scenario is set up for Pinellas (East Lake Woodlands Country Club – Oldsmar) in Flight 3.

With the four clubs in the men’s 8.5 competition just play once today, all remain alive for the sectional crown going into the second day of the event. Hillsborough (Avila Golf and Country Club – Tampa) and SMD (Don Shula’s Athletic Club – Hialeah) were victorious on Saturday, while both Pinellas (East Lake Woodlands Country Club – Oldsmar) and Brevard (Titan Tennis Center – Melbourne) will look to bounce back from their losses. On the 8.5 women’s side, Sarasota (Lakewood National – Bradenton) has put themselves in prime position as the only unscathed team remaining of the five in the division.

Sunday’s matches are set to begin at 8 a.m. For more information on the 2019 USTA Florida Combo 50 & Over Sectional Championships, click here.
