40 & Over Divisions in Action

November 14, 2020

style
article-hero

Saturday marked the beginning of a busy four days of Sunshine State league play on the grounds of the USTA National Campus, as the 2020 USTA Florida 40 & Over Women’s 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships got underway. First matches of the USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Invitational are slated to take the court for the first time Sunday morning.

In the 3.0 competition, a total of five squads made it through the day unscathed. Flight 1 will likely come down to the winner of Sunday’s dual between Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala), both at 2-0, while 1-1 Duval (Jacksonville Golf and Country Club) also remains in the hunt.

Meanwhile, Flight 2 is guaranteed to be taken by either homestanding Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) or Miami-Dade (Doral Park Country Club), as they sit atop the standings with one round left to play. Another team representing Miami-Dade, the crew out of Kendall Tennis Training Center in Miami, has full control of Flight 3 as the lone unbeaten.

For the 4.0 division, any of the squads in Flight 1 could take the top spot after all four went 1-1 on Saturday; Sunday morning’s duals will see Brevard (Rockledge Park) take on Miami-Dade (Dante Fascell Park – Miami) and Sarasota (Longwood Athletic Club – Sarasota) go up against Hillsborough (FishHawk Ranch – Lithia). Flights 2 and 3 mirror each other, with a pair of teams going 2-0 and another pair slipping to 0-2; Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) or Pinellas (City of Clearwater) will emerge from Flight 2 while Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) or Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) will grab Flight 3’s automatic berth into the semifinals.

Sunday’s 40 & Over duals are slated for a 7:30 a.m. start and the 65 & Over Invitational will begin at 9 a.m.
