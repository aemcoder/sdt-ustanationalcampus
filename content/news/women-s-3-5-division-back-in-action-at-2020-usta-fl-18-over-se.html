3.5 Women Back in Action

December 8, 2020

Day five of the 2020 USTA Florida 18 & Over Men’s 3.0/3.5/4.5 & Women’s 3.5/4.0 Sectional Championships was all about the women’s 3.5 competition, which did not play a single point on Monday due to rain. By the end of Tuesday, all but the champion had been determined; the final dual of the women’s 3.5 division is set for 7:30 a.m. Wednesday morning.

A full slate of round-robin matches over the course of Tuesday morning and afternoon produced the division’s four semifinalists. Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) and home-standing Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) claimed Flights 1 and 2, respectively, without suffering a loss. Meanwhile, Miami-Dade (Don Shulas Athletic Club – Hialeah) was the only squad in Flight 3 to post a winning record, also allowing them to advance. Rounding out the crews moving on was wild card entrant Collier (Riverstone Community – Naples), which was the top second-place finisher as the runner-up in Flight 3.

Miami-Dade powered their way past Hillsborough at the semifinal stage, headlined by Ali Quintero’s 6-0, 6-2 victory in singles. Audrey Gayle and Ivet Hernandez took the first doubles court 6-2, 7-6, while teammates Andrea Gil and Rossana Okamura won at the second spot by the exact same score line to close out the sweep.

Orange/Seminole also received a pair of straight-set results over Collier to reach the divisional final. Alisa Wolfe won a tough first set before prevailing 7-5, 6-2 in singles; the duo of Amanda Barritt and Natalie Beavers dominated doubles line two 6-0 6-2. Collier’s Amanda Mallette and Eileen Valerio staved off the sweep thanks to a 2-6, 6-0, 1-0 comeback at the top doubles court.

Earning titles earlier in the event were the Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) 3.0 men, the Collier (Club at Longshore Lake – Naples) 3.5 men, the Miami-Dade (Kerdyk Biltmore Tennis Center – Coral Gables) 4.0 women, and the men’s 4.5 squad representing Hillsborough (Sandra W Freedman Tennis Complex – Tampa).
