A rain delay pushed play deep into Friday night, but the full Day 1 schedule of the 2017 USTA Florida Adult 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships was in the books by the time the clock struck midnight. Most teams got two matches under their belts as the divisional flights began to take shape.

In 3.0 women’s action, five teams remain undefeated going into Saturday's play, including both teams representing Duval. The Duval Florida Yacht Club squad sits atop Flight 1 along with Escambia, while Duval Eagle Harbor and SMD are both 2-0 in Flight 2. Collier is the lone team without a loss in Flight 3.

The 3.0 men’s division is composed of just three teams, meaning the champion will be decided after the double round-robin slate is complete. Leon leads the way so far, having defeated the other two teams in the flight on Friday.

Each of the women’s 4.0 flights saw only a single team survive Friday unscathed. Hillsborough (Flight 1), Orange/Seminole (Flight 2), Broward (Flight 3) and Volusia (Flight 4) have all positioned themselves to move on to the divisional semifinals. Both Orange/Seminole and Broward won all 10 individual courts contested on the day.

The men’s 4.0 division features three flights, two of which feature a pair of undefeated squads. SMD and Orange/Seminole have the inside track in Flight 2 while the winner of the Leon and Polk matchup on Saturday is likely to claim Flight 3. In Flight 1, Broward is one victory away from taking the four-team flight after a 2-0 effort on Friday.

Play continues on Saturday morning beginning at 8:30 a.m. All flight winners will be decided by the end of the day except for the lone men’s 3.0 flight.
