Field Set for Monday

The opening day of play at the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships, held at the USTA National Campus, just beat out the rain. All teams completed two matches over the course of Saturday.

The two dominant teams in the 6.0 division capped off undefeated runs through the round robin portion of the tournament, setting up a clash with the championship on the line Monday at 8:30 a.m. Alachua (Westside Park – Gainesville) cruised through the three-team Flight 1, taking 11 of the 12 courts they contested on the strength of just four sets lost. Duval (Fleming Island Plantation) was challenged a bit in Flight 2, but still managed to finish undefeated at 3-0 thanks to eight court victories and two sets dropped.

Four teams remain in the hunt for the 8.0 crown, as the divisional semifinals will get underway Monday morning at 8 a.m. Marion (Golden Ocala Golf and Equestrian Club) suffered a loss on Sunday, leaving them in a three-way tie atop Flight 1 and their fate to be determined by the tiebreaking procedure. It would be Marion’s 2-1 victory over Polk (City of Winter Haven Municipal Courts) all the way back on Saturday morning that proved the difference and pushed them into the semifinals.

Sarasota (Longboat Key Tennis Center) eased their way through Flight 2, winning all four duals thanks to letting just one court and two sets slip away. In Flight 3, Pinellas (East Lake Woodlands Country Club – Oldsmar) also posted a 4-0 record in Flight 3, but it was much more of a struggle, as they claimed eight courts with 12 sets lost. Rounding out the semifinalists is Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) after they went 3-1 overall, with their only setback coming against Sarasota. Monday’s matchups will pit Hillsborough taking on Pinellas and Sarasota going up against Marion.
