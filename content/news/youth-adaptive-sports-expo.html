The USTA will be hosting a Youth Adaptive Sports Expo on Saturday, May 25, 2019. The event will take place at the UCF College of Education Gymnasium from 10am to 4pm. This event is open to the public and kids are invited to participate in a fun and unique adaptive sports experience while there will be educational sessions offered for parents. Lunch will be provided.
