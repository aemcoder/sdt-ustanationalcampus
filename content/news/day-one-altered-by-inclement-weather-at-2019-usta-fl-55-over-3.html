Day One Altered by Inclement Weather

Rain and lightning arrived to the USTA National Campus late Saturday afternoon, forcing the opening day of the 2019 USTA Florida 55 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships to finish up indoors.

In the women’s 3.0 division, Collier ruled the day, as the squad representing the Greater Marco Family YMCA (Marco Island) went 2-0 in Flight 1 while The Players Club and Spa (Naples) team accomplished the same feat in Flight 2. Escambia (Independent) holds the inside track for Flight 3 thanks to a 2-0 effort on the day.

For the women’s 4.0 competition, Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) is one dual win shy of advancing to the semifinals after claiming two on Saturday to sit atop the Flight 1 standings. In the five-team Flight 2, only Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) ended the day unscathed at 2-0, with a trio of teams behind them at 1-1 overall. Meanwhile it appears Flight 3 will come down to a head-to-head clash between Duval (Boone Park – Jacksonville) and Orange/Seminole (Sylvan Lake Park – Sanford).

The lone men’s division will see Flight 1 be decided when Collier (Pelican Bay Community Park – Naples) and Duval (Amelia National – Fernandina Beach) take to the courts for a dual contest on Sunday; the two teams will enter day two undefeated at 2-0. South Palm Beach (Parkland Tennis Center) cruised through the opening day of Flight 2 while Volusia (Daytona Beach Shores Parks and Recreation) battled their way to first place in Flight 3.

Action picks back up at 8 a.m. Sunday. Included in the day’s scheduled matches are the women’s 3.0 and men’s 4.0 semifinals.
