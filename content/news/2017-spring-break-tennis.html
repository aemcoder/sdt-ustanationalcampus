Approximately 300 collegiate tennis programs have already started making their way to the USTA National Campus for Spring Break Tennis. This page will provide visitors with the schedule of events throughout February and March.

Spring Break Tennis is a program that helps schools organize their practices and matches while traveling throughout the Sunshine State. It has partnered with the Home of American Tennis to develop an entertaining 2017 schedule.

Live streaming may be available for select matches at PlaySight.com.
