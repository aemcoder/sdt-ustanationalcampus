Singles qualifying wrapped up while singles and doubles main draw action got underway on Tuesday at the 2019 Oracle Pro Series Orlando, held at the USTA National Campus. Marking the sixth of seven stops of the Oracle Pro Series this fall, the week features men’s and women’s ITF World Tennis Tour $25k events.

In the men’s singles main draw, top seed Javier Barranco Cosano of Spain was pushed to the brink by American Justin Butsch, needing a tiebreak in the final set to prevail 6-2, 2-6, 7-6(1). Other seeded competitors in action were No. 2 Pedro Sakamoto (Brazil), who handled Jose Olivares of Dominican Republic 6-2, 6-3, and seventh-seeded Adam Moundir (Morocco), who rallied back from a set down to defeat Nathan Ponwith (United States) 4-6, 7-6(3), 6-4. Additionally, wild card American Emilio Nava outlasted countryman and fellow wild card Logan Zapp 1-6, 6-4, 7-5.

Alfredo Perez was the lone American to qualify in the men’s competition. He will be joined in the main draw by Gonzalo Lama (Chile), Jose Pereira (Brazil), Alejandro Moro Canas (Spain), Andrea Bolla (Italy), Alan Kohen (Argentina), Dragos Constantin Ignat (Romania), and Joao Hinsching (Brazli).

Only one men’s doubles match was contested on Tuesday, as Perez capped off a tremendous day by teaming up with Charlie Emhardt to stun second seeds Martin Damm and Toby Kodat 6-4, 6-3 in an all-American matchup.

Of the three women’s singles seeds in action, only No. 4 Georgina Garcia-Perez (Spain) won in straight sets, holding off American wild card Carly Briggs 6-4, 6-4. Sixth seed Daniela Seguel (Chile) narrowly escaped her tilt with Leonie Kung (Switzerland) 6-4, 3-6, 6-4 and No. 8 seed Stephanie Wagner (Germany) needed to stage a comeback to get past wild card Alexandra Yepifanova (United States).

Navigating their way through qualifying on the women’s side were Americans Chanelle Van Nguyen, Dasha Ivanova, and Tori Kinard along with Nastja Kolar (Slovenia), Panna Udvardy (Hungary), Maria Lourdes Carle (Argentina), Corinna Dentoni (Italy), and Maria Fernanda Herazo Gonzalez (Colombia).

A trio of women’s doubles matches was on the schedule as well, two of which saw American tandems come out on top. Angela Kulikov and Rianna Valdes turned in an impressive 6-3, 6-1 effort against Ana Sofia Sanchez (Mexico) and Melanie Stokke (Norway); the pairing of Rasheeda McAdoo and Alycia Parks knocked off another American team in Elizabeth Mandlik and Emma Navarro 6-7(5), 6-4, 10-4. Also securing a victory were Maria Jose Portillo Ramirez (Mexico) and Gabriela Talaba (Romania) with a 6-4, 6-2 result over Arantxa Rus (Netherlands) and Bianca Turati (Italy).
