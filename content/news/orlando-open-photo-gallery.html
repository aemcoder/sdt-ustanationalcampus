The first ATP Challenger event hosted by the USTA #NationalCampus attracted top American talent to compete in the Orlando Open. Numerous players made the short commute from USTA Player Development, where they completed their pre-season training, to the Collegiate Courts at the Home of American Tennis. See which Americans got their 2019 seasons off to a great start with this USTA Pro Circuit tournament in Orlando, FL.
