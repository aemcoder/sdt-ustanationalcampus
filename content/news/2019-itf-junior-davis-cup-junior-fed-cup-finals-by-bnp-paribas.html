2019 ITF Junior Davis Cup & Junior Fed Cup Finals by BNP Paribas

Japan Boys, USA Girls Prevail

September 26, 2019

style
article-hero

For the first time since 1994, the ITF Junior Davis Cup and Junior Fed Cup Finals by BNP Paribas is taking place in the United States. Round robin group play kicked off the event, spanning Tuesday, Sept. 24 through Thursday, Sept. 26. Results from those ties placed teams into one of two playoff groups, where three more days of competition rounded out the week. 

Team USA advanced both of its squads to the championship round, where the boys came up just short versus Japan while the girls held off the Czech Republic to raise the winners' trophy. Below are the results from the three days of playoff ties.
