Mardy Fish USTA Futures Lake Nona Wild Card

Total of 40 Players Seek Vero Beach Wild Card

April 7, 2018

style
article-hero

A busy slate of tournament action for the month of April at the USTA National Campus kicks off Monday with the start of the Mardy Fish USTA Futures Lake Nona Wild Card event. This is the second of two wild card singles tournaments for the Mardy Fish Children’s Foundation Tennis Championships, a $15,000 USTA Pro Circuit event played at the Grand Harbor Golf & Beach Club in Vero Beach, Fla., at the end of the month.

A total of 40 players have entered the draw at the National Campus with aspirations of claiming the title and wild card berth. Opening round matches are slated for 5:00 p.m. on Monday with the round of 32 to follow at 8:00 p.m.

The event’s top seeds include No. 1 seed Trent Bryde, second seed Karl Poling, third-seeded Axel Nefve and No. 4 seed Robert Hammond.

Bryde, a recent University of Georgia commit, is an 18-year-old native of Suwanee, Ga. He was ranked as high as eighth in the ITF World Junior rankings last year, when he qualified for the 2017 ITF Junior Masters to end the season. In total, he owns 87 singles and 92 doubles victories on the ITF World Junior circuit. Bryde won his first USTA Pro Circuit title at the beginning of 2018, teaming up with Louis Wessels to win the doubles crown at the $15,000 Naples Futures. He reached the second round in singles and semifinals in doubles at the 2017 US Open Junior Championships.

Poling is an 18-year-old from West Point, N.Y. He has collected 37 singles and 37 doubles wins on the ITF World Junior circuit overall, with his best ranking being No. 131 in the ITF World Junior rankings. Poling qualified for the singles main draw at the Orange Bowl International Championship to wrap up his 2017 season. Additionally, he reached the doubles final of the 2017 USTA International Spring Championships with fellow American Boris Kozlov. Poling has competed in the qualifying draws of two USTA Pro Circuit events so far this year, the $15,000 event in Sunrise, Fla. and the $125,000 event in Dallas, Texas.

Nefve, a 17-year-old from Boca Raton, Fla., reached a career-high ITF World Junior ranking of 60 in January. In total, he has 46 singles and 50 doubles wins on the ITF World Junior circuit. Nefve has been highly successful in doubles with Emilio Nava so far in 2018, advancing to the quarterfinals of the Easter Bowl Championships in March. The duo backed that performance up by winning the International Spring Championships without dropping a set, posting a 7-5, 6-0 performance in the championship match on April 7. Nefve competed in the singles draw of the 2017 US Open Junior Championships after receiving  a wild card.

Hammond, a 17-year-old native of Highland Beach, Fla., has 34 singles and 29 doubles victories on the ITF World Junior circuit. Last year he reached the quarterfinals of the Evert American ITF event along with qualifying for the Easter Bowl Championships. He also claimed the men’s open singles draw at the 2017 Delray Prize Money Open, not surrendering a single set along the way. Hammond earned the doubles title at the 2016 ITF Grade 5 Bank of Bermuda Foundation with partner Alexander Rushin.

Other seeded players include Brian Battistone (Las Vegas), Roberto Livi (Long Island City, N.Y.), Vinicius Nogueira (Teresina, Brazil), and Bruno Pessoa (Orlando, Fla.).
