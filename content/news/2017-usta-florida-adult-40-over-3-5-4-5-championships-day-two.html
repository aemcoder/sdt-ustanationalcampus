USTA Florida Adult 40 & Over 3.5/4.5+ Sectional Championships

Day Two

July 28, 2017

style
article-hero

All 54 teams were in action on Day 2 of the 2017 USTA Florida Adult 40 & Over 3.5/4.5+ Sectional Championships. Both the Har-Tru and Team USA courts of the USTA National Campus were filled over the course of Friday as the first full day of the tournament’s schedule got underway.

Six teams remain undefeated in the women’s 3.5 division: Collier, Pinellas, SMD-Doral Park, Marion, Volusia and Orange/Seminole. Collier is the lone unbeaten in Flight 1, sitting with a 2-0 record after victories over Sarasota/Manatee and IRMSL. Pinellas and SMD-Doral Park are in prime position in Flight 2, each recording one win comfortably and one by the slim margin of 3-2. Marion enters Saturday with control of Flight 3, beating Alachua and SMD-Dante Fascell despite losing 11 total sets on the day. The only flight to get started on Thursday evening, Flight 4 leaders Volusia and Orange/Seminole have three team wins to zero losses thus far.

The men’s 3.5 division features three flights, meaning there is one wild card spot up for grabs in the semifinals to go along with the trio of flight winners. In Flight 1, Broward is a victory over Lee/Charlotte away from clinching its spot in the semis. Teams in Flight 2 played just once on Friday, with Alachua and Brevard winning. However, both Duval-Jack Russell Park and Volusia remain very much in the hunt, as both matches ended in 3-2 results. Polk and Hillsborough have the inside track in Flight 3 following 2-0 efforts on the day.

With the women’s 4.5+ division being composed of only two flights, teams are battling in round robin action to advance to the championship match. Orange/Seminole and Hillsborough won their lone matches on day two, with Duval and SPB each needing to win both of their duals on Saturday to stay alive. Meanwhile Broward and SMD appear to be on a collision course in Flight 2, with the winner of their clash Saturday likely to claim the flight.

In Flight 1 of the men’s 4.5+ division, Pinellas defeated both Duval and Lee/Charlotte. As the flight features a double round-robin format, Pinellas only needs to get past Lee/Charlotte again on Saturday to continue on to the semis. SMD has already locked up a spot in the final four thanks to wins against Collier and Hillsborough in Flight 2. With only a match against 0-2 Alachua left, SMD holds the tiebreaker in any scenario. The same is true for Broward out of Flight 3, which already owns victories versus Sarasota/Manatee and Orange/Seminole with only the 0-2 Escambia squad left to play. There will be a wild-card semifinalist in this division as well.

Matches will continue on Saturday morning at 8 a.m., marking the final day of the round-robin flights.
