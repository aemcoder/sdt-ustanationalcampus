The USTA National Campus will officially be open for business on January 2, 2017.

Located at 10000 USTA Blvd., Orlando, Fla., in the Lake Nona community, the 64-acre campus sits adjacent to the Orlando International Airport and about 20 minutes from Orlando’s theme parks and Convention Center.

The facility features 100 lighted tennis courts and offers a wide array of amenities, including an expansive retail pro shop, racquet stringing, a full-service restaurant and two cafés – and that is in addition to pavilions throughout the grounds with shade, restrooms and water bottle filling stations.

For as little as $12 an hour, you can come out and play on a variety of different court surfaces, including Plexicushion Acrylic hard courts, Har-Tru green clay courts and 36- and 60-foot sport courts. All will be available to reserve online shortly before the public opening in January.

See below for additional information on court pricing. Rates are per court:

Team USA & Collegiate (Plexicushion Acrylic hard courts)

  • 90 minutes: $15
  • 2 hours: $20
