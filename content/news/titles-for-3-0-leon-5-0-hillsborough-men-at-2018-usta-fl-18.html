Titles for 3.0 Leon, 5.0+ Hillsborough Men

August 12, 2018

style
article-hero

Day two of the 2018 USTA Florida 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional Championships was again impacted heavily by weather, resulting in play finishing indoors. Sunday did produce a pair of champions on the men’s side, as the 3.0 Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) along with the 5.0+ Hillsborough (Northdale Golf and Tennis Club) squads took home sectional titles.

The men representing the Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center survived the weekend unscathed to claim the 3.0 trophy. The feat did not come easy, as two of their three dual wins were by a slim 3-2 margin. Leon produced the decisive victory Sunday morning, a 3-2 defeat of Marion (Fort King Tennis Center), which ended up being the second-place finisher. For the tournament, Leon prevailed on 10 courts while losing 14 sets; Marion came through on eight courts and gave away 18 sets.

Three of the women’s 3.0 teams moving on to the semifinals had no trouble navigating through the flight stage while one was pushed through via the tiebreaking procedure. Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) and SMD (Crandon Park) swept Flight 2 and 3, respectively, with perfect 3-0 records. Marion picked up 11 court victories while SMD took an impressive 13. In the five-team Flight 4, Duval (Florida Yacht Club) was not to be denied, finishing undefeated on the strength of winning 16 of the 20 individual matchups. The drama within the division came from Flight 1, where a trio of teams tied at 2-1 overall; however, SMD (Dante Fascell Park) got the nod after giving away one less set (15) than both Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center) and Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center). The semifinal matchups will pit Dante Fascell Park against Marion and Crandon Park against Duval.

Two of the weekend’s most dominant teams will be across the net from each other Monday in the men’s 4.0 semifinals. Broward (Weston Tennis Center) cruised in Flight 1, nearly going a perfect 15-for-15 in individual courts contested at 13 wins and two defeats. SMD (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center) was close to equaling that effort in Flight 2, closing out the two days with 12 victories to just three losses individually. Things were a bit more challenging for the teams emerging from Flight 3 and Flight 4. Escambia was unable to polish off a perfect 4-0 record in Flight 3 after stumbling against Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center), but still finished atop the standings thanks to a solid 13-7 individual mark, two better than Leon. In Flight 4, Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) did manage to hold off their opponents for an undefeated finish despite having Sarasota (Longwood) compile the same number of court wins (13).

As Collier (Heritage Bay) finished off a sweep of their foes in Flight 4 of the women’s 4.0 competition, the other three flights all needed to be decided via tiebreaker. Ultimately it would be Escambia, Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park) and Hillsborough (Westchase) moving on to Monday. All three closed out the day with 2-1 overall records. Escambia edged out Broward (Sunrise Tennis Club) in Flight 1 thanks to earning 10 individual court wins compared to nine for Broward. It was even tighter for Orange/Seminole, which took the tiebreaking procedure all the way down to sets lost in order to grab Flight 2 from Sarasota (Longwood). In Flight 3, Hillsborough’s nine court victories bested Marion’s (Fort King Tennis Center) seven. Escambia and Orange/Seminole are set to battle in the semifinal round on Monday, as are Hillsborough and Collier.

The 5.0+ Hillsborough (Northdale Golf and Tennis Club) men stormed through the lone flight of the division to claim the team title. Posting a 3-0 overall record, Hillsborough won seven of the nine individual courts they contested while dropping just four sets and 51 games. The squad will return to the National Campus Oct. 5-7 in search of the USTA League 18 & Over 5.0+ national crown.

Clashing for the women’s 5.0+ championship on Monday will be Orange/Seminole (National Campus) and Duval (Florida Yacht Club). Orange/Seminole split their two meetings with Pinellas (Clearwater) in Flight 1, but got through just barely with nine court victories versus only eight for Pinellas. In contrast, Duval was far and away the class of Flight 2, posting a 3-0 team record that featured just one court loss and three sets surrendered.
