Brevard Wins Women's 8.0 Title

October 10, 2020

style
article-hero

A full day of matches made for a busy Saturday at the 2020 USTA Florida 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships, held on the grounds of the USTA National Campus. Highlighting Saturday’s play was the Brevard (Rockledge Park) 8.0 women’s squad taking home a divisional title.

Brevard actually began the day with a loss to Pinellas (Bardmoor Golf Tennis Club – Largo), which resulted in a three-way tie atop Flight 2. But the tiebreaking procedure went their way, as they finished with more individual matchup victories than Pinellas and a head-to-head win over Hillsborough (Apollo Beach Racquet Fitness Club). Meanwhile, Lee/Charlotte (Park Meadow Tennis Club – Fort Myers) breezed through Flight 1, earning seven of the nine courts they contested.

In the championship dual, Brevard’s captain Sylvia Segura teamed up with Anna Butler for a 6-3, 6-1 effort at line two while Jodi Hynes and Kaye Kuhfeld countered for Lee/Charlotte by winning court one 6-4, 6-0. That put all the pressure on Nan Fleming and Robin Stember, who rallied from a set down for a 3-6, 6-3, 1-0 triumph at the third spot to deliver for their squad.

The full Brevard roster includes Segura, Susan Noble, Joni Smith, Stember, Fleming, Patricia Spoerle, Linda Madyda, and Butler.

Saturday also saw the women’s 6.0 and men’s 8.0 competitions get underway. Moving into the driver’s seat in the two women’s 6.0 flights were Hillsborough (Apollo Beach Racquet Fitness Club) in Flight 1 and IRMSL (Magnolia Lakes – Port St. Lucie) in Flight 2; Hillsborough needs two more dual wins to advance to play for the championship while IRMSL just needs one. In the lone men’s division, Orange/Seminole (Tuscawilla Country Club – Winter Springs) sits atop Flight 2 with a 2-0 record while three teams will bring a 1-1 mark into Sunday and Flight 1 will go to the winner between Duval (Boone Park Tennis Courts – Jacksonville) and Pinellas (City of Clearwater).
