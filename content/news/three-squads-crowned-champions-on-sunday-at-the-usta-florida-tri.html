USTA Florida Tri-Level Sectional Championships

Three Squads Capture Titles

January 26, 2020

style
article-hero

Sunday saw a trio of squads bring home sectional titles at the USTA Florida Tri-Level Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Three separate regions were represented among the champions – Duval, Sarasota, and Broward.

While a total of six sectional crowns are on the line this weekend, the headliners are the men’s and women’s 3.5/4.0/4.5 competitions; the two squads prevailing from that division will book their spot in the 2020 BNP Paribas Open Tri-Level Championships March 20-22.

Duval (San Jose Country Club – Jacksonville) swept through the women’s 2.5/3.0/3.5 competition, finishing with a sterling 3-0 record on the strength of seven court victories. They began the day tied atop the standings with Orange/Seminole (Isleworth Tennis Club – Windermere), but two dual wins coupled with two surprising losses by Orange/Seminole gave Duval the team title. Captain Anna Valent’s full Duval roster includes Anita Morrill, Julie Boone, Whitney Riley, Lacy Gregory, Emily Katsikas, Allison Wise, Erica Kirk, Heather Riley, Emily Dostie, Meg Sheffield, Dixie McDaniel de Andrade, Ambur Finley, Kate Jones, Catherine Schaefer, Kathleen Lebet, Vickie Nelson, Michelle Kalil, Betsey Winslow, AnnCandler Strohmenger, and Margaret Samuels.

Sarasota (Landings Racquet Club – Sarasota) was the class of the women’s 4.0/4.5/5.0 division, which featured a double round-robin format. They managed to take down Orange/Seminole (Lake Cane Tennis Center – Orlando) and Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) twice over the course of the weekend, capping off the event with a 4-0 record behind eight individual matchup wins to only four losses. Captained by Barbara Lancer, the Sarasota crew consists of Amy Lyons, Yudi Carpenter, Kate Thayer-Rhodes, Kelly Dancer, Kathy Rosenberg, Sarah Winters, Christina Solomon, Tanya Keith, Jennifer Fiers, Ann Lammers, Jennifer Peters, Rosa Rojas Weis, Jennifer Bright, and Jacqueline Faulk.

With a trip to Indian Wells on the line, the Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) men turned in a commanding effort to capture the 3.5/4.0/4.5 crown. Broward won all three of their duals in Flight 2 to easily advance to the divisional final, where they went up against Duval (Ponte Vedra Club – Ponte Vedra Beach), which advanced out of Flight 1 with a 2-1 record; the tiebreaking procedure went in their favor, as their seven court victories put them at the top of the standings over Pinellas and Marion.

In the championship dual, Broward claimed all three opening sets en route to a 3-0 sweep of Duval. Michael Stites and Marcus Pfenninger cruised to a 6-2, 6-2 result on court three to go along with Anthony Phillips and Jorge Gonzalez taking line two 6-3, 6-4. Marc Winkler and Agustin Crochi Torres hung on to finish things off with a 6-4, 6-7, 1-0 triumph on court one. Captain Kevin Sims leads a Broward squad that includes Timothy House, Pfenninger, Stites, Phillips, Patricio Casali, Andrew Brown, Winkler, Crochi Torres, Gonzalez, Humphrey Allen, and Rene Elvir.

Monday’s 3.0/3.5/4.0 final on the men’s side is set to be a battle, with both Marion (Country Club of Ocala) and Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) posting unblemished records when flight play wrapped up. Marion came through Flight 1 at 4-0 by earning nine of the 12 courts they contested while Broward went 3-0 in Flight 2 with eight individual matchups to their credit.

The women’s 3.0/3.5/4.0 and 3.5/4.0/4.5 competitions will both see four teams enter Monday with a shot at capturing a sectional title. In the 3.0/3.5/4.0 division, Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) went unscathed in Flight 1 while SMD (Founders Park Tennis – Islamorada) survived a very competitive Flight 2 and Escambia (Independent) rallied to topple Broward in Flight 3. The semifinal duals have pitted Marion against Escambia and SMD against wild card entrant Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando), the second-place finisher in Flight 1.

Moving on in the 3.5/4.0/4.5 competition were flight winners Alachua (DB Racquet Club – Gainesville), Brevard (McKnight Family Sports Complex – Rockledge), Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston). Alachua, the lone unbeaten after a 3-0 performance in Flight 1, will be across the net from Broward, which emerged from a three-way tie in Flight 3 on the strength of eight court victories. Brevard took Flight 2 thanks to a head-to-head win over Marion, and will now clash with wild card Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando), the runner-up in Flight 1.
