USTA League National Adult 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Championships

Hit the Courts Starting Friday Morning

October 26, 2017

style
article-hero

The third in a string of five weekends featuring USTA League National Adult Championships play at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., gets going Friday morning, as men’s and women’s teams at the 6.0 and 8.0 skill levels are set to vie for the right to call themselves national champions.

Action will kick off at 7 a.m. Friday morning and run late into the night, with the final round scheduled to take the courts at 7:30 p.m.

Each of the USTA’s 17 Sections will be represented in the women’s 6.0, women’s 8.0 and men’s 8.0 competitions while a total of 13 Sections make up the men’s 6.0 field. Friday and Saturday will see all squads attempt to fight their way through round robin flight play via team dual matches to secure a berth in the championship semifinals. A team dual match is made up of three doubles contests; teams must claim at least two of those matchups to win the dual.

The men’s 6.0 division features just three flights, meaning the top finishers in each plus a wild card team will advance to the final four; in the other three divisions, only the flight winners will earn a semifinal spot.

All semifinals are scheduled for first serve at 7 a.m. Sunday morning with the title and third/fourth playoff matches to follow. A full listing of the 6.0 flights and match schedule can be viewed here, and the 8.0 division here.

In last year’s National Adult 55 & Over 6.0 Championships, the women’s team representing the Southern Section bested Pacific Northwest 2-1 and the Southern California men swept aside Missouri Valley 3-0. At the 8.0 Championships, Intermountain’s women got past  Florida 2-1 while the men of Southern California took down Midwest 3-0.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
