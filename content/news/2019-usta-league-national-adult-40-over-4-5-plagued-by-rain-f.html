Consistent rain over the course of Friday and Saturday has plagued the 2019 USTA League National Adult 40 & Over 4.5+ Championships, held at the USTA National Campus. Despite numerous rain delays and matches being moved indoors, the men’s and women’s semifinalists were eventually determined Saturday evening.

Advancing to Sunday on the men’s side were the undefeated squads representing Southern (The Club at Westport – Denver, N.C.) and Pacific Northwest (Portland Tennis Center – Portland, Ore.) along with one-loss clubs Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Montgomery County, Md.) and Texas (Gleneagles Country Club – Plano). With identical 4-0 records on the strength of 16 court victories, the top seed was awarded to Southern, whose players collected more sets and games than Pacific Northwest. Mid-Atlantic checked in third with a 3-1 record thanks to 15 individual matchups going their way. Meanwhile, Texas grabbed the final semifinal berth, overcoming one dual loss to collect 14 court wins, allowing them to edge out Southern California, Eastern, and Missouri Valley.

For the semifinals, Southern will take on Texas and Pacific Northwest will clash with Mid-Atlantic.

In the women’s competition, only Eastern (Rye Racquet Club – Harrison, N.Y.) navigated their way to a perfect 4-0 finish to take the top seed heading into Sunday. The honor of being the best of the one-loss squads was earned by Midwest (Challenge Fitness – Lockport, Ill.), as they actually won the same number of courts (16) as Eastern. Southern (New Orleans City Park Tennis Center) played their way to the No. 3 seed after coming through with 14 individual matchups to their credit. Finally, Pacific Northwest (Edgebrook Club – Bellevue, Wash.) staged an impressive comeback from losing their first dual of the weekend, capping off Saturday night by defeating Southern; that effort not only knocked Southern out of contention for the No. 1 seed, but also secured the fourth seed for the squad that made the cross-country venture.

Looking ahead, Eastern will be looking to continue their unbeaten streak with a matchup versus Pacific Northwest while Midwest and Southern will square off.
