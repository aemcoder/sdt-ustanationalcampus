Eight Juniors Come Away with Singles Titles

March 19, 2018

article-hero

Monday wrapped up action at the USTA Florida March Level 4 Championships on the grounds of the USTA National Campus, as eight junior players earned singles titles on the day.

Both No. 1 seeds competing in a divisional final came away victorious. In the boys’ 12s division, first seed Yubel Ubri handled fourth seed Tanner Povey 6-2, 6-4, while boys’ 14s top seed Yannik Rahman defeated unseeded Martin Mesia 7-5, 6-1.

The lone unseeded champion on the boys’ side came in the form of Michael Nicholls, who claimed the 16s crown by outlasting No. 2 seed Ben Shelton 6-4, 3-6, 10-5. It was the lone championship contest to be decided by a match tiebreak. In the boys’ 18s draw, third-seeded Logan Zapp toughed out a tight opening frame before racing through the second to get past unseeded Micah Braswell 7-5, 6-0.

On the girls’ side, the four champions surrendered a combined 13 games. The most commanding performance of the day was delivered by No. 2 seed DJ Bennett in the girls’ 14s division, as she swept aside unseeded Mia Slama 6-0, 6-0. For the girls’ 12s finals, which featured fifth-seeded Sophie Llewellyn taking on sixth seed Lauren Kettlewell, it was Llewellyn who prevailed by a 6-2, 6-3 margin.

Natalie Block (girls’ 16s) and Abigail Rencheli (girls’ 18s) each won by the same final score of 6-2, 6-2. Both players took part in matchups of unseeded players; Block knocked off Violeta Martinez and Rencheli beat Emma Shelton.

Below are the results from Monday’s championship matches.

Boys’ 12s: (1)Yubel Ubri def. (4)Tanner Povey 6-2, 6-4

Boys’ 14s: (1)Yannik Rahman def. Martin Mesia 7-5, 6-1

Boys’ 16s: Michael Nicholls def. (2) Ben Shelton 6-4, 3-6, 10-5

Boys’ 18s: (3)Logan Zapp def. Micah Braswell 7-5, 6-0

Girls’ 12s: (5) Sophie Llewellyn def. (6)Lauren Kettlewell 6-2, 6-3

Girls’ 14s: (2)DJ Bennett def. Mia Slama 6-0, 6-0

Girls’ 16s: Natalie Block def. Violeta Martinez 6-2, 6-2

Girls’ 18s: Abigail Rencheli def. Emma Shelton 6-2, 6-2
