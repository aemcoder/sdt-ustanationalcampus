We are excited about the technology at the USTA National Campus which will revolutionize the way players enjoy the game. Thanks to PlaySight's SmartCourts on campus, players will get to use a system that uses advanced image processing and analytical algorithms to capture and log stroke type, ball trajectory, speed and spin, in-depth shot data, player movement and more.

In all, 26 courts will have PlaySight's SmartCourt technology. A total of 84 courts will feature live streaming to USTANationalCampus.com.

ABOUT PLAYSIGHT

Playsight is an interactive system composed of permanently installed high-performance Hd cameras with an on court kiosk connected to the internet, which we call the SmartCourt. The SmartCourt has the potential to turn any court into a line calling, video recording, and analytic system accessible to the user immediately while on court.

PlaySight's SmartCourt cameras communicate with each other using advanced algorithms to track all player and ball movement.  This allows the user the ability to see play from multiple angles, give in depth player statistics, and offer gamified drilling options from the kiosk

Once play on a SmartCourt has been completed  it is automatically uploaded to the PlaySight "cloud", and accessible to the user online at PlaySight.com or via the free PlaySight mobile app. Users can create custom highlight clips and review their SmartCourt session from any computer or mobile device through their Playsight account.
