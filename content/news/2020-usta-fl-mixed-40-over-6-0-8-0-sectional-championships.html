2020 USTA FL Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships

Long-awaited Return

September 18, 2020

style
article-hero

After more than six months off the court, the USTA National Campus welcomes the return of sanctioned tournament play by hosting the 2020 USTA FL Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships. The three-day league event will get underway Saturday, Sept. 19, and wrap up on Monday, Sept. 21.

Sunshine State squads head to Orlando for a weekend of mixed doubles action, squaring off in dual matches made up of three courts. In order to claim the dual win, a team must win at least two of those courts.

The 6.0 division is comprised of five teams from five different regions – Alachua, Brevard, Duval, Hillsborough, and Volusia. The squads will battle it out in a single round-robin flight, with the sectional title going to the crew with the best overall record in its fout dual matches.

It will be a much busier weekend in the 8.0 competition, as a total of 13 teams have been divided into three different flights for the round-robin portion of play. Flights 1 and 2 consist of four crews while the remaining five will look to emerge from Flight 3. All flighted duals are scheduled for either Saturday or Sunday, with the trio of first-place finishers along with the top second-place team moving on to Monday’s divisional semifinals.

The 6.0 champion will be crowned Sunday afternoon while the winner on the 8.0 side will be determined Monday afternoon.

Taking home titles at last year’s event was the 6.0 squad representing Duval (Fleming Island Plantation), thanks to a 2-1 triumph over Alachua (Westside Park – Gainesville), and the 8.0 crew from Sarasota (Longboat Key Tennis Center), which got past Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) by the same margin.
