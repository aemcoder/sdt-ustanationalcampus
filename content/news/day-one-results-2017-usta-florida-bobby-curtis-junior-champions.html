Courtesy of Rick Vach, USTA Florida Marketing & Communications Coordinator

Opening day at the 69th USTA Florida “Bobby Curtis” Junior State Singles Championships kicked off on Saturday with BG18-16 play at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, and BG14-12 play at Sanlando Park in Altamonte Springs.

The Girls’ 18s saw the most upheaval among the Top 10 seeds as Bradenton’s Gia Cohen upset the No. 4 seed, Lithia’s Maggie Pate upended the No. 7 seed, and Weston’s Ayshe Can ousted the No. 8 seed.

Unseeded Grey Cacciatore of Gainesville, last year’s runner-up in the Boys’ 16s, made a statement in the first round of the Boys’ 18s, defeating No. 15 seed Antonio Mora of Coconut Grove from a set down 3-6, 6-1, 6-3.

The Boys’ 16s saw the Nos. 13, 14, and 15 seeds all go out in the first round courtesy of upset-makers Micah Braswell of Sarasota, Caden Sikorski of Ormond Beach, and Benjamin Zipay of Osprey.

The 69th USTA Florida “Bobby Curtis” Junior State Singles Championships runs from June 10-14, 2017.

In 2012 the tournament was renamed the USTA Florida “Bobby Curtis” Junior State Championships in honor of legendary Florida junior tennis organizer Bobby Curtis.

Results will be posted daily in the news section at www.ustaflorida.com. For more info, including links to draws and entry lists, go to www.ustaflorida.com/bobbycurtis.
