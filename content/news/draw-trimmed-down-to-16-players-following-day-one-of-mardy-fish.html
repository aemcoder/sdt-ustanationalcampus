Mardy Fish USTA Futures Lake Nona Wild Card

Draw Trimmed Down to 16 Players After Day One

April 10, 2018

style
article-hero

Despite the threat of inclement weather throughout the day, all matches on Monday’s schedule at the Mardy Fish USTA Futures Lake Nona Wild Card event were completed. With those results, just 16 players remain in the single-elimination draw.

This is the second of two wild card singles tournaments for the Mardy Fish Children’s Foundation Tennis Championships, a $15,000 USTA Pro Circuit event played at the Grand Harbor Golf & Beach Club in Vero Beach, Fla., at the end of the month.

The eight seeded players all received byes into the second round, with only eight opening round matches on the slate. In those contests, all finished in straight sets; the most games lost by a match winner was five.

Top four seeds Trent Bryde (Suwanne, Ga.), Karl Poling (West Point, N.Y.), Axel Nefve (Boca Raton, Fla.), and Robert Hammond (Highland Beach, Fla.) all eased their way into the round of 16, dropping a combined 10 games. The other four seeded players in the draw were equally as impressive, surrendering a total of 11 games on the day.

The second round did feature a pair of matches that went the distance, with Quinn Snyder (Delran, N.J.) defeating Andrew Blair (Fort Pierce, Fla.) 7-5, 4-6, 11-9 and Lawrence Sciglitano (Ojai, Calif.) outlasting Andrew Ely (Boca Raton, Fla.) 5-7, 6-2, 10-2.

Round of 16 matches were originally scheduled to hit the courts at 5:00 p.m. on Tuesday with the quarterfinals to follow at 8:00 p.m. However, due to heavy rainfall in the area, all matches have been postponed until Wednesday.
