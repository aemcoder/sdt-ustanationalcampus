Top Seeds Continue on as Other Seeds Fall

December 28, 2017

The second day of the 2017 USTA National Winter Championships for the 16s and 18s, held at the USTA National Campus, wrapped up Thursday evening, with several more seeds going down in singles play. However, all four No. 1 seeds are safely into the round of 32.

In boys’ 16s singles action, fifth-seeded Hunter Heck (Saint Paul, Minn.) was defeated by Coy Simon (Charleston, S.C.) 6-2, 6-1. Additionally, the third quarter of the draw features just one top 16 seed still alive in the form of No. 6 seed Harsh Parikh (Tucson, Ariz.).

Noah Schachter (Wesley Chapel, Fla.) delivered the biggest upset of the day for the boys’ 18s when he eased past seventh seed Mason Meier (Omaha, Neb.) 6-2, 6-1. Daniel Pellerito (Syosset, N.Y.), seeded 11th, also came up short in his matchup with Marcus McDaniel (Vacaville, Calif.) by the margin of 6-3, 6-2.

For the girls’ 16s, Perene Wang (New York, N.Y.) toughed out a 6-4, 6-3 decision against No. 3 seed Lauren Anzalotta (Trujillo Alto, P.R.), made even more impressive considering Wang was pushed deep into a third set in her opening round contest. In the same section, 14th-seeded Yolanda Lin (Sammamish, Wash.) dropped a 5-7, 6-3, 6-4 marathon to Justine Dondonay (Alhambra, Calif.).

A trio of surprising results highlighted the girls’ 18s singles matches on Thursday. After the No. 2 seed was eliminated in the first round, fourth seeded Sonia Tartakovsky (New York, N.Y.) was stunned by Ava Hrastar (Duluth, Ga.) 6-2, 6-4. No. 11 seed Sophia Hatton (Syracuse, N.Y.) was also upended by Elizabeth Coleman (Midland, Mich.) and 12th seed Josie Frazier (Scottsdale, Ariz.) fell to Luba Vasilyuk (Frisco, Texas), both in straight sets.

Navigating through all the upsets so far have been the four players who earned top billing in their respective draw. Boys’ 16s No. 1 Maxwell McKennon (Newport Beach, Calif.), girls’ 16s top seed Gianna Pielet (El Paso, Texas) and girls’ 18 No. 1 seed Chelsea Kung (Fort Worth, Texas) have all surrendered just eight games each in reaching the round of 32. Carson Haskins (Ballwin, Mo.), the first seed in the boys’ 18s draw, has been more strongly challenged but remains in the hunt for the title.

Of note in the doubles draws is the top half of the boys’ 16s, where just two seeded duos remain. The boys’ 18s and girls’ 16s have seen the top four seeds move into the round of 16, while girls’ 18s No. 4 seeds Callie Creath (Austin, Texas) and Gabriella Cusano (Austin, Texas) lost to Coleman and Emma Jackson (La Grange Park, Ill.) 6-3, 7-6(7).
