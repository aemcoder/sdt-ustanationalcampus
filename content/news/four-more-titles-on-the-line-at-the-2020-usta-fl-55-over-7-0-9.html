Four More Titles at Stake

October 23, 2020

The USTA National Campus welcomes in another group of USTA Florida Adult League players, as the 55 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships will get underway Saturday, Oct. 24 and run through Monday, Oct. 26. First serve is scheduled for 7:30 a.m. on the first two days of the tournament and 8 a.m. for the third.

Teams will be representing regions from across the state in a weekend of doubles play, squaring off in dual matches made up of three courts. In order to claim the dual victory, a team must win at least two of those courts.

Squads from six different regions make up the men’s 7.0 field – Miami-Dade, Collier, Hillsborough, Duval, Broward, and Marion. All six have been placed into one flight, with each taking part in two round-robin duals Saturday and Sunday along with a fifth and final dual Monday.

The busiest division will be the women’s 7.0, which will feature a total of 15 teams divided into three flights at the National Campus over the weekend. The round robin portion of their dual matches is slated to play out all day Saturday and into Sunday morning, after which the trio of flight winners plus the top second-place finisher will advance to the divisional semifinals. Sunday’s semifinals will take to the courts at 1 p.m. with the championship contest scheduled for 8 a.m. Monday morning.

Meanwhile, only Hillsborough, Pinellas, Sarasota, and Broward are in the running for the men’s 9.0 crown. Rounding out the teams participating in this weekend’s sectional event are the five squads in the women’s 9.0 competition – Hillsborough, Broward, Sarasota, Duval, and Orange/Seminole.

Both the men’s and women’s 9.0 champions will be crowned Sunday afternoon while the two 970 titles will be decided Monday morning.
