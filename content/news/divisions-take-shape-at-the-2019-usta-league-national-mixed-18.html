Divisions Take Shape After Day One

The first day of November also marked the first day of play at the 2019 USTA League National Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Championships, held at the USTA National Campus. With every squad taking to the courts to compete in two dual matches over the course of Friday, the divisions began to take shape in the battle to finish in the top four and move on to the playoff stages.

Five teams put themselves atop the standings in the 7.0 competition with 2-0 overall records. Hawaii (Independent – Oahu) and Florida (City of Oakland Park) were dominant forces at all three doubles positions on Friday, managing to go 6-for-6 in the courts they contested with 12 sets won; Hawaii dropped just one set while Florida surrendered only three. Sliding in just behind those two squads was the representative of the Middle States Section (Nassau Racquet & Tennis Club – Skillman, N.J.), coming out on top in five individual matchups with 10 sets to their credit. Rounding out the unbeaten clubs were Northern (Bearpath Golf and Country Club – Eden Prairie, Minn.) and Southern (Burns Park Tennis – Little Rock, Ark.), as both were challenged but battled their way to four court wins.

Additionally, a group of seven crews remained in the hunt for a semifinal berth after going 1-1 to open the weekend. Leading the charge for the one-loss teams is Intermountain (Darling Tennis Center – Las Vegas), which earned the same amount of individual matchups as both Northern and Southern.

On the 9.0 side, Pacific Northwest (Robinswood Tennis Center – Bellevue, Wash.) and Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Northern Va.) put themselves in the best position moving into Saturday after going 2-0 on the strength of five court wins. Pacific Northwest roped in 10 total sets while Mid-Atlantic’s players claimed 11 overall.

Three more squads also went unscathed on the day with four individual matchups under their belt – Northern California (St. Ignatius College Prep – San Francisco), Northern (Life Time Athletic – White Bear Lake, Minn.), and Texas (Lee Le Clear Tennis Center – Houston). Just as with the 7.0 division, seven teams sit on the outside looking in a 1-1, including Southern California (Carmel Valley Tennis – San Diego) with four court victories on the day.
