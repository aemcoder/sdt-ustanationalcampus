No. 1 Seeds Toppled on Sunday

October 18, 2020

style
article-hero

After some challenging matches in the opening rounds, all four No. 1 seeds were toppled on Sunday at the 2020 USTA Florida “Bobby Curtis” Junior Singles Championships. Three of the four players seeded second safely navigated their way through to their respective divisional final while a pair of unseeded competitors will also be playing for a title Monday.

Boys’ 18s No. 1 Adam Duan (Davie) won three consecutive matches in straight sets, never being pushed past 6-4, to reach the semifinal stage Sunday afternoon. There he met eighth-seeded Justin Braverman (Naples), who had come through a trio of tight two-set contests. Having played so many pressure points might have been the difference for Braverman, as he was the better player in most of the crucial moments to tough out a 7-6(5), 7-5 victory over Duan. The other half of the draw saw No. 5 Sami Ozzor (Daytona Beach) survive in a match tiebreak versus third seed Emilio Van Cotthem (Vero Beach) in the quarterfinals before putting an end to the run of unseeded Santiago Cardenas (Tampa) with a comprehensive 6-1, 6-3 result.

Not one to let his opening round upset of the draw’s No. 3 seed go to waste, Leonardo Dal Boni (Sarasota) will return to the courts on Monday just one win away from claiming the boys’ 16s crown. He ousted his second seed of the weekend in the final eight with a solid 6-4, 6-2 effort against No. 5 Calvin Wellman (Delray Beach); Dal Boni was then pushed to the limit in a battle of unseeded semifinalists, hanging on for a back-and-forth 6-1, 0-6, 1-0 triumph over Chase Healey (Saint Augustine) who had outlasted No. 1 Emilio Sanchez (Naples) earlier in the day.

Meanwhile, No. 2 seed Justin Lyons (Pensacola) knocked off two fellow seeds on the second day of play to advance to the final. Lyons has yet to lose a set for the tournament and gave away a mere two games in dispatching No. 6 Roy Horovitz (Key Biscayne) Sunday morning. He did benefit from a retirement by fourth seed Felipe Pinzon (Sunrise) in their semifinal encounter; Lyons was leading 6-3, 4-3 at the time of the retirement.

Seemingly invincible since tournament play has returned in 2020, second-seeded Ahmani Guichard (Seminole) has now won 15 matches in a row to position herself to claim the girls’ 18s title. She is still yet to even be pushed to three sets after cruising past No. 8 Paige Herremans (Orlando) 6-1, 6-3 in the quarterfinals before seeing Sophia Wang (Saint Petersburg) retire in the semifinals after Guichard had won the first four games of the match. Standing in her way will be No. 4 Natalie Block (Plantation), who has turned in three 6-0 sets but also needed to survive two set tiebreaks and a match tiebreak over the course of her four matches. However, Block will be coming in with a lot of confidence following her 2-6, 7-5, 1-0 upset of No. 1 Valeria Ray (Doral) in the semifinals.

Perhaps the most dominant player in the event, girls’ 18s No. 2 seed Gabriela Rondon (Hallandale Beach) has dropped a total of just 15 games en route to the divisional final. She handled both fifth-seeded Rose Seccia (Bradenton) and No. 8 Manuela Van Cotthem (Vero Beach) with relative ease on Sunday. In the top half of the draw, No. 1 Valeria Centeno (Hialeah) could not survive her second match tiebreak of the weekend, as unseeded Taly Licht (Hollywood) rallied for the 4-6, 6-3, 1-0 upset. Licht backed up that impressive victory by turning away Francesca Salvato (Miami Beach) 6-3, 6-1 in a meeting of unseeded hopefuls after Salvato had dismissed the fourth and sixth seeds in straight sets.
