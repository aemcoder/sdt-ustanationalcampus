UPDATE ON MAY 19:

The NCAA has updated their policies regarding the capacity of its championship events. As a result, capacity for the ongoing DI Tennis Championships, being held at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., has increased to 100% for all remaining sessions.

Additional tickets are already on sale for all remaining sessions of the individual event, which takes place from Sunday, May 23 and runs through May 28.  

Tickets for the individual event are for a full day of play. Tickets per session cost $10 and all tickets are general admission seating. 

Tickets are available for purchase HERE. 

All guests at the USTA National Campus are required to fill out this health questionnaire each day they visit.

For more information on this year's NCAA Championships, save this link: http://ustanationalcampus.com/ncaa.
