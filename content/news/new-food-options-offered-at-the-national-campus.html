The USTA National Campus is a one-of-a-kind facility, offering something for every tennis player and fan. And pretty soon, it’ll be an even better place to grab a quick meal to accompany your tennis.

Starting April 1, the Net Post Grill will officially be open for business at the Home of American Tennis. 

The restaurant, which joins the Courtside Café and Collegiate Café as food options on campus, will offer a variety of grill favorites, sandwiches, salads, smoothies and a variety of healthy snacks. 

Also, the dining options at the Net Post Grill will maintain the National Campus’ commitment to affordability, with no entrees exceeding $10 in price.

The space, which is open to the public seven days a week, will also be a place for events, weekly gatherings and team celebrations. Featuring a large outside dining area that overlooks the campus, it will play host to social events such as happy houses, live music and theme nights. 

The official hours for the restaurant are as follows*: 

Mon – Sat: 10 a.m. – 8 p.m.

Sun: 10 a.m. – 3 p.m.

* Times do not include special events, during which hours could vary.
