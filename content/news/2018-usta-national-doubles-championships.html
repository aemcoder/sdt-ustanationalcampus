2018 USTA National Doubles Championships

Sixth Annual Event Starts Thursday

August 22, 2018

style
article-hero

Returning to the USTA National Campus for a second straight year, the 2018 USTA National Doubles Championships is set to feature some of the nation’s most talented junior competitors in the boys’ and girls’ 14s and 16s age divisions. Now in its sixth year of existence, the USTA National Doubles Championships is the lone junior competition devoted to doubles.

The prestigious three-day event will get underway Thursday morning at 8 a.m. With the four divisions consisting of a 32-team compass draw, two rounds will be contested on both Thursday and Friday with all finals and third-place playoffs to take place on Saturday. For the boys’ 14s, the top five seeds have received a bye in the opening round. All matches will be the best of three tiebreak sets and each pair is guaranteed a minimum of four matches for the tournament.

As a Level 1 USTA National Championship, players will earn Level 1 National Rankings Points; additionally, top finishers in each division will also receive the USTA’s coveted Gold, Silver and Bronze Balls.

Duos earning No. 1 seeds on the boys’ side include John Lasanajak (Southern) and Cooper Williams (Eastern) in the 14s along with Connor and Jake Krug (Florida) in the 16s. For the girls’, Liv Hovde (Texas) and Ava Krug (Florida) will be the tandem to beat in the 14s; Sophia Fornaris (Florida) and Mia Kintiroglou (Eastern) have collected top billing in the 16s draw.

Notable among those listed above is Williams, who claimed the doubles title at the USTA Boys’ 12s National Clay Court Championships last summer at the USTA National Campus. Williams’ partner (Lasanajak) comes in fresh off winning two Bronze Balls at the USTA Boys’ 14s National Hard Court Championships less than two weeks ago.

Hovde and Krug will look to complete the rare feat of defending a national junior doubles title, as the two teamed up to best the competition in 2017. As the fifth-seeded duo, they reached the semifinals without dropping a set then toughed out back-to-back lengthy three-set matches to secure the championship victory. Meanwhile, Fornaris owns a doubles title she won at the National Campus as well, with hers coming in the 16s division of the USTA Florida “Bobby Curtis” Doubles Championships in October.

Additionally, 2017 boys’ 14s champions Alex Finkelstein (New England) and Nathan Mao (New England) now have their sights set on the 16s title; they are seeded fourth in the draw.
