Get to know rising pro Emilio Nava in the latest edition of "Campus Cruise."

The Woodland Hills, Calif., native has been training at the USTA National Campus .

Nava, a 2019 Junior US Open finalist, shared insights into his first year on the pro tour, his family's influence on him playing the game and some of his biggest goals for 2020.
