Three Americans Reach Quarterfinals

Thursday’s action at the Orlando Open saw the top two remaining seeds advance, while more upsets saw a pair of Americans knock off higher-seeded fellow countrymen en route to the quarterfinals.

No. 1 seed Darian King of Barbados continued to cruise through the field with a second consecutive straight-sets win. Fifth-seeded Norbert Gombos of Slovakia, on the other hand, needed more than three hours and a third-set tiebreak to defeat American wild card Evan Song. King and Gombos will face off in the quarterfinals.

Also advancing on Thursday were Americans Ulises Blanch, Marcos Giron, and wild card Michael Redlicki. Giron defeated No. 9 seed Dennis Novikov in straight sets, while Redlicki knocked off No. 11 seed Kevin King in a pair of all-American third round matches.

Rounding out the singles quarterfinalists are No. 12 seed Gastao Elias of Portugal and No. 13 seed Nino Serdarusic of Croatia.

The Orlando Open, which is a USTA Pro Circuit ATP Challenger event, is the first USTA Pro Circuit ATP Challenger event of the 2019 season and is the highest level of tennis on display to date at the USTA National Campus, with a prize-money pool of more than $54,000.

Play continues Friday with the singles quarterfinals and doubles semifinals. Play begins at 11 a.m. Admission to the Orlando Open is free.
