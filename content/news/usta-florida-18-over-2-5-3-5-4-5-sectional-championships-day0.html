USTA Florida 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5

Sectional Championships - Day 2

August 19, 2017

style
article-hero

Saturday was another busy one at the 2017 USTA Florida 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships. Both the Team USA and Har-Tru courts of the National Campus were filled with play throughout the day as four of the five divisions were whittled down to the semifinal and final rounds.

Alachua, Brevard, Orange/Seminole and Broward survived the round robin slate to move on to the men’s 3.5 semifinal round. All four squads did not suffer a loss in advancing. Alachua (Flight 1), Brevard (Flight 2) and Orange/Seminole (Flight 3) went 3-0 in their respective flights while Broward (Flight 4) came through with a 4-0 effort. Broward was easily the most dominant of the four, going 18-for-20 on the individual courts they contested and dropping just seven sets. Orange/Seminole will be their semifinal opponent as Alachua and Brevard will also be across the net from one another Sunday morning.

In the men’s 4.5 semifinals, Duval and Sarasota/Manatee will do battle while SMD and Alachua clash in the other matchup. Duval took tricky Flight 1, which used a double round robin format to decide its champion thanks to consisting of only three teams. A pair of wins over IRMSL along with splitting contests against Escambia was good enough to push Duval through. SMD narrowly escaped Flight 2, which saw three teams finish 2-1. With the first step in the tiebreaking procedure being individual courts won, SMD advanced over Broward and Hillsborough thanks to 12 court victories.  Sarasota/Manatee was impressive in defeating all three of their opponents in Flight 3, giving away just seven sets and a combined 88 games in doing so. With the division only having three flights, that left the door open for a second-place team to grab the wild card spot in the semifinals. Alachua (Flight 3) would be that team, as their winning percentage in individual courts played was the top among the non-flight winners.

The sectional champion coming out of the women’s 2.5 division will be the winner of Sunday morning’s match between Duval and Marion. Both teams sit at 3-1 overall, with Duval boasting a better individual court record. Hillsborough, which is also 3-1 with a head-to-head victory over Duval, has been mathematically eliminated from contention per the tiebreaking procedure.

All four women’s 3.5 semifinalists went undefeated in flight play. Broward emerged from Flight 1 unscathed behind 11 individual court wins to just four losses in their trio of dual matches. They will meet Marion, who also claimed 11 of the 15 courts they contested in Flight 2, in one semifinal. Flight 3 champion SMD and Flight 4 representative Duval will battle in the other. SMD survived the only five-team flight of the division while Duval dominated their trio of opponents, giving up a mere two courts and six total sets.

With the women’s 4.5 division being composed of only two flights, Broward (Flight 1) and Orange/Seminole/Lake (Flight 2) will face off in the final at 8:00 am. Broward posted a 3-0 dual match record, securing a place in the final thanks to a victory over Sarasota/Manatee, which finished with a 2-1 team record. Orange/Seminole/Lake came through a similar situation in Flight 2, with their defeat of Duval serving as the decisive win after both teams finished with the same amount of individual court victories (14) and Duval actually losing one less set over the two days of play.

Play continues on Sunday with the women’s 2.5 division wrapping up its round robin slate while the remaining divisions will contest their semifinals and finals. First matches of the day will be on court at 8 a.m. For updated flight standings and match schedules, click here.
