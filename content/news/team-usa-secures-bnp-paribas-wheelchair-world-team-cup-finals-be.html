USA Men Defeat Chile for Team Cup Finals Berth

February 2, 2018

style
article-hero

Reversing their fortunes from Wednesday, the Team USA men defeated Chile by a 2-0 margin on Friday in the championship round of the 2018 BNP Paribas Wheelchair World Team Cup Americas Qualifications. With the team victory, the Americans secured their spot in the BNP Paribas Wheelchair World Team Cup Finals, to be held in Apeldoorn, Netherlands, May 28 through June 3.

Just two days prior, the Chileans earned a 2-1 win over the United States after claiming the second singles position and the lone doubles matchup. Chris Herman faced Chile's Francisco Cayulef, who had beaten Herman 6-3, 6-2 on Wednesday in the squads' round robin tie. Herman won more games in the opening set on Friday than he did in the prior encounter, toughing out the set 6-4. The second frame was equally as tightly-contested, but once again Herman hung on for the 6-4 triumph to put Team USA in front 1-0.

American Casey Ratzlaff was also up against the same foe he took on earlier in the week in Alexander Cataldo. Ratzlaff cruised on that day, losing only three games, but it was a much different story on Friday. He was able to grab the first set by the slimmest of margins, prevailing seven points to three in a tiebreak. Cataldo battled back in the second, 6-4, to force a decisive third frame. From there, Ratzlaff held a 5-2 lead only to see it dwindle to 5-4; however, he found a way to hold off Cataldo in the final game for the 7-6(3), 4-6, 6-4 victory to clinch the tie. The doubles match was abandoned.

In the third-place playoff, Colombia turned in a solid effort to beat Ecuador 2-0. Eliecer Oquendo dropped four games in taking out Eduardo Lopez 6-2, 6-2. That was followed up by Colombian Manuel Sanchez outlasting Ecuador's Francisco Beltran 6-2, 2-6, 7-6(2). Again, with the tie already decided, the doubles contest was not played.

With the conclusion of play, the Argentina women and Team USA men will now represent the Americas region at the World Team Cup this year. Those two teams fell just short of that honor a year ago, both losing to Brazil.
