Broward 3.0 Men Win Title on Sunday

July 21, 2019

style
article-hero

It was another day of delays for the 2019 USTA Florida 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships at the USTA National Campus, forcing a number of matches indoors. Amongst the results on Sunday was Broward taking home one title along with Hillsborough putting themselves in position to claim as many as three crowns Monday.

As expected, the 3.0 division on the men’s side was decided when Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) and Leon (Tom Brown Park – Tallahassee) squared off. Broward entered the day with more court victories and fewer sets lost, so their 3-2 dual win over Leon locked up the sectional title. At the end of play, Broward went a perfect 4-0 while Leon finished 3-1.

A quartet of undefeated teams safely moved into the divisional semifinals in the 3.0 women’s competition. Duval (Southside Tennis Complex – Jacksonville) in Flight 3 and Pinellas (Belleair Country Club) from Flight 4 were absolutely dominant, with Duval grabbing 13 court wins with just four sets lost while Pinellas went 18-for-20 in their individual contest. Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) swept through Flight 1 on the strength of 12 court victories; Flight 2 went to SMD (Don Shulas Athletic Club – Hialeah) despite Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) managing to equal their number of individual contests claimed. In Monday’s semifinals, Hillsborough is set to clash with SMD and Duval will go up against Pinellas.

None of the three men’s 4.0 flight winners got there in convincing fashion on Sunday. Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) clinched Flight 1 with a sterling 3-0 record, although their 10 individual wins and 13 sets lost just edged out SMD (Alper JCC – Miami). Three teams tied atop the standings in Flight 2, where Hillsbrough’s (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) one-sided dual triumph over Orange/Seminole (Villagewalk at Lake Nona) proved to be the difference. All was not lost for Orange/Seminole, however, as they earned the wild card spot into the semifinals. Sarasota (Bath and Racquet Club) fought their way to the top of Flight 3 after defeating IRMSL (Buttonwood Tennis Club – Stuart) to finish undefeated.

The afternoon semifinal duals both ended in 3-2 score lines in favor of Hillsborough and Orange/Seminole, setting up a rematch in Monday’s championship round. Hillsborough drew Broward, a matchup in which they quickly took control. Jeffrey Stern claimed the second singles spot 6-4, 6-4, the same margin Tu Phan and Richard Helme won by at the third doubles position. Marvin Ho and Robert Miller cruised at line one doubles 6-3, 6-3. Hillsborough retired on the other two courts after the dual was clinched. All five courts were completed in the semifinal between Orange/Seminole and Sarasota, but no set was closer than 6-2. Ruben Sepulveda teamed up with Pedro Estrada to drop just one game at the third doubles position while Julio Infante and Ignacio Guida raced to victory at line two 6-2, 6-2. Constantino Trimarchi was victorious 6-2, 6-2 on the second singles court as well. The final is set for 8:30 a.m. on Monday.

The women’s 4.0 final will pit the most dominant team with the squad that just barely snuck into the semifinals. To finish up flight play, Hillsborough (Westchase Community Association – Tampa) and Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) had no issue getting through Flight 1 and 2, respectively. Meanwhile, IRMSL (Buttonwood Tennis Club – Stuart) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) struggled their way to take the remaining flights despite IRMSL losing more sets and games than Sarasota (Lakewood National – Bradenton) in Flight 3 and Marion surviving the same scenario over Polk (Country Club of Winter Haven) in Flight 4.

For the semifinals, Hillsborough outlasted Orange/Seminole thanks to prevailing in a match tiebreak at the No. 2 singles spot. Hillsborough got a quick 6-1, 6-3 win from Sonya Sherrell at the top singles position plus a tough 6-4, 7-5 effort by Charlotte Tao and Melinda London at line two doubles. That set the stage for Wenday Baier’s 6-3, 4-6, 1-0 victory on the other singles court. IRMSL recorded three wins in straight sets to take down Marion in the other semifinal. IRMSL relied on their singles play, coming through on both courts, including a tight 6-2, 7-6 score line from Elisabeth Smith at line two. Guadalupe Avinzano dominated court one 6-1, 6-3 while Somhathai Immerman and partner Theresa Dubiel took the second doubles position 6-2, 6-3. The divisional championship is set for 8:30 a.m. Monday morning.
