Day one of the USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships wrapped up abruptly Saturday evening due to rain, postponing the completion of just one dual match.

Flight 1 of the 7.0 competition was the lone flight to be impacted by the inclement weather. Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) currently sits atop the standings at 2-0 overall, but 1-0 Duval (Huguenot Park – Jacksonville Beach) was on court battling 0-1 Sarasota (Cy Walton Racquet Center – Bradenton) when play was halted. Alachua also wrapped up the day with one win and one loss, leaving them still in the hunt to advance to the knockout stages.

The two other 7.0 flights began to take shape as well heading into Sunday. In Flight 2, Volusia (Florida Tennis Center – Daytona Beach) and South Miami-Dade (Crandon Park Tennis Center – Key Biscayne) will likely decide who moves on to the semifinals when they go head-to-head; the same scenario has developed for Pinellas (Seminole Lake Family Tennis) and Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) in Flight 3.

Meanwhile, the 9.0 division was able to complete all of their scheduled duals before the rain began. Both South Miami-Dade (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center – Miami) and Pinellas (Innisbrook Resort – Palm Harbor) managed to go 2-0 on the day; South Miami-Dade claimed five court victories on the strength of 10 sets won while Pinellas earned four individual matchup wins.
