USTA Florida Tri-Level League Playoffs

Five Titles Decided on Sunday

February 3, 2019

style
article-hero

A total of five sectional titles were won on Sunday at the USTA Florida Tri-Level League Playoffs, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Overall, Marion and Broward came up with two divisional crowns while Sarasota produced one. Two more titles will be on the line Monday.

Teams winning their respective divisional playoff will earn the right to represent the Florida Section at the 2019 Tri-Level Championships, held at the Indian Well Tennis Garden over the final weekend of the 2019 BNP Paribas Open (March 15-17).

Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) swept the 3.5 competition on the day. In men’s action, the squad collected two more victories over Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center) to finish the weekend 3-0; not only did Marion win all nine individual contests, but they also did not surrender even a single set. Their women had a much tougher road to the sectional title, coming through with a dual win Sunday that vaulted them from fourth to first in the division with a 2-1 record. Marion was awarded the team crown by virtue of their head-to-head defeat of Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center), the other team to finish 2-1 for the event.

In the men’s 4.0 competition, Broward (City of Oakland Park) faced little resistance in posting a perfect 4-0 mark to take home the team trophy. Their efforts included going a perfect 12-for-12 in individual court matchups and giving away only one set in those duals.

Monday morning’s semifinals have been set for the women’s 4.0 division. Three squads – Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus), Duval (Palm Coast Tennis Center), and Brevard (DeSoto Recreation Complex) bring undefeated records into the final day of play, while Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) earned the wild card berth by finishing as the runner-up in Flight 2 behind Duval. Marion will face off with Brevard while Duval clashes with Orange/Seminole.

As anticipated, Broward (City of Oakland Park) and Duval (Florida Yacht Club) easily advanced to the championship dual of the men’s 4.5 division. However, the final was all Broward, who toughed out a trio of tightly-contested contests in straight sets to prevail.

While Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park) from Flight 1 and Sarasota (Lakewood National) out of Flight 3 secured semifinal spots by going 3-0, Escambia (Independent) needed the tiebreaking procedure to fall in their favor to move on from Flight 2. Those three were joined by wild card Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) in the semifinal round, where Orange/Seminole swept Sarasota 3-0 and Pinellas slipped past Escambia 2-1.

In the 5.0 women’s division, Sarasota (Esplanade Golf and Country Club) did suffer a loss at the hands of Broward (City of Oakland Park) on the day to leave them both at 3-1 overall, but still managed to finish comfortably atop the lone flight by virtue of dropping three few sets and 32 less games than Broward.
