Singles Finals Set Heading into Last Day of Play

February 11, 2018

style
article-hero

Two days down and one to go at the 2018 USTA February National Level 2, hosted by the USTA National Campus, after the completion of play on Sunday. The final day of action will begin with the doubles and consolation singles semifinals, followed by the finals of the two singles draws.

Contesting the championship match on the boys’ side will be No. 3 seed Henry Patrick Cacciatore (Florida) and No. 8 seed Ronald Hohmann (Eastern). Clashing for the girls’ singles title are third-seeded Briana Crowley (Midwest) and four seed Alana Wolfberg (Florida).

Cacciatore and Hohmann go into Monday having not lost a set throughout the tournament. The day proved to be a relatively easy one for the boys’ No. 3 seed, who handled Daniel Gale of USTA Florida 6-2, 6-4. That was followed by Cacciatore receiving a walkover prior to the start of his quarterfinal matchup with second-seeded J J Mercer (Midwest). Meanwhile, Hohmann delivered a pair of solid performances on day two to reach the final. He came through with a comprehensive 6-3, 6-3 defeat of the top seed, Noah Schachter (Florida) before toughing out a 6-4, 6-2 triumph against No. 4 seed Matthew Segura, also of USTA Florida, in the semifinals.

Hohmann will be in search of his second title already on the year, having captured the singles crown at the Level 3 event at Crandon Park in January.

Crowley and Wolfberg had far more difficult paths to the girls’ final. To get her day started, third seed Crowley won the opening set, 6-4, against No. 8 seed Carly Briggs of USTA Southern only to see Briggs reel off six consecutive games to force a decisive third frame; Crowley narrowly survived seven games to five to move into the semifinals. She faced an even stiffer challenge against another USTA Southern representative in unseeded Kylie Collins, quickly facing a set deficit when Collins won the opener 6-2. Crowley was down a break early in the second as well, but clawed her way back to eventually prevail 7-5. From there, Crowley maintained the momentum for a 6-2 victory in the third frame.

Wolfberg had her chances to take care of No. 6 seed Sophia Hatton (Eastern) in straight sets after pulling out the first 6-4, but failed to capitalize. Hatton extended the second to a tiebreak, which she claimed eight points to six; however, Wolfberg proved too tough in the decider, taking up 6-3. In her semifinal clash with top seed Anika Yarlagadda of USTA Midwest, Wolfberg closed set one by a 6-4 margin after hold a 5-2 lead and followed that up with a more straightforward second set, 6-3, to book her spot in the final.

It would be the second title in two weeks for Wolfberg, who eased through the singles draw at the Level 5 Orange County Girls’ 18s just last week. She is also seeking her second tournament victory at the National Campus after winning the 2017 Bobby Curtis Girls’ 18s singles trophy last summer.

In the boys’ doubles semifinals, No. 1 seeds and Midwest duo Robert Cash and Mercer will be across the net from unseeded Cacciatore and Schachter. The other semifinal pits another USTA Florida tandem, fourth seeds Juan Bianchi and Tyler Stice, versus Russell Berdusco (Florida) and Keshav Chopra (Southern).

The matchups in the final four of the girls’ doubles draw have the top-seeded team of Clarissa and Christina Hand (Midwest) up against third seeds Yekaterina Alferova and Collins (Southern) along with a USTA Southern battle in No. 4 seeds Briggs and AnneMarie Hiser facing off with unseeded Ava Hrastar and Rush Marsh.
