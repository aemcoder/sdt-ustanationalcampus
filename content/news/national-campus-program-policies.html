1.  STANDARDS FOR CONDUCT

By registering for programs or booking reservations, our guests are agreeing to the following: We expect coaches, players, parents of players and spectators to be considerate of others, display courteous behavior including appropriate language and exhibit a spirit of cooperation. Your presence at the USTA National Campus is a privilege to be respected and your cooperation in abiding by posted rules, signs, or verbal instructions from our professional staff is expected. Violation of these standards will not be tolerated and may result in removal from Campus programs and activities.

Prohibited Items

∙ Video recording devices

∙ Outside alcohol 

∙ Pets (except service animals) 

∙ Weapons  

∙ Drones (UAS –Unmanned Aircraft Systems) or other model aircraft

∙ Skateboards, scooters, hoverboards, roller blades, and bicycles 

∙ Any other items that may be potentially harmful or disruptive as determined by USTA 

∙ Smoking (including e-cigarettes)

2.  PROGRAMMING WITHDRAWAL/REFUNDS

There will be no refunds/credits after a program’s start date OR after the first class has occurred when players register after a program start date. Exceptions may be made in instances of medical withdrawal or relocation (see below).

A. MEDICAL WITHDRAWAL: For circumstances due to long term injury or illness, please contact the Programming Department in writing to request your withdrawal. withdrawals will be granted for only classes forward. A credit will be placed on the family’s account to use towards future private lessons, court reservations, or program registrations. Credits will not be given for classes that have passed; only classes moving forward.

i. A Doctor’s note from a medical professional will be required to process medical withdrawal credits.

ii. Requests must be submitted in writing to NationalCampusPrograms@usta.com and take 2-3 business days for review and processing.

B. RELOCATION: A player in an Instructional Clinic that is relocating and can no longer travel to the National Campus, with 30 days’ notice can request a prorated refund for classes not attended.

C. DROP-IN RESERVATIONS: Players may drop-in for available programs up to 1-week in advance of the program date. Players may cancel their reservation up until 24-hrs before the class time to avoid full charge on the program price. Cancellations attempted within 24-hrs of the class time are not eligible for refund. Drop-in registrations are not eligible for make-up classes if an absence occurs and their cancellation window has passed.

D. CAMP CANCELATION/MAKEUP: No make-ups for any missed portion, days, or weeks for adult or junior camps. The terms of cancelation are agreed upon in the terms and conditions when registering for the camp

Cancellations requested after this time will be processed, but refunds will not be given. No exceptions.

PROGRAM/CLASS CHANGES

Players wishing to change their session registration after the program’s start date (OR after the first class has occurred when the player registers after the program start date) may do so if space is available in another program. An administrative fee of $20 will be applied to each class wishing to be changed. If space is not available in another class, the withdrawal policy applies. Exceptions will be made if the program’s Head Professional determines that the player has been placed in the incorrect level of a program.

RAIN CREDITS & REFUNDS DUE TO WEATHER

In instances of weather, the National Campus will attempt to notify players at least 1 hour in advance if a program is canceled. Notifications are distributed in text messages and emails.

A. SESSION REGISTRATIONS: Players registered for the session will receive a rain credit for classes that have been canceled or partially canceled due to weather. Rain Credit Schedule below. Credits are processed on the next office business day and are applied to the family account. Credits may be used towards future program registrations, private lessons, or court reservations.

B. DROP-IN REGISTRATIONS: Players registered as a drop-in for an individual class will receive a refund to their original form of payment if the has been canceled or partially canceled due to weather. Rain Credit Schedule below. Credits are processed on the next office business day and are applied to the player’s original form of payment. 

C. HURRICANES/EXTREME WEATHER: If it is determined that the National Campus will be CLOSED IN ADVANCE for weather due to Hurricanes or other extreme weather, players will be refunded to their original form of payment for classes canceled.

D. COURT RESERVATIONS: Effective May 1, 2026 - Court reservations are open 2 weeks in advance. Additionally, reservations will be charged in full at the time of booking, and rain refunds will be processed back to the original form of payment the following business day according to the Rain Credit Schedule below. 

E. RAIN CREDIT/REFUND SCHEDULE:

PRIVATE LESSON & RESERVATION CANCELLATIONS

A. Private and small group lessons may be canceled without penalty up to 24 hours in advance of the reservation time. Cancellations less than 24 hours in advance will incur the full lesson charge. Same-day lesson reservations are not eligible for a refund if canceled

B. Court reservations may be canceled without penalty up to 24 hours in advance of the reservation time. Cancellations less than 24 hours in advance will incur the full court charge. Same-day reservations are not eligible for a refund if canceled.

JUNIOR PROGRAMS - MOVE UP

A. Please read the following rules and procedures for information regarding Junior Player Move Up Policies:

B. Players are required to meet certain competencies in order to move up a level. National Campus evaluates players 3 times per year for potential pathway promotion. During the approximate time frames of August, December and May.

C. National Campus expects to have players in classes that can beat players from the higher level class. Match score is not always an indicator of readiness to move to the next level of play.

D. National Campus does not move players based on wins. Technical deficiencies such as grips and swing patterns will be taken into consideration.

E. Top players in a class will not automatically be promoted to a higher level class.

F. No child can be moved up without the approval of the Head Professionals. Only the tennis teaching professionals can promote a player to the next level, not the player or the parent. The team of National Campus tennis professionals meets to discuss all players skills, progress and technique. Other player aspects taken into consideration are age, coachability, maturity, level of commitment, ability.

G. Parents and players will be notified prior to the move up months via email from the Head Professionals.

PROGRAM LOCATION & TEACHING PROFESSIONAL ASSIGNMENTS

A. Program and court locations will be moved throughout the session. Players should anticipate their programs/reservations being in a different location weekly. Players are required to check-in at the front desk for court location.

B. Teaching pros assignments for classes are subject to change. A player’s participation in the program may not be contingent on the coach assigned.

LIGHTNING/DANGEROUS WEATHER IN THE AREA

When the lightning alarm sounds, players should evacuate the courts and move to official shelter areas. Players may not leave shelter areas until the “ALL CLEAR” has sounded. 

Players and adults should enter and exit through the Welcome Center, except during rain or Red Alert conditions, when the side gate will be open for indoor classes.

SAFEPLAY

We combine comprehensive education, background screening, and clear policies to implement standards for athlete safety. Working alongside the U.S. Center for SafeSport and the United States Olympic and Paralympic Committee, we develop safety policies and resources. Safe Play empowers the entire tennis community to recognize, prevent, and report misconduct, ensuring a brighter, safer future for our sport.

All USTA employees are required to complete the U.S. Center for SafeSport's training annually. This training equips employees with information on identifying, preventing, and reporting misconduct. View the list of SafeSport Trained USTA-employees.

The USTA National Campus is a Safe Play compliant facility, upholding the Safe Play Policy.
