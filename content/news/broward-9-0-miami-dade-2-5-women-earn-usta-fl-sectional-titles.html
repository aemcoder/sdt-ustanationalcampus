Broward 9.0, Miami/Dade 2.5 Women Prevail

June 23, 2019

style
article-hero

Two team champions were crowned on Sunday with the third to be decided on Monday at the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships and 40 & Over Women’s 2.5 Invitational.

Coming through in the 9.0 division was Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston); the 2.5 squad representing Miami/Dade (Independent) took home the Invitational crown.

The 9.0 final pitted Sarasota (Bath and Racquet Club) against Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) after the two teams fought their way to 3-0 records in flight play. Despite a dominating performance at line one doubles by Sarasota, it would be Broward toughing out two match tiebreaks to clinch the victory. Sarasota’s Dean Dancer and Adriana Garcia looked sharp on court one, coming through 6-3, 6-1. Sarasota was in a winning position after earning opening frames on the other two courts as well, but could not close either out. Broward’s Kenny Torrellas and Barbara Barrios rallied back on court two 2-6, 6-4, 1-0. That set the stage for Justin and Ivy Touchstone to deliver in the clutch in the form of a 3-6, 6-4, 1-0 comeback.

Captain Emilio Baez’s complete Broward roster includes Rubens Jacob, Laura Di Lauro, Gregorio Rodriguez, Ivy Touchstone, Lorena Villar Seminario de Guzman-Barron, Jacqueline Pichardo, Leonardo Torrellas, Justin Touchstone, Kenny Torrellas, Alvaro Ortiz, and Barrios.

Hillsborough (Van Dyke Farms - Odessa) and Miami/Dade (Independent) faced little resistance in claiming their respective flight to advance to the championship round of the Women’s 2.5 Invitational. In their head-to-head contest with the trophy on the line, Miami/Dade grabbed first sets on all three courts en route to a 3-0 sweep. Claudia Paz and Cynthia Turcios easily claimed the second doubles matchup 6-1, 6-0 while Maira Maguire played solid in the lone singles contest, taking it 6-1, 6-4. Belkys Ortiz and Paola Campanella closed out the win with a 6-4, 3-6, 1-0 effort.

The full Miami/Dade roster includes Campanella, Ortiz, Beth Story, Maguire, Paz, Turcios, and Mildred Houdayer.

Four teams managed to survive two full days of round robin action to move on to Monday’s 7.0 semifinals. Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center – Plantation) breezed through Flight 1, rounding up nine court victories while losing a mere six sets and 76 games. Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) went a perfect 6-for-6 in individual contests on Sunday to bump their total up to 10, allowing them to run away with Flight 2. SMD (Crandon Park Tennis Center – Key Biscayne) also posted an unblemished 4-0 record in Flight 3 on the strength of nine courts won despite giving away a total of 92 games. Rounding out the teams still in the hunt for the sectional crown is Collier (Pelican Bay Community Park – Naples) out of Flight 4 – a team that not only needed to win twice on Sunday but also needed Pinellas to stumble. All the breaks went their way as the day played out, setting up their semifinal showdown with SMD; Broward and Alachua are set to square off in the other.

Monday’s 7.0 semifinals will take to the courts at 8 a.m. with the championship dual to follow at 11 a.m.
