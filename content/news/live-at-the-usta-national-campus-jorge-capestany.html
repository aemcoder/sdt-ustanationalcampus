The USTA and the United States Professional Tennis Association (USPTA) will be hosting the 2017 Next Generation: A Tennis Innovation Workshop this week in Orlando. The conference is the country’s largest gathering of tennis professionals, industry leaders and representatives, manufacturers, wholesalers and media. Guest speakers throughout the conference include legendary tennis coach Nick Bollettieri, former world No. 1 and Grand Slam champion Stan Smith and Hall of Famer GiGi Fernandez.
