2018 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships

First Matches Get Underway Saturday

December 5, 2018

style
article-hero

Closing out USTA Florida League action for the 2018 calendar year at the USTA National Campus will be the Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships this weekend. First ball will be struck Saturday morning at 8 a.m.

Each dual match will feature three courts of doubles; in order for a team to claim victory in a dual, two of the three courts must be captured. All matches will use ad scoring and be the best two-of-three sets with a match tiebreak in lieu of a full third.

Just two squads – Alachua (Westside Park Gainesville) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) – will be vying for the men’s 5.5 title this year; seven teams have been divided into two flights in the women’s 5.5 competition, with the flight winners advancing to the divisional championship dual. Three flights compose the 7.5 men’s division, meaning the flight winners plus a wild card entrant will advance to the semifinal round. An even four flights within the 7.5 women’s competition will trim down to just the quartet of flight champions moving on to the semifinals.

Six women’s 9.5 teams have been placed into one flight, as the team finishing with the best overall record will be crowned the champion. SMD (Doral Park) and Broward (Midtown Athletic Center) have opted to stay in Miami and duke it out for the men’s 9.5 title.

Matches will take to the courts starting at 8 a.m. all three days.

Five different regions from across the state were amongst the 2017 sectional champions. In the 5.5 competitions, the Leon (Tom Brown Park) men finished 3-1 to sit atop the lone three-team flight in the division while the Duval (Southside Tennis Center) women bested Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) 2-1. For the 7.5 divisions, Polk (Lake Wales Country Club) took down SMD (Doral Park Country Club) 2-1 in the men’s final and the Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) women got past Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) two matches to one. Additionally, the 9.5 Pinellas (Waterchase) women defeated Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center) by a 2-1 margin.
