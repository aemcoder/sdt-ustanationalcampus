Leon 7.0, Pinellas 9.0 Take Home Titles

June 11, 2017

style
article-hero

Two more teams claimed sectional titles on Sunday at the USTA National Campus, as play in the 2017 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Championships concluded. Leon (7.0) and Pinellas (9.0) came through to earn the state’s top honor, booking their spots in their respective divisional championships later this year.

The morning started off with semifinal action from the 7.0 squads. Leon took on Hillsborough in one semifinal as Polk and Bay took to the courts in the other. Both matches would end in 2-1 decisions, with Leon and Polk moving on. Leon dropped court one 3-6, 6-1, 1-0 but triumphed at the second position 4-6, 7-6, 1-0 and third spot 7-6, 6-4. Polk made much quicker work of Bay, highlighted by a 6-0, 6-0 victory on court two. Bay did manage to win at the top position 6-1, 6-2, but Polk finished off the team win 6-1, 7-6 on court three.

In the championship tilt, Leon raced out to a terrific start, polishing off first sets at the top two spots while Polk took the first set at court three. Polk would win that matchup 6-2, 6-2 while Leon prevailed 6-2, 6-4 at the second position. That directed all eyes to the number one court, which split sets after Polk won the second set 6-2. However, Leon bounced back to claim the decisive super tiebreak and the sectional crown.

The 9.0 final was not full of the same drama. Pinellas was on a mission on all three courts against Escambia, staking leads early on and never looking back. In the end, Pinellas was able to raise the trophy after dropping a combined total of 14 games in the three individual matchups. The team posted final margins of 6-2, 6-4 (court one), 6-3, 6-0 (court two) and 6-2, 6-3 (court three).
