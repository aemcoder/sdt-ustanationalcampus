2018 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships

Sunshine State Back in Action

November 30, 2018

style
article-hero

The last month of the 2018 USTA National Campus event calendar kicks off with the 2018 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships. First ball is slated to be struck Saturday morning with matches continuing through Monday afternoon.

Each dual match will feature three courts of doubles; in order for a team to claim victory in a dual, two of the three courts must be captured. All matches will use ad scoring and be the best two-of-three sets with a match tiebreak in lieu of a full third.

A total of seven teams have been divided into two flights in the men’s 6.5 divisions, with only the two squads finishing atop the flights moving on to Monday’s championship dual. An even 16 teams are set to compete for the women’s 6.5 title, meaning Monday will see the quartet of flight winners move into the championship semifinal round to start the day. Both 8.5 competitions are composed of just three flights, which will produce three top finishers plus a wild card entrant advancing to Monday’s semifinals.

Action is set to get underway at 8 a.m. on both Saturday and Sunday while Monday’s play will take to the courts starting at 8:30 a.m.

At the 2017 Sectional Championships, four different regions were represented amongst the winners. In the men’s 6.5 final, Duval (Julington Creek Plantation) defeated Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center) 2-1, while Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) swept Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) 3-0 for the women’s 6.5 trophy. For the 8.5 champions, Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Center) claimed the men’s title over Sarasota (Payne Park) 2-1 and Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center) got past Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Center) 2-1 on the women’s side.
