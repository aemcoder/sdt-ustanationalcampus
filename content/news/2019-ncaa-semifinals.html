NCAA Finals are Set

Champions will be crowned on May 18 at the USTA National Campus.

USTA Staff | May 18, 2019

style
article-hero

Fans were treated to an incredible day of tennis on May 18 as schools secured their spots in the finals of the 2019 NCAA Division I Men's and Women's Tennis Championships.

The USTA National Campus will showcase the men's final between Texas and Wake Forest on Sunday at 3 p.m., while the women's final pits Stanford vs. Georgia immediately afterward.
