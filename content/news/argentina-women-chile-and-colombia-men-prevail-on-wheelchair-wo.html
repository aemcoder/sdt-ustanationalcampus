Argentina Women, Chile and Colombia Men Prevail

January 31, 2018

style
article-hero

Wednesday saw the women take to the courts for the first time along with the continuation of men's round robin play at the 2018 BNP Paribas Wheelchair World Team Cup Americas Qualifications. It was the Argentinian women who outlasted Colombia while the men of Chile and Colombia also collected dual victories on day two.

Argentina's Antonella Pralong got the women's competition started with a 6-2, 6-0 defeat of Colombia's Johana Martinez at the second singles spot. Colombian No. 1 Angelica Bernal evened the dual after she fought back from a set down to best Florencia Moreno by the final of 3-6, 6-1, 6-2. A tense doubles matchup would decide the winner, where Pralong and Moreno held off Bernal and Martinez 6-1, 2-6, 10-8.

The men of Chile turned in another solid performance on Wednesday, this time securing a 2-1 dual win over Team USA. Francisco Cayulef surrendered just five games against Chris Herman, taking the second position 6-3, 6-2. American Casey Ratzlaff kept the his squad's hopes alive by blitzing through Chile's Alexander Cataldo 6-2, 6-1. However, the Chilean duo of Cataldo and Cayulef proved too strong in doubles, toughing out a tense 6-4, 7-6(7) triumph versus Herman and Ratzlaff.

Looking to bounce back after coming up just short against the Americans on Tuesday, Colombia earned a 3-0 sweep against Ecuador. The opening set of the tie was back-and-forth, eventually going the way of Colombian Eliecer Oquendo, 7-5. He made quick work of the second frame, winning it 6-1 to claim the No. 2 singles spot over Eduardo Lopez. Manuel Sanchez easily clinched the team victory for Colombia, giving away one game only against Francisco Beltran at the top spot. The sweep was then completed after Oquendo and Sanchez took the doubles by the margin of 6-3, 6-2.

Day three's slate of matches will get underway at 1 p.m. and will feature three duals - Team USA vs. Ecuador and Chile vs. Colombia on the men's side along with a rematch of the women representing Argentina and Colombia.
