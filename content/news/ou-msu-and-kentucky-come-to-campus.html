Oklahoma, Mississippi State, Kentucky all take the courts Feb. 23-24

Collegiate Series Schedule Resumes

Brian Ormiston | February 21, 2019

style
article-hero

Three of the best men’s teams in the country flock to Orlando for the USTA National Campus Collegiate Series Feb. 23-24.

First up will be No. 12 Mississippi State tangling with No. 19 Oklahoma on Saturday, Feb. 23, at 2 p.m. Oklahoma will then return to the courts to take on Kentucky on Sunday, Feb. 24, at 12 p.m.

Admission and parking are free for all Collegiate Series events, which take place at the Collegiate Center.
