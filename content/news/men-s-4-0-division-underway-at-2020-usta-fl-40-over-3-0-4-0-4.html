Men's 4.0 Division Underway

October 30, 2020

style
article-hero

The 2020 USTA FL 40 & Over Men’s 3.0/4.0/4.5+ & Women’s 4.5+ Sectional Championships began on Friday with the men’s 4.0 division taking to the courts. All teams in the field played two duals over the course of the day.

Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) took control of Flight 1 with two impressive showings, as their players won five of the six individual matchups they took part in on the strength of 11 sets and 70 games claimed. Even more dominant was Broward (Parkland Tennis Center) in Flight 2, going a perfect 6-for-6 without dropping a single set; in total, Broward earned 73 games while giving away just 29.

Flight 3 saw both Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) and Miami-Dade (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center – Miami) jump to the top of the standings with 2-0 records. The two squads were victorious on four courts with eight sets to their credit. Meanwhile, Volusia (Daytona Beach Shores Parks and Recreation) will bring a 1-1 record in Saturday, keeping them in the running as well.

Men’s 4.0 contests will wrap up on Saturday, which will set the stage for Sunday’s knockout stages, while the remaining three divisions are set to get underway. First matches are scheduled for 7:30 a.m.
