Titles to be Earned Monday

August 11, 2019

style
article-hero

Two days are now in the books at the 2019 USTA Florida 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional Championships. Three divisions will now move into their knockout stages while the remaining three will complete one final round of flight play, with six sectional champions in total to be crowned on Monday.

Leon’s (Tom Brown Park – Tallahassee) afternoon defeat of Broward (Weston Tennis Center) put them in the driver’s seat for the men’s 3.0 division. With one round left to play, Leon sits at 4-0 despite having fewer court victories and more sets lost than Broward. A Leon win over Escambia Monday morning would secure the team title.

Four teams navigated their way through 3.0 women’s flight play to advance to Monday’s semifinals. Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) faced no resistance in Flight 1, taking 14 of the 15 courts they contested while losing just seven sets. Collier (Naples Grande Beach Resort) and Duval (San Jose Country Club – Jacksonville) finished with similar numbers, collecting 11 individual matchups while dropping 10 sets; Duval had a bit of an easier time, as they let 90 games slip away compared to 113 for Collier. Hillsborough (Northdale Golf & Tennis Club) toughed out a 3-2 triumph over Pinellas (Safety Harbor Resort and Spa) to take the top spot in Flight 4. In the semifinals, Broward will be up against Collier and Duval is set to clash with Hillsborough.

Sunday also saw the conclusion of flight play in the men’s 4.0 division, as the remaining four squads will return on Monday to compete in the semifinal round. All four got through Saturday and Sunday without suffering a dual loss. Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) and Broward (Weston Tennis Center) placed atop Flights 2 and 3, respectively, after picking up 12 total court victories; Hillsborough surrendered fewer sets (seven) but Broward gave away a significantly lower number of games (91). Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) from Flight 1 and SMD (Coral Pine Park – Pinecrest) out of Flight 4 also remained in the hunt to take home the title, with each earning 11 of the 15 individual matchups they took part in over the two days. Monday’s semifinal duals have pitted Orange/Seminole versus Hillsborough and Broward battling against SMD.

Winning their respective 4.0 flights on the women’s side to safely move on to the semifinal stage were Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville), Orange/Seminole (Fort Gatlin Recreation Center – Orlando) and Broward (City of Oakland Park). Alachua’s head-to-head win over IRMSL (Lawnwood Tennis Complex – Fort Pierce) proved the difference in Flight 1, as IRMSL actually recorded more court victories with fewer sets lost. With three sweeps in their first three duals, Orange/Seminole had clinched Flight 2 before even taking the court in the final round of flight play; they finished up with 18 individual victories compared to only two losses. Meanwhile, Broward managed to edge out SMD (Kerdyk Biltmore Tennis Center – Coral Gables) in Flight 3 on the strength of 14 individual matchups won. Monday morning’s semifinal pairings have Alachua facing Broward and Orange/Seminole across the net from SMD, which earned the wild card spot.

In the men’s 5.0+ competition, Monday’s head-to-head match between Orange/Seminole (Winter Park Racquet Club) and Hillsborough (East Lake Woodlands Country Club – Oldsmar) will determine which squad is crowned as champion. Hillsborough entered the day undefeated, but stumbled against SMD (Crandon Park Tennis Center – Key Biscayne), who incidentally also handed Orange/Seminole their lone loss for the weekend. The de facto divisional final will get started at 8:30 a.m. Monday morning.

The women’s 5.0+ division was originally scheduled to wrap up Sunday evening, but heavy rain washed out the decisive dual between Orange/Seminole (National Campus – Orlando) and Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa). Hillsborough holds the upper hand with a 3-0 team record, having won eight courts overall while the only they lost was by retirement. Orange/Seminole sits at 2-1, and with just six court victories to their credit, will need to sweep Hillsborough in order to capture the sectional crown. Their match will resume at 8 a.m. Monday.
