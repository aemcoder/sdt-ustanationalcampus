Teams to Compete for National Crowns on Sunday

October 21, 2017

Day two of the 2017 USTA League National Adult 40 & Over 4.0 Championships, hosted at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., saw matches conclude in the early evening on Saturday. With that comes the end of flight play, and the semifinals are set. 

For the women’s side, SSH Green (Intermountain) will be up against Eastern Massachusetts (New England) while Southeast Michigan (Midwest) is slated to take on Dallas (Texas). In men’s semifinal action, Washington, D.C. (Mid-Atlantic) and Philadelphia (Middle States) will square off to go along with the Broward (Florida) versus New Orleans (Southern) contest.

SSH Green was tested consistently in Flight 1, but rose to the occasion each and every time to finish with a perfect 4-0 team record. They lost 19 sets, five more than second place Florida, and 174 games, but came out on top in all four duals.

Eastern Massachusetts escaped Flight 3 thanks to the tiebreaker procedure. After sweeping Hawaii, they picked up a slim victory over Puerto Rico (Caribbean) before falling to Southern Arizona (Southwest). With three squads ending the day at 2-1 overall, Southern Arizona was eliminated from contention due taking only eight individual courts compared to 10 by Eastern Massachusetts and Puerto Rico. That allowed the New England representative to advance via their head-to-head triumph over the Caribbean team.

Southeast Michigan grabbed the top spot in Flight 2 with relative ease, finishing as the lone unbeaten. They took down San Diego (Southern California) in the crucial match of the flight, earning three individual courts in the tense dual. Dallas was far and away the class of Flight 4, ending as the only squad with a winning percentage in individual matchups contested (12 wins, three losses).

Washington, D.C. battled through Flight 5, the lone flight to contain five teams. They were the only undefeated squad by the conclusion of play Saturday thanks to winning 15 of the 20 courts they contested over the two days of action. Philadelphia was not as dominant in Flight 7 but still clawed their way to the top spot. They actually had a worse individual matchup record than San Diego (Southern California), but took the dual when the two teams met.

Broward won Flight 6 convincingly, just as New Orleans did in Flight 8. The semifinal opponents each posted 12 individual court victories to just three setbacks, while New Orleans gave away one less set and six fewer games.

Sunday’s semifinals will hit the court at 7:00 am. After suitable rest, the championship matches along with the third place playoff duals will take place. A full listing of the women’s results can be viewed here, and the men’s here.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
