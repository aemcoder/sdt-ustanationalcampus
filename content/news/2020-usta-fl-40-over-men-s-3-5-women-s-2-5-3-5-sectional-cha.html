2020 USTA FL 40 & Over 2.5/3.5 Sectional Championships

Three More Titles to be Won

November 4, 2020

style
article-hero

Three more Sunshine State team titles are at stake this weekend when the 2020 USTA FL 40 & Over Men’s 3.5 & Women’s 2.5/3.5 Sectional Championships takes to the courts starting Friday, Nov. 6, and wrapping up Monday, Nov. 9.

All squads will be competing in dual matches consisting of three courts – one singles match and two doubles matches. In order to earn the overall victory, at least two of those courts must be won.

The men’s 3.5 division kicks off the event Friday morning with its opening rounds of round-robin play. A total of 12 teams have been equally divided into three flights; the top finisher in each flight along with the best second-place squad will move on to the divisional semifinals.

On the women’s side, both competitions are slated for a Saturday morning start. The 2.5 division consists of just six teams – Alachua, Brevard, Escambia, Hillsborough, Marion, and Orange/Seminole. With no formal championship contest, it will be the crew owning the best record after five rounds of duals taking home the crown. Meanwhile, the 3.5 competition will by far be the busiest for the weekend, as 19 squads have entered the event. Flight play will take place over the course of Saturday and Sunday with the knockout stages scheduled for Monday.

At last year’s Championships, the Broward (Weston Tennis Center) 3.5 men bested Orange/Seminole (Fort Gatlin Recreation Center – Orlando) while the Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville) women claimed the 3.5 title over Broward (Weston Tennis Center).
