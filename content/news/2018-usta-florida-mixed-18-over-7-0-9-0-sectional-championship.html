2018 USTA FL Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships

Sunshine State Teams Back in Action

October 4, 2018

style
article-hero

Hitting the courts for the first time this fall, USTA Florida adult league play makes its return to the USTA National Campus with the Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships. Play is scheduled to get underway at 8 a.m. Saturday morning with the championship day slated for Monday.

A total of 29 squads – 17 in the 7.0 division and 12 in the 9.0 division – will be competing on the weekend. Each dual match will feature three courts of mixed doubles; in order to claim victory in a dual, two of the three courts must be captured. All matches will use ad scoring and be the best two-of-three sets with a match tiebreak in lieu of a full third.

Saturday and Sunday will see teams divided into flights with the goal of finishing in the top spot to secure a place in the semifinal round. There are a total of four flights on the 7.0 side, meaning just the four flight winners advance to Monday’s semifinals. As the 9.0 competition consists of only three flights, there will be a wild card team joining the three flight champions in the knockout stages.

The first ball of the 9.0 final will be struck on Monday at 9 a.m. while the 7.0 championship dual is on the schedule for 11:30 a.m.

Taking home the 7.0 team title in 2017 was the Duval squad representing the Charles J. Williams YMCA of Jacksonville after they went undefeated in round robin action and defeated Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) of Weston by a 2-1 final score. The 9.0 crown was claimed by Pinellas (Clearwater) thanks to a 2-1 triumph over SMD’s Crandon Park Tennis Center team from Key Biscayne in the championship round.
