The USTA Foundation, the national charitable arm of the USTA, and the Orlando Police Department today joined forces to build positive police-youth relationships through the sport of tennis.

This is the continuation of the USTA Foundation’s successful “Serve & Connect” program that was launched in communities around the country earlier this year.

The event was part of the Orlando Teen Police & Fire Academy summer program, which aims to foster relationships between Orlando first responders and local youth in the community, and consisted of a free tennis clinic for the students and officers.

The kids were also surprised with the announcement that two participants will be awarded a week-long scholarship for the USTA National Campus Summer Camp.
