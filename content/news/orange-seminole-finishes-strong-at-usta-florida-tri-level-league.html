Orange/Seminole Finishes Strong

February 4, 2019

The two remaining sectional crowns were collected on Monday to wrap up the USTA Florida Tri-Level League Playoffs, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Orange/Seminole was in position to earn a pair of titles for the day, as the squad from Sanlando Park came through in the women’s 4.5 division while the home team representing the National Campus fell just short in the women’s 4.0 final versus Marion (Fort King Tennis Center).

Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) capped off the weekend with three titles after sweeping the men’s and women’s 3.5 titles; Broward (City of Oakland Park), which reeled in the men’s 4.0 and men’s 4.5 crowns; and Sarasota (Esplanade Gold and Country Club), who prevailed in the women’s 5.0 competition.

Teams winning their respective divisional playoff have earned the right to represent the Florida Section at the 2019 Tri-Level Championships, held at the Indian Well Tennis Garden over the final weekend of the 2019 BNP Paribas Open (March 15-17).

Wild card Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) stunned Brevard (DeSoto Recreation Complex) in one women’s 4.0 semifinal dual. Brevard came into the day boasting a 3-0 record behind a sterling nine-for-nine mark in individual contests with only three sets lost. However, Marion was able to tough out a 7-5, 7-6 result at line two and breeze through the third spot 6-3, 6-1 to advance to the divisional final. Brevard took line one 6-1, 7-5. Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus) continued their march toward capturing the sectional title with a 3-0 sweep over Duval (Palm Coast Tennis Center). The top spot went the way of Orange/Seminole quickly (6-1, 6-3), while court two finished in their favor with a 6-4, 6-2 score line. The sweep was finished off by the Orange/Seminole duo winning 6-3, 7-6 at line three.

The most intense match of the day took place in the women’s 4.0 final. Marion eased their way through the contest on court three (6-2, 6-3), but the other two lines were much tighter. Orange/Seminole hung on to capture the second spot in a match tiebreak (6-4, 2-6, 1-0), but it was Marion who delivered on the majority of the crucial points at the top spot, securing the trophy with a 7-5, 6-4 victory.

A commanding performance by Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park) ended the hopes of wild card Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) in the women’s 4.5 championship. The undefeated favorites gave away just 15 games across the three courts, with no set ending closer than 6-4. The top spot wrapped up in quick fashion via a 6-1, 6-2 final result. Line two was the closest of the contests, with Orange/Seminole coming out on top 6-4, 6-3; court three was closed out by a 6-4, 6-1 margin.
