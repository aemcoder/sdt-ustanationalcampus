2019 USTA FL Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships

Final Sunshine State League Events

December 5, 2019

style
article-hero

The final USTA Florida League events of the year are set to take place at the USTA National Campus over the next two weekends. First up is the Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships, which will get underway Saturday, Dec. 7 and wrap up on Monday, Dec. 9.

Saturday and Sunday will see teams competing in dual matches as part of round robin flight play. A dual match will consist of three courts of doubles, with a team needing to take at least two of those in order to claim the overall victory.

The 6.5 men’s competition features nine squads divided into two flights – four in Flight 1 and five in Flight 2. Only the top finisher in each flight will move on to the divisional championship match scheduled for Monday morning. On the 6.5 women’s side, 16 crews have been divided evenly into four flights; the quartet of flight winners will secure a spot in Sunday afternoon’s semifinal round with the pair of victors earning the right to compete for the title.

A total of 11 teams will start the weekend in contention for the 8.5 men’s crown. The three flights will each see their top finisher lock up a spot in the semifinals, while the best second-place squad across all flights will earn the wild card berth. A similar setup has been laid out for the 8.5 women, which consists of 13 clubs in three flights. All 8.5 semifinals will get underway at 8:30 a.m. on Monday with the finals to follow later that morning.

Last year’s event saw four different regions represented among the champions. In the 6.5 division, the men from Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) and women of Hillsborough (FishHawk Ranch – Lithia) took home the titles. For the 8.5 competitions, top honors went to the Sarasota (Payne Park – City of Sarasota) men and Escambia (Independent) women.
