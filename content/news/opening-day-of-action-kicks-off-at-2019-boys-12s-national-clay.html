Opening Day of Action Kicks Off

July 14, 2019

Sunday marked the opening day of play at the 2019 USTA Boys’ 12s National Clay Court Championships, presented by adidas, as the first round of the singles and doubles draws were contested.  

Only two of the 32 seeded competitors were sent to the consolation bracket on day one, both from the bottom half of the main draw. No. 17 Arjun Prabhakar (Fairfax, Va.) was defeated by Shashwat Srivatsa (Rolling Meadows, Ill.) 6-4, 6-2 while No. 17 Dylan Long (Carmel, Ind.) suffered a 6-1, 6-3 loss at the hands of Jacobus Smit (Fort Mill, S.C.).

The tournament’s top four seeds eased their way into the round of 64, surrendering a combined six games. No. 1 Cooper Woestendick (Olathe, Kan.) swept aside Ethan Leaird (Ocala, Fla.) 6-0, 6-0, the same score line No. 3 Abhishek Thorat (Lithia, Fla.) posted over Diego Woollis (Hato Rey, Puerto Rico). Second seed Maxwell Exsted (Savage, Minn.) also recorded a 6-0 set in his 6-0, 6-2 victory versus Simon Caldwell (Traverse City, Mich.); No. 4 Abhinav Chunduru (Plano, Texas) secured a 6-1, 6-3 result over Jack Di Menna (Charlotte, N.C.).

In total, eight singles matches ended in lopsided 6-0, 6-0 scores. Conversely, 11 contests were extended to a third set, two of which included seeds. No. 17 Hovhannes Khuloyan (Burbank, Calif.) shook off Anirudh Dhanwada (Tenafly, N.J.) 7-6, 6-7, 6-1 and No. 17 Ronit Karki (East Brunswick, N.J.) sprinted through the final set to get by Mathis Valenta (Phoenix) 6-2, 2-6, 6-0.

Exsted and Woestendick, the No. 1 seeds in the doubles draw, teamed up to continue their dominating level of play displayed in singles, dispatching Navneet Raghuram (Fenton, Mo.) and John Varley (St. Louis, Mo.) 6-1, 6-0. Abhinav Chunduru also earned a doubles victory on the day, as he and his brother Prathinav advanced to the round of 32 with a 6-4, 6-0 win over Evan Rigsby (Roswell, Ga.) and James Weber (Marietta, Ga.). The Chunduru brothers are seeded second.

Two upsets of duos seeded ninth unsettled the top half of the doubles draw slightly. Thorat and partner Benjamin Willwerth (Jupiter, Fla.) were downed by Maximus and Tristan Dussault (Leesburg, Va.) 6-3, 6-3 while A Filer (South Bend, Ind.) and Sasha Kremliovsky (Poway, Calif.) came up short against Ethan Barrett (San Marino, Calif.) and Winston Lee (Glendale, Calif.).

Match play continues Monday at 8 a.m. with main draw and consolation singles action.
