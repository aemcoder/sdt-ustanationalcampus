National Crowns Earned by Quartet of Squads

October 29, 2017

The 2017 USTA League National Adult 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Championships, held at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., concluded on Sunday, as four more teams earned the right to call themselves national champions.

Northern California won the women’s 6.0 title behind a pair of 2-1 victories. In the semifinals, they took down the Pacific Northwest representative before securing the championship with a defeat of Florida. The squad from Antelope, Calif., which plays out of the Antelope Community Tennis Association, went undefeated in flight play to qualify for the championship semifinals. They are captained by Michelle Weeks and features a roster of Lori Bonitata, Maria Vacaru, Carmel Lindner, Anne Allen, Kat Beilby, Teddi Maher, Cynthia Fuog, Rosalie Calabrese, Mercy Ford, Cathy Pellegrini, Patricia Malo, Patricia Otis, Lynne Powers, Cindy Taylor, and Tamara Jerd.

Northern was a dominant force on Sunday in the men’s 6.0 division. The group from the Twin Cities swept Texas 3-0 in the semifinal round before producing the same result in the final against Southern California. Representing the St Paul Academy & Summit School in Saint Paul, Minn., captain Eric Narvaez’s and co-captain George Kimball’s team of Tu Van, Gary Bilbrey, Thanh Pham, Peter Haan, William Boyes, and Eric Goodwin were the wild card entrant into the championship semifinals. They finished second to Southern California in Flight 1, but were able to exact revenge when it mattered most.

The women’s 8.0 crown went to the Sunshine State squad from Jacksonville, Fla. Playing out of the Southside Tennis Complex, the Florida Section champion ended flight play undefeated, the only team in the division to accomplish that feat. After coming up with a 3-0 victory over Eastern in their semifinal dual, they held off a challenge from Hawaii to win 2-1. The team, led by Mary Armstrong, is made up of Kelly Groce, Pam Irene Tyler, Teresa Borchelt, Susan Walters, Nina Appleby, Sharon Anderson, Carol Whitmire, Holly Middleton, Diane Smowton, and Robin Albaneze.

In the men’s 8.0 division, Mid-Atlantic bested the rest to claim the national title. Making their home at the Virginia Beach Tennis and Country Club in Virginia Beach, Va., they breezed through flight play only to face an immediate challenge from Southern California in the semifinals. Getting through that by a slim 2-1 margin, they handled Middle States comfortably in the championship match, posting a 3-0 win. Richard Stevens is the team’s captain, which also features Phil Meadows, Roy Loehr, David Cullen, William Haycox, Stover Withers, Mike Sullivan, Paul Barrera, Michael Heisler, David Sloane, Robert Thomson, and Ira Steingold.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
