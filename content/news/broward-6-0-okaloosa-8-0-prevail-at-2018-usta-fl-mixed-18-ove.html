Broward 6.0, Okaloosa 8.0 Prevail

October 15, 2018

style
article-hero

Two more Sunshine State squads took home sectional titles on Monday to wrap up play at the 2018 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Joining the 10.0 Hillsborough group as champions were the 6.0 Broward and 8.0 Okaloosa teams.

In the lone 6.0 match on the day, Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) bested Alachua (Westside Park Gainesville Rec Department) to take home the divisional crown. Maria Suarez and Juan Rios got Broward on the board with a quick 6-1, 6-2 effort at the second spot. Alachua countered at line three, as Marissa Valentine-King and Josh Spink collected a 6-4, 6-0 win on court three. Missed opportunities might haunt Alachua after their top tandem also held a set advantage, but Carlos Alesson and Maria Escarra rallied 1-6, 6-4, 1-0 to clinch for Broward.

Despite the fact that Kenny Oliver and Kristin Knous won in straight sets on court two for SMD (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center), it was Sarasota (Payne Park) who came out on top via a 2-1 final score line to reach the 8.0 final. Sarasota had the opportunity to win in straight sets at lines one and three as well, but dropped the second frames 6-4 in both contests. However, duos Nick Hardy and Jill Lyons at the top spot along with Eric Abbott and Ann Lammers on court three came through with identical 6-2, 4-6, 1-0 victories.

In the other 8.0 semifinal, Okaloosa (Bluewater Bay Tennis Center) also won two courts to one over Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park). Matt Kirkham and McKenna Mills raced through the matchup at line two, winning 6-1, 6-0. Lubomir Kaderjak and Akiko Ichimasa looked to be in position to take line three, earning the first set and locked in a back-and-forth second frame. Despite eventually being extended to a match tiebreak, they survived 6-4, 5-7, 1-0. Juan Delgado and Adania Betancourt were victorious on court one 6-4, 0-6, 1-0 for Orange/Seminole.

Fittingly, the two teams getting through flight play undefeated met up in a battle for the 8.0 trophy. Kaderjak and Ichimasa proved too tough once again, this time cruising 6-2, 6-2 on court two. The new Sarasota tandem of Calvin Hac and Margaret Wieczerzak won by the same score in the third spot, putting all the pressure on the duos competing at line one. Both pairs – Kirkham and Mills of Okaloosa plus Hardy and Lyons of Sarasota – had won in the semifinal round. Hardy and Lyons went up a set after capturing the opening frame 6-4, but Kirkham and Mills competed well to earn the second by the same margin. In the all-important match tiebreak, Kirkham and Mills delivered for their squad.
