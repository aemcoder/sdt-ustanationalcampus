2019 BNP Paribas World Team Cup Americas Qualifications

Regional Qualifying Event Returns

January 28, 2019

style
article-hero

For the second straight year, the USTA National Campus is set to serve as the host site for the BNP Paribas World Team Cup Americas Qualifications event. One of four regional qualifying competitions for the 2019 BNP Paribas World Team Cup, the Americas Qualifications features five men’s teams (Chile, Colombia, Guatemala, Mexico and United States) along with two women’s squads (Colombia and Mexico).

All head-to-head ties will be composed of two singles and one doubles contest. A team will need to win at least two of those courts to gain victory in a tie. For the men’s side, nations will battle for position in round robin play before the competition moves to the knockout stages. Meanwhile, Colombia and Mexico are slated to square off in three separate women's ties.

Other regional qualifiers include the African Qualifications at the Nairobi Club in Nairobi, Kenya (February 14-17), the Asian Qualifications hosted by the National Tennis Centre in Kuala Lumpur (March 1-4), and the European Qualifications at the Vilamoura Tennis Academy in Vilamoura, Portugal (April 2-6).

Teams managing to navigate through a regional qualification event will head to the Larry and Mary Greenspon Israel Tennis Centre at Ramat Hasharon for the 2019 BNP Paribas World Team Cup on May 13-18.

In the 2018 installment of the Americas Qualifications, the men representing the United States and the women from Argentina secured spots in the BNP Paribas World Team Cup. The Americans bested Colombia by a 2-1 margin in the championship round while Argentina defeated Colombia in a best-of-three series.
