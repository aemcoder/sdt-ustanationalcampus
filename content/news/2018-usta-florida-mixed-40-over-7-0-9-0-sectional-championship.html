2018 USTA FL Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships

Two More Sectional Titles on the Line

June 21, 2018

style
article-hero

Continuing a busy summer of USTA Florida League action at the USTA National Campus, the Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships are set to take place this weekend with two sectional titles up for grabs. First matches will take to the court at 8:00 a.m. Saturday morning.

The 7.0 division consists of four total flights, with Flights 1-3 featuring five teams and Flight 4 just four teams. The winner of each flight will move into the semifinal stage slated for 8:30 a.m. Monday. In the 9.0 division, the eight total squads have been divided into two flights, with the championship dual to be played at 3:00 p.m. Sunday.

Each dual match will be made up of three doubles contests; a team must win two of the three courts to claim victory in a dual.

A full listing of the 7.0 divisional flights and schedule can be found here, while more details regarding the 9.0 competition can be found here.

In last year’s championship matches, Leon (Sykes) held off Polk (Winter Haven) by the final of 2-1 to claim the 7.0 crown while Pinellas (St. Pete TC) swept Escambia (Pensacola Athletic Club) 3-0 for the 9.0 title.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
