As the 2019 College MatchDay season comes to a close at the USTA National Campus, here's a look back at the match-deciding moments. Twelve teams walked away from the collegiate courts with memories they'll hold with their teammates for a lifetime, but only six can now call themselves College MatchDay winners. 

See more college tennis action from the Home of American Tennis with #NationalCampus on USTA social media. 

College MatchDay Winners

Florida overcomes in-state rival, Florida State.

Tennessee beats Duke.

UCF women take down top ranked Florida.

Air Force women beat academy rival, Army.

Air Force men defeat Army.

UCF men take down in-state rival, Miami.
