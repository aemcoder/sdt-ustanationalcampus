Day One Action Complete

The opening day of the 2019 USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Championships concluded Saturday evening, with each team contesting two dual matches. Each dual consists of three courts of doubles, with a team needing a win on at least two of those courts to take the dual.

Collier (The Players Club and Spa – Naples) has control of the women’s 6.0 division after claiming 2-1 victories over both Marion (Golden Ocala and Equestrian Club) and Duval (Southside Tennis Complex – Jacksonville). Marion sits in second after besting Duval as well, also by a two courts to one margin. The double round-robin flight will continue Sunday, where another Collier victory over Marion will clinch the title.

In women’s 7.0 action, Flight 1 will come down to Duval (Boone Park – Jacksonville) and IRMSL (North River Shores Tennis Club – Stuart) after the two squads prevailed twice on Saturday; both won four of six courts while giving away five sets. Marion (Citrus Hills Tennis Club – Hernando) surged atop the Flight 2 standings by going undefeated on the day, while Pinellas (East Lake Woodlands Country Club – Oldsmar) and Sarasota (Lakewood Ranch Athletic) are 1-1 overall. The divisional final is set to be played Sunday afternoon.

The lone men’s 7.0 flight saw three teams post 2-0 records on day one while the other three slipped to 0-2 heading into Sunday. Marion (Glenview Champions Tennis Club – The Villages) was the most dominant, taking five of six courts behind just two sets and 29 games lost. Coming in behind them was Collier (Club at Longshore Lake – Naples), also with five court victories; Hillsborough (Temple Terrace Recreation) rounded out the undefeated efforts thanks to four individual wins. Each team will play two more duals on Sunday and an additional two on Monday.

Comprehensive performances by Pinellas (City of Clearwater) in Flight 1 and Collier (Pelican Bay Community Park – Naples) in Flight 2 have put them one team win away from securing a spot in the women’s 8.0 final. Pinellas cruised through the day, collecting five court victories on the strength of two sets and 32 games given away. Collier was challenged, but still ended with five individual wins despite losing four sets. Divisional action continues at 8 a.m. with the final round of flight matches.

For the men’s 8.0 division, three teams are in prime position to advance to the semifinals after solid efforts on the day. Collier (Lighthouse Bay – Bonita Springs) just needs a dual win over Duval (Magnolia Point Golf & Country Club – Green Cove Springs) on Sunday to get through the three-team Flight 1. Meanwhile, both Pinellas (City of Clearwater) in Flight 2 and Broward (Parkland Tennis Center) from Flight 3 are looking to lock up a spot in Monday’s semifinals. A total of five teams capped off day one at 1-1, meaning they will all be fighting for the one wild card semifinal slot.
