2021 Racquet War Tournament Series Orlando

Weekend of Doubles Action in Store

March 25, 2021

style
article-hero

The 2021 Racquet War Tournament Series stops down in Orlando this weekend for three days of women’s doubles and mixed doubles action. Play is set to get underway the morning of Friday, March 26, and run through the afternoon of Sunday, March 28.

A total of 52 women’s teams and 48 mixed doubles pairings have entered the event. For the women’s side, the A Draw consists of 32 duos with a combined NTRP rating of 7.0 to 9.0 while the B Draw features 20 tandems rated between 5.0 and 6.5. In mixed doubles, 32 teams with a rating from 7.5 to 9.5 are set to compete in the A Draw to go along with another 16 falling between 6.0 and 7.0 making up the B Draw.

The Tournament Series uses the Waterfall format, with every team playing two no-ad, eight-game pro sets on Friday in their respective Draw before being placed in a division based on the result of those two contests. All matches played on Saturday and Sunday will use regular scoring and be the best two-of-three sets with a match tiebreak in lieu of a full third set.

Racquet War was started in 2009 by Oakley Foy and Robin Barringer. Foy developed the Waterfall Bracket format with the players in mind, allowing everyone to play with the partner of their choice regardless of rating.
