Tomorrow’s stars will be playing today at the USTA National Campus.

For 2017, in addition to the myriad of play opportunities available at the National Campus, the facility will also offer the Orlando, Fla., community the chance to watch tennis’ rising stars compete on the USTA Pro Circuit. 

The USTA Pro Circuit holds approximately 90 tournaments throughout the U.S. each year. Established in 1979 as a way to provide American players with the opportunity to gain professional rankings points while remaining close to home, it has long been considered the pathway to the US Open and tour-level competition for aspiring players as well as a frequent battleground for established professionals. 

John Isner, Maria Sharapova, Andy Murray, Caroline Woznaicki, Kei Nishikori, Victoria Azarenka and Sam Querrey are among today’s top stars who began their careers on the USTA Pro Circuit, and more than 1,000 men and women from more than 70 countries competed this past season. 

And now, thanks to the National Campus, you’ll have a chance to watch these up-and-coming players up, close and personal. Beginning in January and ending in March, the National Campus will host four tournaments, see schedule below: 

Women’s $25,000 Event: January 17-22, 2017

Men’s $15,000 Futures: February 14-19, 2017 

Men’s $15,000 Futures: February 28 - March 5, 2017

Women’s $15,000: March 7-12, 2017

Be sure to come out and watch the future stars compete!
