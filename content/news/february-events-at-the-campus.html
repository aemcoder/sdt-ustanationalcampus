USTA NATIONAL CAMPUS

Brian Ormiston | February 15, 2017

style
article-hero

From watching tournaments of all levels to enjoying programming and playing with friends, the USTA National Campus is the place to be in February.

All events are free to attend, and parking at the USTA National Campus is free as well.

CLICK HERE for live streaming.

Programming

-Enroll in a class to improve your game.

-USTA teaching pros are available throughout the week.

Book a Court

-Reserve your court today.

-Players can book a court up to 48 hours in advance at the campus.

USTA Pro Circuit Men’s Futures

Feb. 10-19

-Learn More.

-Watch some of the best players in the world compete for a Pro Circuit title on the Har-Tru clay courts.

-Doubles final will be Feb. 17; singles final is slated for Feb. 19.

-Action typically begins at 10 a.m. or 11 a.m. each day.

USTA Pro Circuit Men’s Futures

-Feb. 23-March 5

-Learn More.

-The second Men’s Futures event will invade the National Campus and the clay courts.

-Doubles final is scheduled for March 4; singles final is March 5.

-Play will start at approximately 10 a.m. each day.

USTA National Level 2

Feb. 18-20

-Featuring 128 of the most talented American junior players from across the nation.

-Takes place on the clay courts and will start at 9 a.m. each day.

GLTA World Tour Championship

Feb. 22-24

-The top eight players in each of the Gay & Lesbian Tennis Alliance’s four divisions will compete for Champions Race points in this annual tournament.

-Play begins at 10 a.m. each day on the Team USA courts.

College MatchDay

Feb. 17

-Learn More.

-It will be a 2016 NCAA Finals rematch between Stanford and Oklahoma State.

-The two women’s programs are tied for No. 5 in the USTA College Tennis Top 25 rankings.

-Festivities start at 4 p.m.; first serve is at 6 p.m. at the collegiate complex.

College Events

Feb. 17

-UCF vs. Florida Gulf Coast (women) at 1 p.m. at the collegiate complex.

-UCF vs. Tennessee Tech (men) at 1 p.m. at the Team USA courts.

Feb. 19

-UCF vs. Florida Atlantic (men) at 12 p.m. at the collegiate complex.

Feb. 24

-Florida Atlantic vs. Liberty (women) at 11 a.m. at the collegiate complex.

-UCF vs. Florida Atlantic (women) at 5 p.m. at the collegiate complex.

Feb. 25

-UCF vs. Liberty (women) at 5 p.m. at the collegiate complex.

High School Events

Feb. 18

-Lake Nona High School match at 11:30 a.m. at the collegiate complex.

Feb. 21

-Lake Nona High School match at 3:30 p.m. at the collegiate complex.
