2021 Spring Break Tennis

The Campus hosts an abbreviated schedule due to COVID-19.

Brian Ormiston | February 25, 2021

style
article-hero

The fifth season of Spring Break Tennis at the USTA National Campus features a smaller schedule than usual due to the pandemic. Typically, there are approximately 300 college matches that take place each spring but that number will be around 15-20 in 2021. The facility remains excited to host teams in March, and again in the future.

For teams looking to play during spring break in an upcoming season, email Paul Bellingham - paul@springbreaksports.com.
