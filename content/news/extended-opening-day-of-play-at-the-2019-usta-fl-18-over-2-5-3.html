Extended Opening Day

August 17, 2019

A mid-afternoon rain delay extended Saturday’s schedule late into the night at the 2019 USTA Florida 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships. Teams battled for position throughout the day within the divisional flights, as each squad competed in two duals.

In women’s 2.5 action, Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) holds the inside track to the championship match in Flight 1 while Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) battled their way to the top spot in Flight 2. Alachua prevailed on five of the six courts they contested to turn in a 2-0 overall record; they only need to defeat SMD (Doral Park Country Club) Sunday to move on to Monday’s final. Unbeaten Broward still has to take on both Escambia (Independent) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala), two of the three squads that went 1-1 on Saturday.

Very little has been decided in the men’s 3.5 competition, where only Broward (City of Oakland Park) has a clear path to the divisional semifinals thanks to a 2-0 effort in Flight 2. All four squads in Flight 1 split their duals on day one with five court victories to go along with five losses. Flight 3 will go to the winner of the clash between SMD (Doral Park Country Club) and Duval (Southside Tennis Complex – Jacksonville) after both came through in seven of the 10 individual matchups they took part in. Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) and Brevard (Rockledge Park) dominated Flight 4 to set up a Sunday morning showdown; Alachua did not lose a single court on day one while Brevard went 8-for-10.

For the women’s 3.5 division, undefeated SMD (Key Biscayne Tennis Association) moved to the top of Flight 1 despite having the same number of individual victories and sets lost as second-place Escambia (Independent); the two will go head-to-head Sunday with a spot in the semifinals at stake. Flight 2 will seemingly be taken by either Hillsborough (Waterchase – Tampa) or Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center – Plantation), as they are the only two left unscathed in the flight. Meanwhile, Bay (Panama City Racquet Club) and Collier (Treviso Bay – Naples) have given themselves some breathing room in Flight 3 and 4, respectively. However, the two squads will still need to reel in a pair of team wins on Sunday to ensure a place in the semifinals.

A trio of squads took a big step on Saturday toward advancing to the men’s 4.5 semifinals. Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston), Brevard (Independent) and Hillsborough (Davis Island – Tampa) are just one dual win away from locking up their spot in the divisional knockout stages. Broward and Brevard collected eight court victories in Flight 1 and 2, respectively, while Hillsborough managed to claim a total of seven in Flight 3. Overall, six teams will bring a 1-1 record into Sunday, keeping them in the hunt to win their flight or earn the wild card spot into Monday’s semifinal round.

Broward (Lauderdale Tennis Club – Fort Lauderdale) and Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) are also two of the three flight leaders after one day of play on the 4.5 women’s side. Broward sits atop Flight 1 while Hillsborough has the upper hand in Flight 3; both reeled in seven individual matchups over the course of Saturday. Escambia is in firm control of Flight 2 after they won seven of the 10 courts they contested as well. Just as with the men, six additional teams turned in 1-1 records to stay alive.
