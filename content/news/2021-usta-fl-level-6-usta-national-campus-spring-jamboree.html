2021 USTA FL Level 6 USTA National Campus Spring Jamboree

Over 160 Junior Players in Action

March 26, 2021

style
article-hero

More than 160 junior players will be in action this weekend at the two-day USTA Florida Level 6 USTA National Campus Spring Jamboree. The 12s and 14s divisions will be held on clay courts while the 16s and 18s will compete on hard courts.

Play begins Saturday, March 27, at 8 a.m. with the opening round of singles; competitors are guaranteed a minimum of three singles matches, with the first two rounds taking place on Saturday and final rounds slated for Sunday, March 28. All boys’ and girls’ doubles contests will be held Saturday following the completion of singles while mixed doubles will take to the courts Sunday afternoon.

The event features a total of eight boys’ singles draws and six girls’ singles draws. Listed below are the two seeded players in each of those draws.

Boys’ 12s Blue: Evan Rodenberg (Tallahassee) & Jake Augustyn (Palm Beach Gardens)

Boys’ 12s Red: Madison Huffman (Moultrie, Ga.) & David Bender (Indian Harbour Beach)

Boys’ 12s Green: Eston Akins (Orlando) & Adrian Lozano (Ocala)

Boys’ 14s Blue: Mark Mrcela (Wesley Chapel) & Colin Hui (Syosset, N.Y.)

Boys’ 14s Red: Farley Nunnelley (Mountain Brook, Ala.) & Adam Stochel Polubiec (Palm Coast)

Boys’ 16s Blue: Taylor Huffman (Moultrie, Ga.) & Caleb Domkowski (Indian Harbour Beach)

Boys’ 16s Red: Leo Kwetkus (Ormond Beach) & Anthony Gomez (Orlando)

Boys’ 18s: Kahaka’ioikamalie Companion (Saint Cloud) & James Kober (Davenport)

Girls’ 12s: Natalie Augustyn (Palm Beach Gardens) & Aurionna Wisniewski (Mt. Pleasant, S.C.)

Girls’ 14s Red: Campbell Bundza (Port Orange) & Mia Muniz (Orlando)

Girls’ 14s Blue: Holland Harris (Orlando) & Jordyn Gomez (Orlando)

Girls’ 16s Blue: Ayden Kujawa (Clarksville, Tenn.) & Venus Pereda (Tampa)

Girls’ 16s Red: Martine Sundsten (Orlando) & Deiya Pandya (Jacksonville)

Girls’ 18s: Madison Molock (Ocala) & Noelle DAngelillo (Orlando)
