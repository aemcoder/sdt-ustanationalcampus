2018 USTA FL Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Sectional Championships

Play Gets Underway Saturday Morning

October 11, 2018

style
article-hero

The final USTA Florida league tournament of the fall is set to take place this weekend at the USTA National Campus, as the Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Sectional Championships hits the courts starting Saturday morning. Play is scheduled to run through Monday afternoon.

This weekend’s event will feature a grand total of 30 squads – nine in the 6.0 division, 18 in the 8.0 competition and three vying for the 10.0 title. Each dual match will feature three courts of mixed doubles; in order to claim victory in a dual, two of the three courts must be captured. All matches will use ad scoring and be the best two-of-three sets with a match tiebreak in lieu of a full third.

For the 6.0 division, the top finisher in each of the two flights will qualify for the championship dual, which will be played at 9 a.m. on Monday. In the 8.0 competition, squads have been divided into four flights, with the flight winners moving on to Monday’s knockout stages; the semifinals will get started at 8:30 a.m. with the final to follow at 11:30 a.m.

The 10.0 champion will be crowned Saturday afternoon, as the division is only made up of three teams that will be competing in one double-round robin flight.

Capturing the 2017 sectional title at the 6.0 level was the squad from Alachua (Westside Park Gainesville Rec Dept.) via a 3-0 sweep over Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center). Okaloosa/Walton/Bay (Independent) defeated South Palm Beach (Delray Swim and Tennis Club) by a 2-1 margin for the 2017 8.0 crown. In the 10.0 competition, Brevard (Kiwi Tennis Club) bested South Orange (National Campus) to take home the crown.
