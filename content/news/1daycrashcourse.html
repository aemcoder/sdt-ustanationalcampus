New for 2021!

 

The National Campus is excited to offer 1-Day Crash Courses. Sign up as a group for a fun team-building activity, or individually to take your game to the next level.  The day will be split into two morning and afternoon drill sessions with lunch included.  The day will wrap up with pro supervised match play.

Price: $150/person

Adult Camps are for players ages 18 years and older. 

For Adult Camp questions, please contact us at NationalCampusAdultCamps@usta.com
