Former college teammates traveled to the USTA #NationalCampus to compete in the inaugural College Alumni Classic. Past collegiate players from Gustavus Adolphus College, The Citadel, Wittenberg University, College of Charleston, Middle Tennessee State University, Davidson, and Claremont-Mudd Scripps donned their school colors with pride to hit the court together once again. 

The Gusties from Gustavus Adolphus proved to have kept their skills the sharpest, as they took the inaugural title over Middle Tennessee State 2-0 (8-4, 9-8), with College of Charleston taking third place.
