Broward Doubles Down to Sweep Titles

October 8, 2018

style
article-hero

The 2018 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships, held at the USTA National Campus, conclude Monday afternoon. Teams representing the City of Oakland Park out of Broward owned the day, coming away with both the 7.0 and 9.0 titles.

Finishing first on Monday morning was the Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) and SMD (Coral Gables/Salvadore Park) 7.0 dual, which went to Orange/Seminole by a 2-1 margin. Madelyn Escudero and Jason Shiver took care of court two 6-2, 7-5 to get their team on the board. That was followed up by Richard Perez-Rosa and Michelle Smith coming through with a tough 6-4, 3-6, 1-0 victory at the third spot. William Demetriades and Adania Betancourt retired at the top spot despite leading 6-3, 5-4 to give SMD their lone team point.

Broward (City of Oakland Park) also got through the 7.0 semifinal round with a 2-1 win over Hillsborough (Hunters Green Sports Center). All three courts finished in straight sets; furthermore, each of the three opening sets ended with a 7-5 score line. Kelly Painter and Lissette Loddo survived 7-5, 6-3 on court one for Broward; Michael Stites and Marieli Tirado dropped the fewest number of games en route to a win, claiming court three 7-5, 6-2. At line two, Erik Hajek and Cinthia Cespedes prevented the shutout with a 7-5, 6-4 effort for Hillsborough.

The battle between Broward and Orange/Seminole went down to the wire. Things started off quickly in Broward’s favor thanks to Stites and Tirado cruising 6-1, 6-2 at line three. Julian Gil and Carolina Varela also went up a set on court two, but Orange/Seminole duo Pedro Hernandez and Irene Stephenson turned the tables to prevail 4-6, 6-4, 1-0. In a similar fashion, Broward’s Painter and Loddo shook off a sluggish start at the No. 1 position to beat Escudero and Shiver 2-6, 6-2, 1-0.

In the only 9.0 match of the day, Broward (City of Oakland Park) bested Hillsborough (Harbour Island Athletic Club) thanks to a pair of straight-set results. Augustin Torres and Erin Pasternack turned in the most complete effort, defeating their opponents 6-3, 6-1 on court three. Paired with Sheri Adler and Timothy House’s 6-3, 6-2 victory at the top spot, Broward was crowned the sectional champions. Hillsborough’s Fabiana Taverna-Saballos and Geoff Gale did record a 6-2, 6-4 win at line two for their squad.
