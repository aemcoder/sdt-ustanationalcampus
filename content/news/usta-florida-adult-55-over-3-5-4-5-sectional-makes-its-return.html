Making its return to the National Campus after being postponed last month, the USTA Florida Adult 55 & Over 3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships moved into its final rounds after the completion of Saturday’s matches.

Sunday will feature two championship matches along with the conclusion of round robin action in the women’s 4.5 division, where there was only a single flight to determine the champion.

Due to the shortened schedule on Saturday, the women’s 3.5 division not only finished up all matches in the flight stage, but also contested its semifinals. Sarasota (BNR Encore), Pinellas (Countryside), and Collier (Naples Beach Club) all advanced via winning their respective flights while SMD claimed the wild card spot to advance to the semifinals. Both matchups would end in 2-1 results, with Sarasota and Pinellas moving on to the championship dual.

In the men’s 3.5 competition, Broward (WTC) and Hillsborough (Temple Terrace) went undefeated throughout flight play to earn the right to play for the divisional title. Broward took Flight 1 with a 2-0 overall record and Hillsborough breezed through four challengers in Flight 2, dropping just five sets in those team matches.

The women’s 4.5 title will either be earned by Alachua (Azalea Park) or Hillsborough (Cheval), who will clash on Sunday. Hillsborough will enter the day at 3-0, with their only scheduled dual coming against Alachua. Alachua currently sits at 2-0, but will play twice on Sunday, meaning they can take the crown with a pair of victories on the final day of play.
