Six Americans Reach Quarterfinals

April 16, 2021

style
article-hero

Americans took over the singles draw on Thursday at the ATP Challenger Orlando Open Presented by Nemours, filling six of the eight quarterfinal slots. Additionally, three of the four doubles teams to reach the semifinals are all-American duos.

Christian Harrison and Jack Sock both remained in the hunt to sweep the singles and doubles titles at the event. Harrison prevailed over University of Illinois senior Aleksandar Kovacevic in a clash of American wild cards, coming through with a 6-4, 6-3 result. For Sock, he downed No. 6 seed Prajnesh Gunneswaran (India) 6-3, 6-4 in a contest that lasted 90 minutes despite being only two sets. Harrison and Sock will be across the net from each other Friday morning.

Also making their way to the final eight with wins on Thursday were Michael Mmoh and fifth-seeded Denis Kudla. Mmoh shook off a slow start versus fellow American Christopher Eubanks for a 3-6, 6-3, 6-2 triumph; meanwhile, Kudla survived a marathon battle with qualifier Tim Van Rijthoven (Netherlands) by a slim 5-7, 6-2, 7-5 margin. Mmoh and Kudla will square off in the second match on Court 1 Friday afternoon.

In doubles action, American wild cards Harrison and Dennis Novikov received a walkover from the tandem of Yuki Bhambri (India) and Matthew Ebden (Australia). Their semifinal opponents will be JC Aragone (United States) and Luis David Martinez (Venezuela), who stormed back to down the Dutch duo of Jesper De Jong and Sem Verbeek 3-6, 6-4, 10-8. 

The bottom half of the draw saw Sock and Mitchell Krueger defeat Alexander Ritschard and Alex Rybakov in a contest of American pairings along with Sekou Bangoura and Junior Ore of the United States best the Brazilian team of Joao Menezes and Thiago Seyboth Wild 3-6, 6-3, 10-5.
