Day One

May 27, 2017

style
article-hero

Saturday marked the opening day of the 2017 USTA Florida Doubles Spring Challenge held at the USTA National Campus. The tournament features Boys' and Girls' from the 12s, 14s, 16s and 18s age divisions competing in 32-team draws. Moving into Sunday, the main draw quarterfinals have been set, with all eight top seeds still in contention for the title.

In the Boys' 12s, Leanid Boika and Calvin Wellman dropped just a single game in the second round after receiving a bye. The second-seeded duo of Robert Acosta and Charlie Cowan are also still in the main draw, but the third and fourth seeds were upset on Saturday. The Boys' 14s draw saw all eight seeded teams navigate their way to the quarterfinals. Only two of them lost a set on Saturday, including No. 1 seeds Jack Anthrop and Ben Shelton, who survived 3-6, 6-0, 10-6 in the round of 16.

Joshua Miller and Logan Zapp, seeded first in the Boys' 16s, swept through their first match 6-0, 6-0 to move on to the quarterfinals, joined by the other three seeds in the draw. All four seeded tandems in the Boys' 18s were victorious on Saturday as well, but top seeds Carlos Grande and Antonio Mora were tested in their 7-6(2), 6-3 win.

In the Girls' 12s, the draw nearly held exactly to form, only seeing the No. 5 seeds falter. The top-seeded squad of Natalie Block and Gabriela Smashey did need to pull out a marathon tiebreak in the second set, 17 points to 15, in order to advance. All four seeds in the Girls' 14s made quick work of their matches on Saturday, with no set getting closer than 6-3. DJ Bennett and Sophia Wang, seeded first, won 6-0, 6-1.

Four of the eight seeds were knocked out early in the Girls' 16s draw. However, No. 1 seeds Kensie Cricchio and Emma Shelton along with the second-seeded team of Karly Friedland and Cooper Jackson lost a combined seven games to reach the quarterfinals. In one of the more dramatic draws of the tournament, the Girls' 18s also lost four seeds, including the No. 2 seeds. Six of the eight round of 16 matches were decided by a match tiebreak, but the top-seeded duo of Anna Bright and Sophia Edwards cruised to a 6-1, 6-0 victory in their only match of the day.
