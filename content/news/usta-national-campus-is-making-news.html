The USTA National Campus continues to make news. After officially opening to the public in January, the new Home of American Tennis has been a popular topic of conversation in the tennis world.

Most recently, the 64-acre campus, which features 100 courts of various surfaces and state-of-the-art technology, was featured as the cover story in Tennis Industry Magazine.
