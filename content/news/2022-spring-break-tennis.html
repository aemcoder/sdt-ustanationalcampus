2022 Spring Break Tennis

Colleges swarm the Orlando area for their memorable trips.

February 28, 2022

style
article-hero

Almost 300 teams will be making their way to Orlando as the USTA National Campus once again will be one of the host facilities for Spring Break Tennis in 2022. Schools from all over the country travel to the region for fun, team bonding and of course, tennis. For many, it will be their first time playing outside since the fall season.

For teams looking to play during spring break in an upcoming season, email Paul Bellingham - paul@springbreaksports.com.

And for more information on the program, visit https://www.springbreaktennis.com/. 

Spring Break Tennis is free for all spectators to attend at the USTA National Campus. It runs almost every day throughout the month of March.
