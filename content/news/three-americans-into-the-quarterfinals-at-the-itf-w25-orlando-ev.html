ITF W25 Orlando Event

Three Americans into the Quarters

February 19, 2021

style
article-hero

Singles and doubles matches were part of the schedule Thursday at the ITF W25 Orlando event on the grounds of the USTA National Campus. The singles draw has been whittled down to the quarterfinal round while just four pairings remain in doubles.

Three Americans advanced to the singles quarterfinals on the day, including Robin Anderson, who came out victorious in a matchup with fellow American No.4 seed Usue Arconada 2-6, 7-5, 6-1. Katie Volynets also needed three sets to secure her victory over Mariam Bolkvadze (Georgia) by a 4-6, 6-1, 6-2 margin. Claire Liu did not need to step on the court Thursday, as she received a walkover in her scheduled contest with top seed Amanda Anisimova.

Sixth-seeded Anhelina Kalinina (Ukraine) is the only seeded player remaining in the top half of the draw after she eased her way past Jovana Jovic of Serbia 6-2, 6-3; she will face off with Liu in the quarterfinals. Anderson will be across the net from Russian qualifier Amina Anshba, who downed American Jamie Loeb 6-3, 3-6, 6-3, on Friday.

Great Britain’s Katie Swan rallied past Gabriela Talaba (Romania) 3-6, 6-3, 6-2 to set up her quarterfinal matchup with Volynets. Meanwhile, the only contest on Friday between two seeded competitors will feature No. 3 Renata Zarazua (Mexico) and No. 8 Maria Camila Osorio Serrano (Colombia); Zarazua dismissed American qualifier Katrina Scott 6-4, 7-6(5) while Osorio Serrano cruised past Maria Lourdes Carle (Argentina) 6-1, 6-2.

Doubles was also back in action on Thursday, with only Osorio Serrano still in the hunt for both the singles and doubles titles. She has teamed up with Swiss Conny Perrin, as the duo defeated the Spanish tandem of Irene Burillo Escorihuela and Andrea Lazaro Carcia 6-3, 6-2 to move on to the final four. Their semifinal opponents will be Jovic and Rebeca Sramkova (Slovakia) after they prevailed 7-6(3), 3-6, 10-7 over Ekaterine Gorgodze (Georgia) and Swan.

Talaba and Mexico’s Marcela Zacarias also needed a match tiebreak to secure victory, coming back to down Anshba and Bolkvadze 6-7(3), 6-2, 10-7. American Emina Bektas and Tara Moore (Great Britain) dismissed American sisters Allura and Maribella Zamarripa 7-6(5), 6-0 to move on.

Play continues Friday at 10 a.m. The ITF W25 Orlando event is closed to the public. For more information, including draws and daily schedule of play, click here.
