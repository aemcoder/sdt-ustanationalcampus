Playoff Rounds Underway

October 11, 2017

Wednesday saw the first round of  playoff matches begin for the 2017 ITF Super Seniors World Team Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla.. Playoff action was contested in all nine cups on the day and will continue through Friday.

Highlighting the day’s matches was another outstanding performance from Team USA. The eight American squads that took to the courts on Wednesday finished with a 7-1 overall record, which included remaining in the hunt for the team title in the Britannia Cup (Men’s 65), Gardnar Mulloy Cup (Men’s 80), Kitty Godfree Cup (Women’s 65), Althea Gibson Cup (Women’s 70), Queens Cup (Women’s 75) and Doris Hart Cup (Women’s 80).

The semifinals are set in the Britannia Cup, with three of the top four seeds surviving along with the surprising Austrian team that bested No. 2 seed France in group play. All four quarterfinal encounters were decided via 3-0 sweeps. Top seeded USA will be up against No. 3 seed Australia in one semifinal while fourth-seeded Italy is the next opponent for Austria.

The Nos. 1 and 2 seeds in the Jack Crawford Cup (Men’s 70) were challenged in their quarterfinal bouts but managed to escape, as top-seeded Spain put away Canada, 2-1, and No. 2 Netherlands slipped past Norway by the same margin. The Spaniards are set to clash with No. 4 Switzerland next. The Dutch have drawn Australia after the Aussies upset No. 5 USA.

The championship dual is set for the Bitsy Grant Cup (Men’s 75), where the cups No. 1 seed Australia and No. 6 seed Netherlands will do battle. The Aussie squad had no problem in taking care of eighth-seeded Japan in their semifinal; the Dutch ousted No. 3 seed Great Britain 2-1.

In Gardnar Mulloy Cup action, top seed USA took a big step toward claiming the trophy after posting a 2-1 victory over No. 2 seed Germany. Meanwhile, in the lone Men’s 85 Team Cup contest, Argentina swept past Sweden to claim fifth overall in the division.

The four teams who have found their way into the semifinals of the Kitty Godfree Cup are all seeded. No. 1 seed Australia was tested by Brazil but advanced 2-1, the same margin their next opponent, No. 6 Germany, won by over New Zealand. Second seed USA had little trouble with upset-minded South Africa, triumphing 3-0, while No. 5 Great Britain took out third-seeded France 2-1 to set up a semifinal encounter with the Americans.

Team USA has earned a spot in the final of the Althea Gibson Cup thanks to a tremendous 3-0 defeat of No. 4 South Africa in the semifinals. Awaiting the United States in the championship round is the division's second seed Netherlands, who outlasted Australia, 2-1.

The top seed in both the Queens Cup and Daris Hart Cup, Team USA picked up victories in both cups to move within a single dual victory of earning those titles. The Americans came out the winner of a 2-1 contest against No. 2 Germany in the Queens Cup and swept aside the Kiwis in the Doris Hart Cup.

Play continues Thursday morning at 8:00 am. For the most up-to-date results from the 2017 ITF Super Seniors World Team Championships, click here.

Featuring 123 teams (72 men’s and 51 women’s) representing 31 nations, the 2017 ITF Super-Seniors World Team Championships are being held at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., from Oct. 8-13. The international tournament is composed of nine cups for the first time in its history, as the Men’s 85 Team Cup is in its first year of existence.
