USTA Florida Tri-Level Sectional Championships

Flights Take Shape After Day One

January 25, 2020

style
article-hero

The USTA Florida Tri-Level Sectional Championships got underway Saturday with a full slate of matches to kick off the three-day event. Going into Sunday, three sectional champions will be determined, two on the men’s side and one on the women’s side.

While a total of six sectional crowns are on the line this weekend, the headliners are the men’s and women’s 3.5/4.0/4.5 competitions; the two squads prevailing from that division will book their spot in the 2020 BNP Paribas Open Tri-Level Championships March 20-22.

All squads are mathematically still alive in both the women’s 2.5/3.0/3.5 and women’s 4.0/4.5/5.0 divisions, which will crown their champions Sunday. Orange/Seminole (Isleworth Tennis Club – Windermere) and Duval (San Jose Country Club – Jacksonville) sit atop the lone 2.5/3.0/3.5 women’s flight with 1-0 records, while Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) and Alachua (Westside Park – Gainesville) will look to bounce back on Sunday after suffering a loss. Sarasota (Landings Racquet Club – Sarasota) has their sights set on the 4.0/4.5/5.0 title heading into day two thanks to head-to-head defeats of both Orange/Seminole (Lake Cane Tennis Center – Orlando) and Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park); Orange/Seminole is 1-1 overall and Broward is still winless at 0-2.

Also set to determine its winner on Sunday is the men’s 3.5/4.0/4.5 competition. After the opening day of play, Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) and Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) have control of Flight 1 and 2, respectively. Those two crews need only to prevail in their final round-robin dual Sunday morning to advance to the divisional championship; a loss would leave their fate in the hands of the tiebreaking procedure.

For the men’s 3.0/3.5/4.0 division, Marion (Country Club of Ocala) is a win over second-place Escambia (Independent) away from locking up its place in the final in Flight 1 while Flight 2 will come down to the clash between the unbeaten crews from Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) and Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA – Jacksonville). That divisional title contest will take place on Monday as all teams in Flight 2 still have two duals to play on Sunday.

Day two will be an active one for the women’s 3.0/3.5/4.0 and 3.5/4.0/4.5 competitions, with nothing having been decided in any of those flights after Saturday. In 3.0/3.5/4.0 action, Flight 1 will go to the victor between Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala), all five squads are currently at 1-1 overall in Flight 2, and Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) has seized control in Flight 3 as the only team without a loss. Flight leaders in the 3.5/4.0/4.5 division include Brevard (McKnight Family Sports Complex – Rockledge) from Flight 2 and Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) from Flight 3; for Flight 1, Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) and Alachua (DB Racquet Club – Gainesville) have pulled away with 2-0 starts.
