Sectional Championships - Day One

August 11, 2017

style
article-hero

Friday kicked off action at the USTA Florida Adult 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional Championships, as all divisional flights got underway during the opening day of play.

Duval and Polk sit at 2-0 in the lone men’s 3.0 flight, setting up a Saturday evening clash. The winner of that contest is likely to claim the sectional crown. Duval was solid on day one, with seven of the 10 courts they played going their way en route to dropping eight total sets. Polk posted six court wins against four losses with 10 sets lost.

The winner of the men’s 4.0 match between Duval and Hillsborough will earn the semifinal berth out of Flight 1. The remaining three flights all have one team in command, sitting at 2-0 with one dual left to play. Marion (Flight 2), Broward (Flight 3) and North Miami (Flight 4) all begin Saturday with a leg up on the competition.

With a pair of dual matches in the books for each of the five teams competing for the men’s 5.0+ title, Hillsborough is the lone remaining unbeaten. Having already secured wins over Duval and Broward, two teams that sit at 1-1 going into Saturday, Hillsborough could lock things up by taking down Alachua, which went winless on Friday.

Three of the four women’s 3.0 flights have a clear favorite after day one. Hillsborough (Flight 1), Alachua (Flight 2) and Escambia (Flight 3) are all undefeated and need just one team victory on Saturday to move on to the division semifinals. Meanwhile, Flight 4 appears as if it will come down to the winner between SMD and Leon, both 2-0 so far, but 1-1 Volusia is still in the mix to advance.

All teams in the women’s 4.0 division played just one match on Friday. Flight 1 saw Brevard and Duval put themselves in good position after claiming close team victories. Broward and SPB sit atop Flight 2 after SPB slipped past Pinellas and Broward dominated Lee/Charlotte in the only dual in the division that did not end in a 3-2 result. Sarasota/Manatee and Marion have the inside track in Flight 3 while Hillsborough and Polk lead Flight 4.

Hillsborough also leads the three-team flight in women’s 5.0+ action after defeating both Orange/Seminole/Lake and Broward. All three teams will face off against each other once again on Saturday. The matchup between Hillsborough and Orange/Seminole/Lake will like decide the sectional champion after both defeated Broward. Hillsborough toughed out a 3-2 triumph over Orange/Seminole/Lake in their Friday encounter.
