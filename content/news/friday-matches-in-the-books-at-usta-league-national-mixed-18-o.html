The 2019 USTA League National Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Championships took to the courts of the USTA National Campus on Friday, Nov. 8 for its opening day of competition. All teams competed in two dual matches over the course of the day, which was briefly delayed in the afternoon by rain.

For the 6.0 division, four teams will go into Saturday without a loss, headlined by Missouri Valley (Oklahoma City Tennis Center) going a perfect 6-for-6 in their individual matchups, dropping just one set overall. Coming in right behind them were the crews from Caribbean (Independent – Puerto Rico) and Southern (Burns Park Tennis – North Little Rock, Ark.) with five court victories; Caribbean collected 11 sets and Southern managed to reel in 10.

Meanwhile, Mid-Atlantic (Montgomery County, Md.) came out on top in a pair of hard-fought duals, winning two courts in each to keep pace. In addition to those four, a group of eight teams are sitting a 1-1 to remain in the hunt for a semifinal spot going into day two.

Playing their way to the top of the 8.0 standings were the squads from Texas (Memorial Park Tennis Center – Houston) and Northern California (The Bay Club – Fremont), as both went 2-0 with a total of five court victories to their credit. Four more teams also managed to finish Friday undefeated to put themselves in good position for a semifinal berth – Southern (St. Georges Independent School – Collierville, Tenn.), Middle States (Mt Lebanon Tennis Center – Pittsburgh), New England (Wayside Athletic Club – Marlborough, Mass.), and Caribbean (Independent – Puerto Rico). Should several of those crews stumble, five others that concluded Friday with a record of 1-1 still have hopes of vaulting up into the coveted top four spots.

The 10.0 competition has been whittled down to nine squads left with a chance at playing for a national crown, four of which survived day one unscathed. Eastern (Carefree Racquet & Health Club – North Merrick, N.Y.) turned in the best performance in the division thanks to five court victories on the strength of 11 sets won. Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Washington, D.C.), Middle States (Granite Run Tennis Club – Media, Pa.), and Northern California (Memorial Park – Albany) all battled their way to 2-0 efforts with four individual matchup wins; Mid-Atlantic and Northern California captured 10 sets while Middle States was able to come away with nine. Five more teams split their duals on Friday, meaning they are still in the mix but will need to post an undefeated record on Saturday.
