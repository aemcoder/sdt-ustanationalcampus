The USTA National Campus hosted its first of 100 events from Jan. 6-8 with the USTA Florida League Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships. The facility will get right back to work with an even busier weekend Jan. 13-15 with several college matches, the USTA Florida League Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships and the USTA Pro Circuit Women's $25K.

Congratulations to the following winners who were crowned on Jan. 8, and also started a tradition where the winners of campus tournaments get to sign the tournament desk wall.

For more information on the tournament, full results and photos, visit the USTA Florida Recap page.

6.5 Men’s Champion – Broward

Kevin Sims, Carlo Cantavero, Armando Orozco, Mark Reyes, Ronald Coletto, Todd Wozniewski, Rafael Rigotto, Kevin Alamonte

6.5 Women’s Champion – Escambia

Kimberly Andrews, Anne Elidemir, Elena Sise, Kenzier Shamika McIntyre, Kristyn Miller, Ingrid Hewette, Rachel Dianne Trahanl, Melissa Paravalos
