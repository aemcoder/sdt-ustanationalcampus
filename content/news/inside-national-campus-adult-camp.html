Get an insider's look at the USTA National Campus 3-Day Adult Camp.

Tennis players from all across the country travel to the Home of American Tennis to work on their games and enjoy the Orlando sunshine. Take a look at all the action as they embark on a journey with our USTA National Campus teaching professionals over 3 days to take their games to the next level. 

Follow all the action from the Home of American Tennis with #NationalCampus on USTA social media channels. 

Inside Adult Camp at the USTA National Campus

Begin the weekend by meeting your teaching pros and learning your theme to focus on for the next few days.

Enjoy two action-packed days of instructor-led drills.

USTA National Campus teaching pros are with you every step of the way to offer personalized guidance.

Have a blast playing with tennis players from all across the country.

The courts are overflowing with positive encouragement!

Next, take your skills into match-like scenarios.

Learn more about your game with video analysis through PlaySight Technology.

It's time to implement everything you've learned in tournament-style play.

Celebrate your new skills and get ready to take them back home for your next match.

It's time to wave goodbye to your teaching pros & new friends as the weekend comes to a close.
