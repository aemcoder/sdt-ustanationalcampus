The first day of the 2018 USTA League National Adult 18 & Over 5.0+ Championships wrapped up Friday evening at the USTA National Campus. Each team competed in two matches over the course of the day in a battle for position, as the top four squads (based on TennisLink standings) will advance to the semifinal round.

Heading into Saturday a combined 10 teams – five men’s and five women’s – still hold perfect records.

In the men’s competition, Eastern (Rye Racquet Club – Harrison, N.Y.), Florida (Northdale Golf & Tennis Club – Tampa), Missouri Valley (Kossover Tennis Center – Topeka, Kan.), Northern California (Independent), and Texas (River Crest Country Club – Fort Worth) have moved to the top of the standings. Florida, Missouri Valley and Texas took five court victories each while losing two sets and Eastern recorded the same number of individual matchup wins with four sets surrendered. Northern California came away with four courts after dropping five sets overall.

Women’s teams posting 2-0 records on the day included Mid-Atlantic (Washington, D.C.), Missouri Valley (Oklahoma City Tennis Center – Oklahoma City, Okla.), Pacific Northwest (Sunset Athletic Club – Portland, Ore.), Southern California (Los Caballeros Sports Village – Fountain Valley), and Texas (Greenhill Tennis Center – Addison). Texas was a dominant force, sweeping both matches without dropping a set and behind a meager total of 16 games lost. Missouri Valley and Southern California claimed five of the six courts they contested; Mid-Atlantic and Pacific Northwest earned four court victories.

Complete men’s standings can be viewed here; updated women’s standings can be found here. Play starts back up Saturday morning at 7 a.m.
