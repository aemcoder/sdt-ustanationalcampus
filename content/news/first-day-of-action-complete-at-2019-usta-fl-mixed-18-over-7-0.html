The opening day of match play at the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships, held at the USTA National Campus, wrapped up Saturday evening. Each squad took part in two duals over the course of the day, helping the divisional flights take shape.

For the 7.0 competition, three teams grabbed full control of their respective flights while little has yet to be determined in Flight 4. Alachua (Westside Park – Gainesville) moved atop Flight 1 with a pair of victories on the day, but still needs to get past second-place Collier (Heritage Bay Golf Country Club) on Sunday to safely advance into the semifinals.

Both Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) in Flight 2 and South Palm Beach (Stonebridge Country Club – Boca Raton) in Flight 3 have positioned themselves well after the opening day; however, since those flights are composed of five squads, they still need two more dual wins to qualify for Monday’s semifinal round. Flight 4 features two undefeated teams, with defending champion Broward (City of Oakland Park) and Okaloosa/Walton (Bluewater Bay Tennis Center – Niceville)at 2-0 overall.

In 9.0 action, SMD (Coral Pine Park – Pinecrest) owns the top spot in Flight 1 thanks to defeating IRMSL (Vero Beach Tennis and Fitness Club) and Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) on Saturday. With the trio of squads competing in a double round robin format, SMD only needs to take care of IRMSL again on day two to guarantee a semifinal berth. Pinellas (Total Tennis LLC – Clearwater) along with Sarasota (Lakewood National – Bradenton) also look to be on their way to booking a place in the final four thanks to solid efforts out of Flights 2 and 3, respectively. Despite losing two courts, Pinellas already has a head-to-head victory over second-place Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville), while Sarasota was a perfect 6-of-6 in their individual matchups on day one.
