SMD 4.5+ Men Earn Divisional Title

July 28, 2019

style
article-hero

One division wrapped up on Sunday while the other three will conclude on Monday at the 2019 USTA Florida 40 & Over 3.5/4.5+ Sectional Championships. The men’s 4.5+ sectional title went to the squad representing SMD (Next Level Tennis Ktown – Miami), as they defeated Duval (San Jose Country Club – Jacksonville) by a 4-1 margin.

Three of the men’s 3.5 semifinalists moved through their respective flights with undefeated records. Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) and Polk (Beerman Family Tennis Center – Lakeland) finished the first two days of play at 3-0 with 10 individual victories, as Marion dropped 14 sets in Flight 1 and Polk gave away 12 sets in Flight 2. Broward (Weston Tennis Center) cruised in Flight 3 action, posting a 4-0 record overall after collecting 14 court wins. The semifinal round will see Marion going up against Broward and Polk taking on wild card entrant Orange/Seminole (Fort Gatlin Recreation Center – Orlando), who placed second in Flight 3.

Of the four squads moving on to the women’s 3.5 semifinals, Pinellas (City of Clearwater) had the easiest time securing their spot, dominating the competition in Flight 3 thanks to going 12-for-15 in individual matchups. Duval (Julington Creek Plantation – Jacksonville) did come out of Flight 2 unscathed despite losing more sets and games than second-place Collier (Treviso Bay –Naples); Alachua (DB Racquet Club – Gainesville) won all four of their duals in Flight 4, with their head-to-head defeat of Escambia (Independent) proving the difference. Head-to-head wasn’t enough to propel Orange/Seminole (Lake Cane Tennis Center – Orlando) to the top of Flight 1, as Broward (Weston Tennis Center) claimed that spot with nine court wins compared to the eight earned by Orange/Seminole. Monday’s semifinal duals will feature Broward versus Duval and Pinellas clashing with Alachua.

The men’s 4.5+ championship dual was contested between Duval (San Jose Country Club – Jacksonville) and SMD (Next Level Tennis Ktown – Miami). Duval did stumble in their final match of Flight 1, but still managed to end up with the most court wins to advance to the final. Meanwhile, SMD swept through Flight 2 on the strength of 13 individual matchups taken, a level of play they continued against Duval. Pedro Leon and Marcos Lopez lost just 10 games at singles lines one and two, respectively, to give SMD full control of the final. The SMD pairing of Kenneth Thome and Pedro Pozzobon breezed through the top doubles spot 6-1, 6-1; Javier Bolanos and Jorge Ramirez took line three 6-2, 6-4. Duval’s Kevin Grant and Brian DeAlexandris got their squad on the board with a 7-6, 6-1 effort at the second position.

Captain Herbert Portillo’s full roster includes Lopez, Ramirez, Ramon Hudec, Pozzobon, Patricio Madariaga, Thome, Martin Escobedo, Alexander Romero, G Emerson Fales, Leon, Bolanos, Luis Portillo, Mike Fields, Sergio Campos, Randolph Sievane, and Tomas Rodriguez.

An impressive display from Escambia (Independent) in Flight 1 of the women’s 4.5+ division moved them into the semifinals. The team claimed all but one of the 15 courts they contested while surrendering a mere two sets. Sarasota (Longboat Key Tennis Center) and Pinellas (City of Clearwater) had little issue playing their way to the top of Flights 2 and 3, respectively, with Sarasota going 3-0 behind 11 individual matchups won while Pinellas posted a 4-0 record with 17 court victories. For the divisional semifinals, Escambia has drawn Pinellas and Sarasota will be across the net from wild card entrant Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs), the runner-up in Flight 3.
