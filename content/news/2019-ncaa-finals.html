All of the hard work from the 2018-19 college tennis season paid off for the Texas men on May 19 at the USTA National Campus. The Longhorns claimed the title at the 2019 NCAA Men's Tennis Championships in front of a vocal crowd and a live national TV audience thanks to Tennis Channel.

Relive the Longhorns' path to the title with the USTA's recap video, as well as a photo gallery from the finals vs. Wake Forest.
