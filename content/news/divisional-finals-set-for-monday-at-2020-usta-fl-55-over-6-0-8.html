Divisional Finals Set for Monday

October 11, 2020

style
article-hero

After three days of play at the 2020 USTA Florida 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships, the matchups for the women’s 6.0 and men’s 8.0 divisional finals are set. Both titles will be decided Monday morning at the USTA National Campus, when teams will be looking to join the 8.0 women’s team representing Brevard (Rockledge Park) as champions.

Before the championship dual takes to the courts on the women’s side, one final match from the round-robin portion of the event is to be played at 8 a.m. after heavy afternoon rain on Sunday postponed the match. It will feature Hillsborough (Apollo Beach Racquet Fitness Club) facing off against Collier (The Players Club and Spa – Naples); however, the contest is inconsequential to the Flight 1 standings, as undefeated Hillsborough has also secured their place in the divisional title tilt. 

IRMSL (Magnolia Lakes – Port St. Lucie) is awaiting Hillsborough thanks to their 3-0 effort in Flight 2, where they won eight of nine courts and 16 of 19 sets.

For the men’s competition, the only match on Monday will pit two unbeaten crews battling for sectional supremacy. Duval (Boone Park Tennis Courts – Jacksonville) emerged from Flight 1 at 3-0 on the strength of seven individual matchup victories with 15 sets to their credit; Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) also cruised through their trio of duals in Flight 2, dropping only one court and five sets.

The 8.0 men’s final is slated to begin at 8 a.m. while the 6.0 women’s title dual is scheduled for 11:30 a.m. For more information on the 2020 USTA Florida 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships, click here.
