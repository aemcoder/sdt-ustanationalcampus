It’s all fun in the sun at the USTA National Campus during Junior Summer Camp! Hear from USTA National Campus guest and tennis dad, Peter Klomp, on how his daughter is enjoying her experience.

Testimonial: “As a first time camper with USTA, I must say it is more than I could have expected. Imogen [Peter’s daughter] has learned so much in her first few days and has really enjoyed how the coaches have made tennis so much fun for a beginner.

Having themed tennis days to make her look forward to the next day of camp is such a great idea. As a teacher, I know how hard it can be to keep everyone interested, and Coach Martha is a pro at keeping everyone learning and having fun, even when it feels like 100 degrees out.

Having played tennis my whole life, I am truly thankful that [USTA National Campus] camp is helping to create something my daughter and I can enjoy together for many years to come. [We’re] looking forward to many more weeks of camp this summer!”
