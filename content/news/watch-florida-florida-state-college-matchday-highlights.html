For the fourth consecutive year, Florida and Florida State renewed their rivalry as part of College MatchDay at the USTA National Campus.

The intrastate rivals met in front of a sold-out crowd with the Gators prevailing 4-1. Andy Andrade clinched the match for Florida with a 6-1, 6-4 victory on Court #4.

Relive the excitement of the match with highlights of the action.

Two more College MatchDays are scheduled this season with Notre Dame and Southern California (Women) facing off on Feb. 16, and Ohio State vs. Florida State (Women) on March 1.

Follow the action on USTA social media with #CollegeMatchDay.
