2017 ITF Super-Seniors World Team Championships

Opening Days Eventful, Challenging

October 9, 2017

style
article-hero

The opening days of the 2017 ITF Super-Seniors World Team Championships, hosted at the USTA National Campus, have presented several challenges. From the high-level competition to the extreme heat and heavy rainfall, teams from around the world have been put to the test from the opening serve.

So far the squads have been engaged in group play, battling to position themselves for a berth into the championship draw when the tournament turns to its knockout stages.

Having entered a team into each of the nine cups, Team USA remains undefeated through two days of action. Furthermore, the Americans have yet to surrender as much as an individual rubber during the event. The United States is one of just five nations to field a team in all nine Cups, joined by Argentina, Australia, Canada and Great Britain.

Team USA has already clinched the top spot in its' group in the Jack Crawford Cup (Men’s 70) and Althea Gibson Cup (Women’s 70). The remaining American teams in the seven other divisions are either in full control of their group or tied atop the standings going into Tuesday.

The seeds have held true to form for the most part, but a few surprising results have been posted.

In the Britannia Cup (Men’s 65), Netherlands upset No. 7 seed Great Britain in group G to move into first place. Canada has already clinched group B, which included a defeat of No. 2 Australia thanks to winning both singles rubbers, in the Championships' inaugural Men’s 85 Team Cup. Team USA took group A in the Althea Gibson Cup after sweeping top-seeded Great Britain. No. 3 seed France suffered a setback against Australia in group C of the Queens Cup (Women’s 75). Finally, second seed Great Britain fell against New Zealand in Doris Hart Cup group B action, allowing the Kiwis to grab the group’s top slot.

For the most up-to-date results from the 2017 ITF Super-Seniors World Team Championships, click here.

Featuring 123 teams (72 men’s and 51 women’s) representing 31 nations, the 2017 ITF Super-Seniors World Team Championships are being held at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., from Oct. 8-13. The international tournament features nine cups for the first time in its history, as the Men’s 85 Team Cup is in its first year of existence.
