Since the USTA National Campus opened its doors on Jan. 2, hundreds of players have joined in on the fun by signing up for programming led by the USTA teaching pros.

A total of 25 programs and 86 classes are offered Mondays to Saturdays at the new Home of American Tennis. Players are welcome to register at any time, and the cost will be prorated based on the sign-up date.

Create Your Account, Register for a Program or Book a Court

The Nemours Family Zone serves as a destination for youngsters and even adults who are learning the game. Held on 36- and 60-foot courts, several programs are available in the mornings and evenings.

For children ages 11-17, Youth Development helps deliver a pathway for competitive excellence while emphasizing team participation. Cardio Tennis classes and Match Play Fridays are a great compliment to further develop one’s game.

Adult programs offer something for all levels of play and experience. From beginners to the “High Intensity Training Sessions,” classes are offered as a daily drop-in as well.

For players looking to experience the campus on their own, hard and clay courts are available for public booking seven days a week. Nemours Family Zone courts are complimentary for everyone.

To learn more, click on Book a Court at USTANationalCampus.com or call (844) 872-8667.

Tennis Tip from USTA National Campus Teaching Pro Ben Zaiser

Confidence on a tennis court is very fluid and hard to maintain. Regardless of how well you may be competing at any given moment, that confidence can quickly dissipate. Because of this, protecting your confidence is vital to consistently successful outcomes.

In your next tennis match, take a simple approach. Focus on stringing points together. Winning two points in a row can go a long way to keeping you in a confident and winning mindset. No matter how the match may be going for you, a keen focus on simply winning that next point can make the difference in your match.
