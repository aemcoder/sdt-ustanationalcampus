USTA FL Mixed 18 & Over and Mixed 55 & Over Sectional Championships

Trio of Divisional Titles Claimed

January 9, 2021

style
article-hero

Saturday saw three teams claimed divisional titles during the second day of action at the USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 and Mixed 55 & Over 7.0 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus.

The 18 & Over 6.0 Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) crew dominated the field to take home the crown with a perfect 3-0 record on the weekend. In total, duos representing Broward earned seven individual matchup victories on the strength of 15 sets won; furthermore, they amassed 98 games in their three duals, which was 17 more than second-place Alachua (Haile Plantation Country Club – Gainesville).

Captained by German Avellaneda, the full Broward roster includes Sima Sadoughi, Romina Fattore, Ili Montnor, Andrea Cordova, Natalia Azuero, Saeid Sadoughi, Jessika Bolin, Aldri Franco, Fernando Machado, Eduardo Cordova, and Eduardo Lancara.

Sunday marked the lone day for the 18 & Over 10.0 competition to be in action. With just two squads – Alachua (Westside Park Recreation – Gainesville) and Brevard (Kiwi Tennis Club – Indian Harbour Beach) entered into the event, a best-of-three series was on the slate. Ultimately, the “if necessary” dual would not be played after Alachua defeated Brevard twice by a 2-1 margin.

James Bratt leads the Alachua team that also includes Campbell Harmon, Noah Shever, Erica Braschi, Isabella Herrero, Paulo Sicangco, Emmanuelle Schue, Coley Hungate, and Gabriela Portmann.

A sterling 4-0 record gave the crew from Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) the 55 & Over 7.0 crown on Saturday. The crucial dual triumph came in the morning session, where Alachua took care of eventual runner-up Escambia (Independent) with a 2-1 win; that would be Escambia’s only setback for the weekend.

Captain Susie Finfrock’s Alachua roster is composed of Mark Diblin, Christa Arnold, Philip Lancaster, Ed Stark, Jorg Bungert, Tahereh Toghiyani, Muhammad Ali, Donald van Schuppen, Pamela Wright, Anita Frankenberger, and Scott Thompson.

The 18 & Over 8.0 division is the only one that will continue on into Sunday after flight play wrapped up Saturday afternoon. Flight 2 saw Okaloosa/Walton/Bay (Shalimar Pointe Country Club), perhaps the most dominant team competing this weekend, finish off a perfect 4-0 effort by winning 10 of the 12 courts their duos contested. Meanwhile, unlikely Flight 1 winner Sarasota (Sarasota Sports Club) began the day sitting third in the standings, needing to sweep both of their duals and get some help in order to move past both Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) and Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville). Those three squads would end in a tie at 3-1, with Sarasota’s 10 court victories proving the difference.
