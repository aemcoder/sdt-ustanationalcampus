From NCAA national titles won to Team USA winning the Junior Fed Cup, there were no shortage of great moments in 2019 at the USTA National Campus.

Take a look back at some of the best moments of the year involving big tournaments and big players. 

Follow the action on USTA social media with #NationalCampus.

Top Moments of 2019

College MatchDay provided some big upsets and big moments.

The USTA collegiate combine provided the opportunity to show their skills.

Team USA won the Junior Fed Cup in September.

USTA Leagues played several of its national championships at the USTA National Campus.

The NCAA Championships were filled with great competition.

Sloane Stephens paid a visit to the Net Generation Kids' Zone.

The USTA National Campus provided a spectacular backdrop for the Florida-Florida State rivalry.

The American Tennis Showcase allowed for top pros to show their skills off.

David Wagner won his 11th career NEC Wheelchair Masters.
