2019 USTA Keith Donoff Cup

Southern Goes for Sixth-Consecutive Title

November 14, 2019

style
article-hero

The annual USTA Keith Donoff Cup, the USTA’s national men’s 55 & Over Intersectional event, returns to the USTA National Campus this weekend. Starting the morning of Friday, Nov. 15 and running through the afternoon of Sunday, Nov. 17, this year’s tournament will see a total of five Sections in action – Florida, Mid-Atlantic, Middle States, Midwest, and Southern.

The Donoff Cup is composed of the following age divisions: 55s, 60s, 65s, and 70s. The five Sections will have a team competing in each of those divisions, where they will face off in round robin dual matches.

A dual match consists of three singles and two doubles courts per division, each counting for one team point. Singles contests will utilize the Fast4 format, meaning each set will be the first to four with a tiebreak at 3-3 and no-ad scoring. All doubles matches will be one eight-game pro set.

The winner of a team match is the Section that earns the most individual matches; a team can earn up to 20 points in a single dual. If two or more Sections end the weekend with the same number of individual victories, head-to-head results will determine the overall team champion.

Southern will be looking for an incredible sixth-consecutive title in the event, while last year’s runner-up Florida looks to lead the charge at toppling the powerhouse defending champions.
