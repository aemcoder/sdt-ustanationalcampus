The first professional tournament at the USTA National Campus is taking place Jan. 15-22, with the USTA Women's Pro Circuit $25K. It is one of four USTA Pro Circuit events slated to be held at the new facility during the first three months of 2017.

Orlando Professional Tennis Series at the USTA National Campus

USTA Pro Circuit Women's $25K - Jan. 14-22

USTA Pro Circuit Men's Futures $15K - Feb. 9-19

USTA Pro Circuit Men's Futures $15K - Feb. 23-March 5

USTA Pro Circuit Women's $10K - March 4-12

Starting Jan. 17, watch the live stream for free.

Jan. 14-22 Fan Information

-Admission and parking is free

-Phone: (407) 675-2500

-Address: 10000 USTA Blvd., Orlando, FL 32827

Jan. 14-22 Player Information

-Entry Deadline: Dec. 22

-Withdrawal Deadline: Jan. 3

-Freeze Deadline: Jan. 12

Schedule of Play

-Singles Qualifying: Jan. 15

-Doubles Qualifying: Jan. 16

-Singles Main Draw Begins: Jan. 17

-Doubles Main Draw Begins: Jan. 17

-Doubles Final: Jan. 21

-Singles Final: Jan. 22

The USTA Pro Circuit is the largest developmental tennis circuit in the world, hosting approximately 100 events across the U.S. each year. Prize money ranges from $10,000 to $100,000, and the events feature a range of players, from rising young stars to established veterans looking to jump-start their careers. 

To learn more, visit the USTA Pro Circuit.
