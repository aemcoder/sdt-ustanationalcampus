Spring Break Tennis

USTA Staff | April 2, 2018

More than 300 college matches took place at the USTA National Campus in 2018 as Spring Break Sports delivered constant action during February and March. It was the second straight year that the Campus featured at least 300 dual-matches on its grounds. Schools from all over the country, from every level of college tennis, partner up with Spring Break Sports and the USTA to celebrate their spring break in Orlando.
