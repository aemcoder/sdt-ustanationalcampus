2019 USTA Florida 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships

First Matches Slated for Saturday Morning

August 15, 2019

style
article-hero

The second of consecutive weekends of USTA Florida sectional play at the National Campus is scheduled to get underway Saturday, August 17, as the women’s 2.5 plus the men’s and women’s 3.5 & 4.5 team titles will be up for grabs. The event will run through the afternoon of Monday, August 19.

All dual matches for the women’s 2.5 level will consist of one line of singles and two lines of doubles; for the remaining divisions, a dual match is made up of two singles courts and three doubles courts.

Nine women’s 2.5 squads have been divided into two flights for the weekend, with four in Flight 1 and five in Flight 2. The pair of teams finishing atop those flights will advance to the championship matchup slated for Monday at 9 a.m.

It will be an especially busy weekend of 3.5 action, with 16 men’s and 19 women’s teams taking to the courts over Saturday and Sunday during the round-robin portion of the tournament. Both competitions feature four flights, with the flight champions locking up their spot in the knockout stage. The semifinals will be played Monday at 8 a.m. with the finals to follow at 11:30 a.m.

The men’s and women’s 4.5 divisions also parallel each other, with 12 squads having entered on both sides. As there are only three flights, the semifinal round will include the three flight winners and the top second-place finisher, which will earn the wild card spot. The championship day schedule is also 8 a.m. for the semifinals and 11:30 a.m. for the championship contests.

Last year’s sectional champions included Duval (Deerwood Country Club – Jacksonville) in the women’s 2.5 competition, the Orange/Seminole men (National Campus – Orlando) and women (Red Bug Lake Park – Casselberry) in the 3.5 division, and the Duval men (Independent) along with the Duval women (Glen Kernan – Jacksonville) for the 4.5 divisions.
