Orange/Seminole 3.5 Women Prevail

December 9, 2020

style
article-hero

The Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) women defended their home turf on Wednesday, capturing the women’s 3.5 team title by a 2-1 margin to wrap up the 2020 USTA Florida 18 & Over Men’s 3.0/3.5/4.5 & Women’s 3.5/4.0 Sectional Championships.

Orange/Seminole was up against Miami-Dade (Don Shulas Athletic Club – Hialeah), a squad fresh off recording a commanding 3-0 sweep at the semifinal stage, in the divisional final. The home-standing crew quickly took control of the dual, claiming two opening sets.

Natalie Beavers and Amanda Barritt moved up in the lineup from the semifinals to play on the first doubles court, blitzing through the first frame without dropping a game before holding off Audrey Gayle and Ivet Hernandez 6-0, 7-6. However, the Miami-Dade duo of Rossana Okamura and Andrea Gil responded with a solid 6-2, 7-5 effort at the second doubles spot over Cynthia Curtis and Natalia Bonavia. That set the stage for Orange/Seminole's Alisa Wolfe, who once again represented her squad in singles; she outlasted Ali Quintero 6-1, 2-6, 1-0.

Captain Barritt’s full roster includes Christine Godek, Cheryl Tolentino-Bernabe, Curtis, Bonavia, Victoria Karen, Beavers, Leslie Weeks, Beatriz Coelho, Manuela Vasconcelos, Leslie Hartog, Kathleen Sipio, Wolfe, Lori Burshan, Pamela Collins, and Christine Noddings.

Earning titles earlier in the event were the Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) 3.0 men, the Collier (Club at Longshore Lake – Naples) 3.5 men, the Miami-Dade (Kerdyk Biltmore Tennis Center – Coral Gables) 4.0 women, and the men’s 4.5 squad representing Hillsborough (Sandra W Freedman Tennis Complex – Tampa).
