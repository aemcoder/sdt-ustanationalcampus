From Sunrise to Sunset

October 18, 2019

Matches were on court as the sun was coming up Friday morning and still going strong as it went back down on the opening day of the 2019 USTA League National Adult 40 & Over 3.0 Championships. Due to the projected impact of Tropical Storm Nestor, Friday’s schedule of play heavily increased in order to reduce the number of matches potentially affected on Saturday.

The disjointed schedule resulted in three men’s teams playing two duals while the other 14 played a total of three. Those three made the most of their time on court, coming away with 2-0 records to stay in the mix for a semifinal berth. Caribbean (Independent – Puerto Rico) look the part of a championship contender, going 10-for-10 on the courts they contested with 20 set victories and only two surrendered. Southern (Independent – N. East, Miss.) and Missouri Valley (Lafortune Tennis Center – Tulsa, Okla.) also turned in solid days, collecting eight individual matchup wins over the course of the day.

Sitting atop the men’s division with sterling 3-0 records are the squads from Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Howard County, Md.), with 11 courts and 23 sets to their credit, and Texas (Independent – Dallas), who preserved their unbeaten mark by pulling out a dual win in the last match of the day to finish. Meanwhile, a quartet of teams sits on the outside of semifinal contention looking in after going 2-1 on Friday – Intermountain (East Junior High – Boise, Idaho), Southwest (Jerry Cline – Albuquerque, N.M.), Pacific Northwest (Eastside Tennis Center – Kirkland, Wash.), and Eastern (Deer Park Tennis Center – Deer Park, N.Y.). 

For the women’s competition, four teams are just one dual win away from locking up a place in the national semifinals following a 3-0 effort. The representatives of the Middle States Section (Kinetix Sports Club – Norristown, Pa.) dropped the fewest number of sets (nine), while the squad from Midwest (Carmel Racquet Club – Carmel, Ind.) claimed 64% of the games they played, which was the highest percentage on the day. Both Middle States and Midwest claimed 11 court wins to just four losses. Sitting just behind those two are Southern (Rock Hill Tennis Center – Rock Hill, S.C.) and Florida (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) with 10 and nine individual matches victories, respectively. 

Also remaining in the hunt heading into Saturday with 2-1 overall records are the squads from Intermountain (South Jordan Tennis Association – South Jordan, Utah) and Missouri Valley (Elite Squad Tennis Club – Overland Park, Kan.). Each offset the one loss – Intermountain falling to Southern and Missouri Valley coming up short versus Midwest – with two dominating wins. Additionally, the lone team to play just twice on the day was Southern California (Manhattan Country Club – Manhattan Beach), splitting those decisions. Their major dual will come against Florida as they battle to move into the top four.
