Three Divisions in Action

Saturday marked day two of the 2020 USTA Florida 18 & Over Men’s 3.0/3.5/4.5 & Women’s 3.5/4.0 Sectional Championships, in which three of the five divisions were in action.

Teams are competing in dual matches consisting of three matchups in total – one singles and two doubles; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

The men’s 4.5 championship tilt will feature Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville) going up against Hillsborough (Sandra W Freedman Tennis Complex – Tampa). Alachua suffered a loss in its final dual out of Flight 1 against Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center), but had already earned the right to play for the title going into the match. For Hillsborough, they posted a perfect 4-0 record in Flight 2 on the strength of 10 court victories.

Saturday also marked the first day of play for the men’s 3.5 competition. Three squads went undefeated, putting them one dual victory away from advancing to the semifinal stage. Brevard (Kiwanis Island Tennis Association – Merritt Island) dominated Flight 1, taking five of six individual matchups while dropping just two sets. Miami-Dade (Crandon Park Tennis Center – Key Biscayne) put themselves in the driver’s seat in Flight 2 with a pair of wins, as did Collier (Club at Longshore Lake – Naples) playing out of Flight 3.

The women’s 4.0 division saw every team but one take on at least one dual loss. The standings in Flights 1 and 2 display a four-way log jam at 1-1 going into the second day of play; meanwhile, 2-0 Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park – Casselberry) is in good shape to safely move on to the divisional knockout stages from Flight 3.

Sunday’s slate will begin at 7:30 a.m. as the men’s 3.0 and women’s 3.5 divisions will be in action for the first time on the weekend.
