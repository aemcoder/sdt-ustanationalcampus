Come get a taste of everything the USTA National Campus has to offer. 

Starting Jan. 3 and ending on Jan. 5, the National Campus will host a tennis program open house for the Orlando community, offering a sampling of programs at absolutely no cost. 

The open house will be broken up into two sessions, the first beginning at 9:30 a.m. and running until 11:30 a.m., and the second starting at 5 p.m. and lasting until 7 p.m. There will also be a daily 7 p.m. Q&A youth program meeting for parents.

The tennis programs that will be available to try are open to the entire family and include: 

NEW-TO-TENNIS PROGRAMS

YOUTH

• Beginner and intermediate (Red and Orange ball play)

• Mornings and after-school hours

• Match play Fridays

• Play-based learning programs

ADULT

• “Play Tennis Fast” beginners program

• Tennis 101

FITNESS-FOCUSED

• Cardio Tennis and Live-Ball program

• Youth cardio training

 

ELEVATED TRAINING AND ADVANCED PLAY

• Youth development through Campus Excellence training (middle and high school)

• Doubles drill and play, singles drilling, High Intensity Training Sessions (HITS)

• Match play programming

• Ball machine lessons

The campus, which officially opens to the public on Jan. 2, houses 100 courts of various surfaces. Program rates are $15 per person/ per hour, while court rental rates can be found below: 

COURT RENTAL RATES         1 hr 1.5 hr 2 hr

Plexicushion Hard Courts      $12   $15    $20

Har-Tru Green Clay Courts     $15   $20   $25
