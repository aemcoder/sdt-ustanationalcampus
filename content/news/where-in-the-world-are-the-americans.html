Many U.S. tennis players call the USTA National Campus in Orlando, FL home for non-stop training and practicing.  See what places around the world our resident players have travelled to this past week to compete. 

Follow them on social media to continue cheering them on from home as they battle both oversees and at the USTA National Campus.
