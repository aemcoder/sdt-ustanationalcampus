Day One Disrupted by Weather

July 20, 2019

style
article-hero

Saturday at the 2019 USTA FL 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships turned into a 15-hour day thanks to an extended weather delay in the early afternoon. Eventually, all matches scheduled for the opening day of the event were completed.

The men’s 3.0 division will come down to the dual between Broward (Midtown Athletic Club –Weston) and Leon (Tom Brown Park – Tallahassee) on Sunday. The two squads went 2-0 on day one, with Broward taking eight court victories compared to seven for Leon. Moreover, Broward dropped just six sets and 67 games while Leon gave away nine sets and 74 games. They will face off at 7:30 a.m. Sunday morning.

In the women’s 3.0 competition, three teams have moved just one dual triumph away from advancing to Monday’s semifinals. Duval (Southside Tennis Complex – Jacksonville) has virtually locked up Flight 3 after earning nine individual victories with a mere two sets lost. Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) and SMD (Don Shulas Athletic Club – Hialeah) are also in control of Flights 1 and 2, respectively; Hillsborough collected eight court wins and SMD owns seven so far. Flight 4, the lone flight composed of five teams, meaning teams still have two duals remaining. Heading into the second day of the tournament, Pinellas (Belleair Country Club) and Brevard (Suntree Country Club – Melbourne) sit at 2-0 overall.

Very little has been decided so far in the men’s 4.0 division. Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) is atop Flight 1 at 2-0; however, their six individual wins compared with four losses is the same as both Duval (Boone Park – Jacksonville) and SMD (Alper JCC – Miami), who are 1-1. A similar situation has developed in Flight 2, where Escambia (Independent) is undefeated, but did not get there in a commanding fashion; both Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) and Orange/Seminole (Villagewalk at Lake Nona) remain in the hunt to advance to the knockout stage. Flight 3 will go to either IRMSL (Buttonwood Tennis Club – Stuart) or Sarasota (Bath and Racquet Club), both sitting at 2-0.

Hillsborough (Westchase Community Association – Tampa) breezed through the opening day in women’s 4.0 action, taking a stranglehold on Flight 1 on the strength of nine individual wins and only five sets dropped. Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) is the favorite to get through Flight 2 after reeling in seven court victories for a 2-0 record, while IRMSL (Buttonwood Tennis Club – Stuart) tenuously holds the top spot in Flight 3 with six individual wins. Flight 4 is to be determined by the showdown between 2-0 Polk (Country Club of Winter Haven) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala); Polk was one of the most dominant squads across all divisions on Saturday, going 9-for-10 in courts contested while surrendering three sets and 60 games.
