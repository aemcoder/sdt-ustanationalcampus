The Home of American Tennis celebrates Hispanic Heritage Month by showcasing three of the Spanish-speaking staff teaching professionals that call the USTA National Campus home.

Gus Munoz, Maria Garcia and David Hernandez share their thoughts on teaching to the heavily Hispanic Orlando community here at the USTA National Campus. From conversing with guests every day in Spanish, to teaching full lessons to our Spanish players of all ages--the Home of American tennis is growing the game by making it available for all.
