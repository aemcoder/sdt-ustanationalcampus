Divisional Finals Set for Monday

March 18, 2018

article-hero

On Sunday, the eight draws of the USTA Florida March Level 4 Championships were whittled down to the finals, setting the stage for champions to be crowned on Monday.

None of the eight divisions had their top two seeds safely advance to the final. Boys’ 12s No. 1 seed Yubel Ubri along with boys’ 14s No. 1 seed Yannik Rahman have earned the right to play for a title on Monday, as have divisional second seeds Ben Shelton (boys’ 16s) and DJ Bennett (girls’ 14s).

The biggest story on the weekend was the success of unseeded players, which largely continued on Sunday. In the girls’ 16s draw, an unseeded final between Natalie Block and Violeta Martinez will be contested. The same story played out in the girls’ 18s, where either Emma Shelton or Abigail Rencheli will take home the trophy.

Other unseeded players to reach a final include Martin Mesia (boys’ 14s), Michael Nicholls (boys’ 16s), Micah Braswell (boys’ 18s), and Mia Slama (girls’ 14).

Listed below are Monday’s finals matchups:

Boys’ 12s: (1)Yubel Ubri vs. (4)Tanner Povey

Boys’ 14s: (1)Yannik Rahman vs. Martin Mesia

Boys’ 16s: (2)Ben Shelton vs. Michael Nicholls

Boys’ 18s: (3)Logan Zapp vs. Micah Braswell

Girls’ 12s: (5) Sophie Llewellyn vs. (6)Lauren Kettlewell

Girls’ 14s: (2)DJ Bennett vs. Mia Slama

Girls’ 16s: Natalie Block vs. Violeta Martinez

Girls’ 18s: Emma Shelton vs. Abigail Rencheli

Match play continues on Sunday morning at 8:00 a.m. For more information on the USTA Florida March Level 4 Championships, click here.
