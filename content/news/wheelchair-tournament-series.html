The 2017 USTA Wheelchair Tournament Series will kick off Saturday, Aug. 26, at the USTA National Campus. The tournament will follow the Fast Four format (first to four games, no-ad scoring, tiebreak at 3-3), which is great for one-day events. Participants will be able to play multiple matches in a short period of time, alleviating the large time commitment common with most traditional tournaments.  

Date: Saturday, August 26

Cost: $23.00 per player

Location: USTA National Campus, Orlando, FL

Tournament Signup Link: 2017 USTA Wheelchair Tournament Series

Levels: Open

Format: Fast four round robin

Scheduling: First-round matches will start at 9:30 a.m.

Subsequent round start times: TBD - will follow first round matches

Lunch will be provided at 12:30 p.m.

How Many Matches will I play? Players will be guaranteed four matches.

Questions? Contact Jason Allen.
