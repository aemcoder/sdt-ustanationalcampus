2019 USTA May National Level 2

Annual Junior Event Returns this Weekend

May 17, 2019

style
article-hero

A junior tournament that has become an annual event on the USTA National Campus calendar, the 2019 edition of the USTA May National Level 2 returns to the courts this weekend. Featuring some of the nation’s top competitors in the boys’ and girls’ 16s, play will get underway at 8:30 a.m. Saturday morning and run through Tuesday afternoon.

The singles draw is a full 64-player draw with a feed-in consolation through the round of 16; singles matches will be the best of three sets with a 10-point match tiebreak in lieu of a full third and will be using regular scoring. The 32-team doubles draws are single elimination with a third-place playoff match; all doubles contests will be one eight-game proset (tiebreak at 7-7) with no-ad scoring.

Kian Vakili, representing the Florida Section, heads into the weekend as the top seed on the boys’ side. He experienced success at the event in 2018, claiming the consolation singles titles as the No. 8 seed while also reaching the final in doubles.  His most recent appearance at the National Campus came in the 2018 USTA National Winter Championships, where he played his way to the quarterfinals in consolation action.

Rounding out the top four seeds for boys’ singles is second-seeded Sam Feldman (New England), No. 3 seed Adit Sinha (Eastern), and four seed Eli Gordon (Midwest).

Feldman and Sinha will be teaming up in doubles, combining to form the top-seeded duo in the boys’ draw; Vakili and Gordon come in right behind them as the second seeds. Those pairs along with No. 3 seeds Ryan Colby (Mid-Atlantic) and Ian Rasheed (Southern) have received byes in the opening round.

Southern Section standout Emma Charney comes in on a wave of confidence, having claimed the singles and doubles trophies at last month’s USTA Southern Level 1A Championships – a National Level 4 tournament. She also had a very strong showing during the 2018 USTA National Winter Championships, advancing to the quarterfinals in singles while also taking home a Silver Ball in doubles.

Potential challengers to Charney this weekend will be No. 2 seed Ria Bhakta (Northern California), third-seeded Sara Snyder (Caribbean), and fourth seed Vivian Miller (Midwest).

Miller and Whitley Pate (Southern) have earned the No. 1 seed for the girls’ doubles competition, while Charney and Snyder check in right behind them as the second seeds. The top seven duos received a bye to the round of 16.
