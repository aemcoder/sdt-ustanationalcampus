Hillsborough Dominates 10.0 Division

October 14, 2018

article-hero

Hillsborough’s complete domination of the 10.0 division highlighted day two of the 2018 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. The 6.0 division is down to the final dual while the 8.0 division will roll out their semifinal and championships matches on Monday.

A Sunday rally strung together by Alachua (Westside Park Gainesville Rec Department) propelled them into the 6.0 championship dual out of Flight 1. Starting the day one match back of Polk (Lake Wales Country Club), they took down both Polk and Hillsborough (Grand Hampton). That left both Alachua and Polk at 3-1 overall. The tiebreaking procedure was taken all the way down to games lost, which went in favor of Alachua (77 games lost versus 88 for Polk).

The opponent for Alachua will be Broward (Midtown Athletic Club), who did suffer a loss on Sunday, which also forced them to need the tiebreaking procedure to move on. Being the most consistent team in Flight 2 paid off, as their nine individual wins put them on top of the standings despite Volusia (Florida Tennis Center) equaling their team record of 3-1. Alachua and Broward will take to the court at 9 a.m. Monday morning.

In the 8.0 competition, Okaloosa (Bluewater Bay Tennis Center) from Flight 1 along with Sarasota (Payne Park) of Flight 3 easily booked their spot in the semifinals. Okaloosa swept through the competition on the strength of seven court wins and just four sets lost in three team matches; Sarasota went 4-0 with 10 individual matchups going their way.

A competitive Flight 2 went to Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park) despite going just 2-1 as a team. The tie atop the standings with Collier (Treviso Bay) was broken in their favor by a fairly comfortable margin of two court wins. Flight 4 turned into mass chaos on Sunday. After all duals were complete, the five squads owned identical 2-2 slates. The semifinal representative was decided by just a singles individual victory, as SMD (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center) was the only team which managed to finish above .500 in that category, taking seven wins against five losses.

Okaloosa matches up with Orange/Seminole in one semifinal while Sarasota and SMD will clash in the other at 8:30 a.m.

Hillsborough (Macfarlane Park) ran away with the three-team 10.0 division. Starting the day in an outstanding position to win the title, Hillsborough left nothing to chance, sweeping both Alachua (Haile Plantation Country Club) and Pinellas (Clearwater). They wrapped up the two days of play with four dual triumphs after claiming 11 of a possible 12 court wins.
