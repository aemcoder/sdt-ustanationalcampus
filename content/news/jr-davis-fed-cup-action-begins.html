Top American junior players come together at the USTA National Campus to prepare for the upcoming Jr. Davis & Fed Cup Finals. These teams will battle players from around the globe next week on home soil here in Lake Nona, FL. The 2019 Jr. Davis Cup team will include Martin Damm, Toby Kodat, and Dali Blanch; while the Jr. Fed Cup team will be made up of Katrina Scott, Robin Montgomery, and Connie Ma. 

Follow all the event action with #TeamUSATennis at the USTA #NationalCampus.
