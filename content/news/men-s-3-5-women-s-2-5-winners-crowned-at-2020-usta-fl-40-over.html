Men's 3.5, Women's 2.5 Winners Crowned

November 8, 2020

style
article-hero

Weather wreaked havoc from start to finish on Sunday at the 2020 USTA Florida 40 & Over Men’s 3.5 & Women’s 2.5/3.5 Sectional Championships. After an early morning rain delay, the tournament spread out onto two surfaces and used a shortened scoring format in an effort to complete all matches.

Fortunate enough to finish up and crown a champion were the men’s 3.5 and women’s 2.5 divisions. The Brevard (Kiwi Tennis Club – Indian Harbour Beach) men bested Pinellas (Seminole Lake Family Tennis – Seminole) for the second time on the weekend in the final while the Alachua (Haile Plantation Country Club - Gainesville) women went a perfect 5-0 to sweep through the competition. Meanwhile, semifinal action in the women’s 3.5 division was halted due to rain; with more inclement weather in the Monday forecast, those matches along with the championship dual will be rescheduled for a later date.

The day’s first team match featured Brevard and Pinellas clashing for the second time in the event, this time with the men’s 3.5 trophy at stake. Just as he did in the semifinals, Matthew Kerr claimed the singles court without surrendering a game. The two doubles contests were much tighter, as the Brevard duos won the opening frames only to see those leads slip away. Ultimately, Brevard would come through on both, as Doug Young and Shane Jiruska took line one 7-6, 4-6, 1-0 while Juan Lebron and Ron Dulay teamed up for a 7-6, 4-6, 1-0 victory on court two.

Captained by Dulay, the full Brevard roster includes Tony Giacoletto, Kenton Anz, Young, Jiruska, Cletus Obrien, Martin Rojas, Robert Intille, Lebron, Sean Murphy, Nick George, Roger Weinreb, William Sulick, Steven Brandon, and Kerr.

Entering Sunday as one of two undefeated squads in the women’s 2.5 competition, Alachua prevailed in three duals over the course of the day to take home the divisional crown. In their five dual matches, Alachua won 12 courts and gave away only three. The crucial triumph came when they swept Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala), the other team to start the day without a loss.

Captain Carmen Cornell’s complete Alachua roster consists of Jodi Cox, Tara Bromirski, Christa Cook, Kirsten King, Rachel Brock, Diana Beck, Susan Berg, Rhoda Wilburn, Shannon Sumerlin, Marisa Rodriguez, Shannon Davis, and Stephanie Tangeman.

For the women’s 3.5 division, all flights wrapped up their matches to set the stage for the semifinal round before the cancellation. Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center – Plantation) owned Flight 1 with a sterling 4-0 record with nine court victories and Orange/Seminole (Grand Cypress Racquet Club – Orlando) cruised through Flight 3 by taking all 12 individual matchups with a mere three sets lost. Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) also advanced by going unscathed, winning Flight 2 with a 3-0 mark, and Pinellas (Bardmoor Golf Tennis Club – Largo) emerged from Flight 3 after overcoming a defeat on the opening day of play.
