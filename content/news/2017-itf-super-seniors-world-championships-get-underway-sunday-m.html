Top senior players from around the world will converge at the USTA National Campus to compete in the 2017 ITF Super Seniors World Team Championships.

The competition is set to begin on Sunday, Oct. 7, and conclude on Friday, Oct. 13. The tournament is the 65- to 85-and-over equivalent of the Davis and Fed Cup and is the most prestigious team event on the ITF Seniors Circuit.

A total of 30 countries will be represented in this team competition, which is composed of nine draws, known as Cups – Kitty Godfree Cup (Women’s 65 & Over); Althea Gibson Cup (Women’s 70 & Over); Queens’ Cup (Women’s 75 & Over); Doris Hart Cup (Women’s 80 & Over); Britannia Cup (Men’s 65 & Over); Jack Crawford Cup (Men’s 70 & Over); Bitsy Grant Cup (Men’s 75 & Over); Gardnar Mulloy Cup (Men’s 80 & Over); and Men’s 85 Team Cup.

The ITF Super-Seniors World Championships will be played on a round robin basis followed by knock out rounds to give a complete ranking of all teams. Each team tie will consist of two singles and one doubles rubber. Matches will be played over the best of three tiebreak sets with a tiebreak at six games all in each set. In the Gardnar Mulloy, Doris Hart and Men’s 85 Team Cups, should the score reach one set all, a ten-point tiebreak will be played in lieu of a full third set.

Team USA will have a squad taking part in each of the nine Cups. The American teams competing in the Queens Cup, Doris Hart Cup and Gardnar Mulloy Cup will enter as the defending champions. The U.S. will be in search of its eighth-consecutive Queens Cup title.

For a complete listing of each of the nine rosters for Team USA, click here.

Following the completion of the ITF Super-Seniors World Team Championships, the ITF World Individual Championships will be contested at the National Campus from Saturday, Oct. 14-Saturday, Oct. 21.
