Players of all ages and levels invaded the grounds of the USTA National Campus Jan. 3-5, as the new Home of American Tennis wrapped up its first six Open House sessions – and all were great successes.

Youngsters in their favorite Batman clothes all the way to adults who have been playing since they were little tykes themselves made the most of the Open Houses. They received coaching from the USTA teaching pros on campus and got to learn with players from around the world.

One family that ventures to Florida each year for tennis tournaments and Disney World decided to alter its schedule in 2017.

“My son, Quang, plays the Eddie Herr and Orange Bowl,” said Duc Duong, who is a coach for the Vietnam national team, lives in Southern California and has a house in Lake Nona. “We scheduled to go to Disney World Wednesday, but we came here on Tuesday and he said he wanted to keep training.”

Quang picked up a racquet when he was just 3 or 4, and started seriously training at age 6. He ended up coming to all six Open House events.

“We have come here in the morning and at night,” said Duc. “We love it here. The center is just like a candy store. It’s much more fun than training at home. Most of the time I’m his main coach, so this is a different perspective. He loves it.”

With the Open House events wrapped up, the USTA teaching pros will begin programming on Jan. 9. Players can sign up for one of 25 programs at USTANationalCampus.com.
