ENJOYING THE COURTS

Brian Ormiston | January 4, 2017

style
article-hero

Hundreds of visitors have swarmed the USTA National Campus this week for its free Open House events. Players of all ages have experienced coaching from the USTA teaching pros, getting a taste of the 25 different programs the Campus has to offer.

Open House started on Jan. 3 and runs through Jan. 5 with sessions taking place from 9:30-11:30 a.m. and 5-7 p.m. each day. Here are the types of programs provided in the spring:

YOUTH

-Beginning and intermediate (Red and Orange ball play)

-Mornings and after-school hours

-Match play Fridays

-Play-based learning programs

ADULT

-"Play Tennis Fast" beginners program

-Tennis 101

FITNESS-FOCUSED

-Cardio Tennis and Live Ball program

-Youth cardio traininig
