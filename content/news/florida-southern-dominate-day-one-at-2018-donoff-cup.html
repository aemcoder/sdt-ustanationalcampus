Florida, Southern Dominate Day One

The opening day of the 2018 USTA Donoff Cup concluded with Florida and Southern cruising to team victories. Florida took down Middle States by a 17-3 margin while defending champion Southern turned in a sweep of Midwest, 20-0.

Florida kicked off the morning by claiming 10 of the 12 singles contests versus Middle States, winning all three in the 60s and 70s age divisions. Middle States did manage to prevail at line one in both the 55s and 65s, but Florida came through on the other four courts. The group representing the Sunshine State took care of the doubles handily as well, winning all but line two in the 70s division, where Middle States outlasted Florida in the match tiebreak, 10 points to five.

Southern was absolutely dominant across the board to kick off the defense of their title. In singles play, they lost a total of seven games in the 60s, eight games in the 70s, and nine games in the 55s. The only division where Midwest posed a challenge was in the 65s, as they took a set but still could not get in the win column. Moving into the doubles portion of the day, Southern polished off all eight courts without surrendering even one set.

The Donoff Cup is composed of the following age divisions: 55s, 60s, 65s, and 70s. The four Sections have teams competing in those divisions, facing off in round robin dual matches.

A dual match consists of three singles and two doubles matches per division, each counting for one team point. Singles contests will be the best of three tiebreak sets using regular scoring; doubles matches will be two tiebreak sets using regular scoring with a 10-point match tiebreak as a deciding set.

The winner of a team match is determined by the Section that wins the most individual matches; a team can earn up to 20 points in a single dual. If two or more Sections end the weekend with the same number of individual victories, head-to-head results will determine the overall team champion.
