With more than 110 players representing 25 states and Puerto Rico, the inaugural USTA All-American College Combine is poised to have a memorable debut at the USTA National Campus June 14-16.

Created for American junior players of all levels who have the goal of playing college tennis in mind, the event will allow them to showcase their athleticism and tennis skills for college coaches from NCAA Divisions I, II and III. There will be a coaches symposium hosted by the ITA as well, and include special guest speakers.

Schedule*

Wednesday, June 14:

4-7 p.m. – Check-in

4-8 p.m. – Practice

Thursday, June 15:

7 a.m. – Warm-ups

8 a.m.-2 p.m. – Singles Matches

3-6 p.m. – Agility Testing, Win4Life, College Planning & Placement

4-8 p.m. – USTA/ITA College Coaches Symposium (For Coaches Only)

8 p.m. – Coaches Reception (For Coaches Only)

Friday, June 16:

8 a.m. – Warm-ups

9-11:30 a.m. – Singles and Doubles Matches

11:30 a.m. – Finals

1 p.m. – Awards Presentation

*Schedule Subject to Change, and Times are Approximate

The Combine will be point based, driven by results from the agility tests and matches. The boys and girls winner with the most overall points will receive a wild card into the main draw of a USTA Pro Circuit $15K tournament.

Main draw matches will be one full set with a 7-point tiebreaker at 6-6, while consolation matches are the first to four games, win by two, and if it is 3-3 then a 12-point tiebreaker will be used to determine the winner.

Fans, family and friends are all welcome to watch the USTA All-American College Combine. Admission and parking is free.
