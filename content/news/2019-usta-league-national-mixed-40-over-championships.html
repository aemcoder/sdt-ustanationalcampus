The 2019 USTA League Nationals schedule is set to wrap up this weekend, with the USTA National Campus serving as the host site for all four Mixed 40 & Over divisions. First matches will be on court at 7:30 a.m. Friday, Nov. 15 with the quartet of champions to be crowned Sunday, Nov. 17.

For Friday and Saturday, teams will compete in four dual matches as part of divisional flight play with the goal of locking up a semifinal berth. Sunday will see the semifinal matchups take to the court at 7:30 a.m. with the finals and third-place playoffs to follow at approximately 10:30 a.m.

Each dual match will consist of three courts of doubles, meaning a squad will need to capture at least two of those in order to claim the overall victory.

Both the 7.0 and 8.0 competitions have representation from all 17 USTA Sections. The 6.0 division features all Sections with the exception of Northern California, and the 9.0 division will be without Hawaii and Southwest.

Taking home titles at the 2018 Championships was the squad from Caribbean (Independent – Puerto Rico) in the 6.0 division, Southern (Germantown Country Club – Memphis, Tenn.) for the 7.0 competition, Missouri Valley (Aspen Athletic Club – Des Moines, Iowa) in the 8.0 division, and Missouri Valley (Lafortune Tennis Center – Tulsa, Okla.) as well for the 9.0 competition.
