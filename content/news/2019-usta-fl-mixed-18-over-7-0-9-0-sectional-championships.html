2019 USTA FL Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships

Fall League Play Kicks Off

October 2, 2019

style
article-hero

Kicking off a full slate of league play at the USTA National Campus this fall will be the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships, which get underway the morning of Saturday, Oct. 5 and is scheduled to run through the afternoon of Monday, Oct. 7.

Saturday and Sunday will see all teams compete in head-to-head duals as part of round robin flight play in hopes of advancing to Monday’s semifinal stage. Each dual match will feature three courts of doubles, with a crew needing to capture at least two of those courts to secure the dual victory.

In the 7.0 competition, a total of 19 squads start the weekend with the hopes of earning a sectional crown; Flight 1 consists of four teams while Flights 2, 3, and 4 are made up of five. On the 9.0 side, 11 crews will make the trip over to the National Campus. Flight 1 will see its trio of teams battle in double round-robin action to go along with Flights 2 and 3 playing a traditional round robin schedule. As there are only three flights in the 9.0 division, a wild card entrant in the form of the best second-place finisher will round out the semifinalists.    

Last year’s champions both represented the City of Oakland Park facility out of the Broward region, as the 7.0 squad defeated Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park – Casselberry) in the final while the 9.0 team bested Hillsborough (Harbour Island Athletic Club – Tampa) by the same margin for the title. Those two will be in action this weekend in an effort to defend their titles.
