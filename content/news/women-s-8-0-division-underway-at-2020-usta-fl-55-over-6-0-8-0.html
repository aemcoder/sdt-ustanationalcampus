Women's 8.0 Division Underway

October 9, 2020

style
article-hero

A rainy afternoon extended Friday’s matches by several hours on the opening day of the 2020 USTA Florida 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Teams in the women’s 8.0 competition kicked off the event with their first two rounds of dual matches.

Flight 1 saw Lee/Charlotte (Park Meadow Tennis Club – Fort Myers) dominate in both of their dual matches, taking five of the six individual matchups contested by their players on the strength of 11 set victories. They have all but locked up a place in the divisional final after that effort, with both Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville) and South Miami-Dade (Dante Fascell Park – Miami) clinging onto a slight chance to upend Lee/Charlotte.

Brevard (Rockledge Park) posted similar numbers to sit atop the Flight 2 standings, also coming through with five court wins and 11 sets to their credit. Sitting just behind Brevard with an overall record of 1-1 is Hillsborough (Apollo Beach Racquet Fitness Club), leaving them still in the mix.

The women’s 8.0 division will continue tomorrow with the final round of its flight play, while all women’s 6.0 and men’s 8.0 squads will be in action for the first time in the event.

Saturday’s slate of matches is scheduled to begin at 7:30 a.m. For more information on the 2020 USTA Florida 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships, click here.
