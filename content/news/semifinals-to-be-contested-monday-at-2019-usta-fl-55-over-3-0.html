Semifinals to be Contested Monday

Day two of the 2019 USTA Florida 55 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships, held at the USTA National Campus, was much like the first, with heavy rain and lightning disrupting the afternoon schedule. With several rounds of matches moved indoors, the women’s 3.0 and men’s 4.0 semifinals were postponed until Monday.

Both Collier representatives in the women’s 3.0 division wrapped up flight play undefeated to easily advance to the semifinal stage. The team from the Greater Marco Family YMCA (Marco Island) turned in a 3-0 effort in Flight 1 and The Players Club and Spa (Naples) claimed the top spot in Flight 2. Emerging from Flight 3 was Escambia (Independent), while Flight 1 runner-up Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) earned the wild card spot. The possibility of an all-Collier championship dual remains, with Greater Marco Family YMCA facing Escambia and The Players Club and Spa matched up with Marion.

Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) heads into the women’s 4.0 semifinals as the only team without a loss after sweeping through Flight 1. It was a more even race to the top in Flights 2 and 3. Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) dropped out of the running in Flight 2 thanks to a pair of defeats on Sunday; conversely, Sarasota (River Strand Golf and Country Club – Bradenton) and Brevard (Rockledge Park) finished tied at 3-1, with Sarasota’s eight court victories sending them into the final four. Duval (Boone Park – Jacksonville) earned the crucial head-to-head victory in Flight 3 over Orange/Seminole (Sylvan Lake Park – Sanford) to lock up their semifinal spot as well. Monday’s matchups will pit Pinellas against Duval in one semifinal while the other will be a battle of Sarasota squads after the Longwood Athletic Club squad grabbed the wild card.

Three unbeaten teams along with a one-loss wild card entrant will compete for the men’s 4.0 title on Monday. Duval (Amelia National – Fernandina Beach) bested Collier (Pelican Bay Community Park – Naples) with Flight 1 on the line, while South Palm Beach (Parkland Tennis Center) dominated Flight 2. Volusia (Daytona Beach Shores Parks and Recreation) managed to finish atop Flight 3; Flight 2 runner-up SMD (Royal Palm Tennis Club) stayed alive by virtue of being the wild card. They will be across the net from Duval and South Palm Beach is set to take on Volusia.

All semifinals are scheduled for 8:30 a.m. Monday. For more information on the 2019 USTA Florida 55 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships, click here.
