2018 USTA League National Adult 18 & Over 5.0+ Championships

Teams Qualify for Sunday’s Semifinals

October 6, 2018

style
article-hero

After two days of play at the 2018 USTA League National Adult 18 & Over 5.0+ Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus, four men’s and four women’s teams remain in the hunt for a league national title. Only Northern California was able to push both of their squads into the semifinal round, which will take to the courts at 7 a.m. Sunday morning with the championship and third-place duals to follow.

For the men, the four teams in contention are Florida (Northdale Golf & Tennis Club – Tampa), Northern California (Independent), Caribbean (Puerto Rico), and Southern (Topspin Racquet and Swim Club – Lexington, S.C.).

Florida and Northern California went undefeated over the course of Friday and Saturday to secure the top two seeds, with Florida earning the No. 1 spot thanks to 11 total court wins compared to eight for Northern California. Caribbean finished third overall, posting a team record of 3-1 after having their backs against the wall following a 2-1 loss in their opening match to Eastern. They were able to sweep the three subsequent matches to move on to Sunday.

Grabbing the final semifinal berth was Southern, who just edged out Mid-Atlantic via the total number of games lost tiebreaker. In the end, one game made all the difference, as Southern lost 101 compared to 102 for Mid-Atlantic.

Florida and Southern will face each other while Northern California and Caribbean go head-to-head.

In the women’s division, Texas (Greenhill Tennis Center – Addison), Southern California (Los Caballeros Sports Village – Fountain Valley), and Mid-Atlantic (Washington, D.C.) all went undefeated in their four dual matches to reach the semifinals.

Texas maintained their impressive form from day one, wrapping up play without the loss of an individual matchup or set; combined, players representing the Lone Star State gave away 31 games. Southern California was nearly untouchable as well, surrendering just one court and four sets in all. Mid-Atlantic faced some adversity along the way, but still maintained their perfect record despite losing four individual matchups and 11 sets.

Slipping into the fourth semifinal slot was Northern California (Berkeley Tennis Center). The highest finisher amongst the teams with 3-1 records, Northern California overcame a loss to Mid-Atlantic on Friday to survive. Coincidentally, they would end up with fewer sets (eight) and games (80) lost than the lone team to defeat them.

Northern California will be looking to derail Texas in one semifinal; Southern California and Mid-Atlantic will clash in the other.
