College MatchDay makes its anticipated return to the USTA National Campus on Friday, Feb. 3, with two of the top women’s programs in the country – No. 2 North Carolina takes on No. 12 Texas Tech – facing off in Orlando.

The match is scheduled to start at 6 p.m. ET. Gates open at 4 p.m. with food, drinks, games, music and a free kids’ clinic.

Admission to all College MatchDay events at the USTA National Campus is free, and fans can RSVP by heading to CollegeMatchDay2017.com. For those who cannot make it to the campus, ESPN3 will cover the action live.

If you cannot make it to the National Campus, watch LIVE ON ESPN3. Live stats can also be found HERE.

North Carolina holds a perfect 4-0 record this season, with shutouts of Ball State, UNC-Greensboro, Oregon and Texas. The Tar Heels have three singles players ranked in the ITA Top 10: No. 3 Sara Daavettila (Williamston, Mich.), No. 5 Hayley Carter (Hilton Head, S.C.) and No. 9 Jessie Aney (Rochester, Minn.)

Texas Tech, meanwhile, is 2-0 thanks to victories over UC Santa Barbara and Arizona State. The Lady Raiders also bring a stellar lineup: Gabriela Talaba is ranked No. 26 in the nation and Felicity Maltby (Sunnyvale, Calif.) sits at No. 81.

The campus entertained Michigan and Alabama for the first College MatchDay on Jan. 21, which featured the Wolverines walking away with a 4-0 victory in front of 1,089 spectators.
