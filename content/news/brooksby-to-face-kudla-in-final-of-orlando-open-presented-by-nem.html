Orlando Open Presented by Nemours

Brooksby to Face Kudla in Final

April 17, 2021

style
article-hero

Americans Jenson Brooksby and fifth-seeded Denis Kudla were victorious on Saturday to advance to the championship match of the ATP Challenger Orlando Open Presented by Nemours. Additionally, Mitchell Krueger and Jack Sock secured the doubles crown in an extended final that featured two American duos.

Brooksby has still yet to drop a set on the week after ending the run of fellow American and wild card Christian Harrison 7-5, 6-1; the opening frame on Saturday was the first time he has lost more than four games in any set. It was the first serve of Brooksby that proved the difference, as he put his opening delivery in play 75% of the time, winning 32 of those 45 points. While Harrison did manage two breaks on the day, Brooksby countered with an impressive six breaks of his own, highlighted by claiming half of the points on Harrison’s first serve.

The final marks the third of the year already for Brooksby on the ATP Challenger Tour, previously finishing as the runner-up in Cleveland last month while raising the first-place trophy in February at the event in Potchefstroom, South Africa.

No. 5 seed Kudla staged his second comeback from one set down on his journey to the final with a 2-6, 6-2, 6-2 effort over Roberto Cid Subervi (Dominican Republic). The match lasted just over two hours, with Kudla earning six service breaks on the strength of prevailing in 68% of Cid Subervi’s second serve points. For his career, Kudla owns seven ATP Challenger Tour titles, with his most recent coming in Cary, N.C., in November of last year.

The doubles final was tightly-contested from start to finish. The wild card pairing of Harrison and Dennis Novikov took set one 6-4 and found themselves mere points away from the victory before Sock and Mitchell Krueger eventually grabbed the second 7-5. The decisive match tiebreak went back-and-forth, with Sock and Krueger coming through 13 points to 11.
