2019 USTA Florida 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional Championships

Play Takes to the Courts Starting Saturday

August 8, 2019

style
article-hero

Summer league play at the National Campus picks back up this weekend, with six divisional titles up for grabs at the USTA Florida 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional Championships. Action begins Saturday, August 10 and concludes on Monday, August 12.

At the 3.0 and 4.0 levels, each dual match will consist of two lines of singles along with three lines of doubles, meaning a team needs to claim at least three of those matchups for the win. The duals in the 5.0+ competitions are composed of one singles court and two doubles courts.

Six teams are in the running for the men’s 3.0 crown, all placed into a single round-robin flight. They will each square off head-to-head once, with the champion to be determined as early as Sunday evening or as late as Monday afternoon. The women’s 3.0 competition will see 16 teams competing in four equally-divided flights over Saturday and Sunday. The semifinal round will take to the courts Monday morning at 8 a.m. with the championship contest to follow at 11:30 a.m.

Mirroring the women’s 3.0 schedule is the men’s 4.0 division, which also features a total of 16 teams filling out four divisions. For the women’s 4.0 side, 14 teams will be in action on the weekend, with four in Flight 1 and five in Flights 2 and 3. The trio of flight winners will move on to the knockout stages Monday, where the top second-place squad will join them as the wild card entrant.

Both 5.0+ competitions will see teams battling in just a single flight. The six men’s teams will play their duals out over the course of all three days of the event, while the five women’s squads are set to have the champion decided by Sunday afternoon.

Last year at the Sectional Championships, the Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center - Tallahassee) men and SMD (Crandon Park Tennis Center – Key Biscayne) women prevailed at the 3.0 level. The 4.0 titles went to the men representing Broward (Weston Tennis Center) and women from Collier (Heritage Bay Golf Country Club – Naples). Victors in the 5.0+ competitions were Hillsborough (Northdale Golf & Tennis Club – Tampa) on the men’s side and Duval (Independent) for the women.
