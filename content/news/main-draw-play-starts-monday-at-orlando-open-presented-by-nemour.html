Main Draw Play Starts Monday

April 12, 2021

Main draw singles action is set to take to the courts for the first time on Monday at the ATP Challenger Orlando Open Presented by Nemours while qualifying will also be featured prominently on the schedule of play. Following a washout on Sunday, two rounds of qualifying contests are slated to be played.

Getting underway in the 32-player main draw is a quartet of matchups scheduled to begin not before 1 p.m. Two seeded competitors, No. 7 Jason Jung (Chinese Taipei) and No. 8 Alejandro Tabilo (Chile), are among the opening day’s matches; Jung will be across the net from American Jenson Brooksby while Tabilo’s opponent is Canadian Brayden Schnur. Also on Monday’s schedule of play is the clash between Americans Thai-Son Kwiatkowski and Ulises Blanch plus Emilio Gomez (Ecuador) going up against Bjorn Fratangelo (United States).  

American Steve Johnson, ranked No. 83 in the FedEx ATP Rankings, is the top singles seed and has drawn fellow American Christopher Eubanks in round one. Japan’s Yasutaka Uchiyama comes in as the second seed, where he will begin his week with a meeting with Peter Polansky (Canada), while third-seeded Mackenzie McDonald is part of another first round all-American clash with wild card Christian Harrison.

Rounding out the singles seeds are No. 4 Thiago Seyboth Wild (Brazil), fifth seed Denis Kudla (United States), and No. 6 Prajnesh Gunneswaran (India). Additional wild cards were awarded to University of Illinois senior Aleksandar Kovacevic (United States) and Zane Kahn (United States).
