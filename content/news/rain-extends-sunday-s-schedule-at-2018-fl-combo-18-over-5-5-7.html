Rain Extends Sunday's Schedule

December 9, 2018

article-hero

Early rain pushed play late into the evening on Sunday at the 2018 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. After a lengthy delay and major schedule adjustment, the majority of matches originally slated to take place on day two of the Championships was completed.

One sectional title was earned Sunday, as Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) produced a sweep over Alachua (Westside Park Gainesville) in men’s 5.5 action. After the squads split their two matches on the opening day, Marion emphatically claimed all three courts of the third dual to mathematically lock up the crown.

Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center) finished off a sweep of the competition in Flight 2 to book their spot in the women’s 5.5 final. They finished with a perfect 3-0 record on the strength of eight court victories and just four sets lost. Taking on Alachua in Monday’s final will be Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center), which survived a tough Flight 1 with a 3-1 record despite splitting the 12 individual matchups they contested.

In the men’s 7.5 division, it was Orange/Seminole (National Campus), Broward (Midtown Athletic Club), and Brevard (Fee Ave Courts) finishing off undefeated efforts in flight play to advance to Monday’s semifinal round. None of the three squads were challenge, as Orange/Seminole won Flight 1 with a 3-0 record behind eight individual matchup wins while Broward and Brevard went 4-0 in Flight 2 and 3, respectively, thanks to coming away victorious in 10 individual matchups each. Joining those three in the semifinals is SMD (Doral Park Country Club), which claimed the wild card slot with a second-place showing in Flight 1. Monday’s matchups have pitted Orange/Seminole against Brevard and Broward versus SMD.

Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) and SMD (Coral Gables/Kerdyk Biltmore Tennis Center) could be on a collision course in the women’s 7.5 competition. The two squads were nearly flawless throughout Saturday and Sunday, with Orange/Seminole losing just one set in three duals in Flight 1 and SMD not even having to play a third match after dropping one set and a mere 39 games through their first two. Also surviving to qualify for the final four despite one loss were Hillsborough (Harbour Island Athletic Club) from Flight 2 and a second Orange/Seminole team representing the National Campus out of Flight 3. Hillsborough will be across the net from Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) while the National Campus squad faces off with SMD.

While there is not officially a championship dual for the women’s 9.5 trophy, there will be a de facto final when Hillsborough (Waterchase) and SMD (Coral Gables/Salvadore Park) clash on Monday. With both teams sitting atop the lone flight at 3-0, the winner will most likely be decided by that match, as all other squads have at least two losses.
