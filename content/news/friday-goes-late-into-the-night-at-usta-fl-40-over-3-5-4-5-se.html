Friday Goes Late into the Night

After several weather delays, the opening day of the 2018 USTA Florida 3.5/4.5+ Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus, completed play just shy of 10:00 p.m. All teams contested two matches on Friday to kick off the weekend.

Three of the flights in the women’s 3.5 division saw a pair of squads post 2-0 records on the day. Orange/Seminole (Red Bug) and Duval (Boone Park) were the most dominant, both going a perfect 10-for-10 in individual matchups in Flight 1. Orange/Seminole did not surrender a set while Duval dropped only one. Hillsborough (Fishhawk Ranch) and Marion (Country Club of Ocala) faced more of a challenge in Flight 2, but still managed to finish Friday undefeated, claiming eight and seven courts, respectively. Volusia (De Land) and Broward (Midtown) will clash on Saturday with the top spot in Flight 4 on the line, while SMD (Dante Fascell Park) is the lone unbeaten remaining in Flight 3.

For the men’s 3.5 competition, Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) claimed the inside track in Flight 1 behind seven court wins on Friday. Broward (Weston) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) both went 2-0 in Flight 2, with Broward accomplishing the feat much more easily thanks to nine individual victories and only four sets lost. In the three-team Flight 3, Alachua (Westside Park) defeated both Lee/Charlotte (Cypress Lake) and Hillsborough (Temple Terrace), meaning they only need to take down Lee/Charlotte again on Saturday to take the flight.

The two women’s 4.5+ flights feature very different stories. In Flight 1, Duval (Independent) and Sarasota (Longboat Key Tennis Center) sit at 2-0 while Broward (City of Oakland Park) split their two duals. Despite suffering one loss, Broward gave away fewer sets and games than the two undefeated teams ahead of them. Pinellas (Tarpon) grabbed ahold of Flight 2 thanks to a pair of team wins on Friday, leaving them as the lone squad to go unscathed. South Palm (Jupiter CC), Orange/Seminole (Sanlando) and Brevard (Rockledge) bring 1-1 records into the second day of play.

Orange/Seminole (OTC) and SMD (Kendalltown) will enter Saturday just one dual victory shy of moving into the men’s 4.5+ championship match. Orange/Seminole prevailed on nine of the 10 courts they contested in Flight 1 on day one on the strength of dropping just five sets and 55 games. SMD was the only team in Flight 2 to claim more than five individual matchups, finishing with seven.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
