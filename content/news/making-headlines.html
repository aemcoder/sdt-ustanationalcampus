USTA's brand new National Campus in Orlando has been receiving media attention from across the country. The facility has earned rave reviews from numerous outlets, and the publicity keeps coming.

Along with appearing on The Tennis Channel, Telemundo, Univision and networks in Florida, here are just some of the highlights from November and December:

Orlando Business Journal - Tennis PowerShares Series en route to Orlando as USTA National Campus plans debut

Tennis World Magazine - The new magnificent tennis center in Orlando to be opened in three months

WTA - Watch Christina McHale's Exclusive Tour of New USTA Orlando Facility

Nonahood News - USTA is Opening its National Campus on January 2nd and Invites the Community Along to Play and Celebrate

WFTV - 64-acre, 100-court USTA facility to open in Orlando Jan. 2

Orlando Sentinel - National tennis campus to open Jan. 2

The National Campus also has received headlines from even more top publications since its announcement:

USA Today - USTA to build new home for American tennis (2014)

Reuters - Tennis megaplex in central Florida to help sport and region grow (2014)

Tennis Magazine - USTA National Campus in Lake Nona looks to promote tennis, produce champions (2015)

Orlando Sentinel - USTA's campus set to anchor Lake Nona sports district (2015)

El Sentinel - Las futuras estrellas del "deporte blanco" entrenarán en Orlando

Orlando Sentinel - USTA's Lake Nona campus sports 'smart courts,' Italian clay (2016)

AP/FOX Sports - The Latest: USTA National Campus in Florida opens in January (2016)

Nonavibe - USTA National Campus will Launch the Next Generation of Tennis Champions (2016)

Keep checking USTANationalCampus.com for even more coverage of the Home of American Tennis.
