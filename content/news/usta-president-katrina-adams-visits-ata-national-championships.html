USTA President, Katrina Adams, visits the USTA National Campus during the American Tennis Association's 101st National Championships. This historic organization produced such tennis legends as Althea Gibson, Arthur Ashe, and Katrina herself was also a previous ATA national champion.

The event has been called a, "tennis family reunion," as it brings together old friends, family members and teammates from across the country to compete in 30 competitive divisions. 

Follow the USTA's inclusive initiatives with #USTAEmbraceAll on USTA social media channels.
