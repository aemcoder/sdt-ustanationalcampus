Wrapping up the National Campus tournament schedule for the month of August, the 2017 USTA National Doubles Championships gets underway Thursday, August 24. The tournament runs through Saturday, August 26.

Duos from the Boys’ and Girls’ 14s and 16s age divisions will compete in full compass draws, each featuring a total of 32 tandems. Every doubles team in the tournament is guaranteed a minimum of four matches.

The first of its kind, the 5th Annual USTA National Doubles Championships is the only junior competition devoted solely to doubles. Along with Level 1 National Ranking Points, the USTA’s prestigious Gold, Silver and Bronze Balls will be awarded to each draw’s top finishers.

Players with local ties in the event include Jack Anthrop (boys’ 14s), Mary Brumfield (girls’ 16s), Charlie Cowan (boys’ 14s), Paige Herremans (girls 14s), Victoria-Jacynthe Oppel (girls 16s), Devon Stringer (boys’ 14s), Compassion Tsai, and Zak Vince (boys 14s).
