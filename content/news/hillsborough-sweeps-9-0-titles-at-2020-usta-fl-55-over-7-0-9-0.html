Hillsborough Sweeps 9.0 Titles

October 25, 2020

The second day of matches at the 2020 USTA Florida 55 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships was highlighted by the dominance of Hillsborough in the 9.0 divisions along with the women’s 7.0 competition being trimmed down to the final two.

For the 9.0 men, Hillsborough (Palma Ceia – Tampa) posted a sterling 3-0 mark on the strength of eight court victories; perhaps even more impressive, duos representing the team dropped a mere three sets over the course of the weekend while winning a total of 17.

Captain Scott Bartos’ full Hillsborough roster includes Chris Canfield, Ignacio Molina, Michael Erbe, Jonathan Lurie, Steven Brady, Richard Sobieray, Marc Mazo, Michael Rothberg, Robert Graham, Egan Adams, and Gary Downing.

Not to be outdone by her husband, Chris Bartos captained her Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) crew to four dual wins to take home the trophy. They actually ended up with as many court victories as runner-up Orange/Seminole (Heathrow Country Club), but won the head-to-head meeting between the two. Making up their winning squad along with Bartos is Denise Agnew, Kathleen Stein, Karen Olson, Susan Williams, Brenda Wiser, Sharon Benson, Mary Turnes, Allegra Campos, and Stephanie Gilcrease.

With one round of flighted duals left on the 7.0 men's schedule, five of the six squads are still in the running to take home the crown. Collier (Arthur Allen Tennis Center at Cambier Park – Naples) began the day as the lone unbeaten at 2-0, but suffered a pair of setbacks to slip to fourth in the standings. Broward (Weston Tennis Center) and Hillsborough (Temple Terrace Recreation Department) also boast a record of 2-2, while division leader Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA – Jacksonville) can clinch the title by besting fifth-place Broward.

Flight play wrapped up Sunday morning in the women’s 7.0 division, as four teams advanced to the semifinal round. Both Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) in Flight 1 and Duval (Magnolia Point Golf & Country Club – Green Cove Springs) from Flight 3 swept through the field by winning eight courts and 16 sets in total. It was a much different story in Flight 2, where IRMSL (The Legacy Golf & Tennis – Port Saint Lucie) lost to Hillsborough (Buckhorn Springs Tennis and Swim Club – Valrico), which left the flight in a three-way tie for first. Ultimately, the tiebreaking procedure would result in Collier (Lighthouse Bay – Bonita Springs) moving on.

The afternoon’s semifinals pitted Orange/Seminole against Duval while Collier faced wild card entrant Escambia (Independent), the second-place team in Flight 1. Orange/Seminole managed to outlast Duval when Susan Uhrig and Cynthia Rawlins rallied back to take line two 4-6, 7-5, 1-0; teammates Terri Bahr and Dorothy Harris dominated 6-2, 6-1 on court three. It was a bit of an easier road to the final for Escambia, as they prevailed at all three positions. Mecy Wimberly and Kristyn Miller claimed the top spot 6-4, 6-2 to go along with Vicki Carson and Terry Armstrong coming through 6-4, 7-5 at line three. The sweep was finished off by Kelly Dempsey and Karen Penn fighting back 5-7, 6-3, 1-0 at the second position.

The remaining men’s 7.0 matches along with the women’s 7.0 championship dual are scheduled for an 8 a.m. start Monday morning.
