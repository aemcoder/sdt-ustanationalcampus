Three Divisions in Action

January 8, 2021

Three of the four divisions took to the courts for day one of the USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 and Mixed 55 & Over 7.0 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus.

Squads are competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

With just one round remaining in the 18 & Over 6.0 division, the title will go to the winner between Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) and Alachua (Haile Plantation Country Club – Gainesville) after the two crews posted 2-0 records on Friday. Broward did so in commanding fashion, claiming five court victories while surrendering just three sets; Alachua has four individual matchups to its credit with five sets lost.

In the 18 & Over 8.0 competition, Okaloosa/Walton/Bay (Shalimar Pointe Country Club) seized firm control of Flight 2 while Flight 1 looks to be decided when undefeated teams Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville) and Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) clash Saturday. Okaloosa/Walton/Bay was the lone squad to win both duals on the opening day of play, while three others went 1-1 to stay in the running. Also remaining mathematically alive is Sarasota from Flight 1, although they will need a lot of help to vault past the two unbeatens in the flight.

Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) also had a good day in 55 & Over play, as they ended Friday at 2-0 in the 7.0 division along with Escambia (Independent). Two more rounds are left to decide the eventual champion in the division, where 1-1 Brevard (Kiwanis Island Park Recreation Center – Merritt Island) is also looking to make a move or at least step in as a spoiler.
