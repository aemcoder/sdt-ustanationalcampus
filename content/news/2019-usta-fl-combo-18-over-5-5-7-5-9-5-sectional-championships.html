2019 USTA FL Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships

Last Three Days of Sunshine State Play

December 11, 2019

style
article-hero

The 2019 USTA Florida Sectional Championships calendar concludes this weekend with the Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 titles on the line. Play gets underway Saturday, Dec. 14 and runs through Monday, Dec. 16.

All dual matches for the event will feature three courts of doubles, with a squad needing to win at least two of those contests for the overall team victory.

Just two crews are making their way to the National Campus to compete for the men’s 5.5 crown – Leon (Tom Brown Park – Tallahassee) and Alachua (Westside Park – Gainesville). The two will square off four times over the course of Saturday and Sunday. Meanwhile, the women’s 5.5 division is composed of seven teams split into two flights, three in Flight 1 and four in Flight 2. Only the pair of flight winners will advance to Monday’s final.

For the 7.5 men, the three flights are set to send their top finisher on to the semifinals while the best second-place squad across all flights will earn the wild card berth. The semifinals will pit the representative from Flight 1 taking on the Flight 3 winner while the Flight 2 champion has drawn the wild card team.

In the largest division of the weekend, 19 different women’s 7.5 crews have been divided into four flights, with only the first-place finishers booking a spot in the semifinals. For those matchups, it will be Flight 1 versus Flight 2 and Flight 3 across the net from Flight 4.

The 9.5 men’s and women’s competitions are each made up of just one flight, with four men’s and three women’s clubs in the running; both champions will be determined by Sunday afternoon.

Last year’s sectional champions at the event included the Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) men and Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) women for the 5.5 division, while Orange/Seminole swept the 7.5 competitions thanks to the men from the USTA National Campus and the women representing Red Bug Lake Park (Casselberry). For the 9.5 division, titles went to the SMD (Doral Park Country Club – Doral) men and Hillsborough (Waterchase – Tampa) women.
