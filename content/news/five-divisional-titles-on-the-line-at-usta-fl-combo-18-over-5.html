USTA FL Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships

Five Divisional Titles on the Line

February 26, 2021

style
article-hero

Another USTA Florida sectional event will help close out the month of February at the USTA National Campus, as the 5.5, 7.5, and 9.5 competitions of the Combo 18 & Over division are slated to be in action Saturday, Feb. 27-Monday, March 1. Two men’s and three women’s titles will be on the line over the course of the tournament.

Teams will be competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Both of the men’s divisions will wrap on Sunday afternoon. For the eight 7.5 squads, they have been equally divided into two flights, with the round-robin matches being played out over the course of Saturday and into Sunday morning. The championship dual will take to the courts Sunday at 1:30 p.m. Just four crews have entered the 9.5 competition, meaning the team with the best overall record after three rounds will be crowned the champion.

The women’s 9.5 winner will also be determined on Sunday, with a group of five squads battling it out in a single flight. Meanwhile, the 5.5 and 7.5 divisions will put their finals on court Monday morning following two days of flighted duals. For the 5.5 competition, seven teams were split into two flights. On the 7.5 side, the busiest of all the divisions, 12 crews make up the three flights; the top finisher in each flight along with the runner-up owning the best record will advance to Sunday’s semifinal stage.

Divisional defending champions include the Hillsborough (Westchase Community Association – Tampa) 5.5 women, the Alachua (Westside Park – Gainesville) 7.5 women, the Broward (Weston Hills Country Club) 9.5 women, the Broward (Dillon Tennis Center –Oakland Park) 7.5 men, and the Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) 9.5 men.
