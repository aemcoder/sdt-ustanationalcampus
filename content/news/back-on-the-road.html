Orlando Residents Win Titles

at Home and Abroad

September 28, 2017

style
article-hero

The $100,000 ITF Pro Circuit women’s event in Tampico, Mexico, was dominated by Orlando residents this past week, as three of the four semifinalists train at the USTA National Campus. Irina Falconi took home the title after upsetting top-seeded Jennifer Brady in the semifinals. Falconi defeated fellow Orlando resident Louisa Chirico (pictured above) in a three-set final by a score of 7-5, 6-7(3), 6-1.

On American soil, Vicky Duval won the doubles title at the $25,000 USTA Pro Circuit event in Lubbock, Texas, with doubles partner Alisa Kleybanova. Duval reached the singles final as well, only to fall to partner Kleybanova in straight sets.
