2019 USTA FL 40 & Over 3.5/4.5+ Sectional Championships

Match Play Starts Saturday Morning

July 24, 2019

style
article-hero

The 2019 USTA Florida 40 & Over 3.5/4.5+ Sectional Championships will wrap up the July calendar of events at the USTA National Campus. Play is slated to get underway at 7:30 a.m. on Saturday, July 27 and run through the afternoon of Monday, July 29.

Each dual match consists of two lines of singles and three lines of doubles, meaning a team needs to capture at least three of those matchups to secure the overall victory.

For the men’s 3.5 competition, a total of 13 squads have been placed in three separate flights, with Flights 1 and 2 containing four teams and Flight 3 consisting of five. The trio of flight winners will be joined by a wild card entrant, the top runner-up from all of the flights, in Monday’s semifinal stage.

The largest division on the weekend is the women’s 3.5, where 18 teams will battle for the sectional title. Flights 1 and 2 are made up of four teams; Flights 3 and 4 have five teams each. Only the four flight winners will move on to the knockout stage, with the semifinal round set for Monday morning.

Nine squads make up the two men’s 4.5+ flights – four in Flight 1 and five in Flight 2. The pair of flight winners will move on to the divisional championship dual at 8:30 a.m. Monday morning. On the women’s 4.5+ side, 13 teams begin the weekend in contention. Flights 1 and 2 have four squads to go along with the remaining five in Flight 3. Just as with the women’s 3.5 division, Monday’s semifinals will feature the three flight winners along with a wild card team.

Collecting divisional titles one year ago at the Sectional Championships were the Broward (Weston Tennis Center) 3.5 men and Broward (Midtown Athletic Club - Weston) 3.5 women along with the Orange/Seminole (Orlando Tennis Center) 4.5+ men and the Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club – Tarpon Springs) 4.5+ women.
