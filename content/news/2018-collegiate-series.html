Collegiate Series at the Campus

Brian Ormiston | January 11, 2018

style
article-hero

The USTA National Campus showcases college tennis like never before in 2018, hosting approximately 300 colleges and universities in College MatchDay events as well as the new USTA National Campus Collegiate Series.

The inaugural USTA National Campus Collegiate Series opened Jan. 13-15 with dual matches between BYU, UNC Wilmington and Florida Gulf Coast.

See below for the 2018 USTA National Campus Collegiate Series results:

2018 USTA National Campus Collegiate Series
