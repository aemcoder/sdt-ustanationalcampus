Saturday's Matches in the Books

Saturday marked the opening day of play at the 2019 USTA Florida 40 & Over 3.5/4.5+ Sectional Championships. While no flights have been determined yet, several squads moved into position to advance.

In the men’s 3.5 division, two teams are one dual victory away from securing a place in the semifinals while another needs to pick up two wins on Sunday. Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) took control of Flight 1 as Polk (Beerman Family Tennis Center – Lakeland) did the same in Flight 2 with a 2-0 record on day one; both squads have just one round robin match remaining, meaning a win will advance them to Monday’s semifinals. Flight 3 saw Broward (Weston Tennis Center) also go 2-0, but with the flight consisting of five teams, they must win twice on Sunday to ensure a semifinal berth. Every team winning at least once on the opening day stayed alive for the wild card slot into the divisional semifinals.

On the 3.5 women’s side, Broward (Weston Tennis Center) and Pinellas (City of Clearwater) took firm control of Flight 1 and 3, respectively. The two teams not only posted 2-0 records overall, but reeled in seven individual matchups on day one, with their closest competition sitting at 1-1 with just five court victories. Flight 2 will go to the squad prevailing from the head-to-head matchup between Collier (Treviso Bay – Naples) and Duval (Julington Creek Plantation – Jacksonville). Flight 4 has been whittled down to either Alachua (DB Racquet Club – Gainesville) or Escambia (Independent).

Taking the inside track on moving on the men’s 4.5+ championship round were Duval (San Jose Country Club – Jacksonville) and SMD (Next Level Tennis Ktown – Miami). Duval sits atop Flight 1 thanks to two dual wins and seven individual matchups to their credit while SMD has dominated Flight 2 on the strength of nine court victories and just two sets dropped. The divisional final is scheduled for Sunday at 2:30 p.m.

Escambia, going 9-for-10 in courts contested, has all but locked up their spot in the women’s 4.5+ semifinals; they only need to avoid being swept by Collier in Flight 1 action Sunday. Flight 2 will be taken by either Sarasota (Longboat Key Tennis Center) or Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center – Plantation) after both came through with 2-0 efforts on the opening day of the tournament. Pinellas moved to the top of Flight 3 with a pair of impressive wins Saturday, but have three squads right behind them at 1-1 with two rounds of play remaining. Teams not winning one of the three flights are battling to be the wild card entrant into Monday’s semifinals.
