Fan Guide to MatchDay on Feb. 24

Free tickets available on Ticketmaster.com.

Brian Ormiston and Christina Aguis | February 20, 2018

style
article-hero

A Top 25 showdown between the Oklahoma and Texas A&M men’s tennis teams will highlight the next College MatchDay at the USTA National Campus on Saturday, Feb. 24, at 10:30 a.m. ET.

Before making your way to Lake Nona, here is everything you need to know about attending the match:

Matchup

-Oklahoma vs. Texas A&M (men)

Date and Time

-Saturday, Feb. 24

-Gates open: 8:30 a.m.

-First serve: 10:30 a.m.

 

Location

-USTA National Campus (Collegiate Complex)

-Address: 10000 USTA Blvd., Orlando, FL 32827

-Phone: 844-872-8667

Tickets

-Admission is free.

-Reserve complimentary tickets by visiting Ticketmaster.com and searching College MatchDay.

-Or click here: 2018 College MatchDay Complimentary Tickets.

Parking

-Parking is free and available on a first-come, first-serve basis.

RV Parking

-Fans with RVs are directed to park in the back row of the main parking lot.

Bus Parking

-The bus lane is located near the front of the main parking lot.

Pre-match Festivities

-Starting at 8:30 a.m., fans can experience a live DJ, tailgating games, face painting and much more, all for free.

-A free interactive kids’ clinic led by USTA teaching professionals will begin at 9 a.m.

-Giveaways will be distributed throughout the day.

-Event programs and merchandise from Fromuth Tennis will be available.

-Food and refreshments will be available for purchase.

Match Format

-Doubles will open the match. The first team to win two of the three matches earns one overall team point. Matches are one six-game set.

-Following doubles, six singles matches will take place. Each match is worth one team point.

-The first school to secure four team points wins the match.

Court Assignments

-The national anthem and team introductions will take place on Court 1 (the side facing the parking lot).

-The match will be held on the south side of the Collegiate Complex, Courts 1-6.

Match Preview

The Oklahoma (7-1) and Texas A&M (7-2) men’s tennis teams will clash at College MatchDay this Saturday, Feb. 24, at 10:30 a.m. at the USTA National Campus, with gates opening at 8:30 a.m.

The last time Oklahoma and Texas A&M met was in 2015, when Texas A&M defeated Oklahoma, 4-3, at the George P. Mitchell Tennis Center in Texas. It was the first time in the program’s history that Texas A&M defeated a No. 1-ranked team in the country.

Fans and kids can enjoy games, giveaways, face painting and more. A kids’ clinic will take place at 9 a.m.

Tickets are complimentary and available on site but fans are encouraged to reserve their tickets at Ticketmaster.com.

For those who can’t make it, College MatchDay will be aired live on Tennis Channel.
