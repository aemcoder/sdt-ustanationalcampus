Four Players Eye Vero Beach Entry Thursday

April 11, 2018

style
article-hero

After Tuesday’s washout, both the round of 16 and quarterfinals were contested on Wednesday at the Mardy Fish USTA Futures Lake Nona Wild Card event, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Only four players remain heading into the final day of action.

This is the second of two wild card singles tournaments for the Mardy Fish Children’s Foundation Tennis Championships, a $15,000 USTA Pro Circuit event played at the Grand Harbor Golf & Beach Club in Vero Beach, Fla., at the end of the month.

No. 1 seed Trent Bryde (Suwanne, Ga.) made quick work of his two opponents on the day, posting a 6-0, 6-0 sweep in the round of 16 before breezing past seeded opponent Vinicius Nogueira (Teresina, Brazil) 6-0, 6-1 to reach the semifinals. Second seed Karl Poling (West Point, N.Y.) also had little trouble advancing to Thursday, losing a combined five games in the four sets he played.

Third-seeded Axel Nefve (Boca Raton, Fla.) was also able to secure a sweep in his first match on Wednesday. He was slightly pushed by unseeded Lawrence Sciglitano (Ojai, Calif.) in the quarterfinals, but managed to prevail 6-1, 6-3.

The fourth semifinalist is Quinn Snyder (Delran, N.J.), who had a bit of luck on his side in both of his matchups. The unseeded player drew No. 4 seed Robert Hammond (Highland Beach, Fla.), in which Hammond was forced to retire with Snyder holding a 6-5 lead in the first set. That was followed by his quarterfinal opponent, Roberto Livi (Long Island City, N.Y.) withdrawing before the start of their match.

Looking ahead to Thursday, both semifinals will take to the courts at 1:00 p.m. with the championship match slated for 3:00 p.m.
