USTA FL Mixed 18 & Over and Mixed 55 & Over Sectional Championships

Kicking Off 2021

January 7, 2021

style
article-hero

The 2021 USTA National Campus events calendar is set to get underway this weekend with the USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 and Mixed 55 & Over 7.0 Sectional Championships. First serve is set for the morning of Friday, Jan. 8 with all four team champions to be crowned by Sunday, Jan. 10.

Squads will be competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Three of the four divisions will get underway on the opening day of the event, including the 18 & Over 6.0 and the 55 & Over 7.0 competitions. Both feature one lone flight, with four teams vying for the 6.0 title while the 7.0 field features five squads in total.

The 18 & Over 8.0 division, which is the only one that will take to the courts all three days, kicks off its round-robin play Friday as well. Ten crews have entered and been equally split into two flights of five, with the pair of flight winners advancing to Sunday’s final.

With only Brevard and Alachua competing, the Mixed 18 7 over 10.0 competition will see those two squads squaring off in a best-of-three format over the course of Sunday.

Divisional reigning champions include the 18 & Over Brevard (Viera Regional Park) 6.0, Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) 8.0, and Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) 10.0 clubs along with the 55 & Over 7.0 team representing Collier (Greater Marco Family YMCA – Marco Island).

First matches are slated for a 7:30 a.m. start Friday morning. For more information on the Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 divisions, click here; details from the Mixed 55 & Over 7.0 division can be viewed here.
