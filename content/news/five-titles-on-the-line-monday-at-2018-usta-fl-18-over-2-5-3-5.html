Five Titles on the Line Monday

August 19, 2018

At the conclusion of Sunday’s play in the 2018 USTA Florida 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships, the 2.5 women are down to the championship round while the four other divisions are at the semifinal stage going into Monday’s schedule.

The women’s 2.5 championship dual will pit Orange/Seminole (Isleworth) against Duval (Deerwood). Duval had already locked up their spot before the day started, but still capped off a 3-0 effort thanks to their third sweep on the weekend. In those three matches, Duval only dropped one set and 45 games. Orange/Seminole did stumble in their last dual on the weekend against Hillsborough (Arbor Greene), but still comfortably took Flight 1 as the only team with a winning record.

Two of the 3.5 women’s flights were claimed by teams who went undefeated while the other two came down to the tiebreaking procedure. Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) had little trouble navigating through Flight 1, defeating the three other squads on the strength of 11 court wins. In Flight 3, Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) put together a comprehensive 4-0 effort that included going a perfect 20-for-20 in individual matchups without giving away a set. Meanwhile, both Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) along with Volusia (Florida Tennis Center) did not end the weekend unscathed but still managed to advance. Hillsborough edged out Brevard (Titan Tennis Center) by a single court victory (10 versus nine) in Flight 2; Volusia moved on thanks to a similar circumstance in Flight 4, collecting 14 individual wins compared to 13 for Alachua (Westside Park) despite losing one more set. Monday’s matchups will be Broward taking on Hillsborough and Orange/Seminole clashing with Volusia.

In the men’s 3.5 division, the four flight winners posted 3-0 team records to advance to the semifinal round. Orange/Seminole (National Campus) was the most dominant, claiming 13 individual courts of the 15 they contested in Flight 1 while losing just five sets. Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) and Duval (Eagle Harbor) finished atop Flight 2 and 3, respectively, after winning 10 individual matchups. Meanwhile, Collier (Longshore Lake) fought their way through Flight 4 behind nine court victories. The semifinal pairings will see Orange/Seminole clash with Marion and Duval across the net from Collier.

Broward (Weston Hills) and Hillsborough (Cheval Athletic Club) fought for the automatic berth into the women’s 4.5 semifinals out of Flight 1 in one of the two duals that finished indoors Sunday evening. Hillsborough had prevailed when the teams met on the opening day of the tournament by a slim 3-2 margin; however, with their backs against the wall, Broward came through with the 3-2 triumph of their own to clinch a place in the semifinals. The disappointment didn’t last long for Hillsborough, as they found out moments later they had earned the semifinal wild card. Broward’s opponent Monday will be Duval (Glen Kernan) after they breezed through Flight 3 with 13 court wins. Hillsborough will face Flight 2 representative Orange/Seminole (Sanlando), who just edged out Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club).

A big day for the squad from Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex) highlighted the men’s 4.5 action. After toppling Flight 3 leader Escambia, they still needed to take care of Orange/Seminole (Lake Cain) in a match that was completed indoors to lock up the flight. They did so in commanding fashion, recording a sweep to give them momentum heading into Monday, where they will square off with unblemished Flight 1 champion Duval (Independent). Also reaching the semifinal round was Broward (City of Fort Lauderdale) after they cruised past the field in Flight 2. Broward took 13 of the 15 courts they contested in three duals. Wild card entrant Escambia, moving through thanks to owning the best game winning percentage of the flight runners-up, rounds out the semifinal squads.

Play begins at 7:30 a.m. with all semifinals. The women’s 2.5 championship will get underway at 8:00 a.m. For more information on the 2018 USTA Florida 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships, click here.
