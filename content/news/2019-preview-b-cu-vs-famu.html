Bethune-Cookman and Florida A&M renew rivalry at 6 p.m.

Brian Ormiston | April 2, 2019

style
article-hero

The final USTA National Campus Collegiate Series event of 2019 is set for Thursday, April 4, when the men and women of Bethune-Cookman and Florida A&M collide at 6 p.m.

Live Stats (Women)

Live Stats (Men) 

These two programs have one of the most historical rivalries in college sports. Dating back to 1925, their football programs and bands have put on a show to highlight these two historically black colleges and universities (HBCUs), and the rivalry has since grown to all sports.

Both the Wildcats and Rattlers ventured to the Campus in September 2018 for the HBCU Championships. Bethune-Cookman captured the men’s title, with Florida A&M finishing fifth. On the women’s side, FAMU placed third and B-CU completed its tournament in sixth.

Last year, FAMU defeated B-CU in both the men’s and women’s matches.

Admission and parking are free for all Collegiate Series matches.
