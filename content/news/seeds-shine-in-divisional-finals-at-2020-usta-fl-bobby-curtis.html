2020 USTA FL "Bobby Curtis" 16s/18s Junior Singles Championships

Four Divisional Winners Crowned

October 19, 2020

style
article-hero

Monday brought the divisional finals to the courts as the 2020 USTA Florida “Bobby Curtis” 16s/18s Junior Singles Championships concluded at the USTA National Campus. All four champions entered the event as a top eight seed, with the two boys’ finals ending in straight sets while both girls’ finals were decided in a match tiebreak.

Walking away with the coveted sectional crowns were fifth-seeded Sami Ozzor (Daytona Beach) from the boys’ 18s, No. 2 Justin Lyons (Pensacola) in the boys’ 16s, No. 4 Natalie Block (Plantation) out of the girls’ 18s, and second seed Gabriela Rondon (Hallandale Beach) in the girls’ 16s.

Boys’ 18s: No. 5 Sami Ozzor def. No. 8 Justin Braverman 6-1, 6-3

Boys’ 16s: No. 2 Justin Lyons def. Leonardo Dal Boni 6-4, 6-1

Girls’ 18s: No. 4 Natalie Block def. No. 2 Ahmani Guichard 5-7, 6-1, 1-0

Girls’ 16s: No. 2 Gabriela Rondon def. Taly Licht 4-6, 6-3, 1-0

Other than his quarterfinal victory in which he squandered a lead and had to regroup in the match tiebreak, Ozzor breezed through the competition. In Monday’s final he was up against No. 8 Braverman (Naples), who was fresh off taking out top seed Adam Duan (Davie) in straight sets. Ozzor turned in perhaps his most solid performance of the event, sprinting through the opening frame 6-1 and then hanging on to close out the second by a 6-3 margin. Duan managed to rebound from his semifinal disappointment to claim third with a 7-6, 6-2 win over Santiago Cardenas (Tampa) while Zak Vince (Clermont) finished fifth.

Lyons was even more impressive en route to the boys’ 16s crown, never dropping a set and only being pushed to 6-4 twice over his five matches. Across the net from him Monday morning was unseeded hopeful Dal Boni (Sarasota), who had already bested two seeded players to earn his spot in the championship contest. A back-and-forth first set went the way of Lyons before he took full control to cross the finish line with a 6-4, 6-1 triumph. No. 4 seed Felipe Pinzon (Sunrise) claimed third overall when he received a walkover from Chase Healey (Saint Augustine) and top-seeded Emilio Sanchez (Naples) placed fifth in the division.

The most intriguing matchup of the day featured Block, who fought back to stun No. 1 seed Valeria Ray (Doral) in a match tiebreak at the semifinal stage on Sunday, and Guichard (Seminole), riding an impressive 15-match winning streak in which she had not surrendered a single set. An extended opening frame could have gone either way, with Guichard eventually prevailing seven games to five. Block quickly put the that behind her, raising her level of play to send the final to a decisive match tiebreak; from there, Block hung onto the momentum to close out the victory. 

A very accomplished junior player, some of Block’s best results have happened at the National Campus, as she adds this prestigious sectional singles trophy to the two USTA Gold Ball victories she was a part of in girls' 16s doubles competition. Additionally, this marks Block's second "Bobby Curtis" singles crown in three years after she was the 14s champion in 2018.

In other girls’ 18s results Monday, Ray finished third thanks to a walkover from fifth seed Sophia Wang (Saint Petersburg) while No. 3 seed Anushka Khune (Plantation) won consecutive matches in three sets for a fifth-place showing.

The girls’ 16s final was seemingly a mismatch on paper. Second-seeded Rondon, having lost a mere 15 games in her four matches, faced off with unseeded Licht (Hollywood); however, Licht had dismissed two seeds already, including toppling No. 1 Valeria Centeno (Hialeah) in a match tiebreak. Licht proved she belonged right away, battling her way to a one-set advantage. Tense moments throughout the second frame brought out the best in Rondon, and once she pushed the tilt to a match tiebreak, it seemed inevitable that she would be the one to raise the champion’s trophy. Fifth-seeded Rose Seccia (Bradenton) was the third-place finisher in the draw over Francesca Salvato and No. 6 Madison Smith (Boynton Beach) ended up fifth.

Winners of the divisional consolation draws included Jonathan Irwanto (Miami Shores) in the boys’ 18s and Daniel Cowan Jr. (Boca Raton) in the boys’ 16s. Both girls’ consolation finals were abandoned after being halted mid-match due to rain.
