Five Divisions in Action

April 16, 2021

USTA Florida league play will make its return to the USTA National Campus this weekend after a brief hiatus, with five divisional titles up for grabs at the 65 & Over Sectional Championships. Matches are slated to begin Saturday, April 17, and run through Monday, April 19.

The divisions that will be in action this weekend are the 6.0 women, the 7.0 women, the 7.0 men, the 8.0 women, and the 8.0 men.

Only three squads have entered the women’s 6.0 competition – Duval, Leon, and Marion. That trio will battle it out in a double round-robin format over the course of Saturday and Sunday, with the sectional crown being awarded to the crew finishing with the best overall record.

For the 7.0 women, eight teams have been equally divided into two flights. Their round-robin portion will take place all day Saturday and into Sunday morning, with the winner of each flight going head-to-head Sunday afternoon in the divisional final.

Five regions – Collier, Duval, Hillsborough, Marion, and Pinellas – will be represented in the men’s 7.0 competition on the weekend. Four rounds of flight play will make up the entirety of play for this division, as the team champion will be crowned Sunday afternoon.

Collier, Escambia, Hillsborough, Marion, Pinellas, and Sarasota are the six squads in the women’s 8.0 competition, which will consist of five rounds of flighted duals. This is the lone flight that will take place over all three days of the event, with the last round of contests slated for Monday morning.

The 8.0 men’s schedule mirrors that of the 7.0 women, where eight crews begin the weekend in a four-team flight with the two flight champions advancing on to the divisional championship matchup.

First matches are set for a 7:30 a.m. start Saturday morning. For more information on the USTA Florida Adult 65 & Over Sectional Championships, click here.
