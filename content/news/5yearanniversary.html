CELEBRATING 5 YEARS OF THE NATIONAL CAMPUS

February 1, 2022

style
article-hero

THANK YOU for being a loyal patron of the USTA National Campus.  

We opened our doors back in January 2017 and have moved ahead steadily since. A game-changer for the sport of tennis and the USTA, the USTA National Campus has become the center of all things tennis in the U.S. with more than 9,000 people per week visiting the Campus.  

To celebrate our amazing five years, we want to let you know that we will be rolling out specials on the fifth of each month throughout 2022 to celebrate our five years as a proud part of the Orlando community.

The return of the popular “College MatchDay” event on Feb. 5th pitting two of the biggest rivals in collegiate sports, the University of Florida Gators against the Florida State Seminoles, will kick off our “Five” themes.  Come join us on the courts before the match as all racquet sport court reservations will be just five dollars per hour *.  After working up an appetite on court, stop by the Net Post Grill for some $5 food specials too.

Stay tuned for more specials and announcements throughout the year as we celebrate YOU for being a huge part of our success of these last five years. And again, we thank you for being a loyal customer–and we hope to see you on the courts real soon.

-The USTA National Campus Staff

*To reserve a court for $5/hour please call the front desk at: (407)-675-2500 x2 or reserve a court online and pricing will be adjusted upon arrival
