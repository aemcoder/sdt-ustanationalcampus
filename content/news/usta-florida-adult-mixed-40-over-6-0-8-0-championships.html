Day One

The 2017 USTA Florida Adult Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championship began on Friday, June 2 on the Har-Tru courts at the USTA National Campus. Rain prevented the day’s schedule from being completed, with two matches suspended due to weather.

In the 6.0 division, the teams from Volusia and Alachua went 2-0 in their dual matches for the day. Both squads won four of six individual matchups, but Volusia sits just in front of Alachua thanks to surrendering only four sets (to five for Alachua) and 45 total games (to 51 for Alachua). Marion and Leon were 1-1 on Friday while Duval and Polk were 0-2.

The 8.0 division is divided into four flights. Broward has a firm control of Flight 1 after winning both dual matches on day one, losing four sets in the process. SMD and Orange/Seminole hold records of 1-1 and Alachua is currently 0-2. Flight 2 will come down to the clash between Polk and Marion after they both secured a 2-0 opening day. Polk was absolutely dominant, not losing a single court and only giving away two sets. The teams from Collier and Duval struggled, going 0-2.

Hillsborough took control of Flight 3 thanks to winning five of its six individual contests en route to a 2-0 dual match record. Okaloosa and Volusia each ended the day at 1-1, leaving IRMSL at the bottom of the standings at 0-2. In the only flight to not finish up its scheduled matches, Pinellas and Lee/Charlotte sit with 1-0 records. Pinellas looked sharp, giving away a slim total of 12 games in its lone dual match. Sarasota/Manatee and Escambia are 0-1 but remain alive to advance on to the semifinals.

Updated standings and match results can be found here for the 6.0 division and here for the 8.0 division.

Play picks back up at 8:00 am on Saturday morning, with all flights scheduled to wrap up by the end of the day weather permitting.
