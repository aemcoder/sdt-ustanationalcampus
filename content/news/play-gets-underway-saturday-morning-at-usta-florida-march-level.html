Schedule of Play Kicks Off Saturday Morning

March 16, 2018

Action at the USTA Florida March Level 4 Championships, the only junior tournament to be held at the USTA National Campus in the month of March, is slated to kick off on Saturday morning.

All eight age divisions (boys’ and girls’ 12s-18s) compose this weekend’s event. The boys’ 18s draw will be contested on the Team USA hard courts while the remaining seven draws will be played out on the Har-Tru courts.

The top seeds in each respective division include Yubel Ubri (boys’ 12s), Yannik Rahman (boys’ 14s), Chase Martin (boys’ 16s), Matthew Fung (boys’ 18s), Catherine Goldman (girls’ 12s), Jasmin Goldman (girls’ 14s), Kassandra Di Staulo (girls’ 16s), and Casey Accola (girls’ 18s).

Ubri has had a successful start to 2018, reaching the finals of the USTA Florida Level 3 at the HCC Tennis Complex to start the year before earning the title at the USTA Florida Level 5 Seminole County even last month. He last played at the National Campus at the end of October as part of the Bobby Curtis Junior Doubles Championships.

Rahman has already contested six tournaments on the year, most recently advancing to the finals of the Tropical Park Level 7 in the boys’ 16s draw. Additionally, he won the Kendall Tennis Level 7 tournament in February, also playing up in the boys’ 16s division. Rahman took part in the USTA National February Level 2 at the National Campus last month, playing both singles and doubles in the event.

Martin’s 2018 season kicked off with him playing up in the boys’ 18s division in the trio of tournaments he’s competed in. His best result was making the consolation semifinals at the USTA Florida Level 5 Orange County event. Martin found success in the last boys’ 16s draw he entered, taking fourth in the USTA Florida Level 4 at Daytona Beach. He has not appeared at the National Campus since the Bobby Curtis Junior Singles Championships last summer.

Fung has played a light schedule so far this year, entering in only two tournaments prior to this weekend. His most recent action did take place at the National Campus, where he played both singles and doubles in the boys’ 18s division; he was defeated in the opening round by eventual champion Ronald Hohmann. Of note, Fung has lost his last three encounters with Logan Zapp, who is seeded third in this event and has also landed in the top half of the draw.

It’s already been a busy 2018 for Goldman, as this will be her sixth tournament already. She last played at Tropical Park in the CTA Challenger Level 7 in the girls’ 14s, making the consolation final. Goldman has recorded a large number of wins in the girls’ 12s, advancing to the finals of a Level 3 and Level 5 event. This will be her initial appearance at the National Campus.

Makela returns to the National Campus for the first time since reaching the final of the girls’ 14s draw at the USTA Florida Level 3 Doubles Spring Challenge. Included already in her results for 2018 is a run to both the singles and doubles finals at the Delray Beach Open Level 3 in January plus a consolation singles title at the USTA National February Level 2 at Seminole Lake.

The last tournament Di Staulo competed in was held at the National Campus, as she took part in the National Level 2 event in February. She will look to have better results this time around, dropping both of her matches in that tournament. Di Staulo has had recent success otherwise, winning the 16s singles draw at the USTA Florida Daytona Beach Level 4 in December along with reaching the final of the USTA Florida Level 5 Seminole County tournament last month.

Accola has spent a fair amount of time on the courts of the National Campus, including in the girls’ 18s competition as part of the USTA National Winter Championships in December. Her latest tournament action was also at the national level, as she traveled to Texas for the San Antonio National Level 2 presented by John Newcombe Tennis Academy. In that event, she found success in the doubles draw, teaming with Slade Coetzee to take home the title.

Matches are scheduled to begin at 8:00 a.m. Saturday morning. For more information on the USTA Florida March Level 4 Championships, click here.
