2020 ITF W25 Orlando

Six Americans Reach Singles Quarters

November 13, 2020

style
article-hero

American competitors owned the day Friday at the 2020 ITF W25 Orlando event being hosted by the USTA National Campus, making up six of the eight singles quarterfinalists. It also marked the first day of the tournament that all scheduled matches were completed outdoors with no interruptions due to rain.

Qualifiers Hurricane Tyra Black and Alycia Parks continued their strong week of play with victories in straight sets, as Black defeated American wild card Alexandra Yepifanova 6-4, 6-0 while Parks dismissed Argentina’s Paula Ormaechea 6-1, 6-2. Robin Montgomery, who entered the draw via junior exemption, also impressed on Friday with her 7-5, 6-4 win over Tessah Andrianjafitrimo of France.

Hanna Chang scored the only upset over a seed thanks to her 4-6, 6-3, 7-5 comeback versus No. 3 Kristina Kucova (Slovakia), which was also the only match extended to a third set. In a pair of all-American contests, Jamie Loeb bested Grace Min 6-4, 6-3 and Claire Liu got past Catherine Harrison 7-5, 6-1.

The only two non-Americans into the final eight are qualifier Katarina Jokic of Serbia and second-seeded Katarzyna Kawa (Poland). Jokic dropped just four games in beating fellow qualifier Victoria Rodriguez (Mexico) 6-3, 6-1; Kawa toughed out a 7-5, 6-1 result over American wild card Peyton Stearns.

Two rounds of doubles were also contested on Friday, setting up the championship match to be played Saturday afternoon. Emerging from the top half of the draw was the American duo of Rasheeda McAdoo and Parks. They turned away another American pairing – Sophie Chang and Alexandra Mueller – 6-4, 6-2 in the quarterfinals before surviving a marathon 6-3, 6-7(2), 10-7 affair with Colombian Maria Camila Osorio Serrano and Conny Perrin of Switzerland. 

Awaiting McAdoo and Parks in the final will be No. 2 seeds Loeb and Erin Routliffe (New Zealand). Dropping just nine games, Loeb and Routliffe eliminated Emina Bektas (USA) and Tara Moore (Great Britain) 6-3, 6-2 then followed that up with a 6-4, 6-0 effort over Americans Elizabeth Coleman and Kimmi Hance.
