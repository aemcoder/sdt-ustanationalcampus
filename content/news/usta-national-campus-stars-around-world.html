Many U.S. tennis players call the USTA National Campus in Orlando, FL home for non-stop training and practicing. See what places around the world and stateside our resident players have traveled to this past week to compete.

From the National Campus to Around the Globe, See Where the Americans are Playing!

Chris Herman, top wheelchair competitor, travels to Canada to compete in the Vancouver International ITF Futures.

Noah Rubin competes at the BB&T Atlanta Open.

Frances Tiafoe competes for the Washington Kastles in World Team Tennis.

Tommy Paul heads to Atlanta, GA to join fellow #NationalCampus rising stars to compete in the BB&T Atlanta Open.

Resident #NationalCampus star Madison Keys joins Sloane Stephens at the BB&T Atlanta Open.

Sophie Chang competes in Gatineau, Canada as hard court season begins.

Ellie Halbauer returns from Europe to reach the singles semifinals of the ITF Pro Circuit 25K in Gatineau, Canada.

Madison Keys stops for a few photos with fans at the Tennis Hall of Fame in Newport, Rhode Island.
