USTA FL Adult 40 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0 Sectional Championships

Six Divisions in Action

July 16, 2021

style
article-hero

The first of three weekends USTA Florida Adult League play to take place on the clay and hard courts on the grounds of the USTA National Campus is set for July 17-19. Men’s and women’s team titles in the 40 & Over 3.0, 4.0, and 5.0 divisions will be up for grabs.

Squads will be competing in dual matches consisting of one singles court along with three lines of doubles. Playing their matches on clay will be the women’s 4.0 plus the men’s 3.0 and 4.0 divisions; the women’s 3.0 and the two 5.0 divisions are slated for hard courts.

The men’s 3.0, men’s 5.0, and women’s 5.0 competitions are composed of a single flight, with four teams entered for the men’s 3.0 and women’s 5.0 while the men’s 5.0 will see just three crews battle for sectional supremacy.

For the remaining divisions, 18 squads divided into four flights make up the field for both the women’s 3.0 and 4.0 to go along with the three-flight, 15-team men’s 4.0 competition.

Matches are set to begin Saturday morning at 7:30 a.m. For more information on the 2021 USTA Florida Adult 40 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0 Sectional Championships, click here.
