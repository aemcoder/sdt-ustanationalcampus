Saturday marked the end of round-robin action for three of the four flights at the 2017 USTA Florida Adult 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Championships. The women’s 3.0, along with the men’s and women’s 4.0 divisions, have moved into the semifinals, while the men’s 3.0 division will also have its champion decided on Sunday.

Escambia, SMD and Collier all posted 4-0 records in their respective flights in women’s 3.0 action. Escambia (Flight 1) and SMD (Flight 2) were dominant en route to qualifying for the semifinals, while Collier struggled, losing 17 sets and a higher combined number of games than two other teams in Flight 3. SMD and Collier will face off in one semifinal, with Escambia taking on wild card semifinalist Duval Eagle Harbor in the other.

Leon is in solid position to claim the 3.0 men’s divisional title, having wrapped up play in the three-team flight with a 3-1 mark. Polk and Duval will play in the only match from this flight on Sunday, with Polk needing to sweep all five individual courts without surrendering a single set in order to have a mathematical chance of overtaking Leon for the title.

The women’s 4.0 semifinals will be Hillsborough versus Orange/Seminole and Broward against Volusia. Hillsborough went 3-0 in Flight 1 while Orange/Seminole took Flight 2 by sweeping all 15 individual matchups it contested in round-robin play. Broward (Flight 3) and Volusia (Flight 4) easily navigated their way both easily navigated through their five-team flights.

For the men’s 4.0 division, the three teams earning flight victories were Broward from Flight 1, SMD from Flight 2, and Leon from Flight 3. Each squad produced an undefeated record over Friday and Saturday to advance to the semifinals. Broward is set to battle Leon while SMD has been paired with Polk, which finished second in Flight 3 to earn the wild card.

Semifinal matches, along with the final round-robin match of the men’s 3.0 division, will get underway at 8:30 a.m. Divisional finals are scheduled for 11:30 a.m.
