Two resident USTA National Campus players are making their home courts proud. Both No. 10 Madison Keys and No. 166 Grace Min have made big accomplishments in their recent tournaments.

Keys made her way through the French Open field to finally match-up against fellow American and close friend, Sloane Stephens in the semifinal. Keys dropped the match to Stephens 6-4, 6-4.

Min wins the $25,000 USTA Pro Circuit in Bethany Beach, Delaware to capture her 10th career USTA Pro Circuit title. The top-seeded Min defeated American Katerina Stewart in straight sets to take the final match.
