The USTA National Campus is getting a new online home. 

USTANationalCampus.com officially launched on Dec. 20, serving as the official online touchpoint the USTA’s state-of-the-art-facility located at Lake Nona in Orlando, Fla.

The new site will serve multiple functions:

  • Consumers can visit the new USTANationalCampus.com to stay up to date on all campus happenings.
  • Learn more about the campus facilities and program offerings. 
  • Book court time at the Home of American Tennis (starting on December 31). 
  • Offer the ability to watch select competitions that will be live streamed at no cost (coming soon). 

Be sure to visit us at www.ustanationalcampus.com for more information!
