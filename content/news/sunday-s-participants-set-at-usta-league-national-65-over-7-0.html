Sunday's Participants Set

February 8, 2020

The teams moving on to the playoff stages at the USTA League National 65 & Over 7.0/9.0 Invitational, held at the USTA National Campus, have been determined after two days of round-robin flight action. Leading the way with three squads in the semifinals are the Middle States and Southern Sections.

A big shakeup in the men’s 7.0 division left only Southern (Clarke Oconee Tennis Association – Ga.) and Florida (Glenview Champions Tennis Club – The Villages) without a loss after two days of play. Southern earned the top seed going into the semifinals thanks to 10 court victories compared to eight for Florida. Starting the day with a 1-1 overall record, New England (Willy’s Gym – North Eastham, Mass.) won twice on Saturday to vault all the way up into the third slot in the standings; meanwhile, Middle States (Community Park Tennis Complex – Princeton, N.J.) suffered defeat to slip out of the top spot, but still managed to hang on for the fourth and final semifinal berth. Sunday morning’s duals will pit Southern against Middles States and Florida versus New England.

Florida (Boone Park Tennis Courts – Jacksonville) was the lone squad that turned in a 4-0 effort in the women’s 7.0 competition. Despite that fact, it will be tough to label any of the four semifinalists as the favorite after each finished with eight individual matchup wins along with four losses. Top-seeded Florida will be across the net from No. 4 seed Middle States (Hershey Country Club – Hershey, Pa.) and Southwest (Himmel Tennis Center – Tucson, Ariz.) is set to take on Southern (Independent – Lafayette, La.) in the No. 2 vs. No. 3 contest.

Pacific Northwest (Independent – Yakima, Wash.) coasted their way into men’s 9.0 semifinals, earning the No. 1 seed with a sterling 4-0 team record. Eastern (Independent – Westchester/Rockland, N.Y.) and Middle States (Independent – Manasquan, N.J.) both ended the day at 3-1, with Eastern finishing second overall thanks to nine court victories while Middle States reeled in seven. Those two squads will battle it out in one semifinal dual and Pacific Northwest has drawn Northern California (McInnis Park – San Rafael), which moved on to the playoff stages despite just one dual win over the course of flight play.

The women’s 9.0 division could feature the most competitive semifinals on Sunday. Southern California (Westin Mission Hills – Rancho Mirage) dominated the field to produce a 4-0 record on the strength of 11 individual matchup victories. New England (The Edge at South Burlington – South Burlington, Vt.) also took all four of their duals to check in at No. 2 in the standings. Pacific Northwest (Independent – Northern Ore.) and Southern (Independent – Birmingham, Ala.) closed out Saturday with three team wins and one loss to also place in the top four; Pacific Northwest will be the opponent for New England while Southern is set to clash with Southern California.

The divisional semifinals will get underway at 8:30 a.m. with the championship and third-place matches to follow at approximately 11:30 a.m. For more information on the USTA League National Adult 65 & Over 7.0/9.0 Invitational, click here.
