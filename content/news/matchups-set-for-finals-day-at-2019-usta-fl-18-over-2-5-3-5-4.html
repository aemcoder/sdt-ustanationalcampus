Matchups Set for Finals Day

August 18, 2019

With two days of play in the books at the 2019 USTA Florida 18 & Over 2.5/3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships, the matchups for the divisional semifinals and finals have been set for Monday.

When flight play concluded within the women’s 2.5 division, no teams remained unbeaten. Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) did manage to hang on to the top spot in Flight 1 despite a loss on Sunday. They ended up with six court victories over the course of their three duals, allowing them to hold off Hillsborough (Van Dyke Farms – Odessa) and SMD (Doral Park Country Club). In Flight 2, Escambia’s (Independent) head-to-head defeat of Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) propelled them into the divisional final. Escambia closed out the two days of action with nine individual matchups in four total duals contested. Alachua and Escambia will face off on Monday at 9 a.m.

In the men’s 3.5 division, three of the semifinalists posted 3-0 records to go along with Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) moving on despite one loss on the weekend. The crucial dual in Flight 1 took place Saturday evening, when Marion bested Collier (Club at Longshore Lake – Naples). In much more commanding fashion, Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) dropped just one of the 15 courts they played in going 3-0 to claim Flight 4. Undefeated squads Broward (City of Oakland Park) and SMD (Doral Park Country Club) also safely advanced out of Flight 2 and 3, respectively. In Monday’s semifinals, Marion is matched up with Broward and SMD will be across the net from Alachua.

The women’s 3.5 division was highly competitive, with just one of the 19 squads navigating flight play unscathed. That honor went to Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center – Plantation), who claimed Flight 2 at 4-0 overall. Escambia (Independent) recovered from stumbling in their opening dual to rebound to finish atop Flight 1 thanks to upending SMD (Key Biscayne Tennis Association). Major shakeups occurred in Flights 3 and 4, where the two teams entering the day in control did not end up there. Hillsborough (Hunters Green Sports Center – Tampa) came through in Flight 3 after a pair of dual triumphs on Sunday to finish at 3-1 overall on the strength of 12 court victories. Duval’s (Magnolia Point Golf & Country Club – Green Cove Springs) defeat of Collier (Treviso Bay – Naples) was the deciding result in Flight 4 after both squads reeled in 13 individual wins. The semifinals will take to the courts Monday at 8 a.m., with Escambia clashing with Broward and Hillsborough going up against Duval.

Holding strong in the men’s 4.5 competition were the three teams who started the day in the driver’s seat in their respective flights: Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston), Brevard (Independent) and Hillsborough (Davis Island – Tampa). Broward eased through Flight 1 undefeated by claiming 11 of the 15 courts they contested, the same effort Hillsborough produced in Flight 3. Brevard fell in their final dual of Flight 2, setting up a three-way tie, where Brevard’s 10 court wins and head-to-head defeat of Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville) advanced them to the divisional semifinals. Monday’s semifinal duals have Broward facing Hillsborough and Brevard taking on wild card entrant SMD (Salvadore Park – Coral Gables), the Flight 3 runner-up.

Broward (Lauderdale Tennis Club – Fort Lauderdale) will enter Monday’s women’s 4.5 semifinals as the slight favorites thanks to being the lone squad still undefeated within the division. They gathered 12 court victories in their three duals in Flight 1. Escambia (Independent) had the tiebreaking procedure go in their favor in Flight 2, as they were beaten by Collier (Naples Bay Resort) but finished with more individual matchups to their credit. Brevard (Rockledge) survived a three-way tie in Flight 3 thanks to owning nine court wins, one better than the other two squads they tied with. Looking ahead to the semifinal round on Monday, Broward has been pitted against Brevard and Escambia’s opponent will be wild card Orange/Seminole, the second-place finisher in Flight 1.
