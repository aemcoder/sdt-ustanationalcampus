Broward, Pinellas Prevail on Day Two

February 22, 2021

style
article-hero

Two teams were crowned divisional champions on Sunday to highlight action at the USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships. Taking home titles on the day were the Broward (Lauderdale Tennis Club) 6.5 men and Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club) 8.5 women.

Teams are competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Not only was the Broward 6.5 men the only crew to post a perfect 4-0 record, they were the only team to finish with a winning record. Broward was a dominant force from start to finish, winning 11 of 12 individual matchups on the strength of 23 sets earned.

Joshua Forman serves as the captain with Eliu Montero as the co-captain of the Broward squad that is composed of Michael OMahony, Joseph Chapin, Andras Kovach, Beningo, Shawn Chen, Stephan Maclaurin, Rick Schmutzler, Brooks Yarbrough, Laughton Droze, and Adrain Evans.

In the 8.5 women’s competition, Pinellas cruised through Flight 2 with a 3-0 record while Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) came out on top in Flight 1 thanks to the tiebreaking procedure after being locked in a three-way tie with two wins and one loss. Pinellas capped off their impressive weekend by sweeping aside Hillsborough 3-0 in the championship dual.

Jillene Panella and Marcy Rooks got Pinellas off to a quick start, breezing through court two 6-2, 6-3. The other two matchups were tightly-contested, with Pinellas ultimately prevailing. Maria Stand and Elizabeth Burdick fought their way to a 6-4, 7-6 victory at line three, while Stephanie Means and Abby Kronk hung on for a 6-4, 2-6, 1-0 result at the top spot.

Kronk is the captain of the Pinellas squad with a complete roster of Xiao Zhang, Rooks, Stephanie Geshel, Ethel Murphy, Kayla Heller, Panella, Burdick, Means, and Stand.

The women’s 6.5 final will see Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville) across the net from Orange/Seminole/Lake (USTA National Campus – Orlando). Alachua seemed out of the running in Flight 1 coming into the day, sitting in third in the standings, but managed to end up in a tie at 3-1 with Broward (Weston Tennis Center), advancing to the final by virtue of their head-to-head triumph. Orange/Seminole/Lake was not pushed in Flight 2, taking all nine courts contested while impressively sweeping all 18 sets.

The men’s 8.5 final was nearly an all-Broward affair after the crew from Weston Hills Country Club emerged from a three-way tie with Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) and Brevard (Titan Tennis Center – Melbourne) thanks to owning nine individual matchup victories. However, the group from the Lauderdale Tennis Club came up short in Flight 2 against Hillsborough (River Hills Country Club – Valrico); Hillsborough turned in a sterling 4-0 mark through round-robin action.
