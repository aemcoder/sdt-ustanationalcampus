Saturday's Slate Complete

February 2, 2019

The USTA Florida Tri-Level League Playoffs kicked off on Saturday at the USTA National Campus, with several squads moving into position to claim sectional titles or secure a spot in their respective divisional semifinals.

Teams winning their respective divisional playoff will earn the right to represent the Florida Section at the 2019 Tri-Level Championships, held at the Indian Well Tennis Garden over the final weekend of the 2019 BNP Paribas Open (March 15-17).

In 3.5 action, all four teams went 1-1 for the day on the women’s side. Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center) holds a slight advantage going into Sunday, having dropped just six sets compared to seven by Escambia (Independent) and Duval (San Jose Country Club) along with eight by Marion (Fort King Tennis Center). In Sunday’s matchups, Alachua is set to face Duval while Escambia will go up against Marion. In the men’s division, Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) swept Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center), putting them just one dual victory away from clinching the title.

When the men’s 4.0 squads from Broward (City of Oakland Park) and Escambia (Independent) go head-to-head on Sunday, the winner is likely to be the one walking away with the sectional crown after both went 2-0 on the opening day of the event. Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) sits at 1-1 and could still factor in when all results are final.

For the women’s 4.0 competition, Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus), Duval (Palm Coast Tennis Center), and Brevard (DeSoto Recreation Complex) are in prime position to advance to the semifinals. That trio went 2-0, meaning they need to collect just one dual win on Sunday to secure their respective flight. A total of five squads sit at 1-1 going into the second day of play, locking their sights in on the lone semifinal wild card slot.

Duval (Florida Yacht Club) and Broward (City of Oakland Park) appear to be on a collision course in the men’s 4.5 division. After producing 2-0 efforts on Saturday – going a combined 12-0 in individual court matchups behind a mere four sets surrendered – the two teams have all but set the stage for a clash in Sunday afternoon’s final.

Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) will meet Sunday morning, with the winner taking Flight 1 of the women’s 4.5 competition. The loser will still be a main contender to grab the wild card berth into the semifinal round. In other women’s 4.5 play, IRMSL (North River Shores Tennis Club) and Sarasota (Lakewood National) took control of Flight 2 and 3, respectively. Both of those flights also contain two teams with 1-1 overall records as well.

Sarasota (Esplanade Golf and Country Club) is atop the lone women’s 5.0 flight after day one thanks to defeating Broward (City of Oakland Park) and Marion (Glenview Champions Tennis Club). Broward also earned a victory over Marion, leaving them in the mix if they manage to take down Sarasota.
