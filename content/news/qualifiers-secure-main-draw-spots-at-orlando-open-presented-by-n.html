Orlando Open Presented by Nemours

Qualifiers Secure Main Draw Spots

April 13, 2021

style
article-hero

After a washout on Sunday, both rounds of singles qualifying were on the schedule for Monday at the ATP Challenger Orlando Open Presented by Nemours. Additionally, four main draw contests were also completed on the day.

Managing to secure their place in the main draw with two victories on Monday were Tim Van Rijthoven (Netherlands), Altug Celikbilek (Turkey), Martin Damm (United States), and Roberto Quiroz (Ecuador).

Van Rijthoven was a late entrant into qualifying as an alternate, but made the most of the situation. He was paired up with American wild card Toby Kodat in the first round, where he prevailed 7-5, 6-2, before returning to the court for an impressive 6-4, 7-5 effort over Chile’s Marcelo Tomas Barrios Vera, the top seed in the qualifying draw. Van Rijthoven was the only qualifier to make it through without dropping a set and will now take on American Mitchell Krueger in the main draw.

Celikbilek earned the No. 2 overall qualifying seed, putting him across the net from Japan’s Shuichi Sekiguchi in the opening round. That match would go the distance, with Sekiguchi unable to hold onto his one-set advantage as Celikbilek moved on 4-6, 6-4, 7-5. It was much smoother sailing in the afternoon for Celikbilek thanks to a straightforward 6-4, 6-2 result against Jesper De Jong (Netherlands). He has drawn American Thai-Son Kwiatkowski to begin his main draw venture.

Given a wild card to compete in qualifying, Damm got off to a strong start against third-seeded Hiroki Moriya (Japan), only to be pushed to three sets. However, Damm quickly regained all momentum early in the final frame to run away with the 6-3, 3-6, 6-0 victory. He was mere points away from elimination in his second match of the day, where he took on fellow American JC Aragone. Damm dropped the opening set and found himself at 5-5 in the second before battling his way into the main draw with a 3-6, 7-5, 6-3 triumph, setting up an all-American matchup with Michael Mmoh.

It was an arduous path into the main draw for Quiroz, the fourth seed in qualifying. He spent nearly five hours on court Monday, starting with his 6-4, 2-6, 6-2 win over American wild card Roy Smith in the first round. He would be up against another American in Kevin King for his next contest; King claimed set one in a tiebreak but Quiroz shook off that disappointment impressively over the next two sets for a 6-7(2), 6-2, 6-1 score line. Quiroz’s opponent in round one will be American wild card Aleksandar Kovacevic. King would ultimately get into the main draw as well as a lucky loser, where he will play wild card Zane Khan (United States).

Just three matches from the main draw took place on Monday. From the top half, American Bjorn Fratangelo rallied past Ecuador’s Emilio Gomez 3-6, 6-4, 6-1 while Canadian Brayden Schnur dismissed No. 8 seed Alejandro Tabilo (Chile) 6-2, 6-2. In the bottom half, Jenson Brooksby (United States) downed seventh-seeded Jason Jung (Chinese Taipei) 6-1, 6-4.
