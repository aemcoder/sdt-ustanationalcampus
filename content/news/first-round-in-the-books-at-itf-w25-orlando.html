First Round in the Books

February 18, 2021

Wednesday featured numerous rain delays as matches were contested both indoors and outdoors over the course of the day at the ITF W25 Orlando event, hosted by the USTA National Campus. It would be many hours later than expected, but eventually all first-round singles matchups were completed.

Top seed and WTA No. 31 Amanda Anisimova (United States) was pushed to the brink by fellow American teenager Robin Montgomery. The opening frame was extended to a tiebreak, where Montgomery prevailed seven points to five, and the second set remained tight as well. Anisimova managed to force a decisive third before hanging on for the extended 6-7(5), 6-4, 6-3 victory. Next up for Anisimova is another American, Claire Liu, who lost the first set 6-0 versus Emina Bektas (United States) only to quickly turn the tables for a 0-6, 6-1, 6-1 result.

Romania’s Gabriela Talaba scored the big upset on Wednesday, taking out No. 2 seed Olga Govortsova (Belarus) 6-2, 6-4; her next opponent will be Great Britain’s Katie Swan after she dismissed American Kayla Day 6-3, 6-3. Also in the bottom half of the draw, seventh-seeded Vavara Flink of Russia was on the wrong end of a 6-3, 6-0 score line against Mariam Bolkvadze (Georgia); Bolkvadze will be across the net from Katie Volynets on Thursday, who came out on top 6-3, 6-4 over Maria Mateas in a showdown between American qualifiers.

No. 3 seed Renata Zarazua (Mexico) was mere points away from being ousted by American Hailey Baptiste, surviving a back-and-forth 5-7, 7-5, 6-3 affair. She has drawn Katrina Scott (United States) in round two after the qualifier got the best of American wild card Sarah Hamner 6-1, 6-2. Eighth-seeded Maria Camila Osorio Serrano (Colombia) had little trouble with Hungary’s Panna Udvardy, beating the qualifier 6-0, 6-3, while her round two opponent qualifier Maria Lourdes Carle (Argentina) took care of wild card Ashlyn Krueger (United States) 6-4, 6-1.

In other action from the top half, fourth seed Usue Arconada (United States) was solid in defeating Tereza Mrdeza (Croatia) by a 6-3, 6-1 margin; Arconada faces off with American Robin Anderson on Thursday, who battled past Slovkia’s Rebecca Sramkova 5-7, 6-4, 6-3. Additionally, Jamie Loeb (United States) was the victor in a marathon 5-7, 7-5, 6-4 contest with Great Britain’s Naiktha Bains and Russian qualifier Amina Anshba turned away lucky loser Barbara Gatica (Chile) 7-5, 4-6, 6-3.

No. 6 seed Anhelina Kalinina (Ukraine) got her week off to a good start, dropping just three games in beating Georgia’s Ekaterine Gorgodze 6-3, 6-0. Next up for Kalinina will be Serbian Jovana Jovic after she rallied past American qualifier Sophia Whittle 3-6, 6-3, 6-1.
