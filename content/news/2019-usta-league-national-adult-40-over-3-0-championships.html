2019 USTA League National Adult 40 & Over 3.0 Championships

PNW Looks for Repeat of Sweep

October 16, 2019

style
article-hero

USTA League National Championships action at the USTA National Campus kicks off this weekend, with the 40 & Over 3.0 Championships taking to the courts Oct. 18-20. First matches are scheduled for Friday at 7:30 a.m. It will mark the first of five consecutive weekends in which the highest level of play in the USTA League Adult and Mixed divisions will be on display in Orlando.

All 17 of the USTA’s Sections will be represented in both the men’s and women’s competitions. Friday and Saturday will see teams battle it out in flight play, taking part in two dual matches per day. A dual match consists of five total courts, two of which are singles matchups while the remaining three are doubles contests.

On Sunday, the top four men’s and top four women’s finishers will move on to the divisional semifinals, which are slated for 7:30 a.m. The championship and third-place playoff matches will follow, with first serve at approximately 11:30 a.m.  

At last year’s Championships, the Pacific Northwest Section pulled off a sweep of the titles. On the men’s side, the squad based out of the Mercer Island Country Club in Mercer Island, Wash., finished third in flight play before posting a pair of 3-2 wins to earn the title. The women’s champions hailed from the Sunset Athletic Club in Portland, Ore.; after just sneaking into the playoff rounds as the fourth seed, the crew was victorious in the semifinals by a 3-2 margin before turning in a commanding 4-1 effort in the final.

While new clubs are making their way to the National Campus from the Pacific Northwest for the 2019 installment, the men’s and women’s titles could remain in the same state by virtue of another sweep from the section. Making the cross-country trip from Washington state are the men of the Eastside Tennis Center in Kirkland, while the women’s group that plays out of the Mountain Park Racquet Club in Lake Oswego, Ore., could bring the trophy back to the Beaver State.
