Second Day of Action Complete

May 19, 2019

While national titles were being won by the Texas men and Stanford women on the other side of the National Campus on Sunday, some of the country’s most talented juniors continued to compete with the goal of earning their own title at a national event. Day two of the 2019 USTA May National Level 2 saw one round of main draw singles and two rounds of main draw doubles contested.

A day after the No. 2 seed in the boys’ singles draw was taken down, ninth-seeded Nathan Cox (Southern) did one better by defeating No. 1 seed Kian Vakili (Florida) in straight sets, 7-5, 6-3. Next up for him will be unseeded Ryan Colby (Mid-Atantic). The highest seed left in the draw, No. 3 Adit Sinha (Eastern) continued to move through the draw comfortably, with unseeded Floridian Leanid Boika awaiting him in the final eight.

While three competitors narrowly avoided being sent into the consolation draw in action from the lower half of the draw, unseeded Santiago Giamichelle (Florida) eased his way into the quarterfinals with a 6-1, 6-0 effort against Payton Young (Northern California). Fellow unseeded hopeful Michael Ross (Southern) fought back to oust No. 4 Eli Gordon (Midwest) 2-6, 6-1, 12-10, while eighth-seeded Jared Phillips (Eastern) got the best of Jonathan Irwanto (Florida) 4-6, 6-4, 10-2. Round out the quarterfinalists is No. 9 Julen Morgan (Florida) thanks to his marathon 4-6, 6-3, 10-8 triumph over six seed Jacob Lowen (Florida).

Top-seeded Emma Charney (Southern) is the only top four seed left in the girls’ singles competition thanks to her 7-6(4), 6-1 victory over No. 8 Maria Drobotova (Florida). Her quarterfinal opponent will be eighth-seeded Anushka Khune (Florida). A pair of unseeded Sunshine State representatives performing at a high level on the weekend will also clash in the top half. Valeria Ray did not surrender a game in besting Blanka DeMicheli (Florida); Hope Moulin defeated No. 9 Adrianna Peradoza (Mid-Atlantic) comprehensively, 6-4, 6-2.

The bottom half of the draw features only seeds remaining, but seconded-seeded Ria Bhakta (Northern California) did stumble versus No. 9 Amber McGinnis (Florida) by a 6-3, 6-3 margin. Awaiting her on Sunday morning will be seventh seed Whitney Pate (Southern). Florida’s No. 6 Violeta Martinez and ninth-seeded Daniela Porges will also go head-to-head on Monday.

In doubles action, both draws have been whittled down to the semifinal round. The top-seeded tandem of Sam Feldman (New England) and Sinha won both of their contests 8-1, while No. 5 Sebastian Dounchis (Florida) and Colin Tavares (Florida) survived their first match, 8-6, before impressively beating No. 3 seeds Colby and Ian Rasheed (Southern) 8-3. The other semifinal will pit two Florida duos against each other, as No. 7 seeds Julen Morgan and Nicholas Olomu take on unseeded Boika and Jacob Lowen.

Surprise semifinalists Alice Oits and Piper Charney of the Southern Region will look to keep their impressive level of play going after stunning the No. 1 and No. 8 seeds on Sunday. The tough Sunshine State pairing of Amber McGinnis and Daniela Porges are their next foes. The seventh-seeded team of Bhakta and Lena Kovacevic (Florida) decisively handled the third seeds Paige Herremans (Florida) and Moulin (Florida), 8-2; their semifinal opponent will by No. 2 seeds Charney and Sara Snyder (Caribbean).

Action continues Monday morning at 8:30 a.m., beginning with the girls’ singles quarterfinals. For more information on the 2019 USTA May National Level 2, click here.
