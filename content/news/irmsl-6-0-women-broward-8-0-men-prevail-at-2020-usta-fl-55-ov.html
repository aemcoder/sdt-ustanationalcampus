IRMSL 6.0 Women, Broward 8.0 Men Prevail

The fourth and final day of the 2020 USTA Florida 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships saw two more teams earn titles to close out the event. The 6.0 women’s crew representing IRMSL (Magnolia Lakes – Port St. Lucie) outlasted Hillsborough (Apollo Beach Racquet Fitness Club) by a 2-1 margin to go along with the 8.0 men of Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) sweeping Duval (Boone Park Tennis Courts – Jacksonville). Those two are joined by the 8.0 women’s club from Brevard (Rockledge Park) as sectional champions.

On the women’s side, the day actually began with the last remaining dual match in flight play, in which Hillsborough bested Collier (The Players Club and Spa – Naples) to set up a showdown between the two dominant squads in the competition.

A quick 6-1, 6-2 effort from the Hillsborough duo of Sally Raque and Mary Flynn at line one was countered by IRMSL’s Pat Mazone and Teresa Wood easing to a 6-3, 6-1 win at the second spot. All the pressure shifted to the players battling it out at line three. Hillsborough’s Shirley Payne and Lynn Malarz toughed out the opening set 6-4, only to see Margarite Ponte and Paula Via strike back in the second 6-1. It would be Ponte and Via who managed to hang onto the momentum in the decisive match tiebreak, coming through in the clutch for IRMSL.

Captain Veronica Tempone’s full IRMSL roster includes Mazone, Wood, Ina Singh, Cheri Snyder, Via, Janice McKenna, Regina Meno, Linda Delo, Ponte, and Maureen Holley.

Broward simply proved too tough on courts one and two against Duval in a clash of unbeaten squads. They surrendered a mere 10 games in those two contests, as Rafael Oropeza and Jorge Cortes took line one 6-2, 6-4 while captain Gustavo Risquez and partner Juan Rios cruised to a 6-2, 6-2 victory at the second position. Teammates Andres Cabrero and Ruben Lisman were awarded the matchup on court three when their opponents retired with the score standing at 4-6, 2-1.

The complete Broward roster captained by Rizquez includes Matthew Kenick, Cortes, David Marmol, Steve Glover, William Guasch, Carlos Haupt, Lisman, Michael Srebnick, Rick Buckley, Melvin Villafane, Rios, Cabrero, and Oropeza.
