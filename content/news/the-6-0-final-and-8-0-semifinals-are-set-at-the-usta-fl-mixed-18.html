6.0 and 8.0 Titles on the Line Monday

October 13, 2019

The second day of the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Sectional Championships wrapped up early Sunday evening at the USTA National Campus. As play concluded, the teams competing in the 6.0 final and the 8.0 semifinals on Monday were determined. They will be looking to join the Orange/Seminole 10.0 squad as sectional champions.

It was smooth sailing once again for Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center – Plantation) and Brevard (Viera Regional Park) in the 6.0 division, as those two squads eased their way into Monday’s championships tilt. Broward posted a 3-0 record in Flight 1 on the strength of eight court victories, dropping just three sets. Brevard was even more dominant playing out of Flight 2, sweeping through all four of their duals after going 11-1 in the 12 individual matchups with 23 sets won compared with just four surrendered. Broward and Brevard will clash at 9 a.m. on Monday with the divisional title on the line.

All four squads advancing to the 8.0 semifinal round closed out flight play undefeated. Flight 1 representative Okaloosa/Walton/Bay (Shalimar Pointe Country Club) and Broward (City of Oakland Park) closed out sterling 3-0 efforts on the weekend. Both teams were 9-for-9 for the courts they contested with 18 sets to their credit. SMD (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center – Miami) had faced slightly more resistance in Flight 3, still going 3-0 with eight individual matchup victories. Flight 4 was the lone flight consisting of five teams, and Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) breezed through the competition; they won their four dual matches by giving away just one court and three sets.

Monday’s 8.0 semifinals will see Okaloosa/Walton/Bay across the net from Broward and SMD going up against Hillsborough. Those matches are slated to get underway at 8:30 a.m. with the final to follow at approximately 11:30 a.m.
