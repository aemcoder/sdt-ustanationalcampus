ITA Circuit Coming to Campus

Brian Ormiston | June 24, 2020

style
article-hero

The ITA Summer Circuit makes its way to the USTA National Campus July 11-13 and July 18-20, marking the first two events at the facility since it reopened.

These tournaments are open events and sold out in a matter of days. They feature primarily college and junior players who are looking to get level-based play before their upcoming seasons. Both weekends include men’s and women’s fields of 32 players.

This will be the third straight year the Summer Circuit makes an appearance in Orlando.

View more details on the UTR platform: July 11-13 and July 18-20. 

These open events are held all over the country, and for more information, visit ITA Summer Circuit Homepage.
