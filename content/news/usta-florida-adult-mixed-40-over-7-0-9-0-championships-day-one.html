Day one of the 2017 USTA Florida Adult Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships ended on Friday evening, with each team playing two dual matches as part of the round robin portion of the event.

The 7.0 division is composed of four flights. In Flight 1, both Hillsborough and SMD went 2-0 to open up the tournament. Hillsborough sits atop the standings thanks to winning all six individual courts and only surrendering a single set in its two dual matches. Flight 2 also features a pair of undefeated teams in Leon and Broward. Leon will enter Saturday as the top team by virtue of going a perfect six-for-six in individual matchups, also losing only one set so far.

Bay put some distance between itself and the other Flight 3 teams, winning both dual matches while three teams currently sit with overall records of 1-1. Bay did give away a court in each team match on Friday, but battled to carry a flawless record into day two. Flight 4 saw Polk and Collier post a pair of dual victories. Polk has absolutely dominated the competition so far, dropping a compiled 19 games en route to easily claiming all six individual matchups the team contested.

The 9.0 division consists of just two flights. Pinellas was the only team escaping day one without a loss in Flight 1, and did so even despite letting four sets slip away on the day. Three other teams are 1-1 in the flight. Meanwhile in Flight 2, Hillsborough and Escambia could be heading for a showdown after 2-0 days on Friday. Both squads won four of six individual courts, with Hillsborough losing four sets (compared to five for Escambia) and 37 total games (compared to Escambia’s 51).

Matches begin at 8:00 am Saturday morning. Following the completion of the second day of matches, the four flight winners in the 7.0 division will move on to the semifinals while the top team in each 9.0 division will book a spot in the sectional finals.
