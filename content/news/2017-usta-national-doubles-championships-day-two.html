With day two in the books for the Boys’ and Girls’ 14s and 16s divisions at the Fifth Annual USTA Doubles Championships, being hosted by the USTA National Campus, all main draw finals are set going into Saturday’s slate of matches.

Due to impending weather forecasted for the area on Saturday, match times have been revised and will now begin at 8:00 am. The Boys’ and Girls’ 16s finals will be played at 8:00 am while the Boys’ and Girls’ 14s are scheduled for 10:00 am.

The first of its kind, the USTA National Doubles Championships is the only junior competition devoted solely to doubles. Along with Level 1 National Ranking Points, the USTA’s prestigious Gold, Silver and Bronze Balls will be awarded to each draw’s top finishers.

The unseeded pair of Jonah Braswell and Bruno Kuzuhara, representing the Florida Section, continued their giant killer ways on Friday, knocking out two more seeds to reach the Boys’ 14s final. Losing a total of six games in the process, they defeated No. 5 seeds Jeffrey Etterbeek and Maxwell Sheldon in the quarterfinals and No. 3 seeds Brian Chong and Max Fardanesh in the semifinals. Emerging from the bottom half of the draw to take on Braswell and Kuzuhara is the second-seeded duo of Alex Finkelstein and Nathan Mao of the New England Section. Finkelstein and Mao were put to the test in both matches on the day, coming back from a tough first set loss against fifth seeds John Lasanajak and Cooper Williams to win 5-7, 6-4, 6-1 in the quarterfinals prior to toughing out a 6-4, 7-6(7) semifinal victory versus No. 5 seeds Louis Cloud and Ryan Colby.

Top seeds Phillip Jordan and Andres Martin have lived up to their billing, as the Southern Section tandem navigated their way to the championship match in the Boys’ 16s division. They nearly closed out a quarterfinal triumph over fifth-seeded Juan Bianchi and Quinn McLeod in straight sets, but shook off dropping the second set tiebreak for the 6-1, 6-7(3), 6-0 result. It was easier going in the semifinals for Jordan and Martin, as they got by third seeds Andrew Branicki and Scott Sculley 6-3, 6-4. No. 2 seeds Robert Cash and Andrew Zhang, hailing from the Midwest Section, produced two quick wins on Friday. After dispatching of Christopher Papa and Ivan Smith 6-2, 6-3 in the quarterfinals, they took out the fourth-seeded pair of Russell Berdusco and Davis Lins 6-1, 7-5.

A day after stunning the Girls’ 14s No. 1 seeds in the opening round, Alexis Blokhina and Juliette Finch were involved in two more lengthy matches on Friday. They survived against Regina Alcobe-Garibay and Ada Jane de Alcantara 6-7(5), 6-3, 6-2 in the quarterfinals but ultimately came up short in a semifinal battle between unseeded duos; Anna Babayan and Ari Siegel of the Florida Section prevailed 4-6, 6-1, 6-4 to book their spot in the championship tilt. Babayan and Siegel were also pushed to three sets in the quarterfinals, posting a 6-2, 3-6, 6-3 win over No. 5 seeds DJ Bennett and Sophia Wang. Fifth-seeded Liv Hovde (Texas) and Ava Krug (Florida) worked their way into the final thanks to two triumphs over top four seeds. They came through with a 6-4, 6-4 result against No. 4 seeds Paige Herremans and Daniela Porges in the quarterfinals and needed three sets to take care of second seeds Emma Charney and Whitley Pate 7-5, 4-6, 6-3.

It appeared the Girls’ 16s draw was set for a No. 1 vs. No. 2 showdown for the title after both teams got through the quarterfinals with ease. Top seeds Kylie Collins and Gianna Pielet put away No. 5 seeds Winslow Huth and Jenna Thompson 6-4, 6-3 while the second seeds Carmen Corley and Chidimma Okpara defeated unseeded Karly Friedland and Cooper Jackson 6-3, 6-4. Corley (Southwest) and Chidimma (Mid-Atlantic) held up their end of the bargain, breezing to a 6-3, 6-3 semifinal victory over No. 5 seeds Maribella and Allura Zamarripa. However, Collins and Pielet were upended by fifth seeds Amanda Chan (Southern California) and Sophia Edwards (Florida) in a close 6-4, 6-3 contest. Chan and Edwards were coming off an impressive 6-3, 6-4 showing against the fourth-seeded pair of Brianna Shvets and Main Sung in the quarterfinals.
