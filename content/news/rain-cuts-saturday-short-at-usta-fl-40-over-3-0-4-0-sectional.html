Rain Cuts Saturday Short

July 7, 2018

style
article-hero

While all men’s matches were completed on the hard courts, the women were not as fortunate on the clay at the opening day of the 2018 USTA Florida 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. The final round of women’s matches were on court when the rain and lightning moved in, suspending until Sunday morning.

With that being the case, the women will pick back up play starting at 7:30 a.m. Sunday morning; the men were already slated to begin at that time.

In the women’s 3.0 division, each of the three flights saw a team finish the day 2-0 to take the inside track on advancing to the semifinals while the wild card slot remains very much up for grabs. Orange/Seminole (Star Island) currently leads Flight 1, but Escambia won their only completed dual on Saturday before their second was halted. Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) and Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA) are atop Flight 2 and 3, respectively; each flight also has two teams at 1-1 overall.

For the 3.0 men, Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) defeated both Duval (Jax Golf) and Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center) on Saturday in the competition’s lone flight. Another win by Leon over Duval on Sunday will clinch the section title for the squad.

The first day of action provided little clarity in women’s 4.0 play, where only one of the four flights got two duals in before the suspension. That was Flight 2, where Orange/Seminole (Sanlando) is a win away from moving on to the semifinals. The standings within the other three flights are composed of two teams at 1-0 and two teams at 0-1 heading into Sunday’s schedule.

In the men’s 4.0 competition, two teams have been eliminated from each of the trio of flights, which feature four teams a piece. Sunday will be highlighted by three matchups where the winner automatically moves on to the semifinals, while one of the losers will take the wild card spot. Flight 1 comes down to Orange/Seminole (Villagewalk Lake Nona) and Duval (Orange Park Country Club), Flight 2 will go to either Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA) or Broward (Weston TC), and Flight 3 will be claimed by SMD (Tamiami Park) or Escambia.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
