Men's 3.5 Division Underway

November 6, 2020

The men’s 3.5 competition kicked off the 2020 USTA Florida 40 & Over Men’s 3.5 & Women’s 2.5/3.5 Sectional Championships Friday at the USTA National Campus. The 12 teams in the division played twice over the course of the day in a battle to book a spot in the knockout stages.

A total of four squads stayed unbeaten throughout the day, including Brevard (Kiwi Tennis Club – Indian Harbour Beach) and Pinellas (Seminole Lake Family Tennis – Seminole) in Flight 1; those two will face off on Saturday with the winner automatically moving on to the semifinal round. 

Flight 2 saw Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) prevail in both of their duals to take full control. Meanwhile, players representing Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) claimed all six of their individual matchups on the day, dropping just two sets to vault to the top of the Flight 3 standings.

Saturday’s slate of matches, in which both women’s divisions will take to the courts for the first time, is set for a 7:30 a.m. start. For updated results from the 2020 USTA FL 40 & Over Men’s 3.5 & Women’s 2.5/3.5 Sectional Championships, click here.
