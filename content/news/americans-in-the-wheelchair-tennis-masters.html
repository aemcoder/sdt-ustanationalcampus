#TeamUSATennis

Alyssa Simonin | November 21, 2019

style
article-hero

Five Americans take the court for the international year-end wheelchair competition at the USTA National Campus.

The NEC Singles Masters will feature the legendary David Wagner in the quad division, while his remaining countrymen compete in the UNIQLO Doubles Masters. Dana Mathewson, the sole woman competing for the USA, will battle it out in the women's doubles division.  Conner Stroud and Chris Herman, the young Americans joining the field later than most, are fighting in the men's doubles division. The iconic duo of David Wagner and Nick Taylor will team up once again in quad doubles in search of their 12th title. 

Follow the action on USTA social media with #TeamUSATennis. 

Americans in Action

David Wagner: Quad Singles & Doubles

Nick Taylor: Quad Doubles

Dana Mathewson: Women's Doubles

Chris Herman: Men's Doubles

Conner Stroud: Men's Doubles
