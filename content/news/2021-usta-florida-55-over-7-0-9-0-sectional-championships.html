USTA FL 55 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships

Matches Begin Saturday Morning

May 14, 2021

style
article-hero

The final Sunshine State league event of the spring is set for this weekend at the USTA National Campus, as the USTA Florida 55 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships will be contested Saturday, May 15 through Monday, May 17.

Teams will be competing in dual matches consisting of three doubles matchups; in order to claim the overall dual victory, a team must win at least two of those three courts.

With a total of 14 squads divided into three flights, the women’s 7.0 division is easily the largest on the weekend; flights 1 and 2 contain five teams while the other four make up Flight 3. Following the round-robin portion of the division, Monday will see the knockout stages play out, where the trio of flight winners along with the top second-place team will battle for the sectional crown.

For the men’s 7.0 division, seven crews have entered the event, with three being placed into Flight 1 and four into Flight 2. The championship dual is scheduled for Monday at 7:30 a.m.

Both 9.0 competitions welcome six teams vying for the divisional title, with two rounds played on Saturday and Sunday with the final round slated for Monday morning. A team champion could be decided as early as Sunday evening depending on results.

Defending champions include the Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) 7.0 men, the Orange/Seminole/Lake (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) 7.0 women, the Hillsborough (Palma Ceia – Tampa) 9.0 men, and the Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) 9.0 women.
