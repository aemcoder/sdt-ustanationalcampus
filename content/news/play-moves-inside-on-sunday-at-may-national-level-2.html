Play Moves Inside on Sunday

May 20, 2018

Persistent rain transformed the 2018 USTA May National Level 2, featuring some of the nation’s top boys and girls in the 16s division, into an indoor tournament for the day Sunday at the National Campus.

A heavily revised schedule of play featured just one round of main draw singles and one round of consolation singles, all of which used the short set format. Additionally, second round girls’ doubles matches were also contested on day two.

Boys’ top seed Jack Anthrop (Orlando, Fla.) continued his stellar level of play, sliding by No. 16 Kyle Kang (Fullerton, Calif.) 5-3, 4-1 to reach the quarterfinals. His next opponent is 12th-seeded Gabrielius Guzauskas (Hinsdale, Ill.), who has also yet to drop a set after his 4-1, 4-1 defeat of Zak Vince (Clermont, Fla.) on Sunday. Rounding out the top half of the draw is No. 3 Dillon Blake (Miami, Fla.) – after he survived an extended 4-2, 2-4, 10-4 matchup with No. 10 seed Alan Jesudason (Berwyn, Pa.) – along with unseeded Mateo Martinez (Fort Lauderdale, Fla.), who knocked out No. 14 Carter Morgan (Jacksonville, Fla.) 0-4, 4-1, 10-8.

Four seeded competitors remain in the bottom half of the boys’ draw – No. 2 Jacob Bickersteth (West Bloomfield, Mich.), No. 4 Trinity Grear (Adelphi, Md.), No. 7 seed Billy Suarez (Huntington, N.Y.), and No. 9 Andrew Ilie (Trumbull, Conn.). Bickersteth was once again in control of his match from the start, handling Adam Duan (Davie, Fla.) 4-2, 4-1. He will take on Ilie next, who survived a dramatic matchup of the eighth and ninth seeds by the slim margin of 4-1, 3-5, 12-10. Grear turned in the most comprehensive result of the day, sweeping aside Alexandre Moutran (Reunion, Fla.) 4-0, 4-0. Suarez also had little trouble on Sunday, posting a 4-2, 4-1 score line versus No. 15 Adit Sinha (North Brunswick, N.J.).

Jaleesa Leslie (Apopka, Fla.) gave the girls’ draw a big shakeup with her impressive 4-0, 4-1 upset of fourth seed Ellie Pittman (Indianapolis). Next up for Leslie is No. 6 Sophia Fornaris (Pinecrest, Fla.), who got past No. 9 Sarah Hamner (Fort Collins, Co.) 4-1, 5-4(3). Top seed Madison Sieg (Greenwich, Conn.) was on and off the court quickly Sunday, defeating 14th-seeded Jesse Hollins (Orchard Park, N.Y.) 4-1, 4-0. Her next opponent is No. 11 Sophie Williams (Charleston, S.C.), another player at the top of her game through three rounds.

Just as with the boys’ draw, a pair of seeded matchups fills in the bottom half of the draw. Second seed Leyden Games (Newport Beach, Calif.) moved into the quarterfinals following her 4-1, 5-3 victory over No. 10 Rachel Gailis (Studio City, Calif.). She will meet No. 15 Alexis Blokhina (Plantation, Fla.), who battled back to hold off unseeded hometown rival Daniela Porges (Plantation, Fla.) 3-5, 5-3, 10-4. Third seed Brooke Theis (Lake Forest, Ill.) and No. 5 seed Anushka Khune (Palo Alto, Calif.) are also set to square off in the quarterfinals after besting unseeded hopefuls Violeta Martinez (Port Saint Lucie, Fla.) and Makenna Doyle (Boca Raton, Fla.), respectively.
