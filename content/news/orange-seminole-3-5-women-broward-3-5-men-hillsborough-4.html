The 2017 USTA Florida Adult 40 & Over 3.5/4.5+ Sectional Championships concluded Sunday at the USTA National Campus, with four teams earning sectional titles.

Three of the four finals were moved indoors due to weather, but in the end, Orange/Seminole (3.5 women), Broward (3.5 men), Hillsborough (4.5+ women) and SMD (4.5+ men) all walked away sectional champions.

In the women’s 3.5 semifinals, SMD-Doral Park held off Collier, 3-2, while Orange/Seminole took care of Marion, 4-1. Collier was dominant on both singles courts against SMD-Doral Park, winning three of the four sets 6-0. But it was SMD that owned the doubles contests, securing the team victory and a spot in the finals with a 6-4, 0-6, [1-0] triumph at the third doubles spot after winning in straight sets on the other two courts. Orange/Seminole swept through the doubles matchups against Marion, while also claiming the second singles position to easily advance. The only court Orange/Seminole did not secure was the top doubles spot, where Marion won by retirement.

Orange/Seminole nearly swept through the doubles matchups in the final, which were played first as the dual was moved indoors. However, SMD stayed alive by winning on the second doubles court, 6-2, 6-4, while Orange/Seminole took courts one and three. The title was clinched at the No. 2 singles position when Orange/Seminole won, 7-5, 6-4. SMD would salvage the top spot, 6-7, 6-3, [1-0], to put the final team tally at 3-2.

Both Broward and Alachua got through their men’s 3.5 semifinal encounters, posting victories by a 4-1 margin. Broward was challenged by Escambia, with four of the five courts decided by a match tiebreak. Broward was able to win both singles matchups in three sets to go along with pulling out victories at the first doubles spot in straight sets and the third doubles spot in three sets. Alachua used a similar formula to beat Duval-Eagle Harbor, also pulling out a pair of tight singles contests to go along with taking two of the three doubles courts – one in straight sets and one that went the distance.

The men’s 3.5 division final was the tensest of the bunch on Sunday. The trio of doubles matchups was played first, and all three went the distance. Alachua came out on top, 3-6, 6-4, [1-0], on court one and 6-3, 4-6, [1-0] on court three, with Broward taking the second spot, 1-6, 6-4, [1-0]. That put all the focus on the two singles spots, in which Broward needed both in order to win the sectional crown. The first singles position went their way quickly by the score of 6-2, 6-1. Broward also held a lead on court two after claiming the opening set, but Alachua turned the tide and won the second. That put all the pressure on the deciding match tiebreak, and it was Broward that was able to come through, 6-3, 3-6, [1-0], igniting a raucous court storming following match point.

Hillsborough and SMD faced off for the women’s 4.5+ championship before the rain moved in. It did not take long for Hillsborough to grasp firm control, as they won all five opening sets, including four by the score of 6-1 or 6-2. SMD was only able to mount a comeback at the No. 2 doubles spot, where it rallied to win, 6-7, 6-4, [1-0]. Hillsborough took the top singles position, 6-1, 6-3, and the second singles court, 6-2, 6-1. It cruised on the No. 3 doubles court, 6-1, 6-1, and capped off an impressive championship performance with a 6-2, 7-6 win at the top doubles spot for the 4-1 team victory.

Pinellas advanced to the men’s 4.5+ division final after slipping past Broward, 3-2, while SMD handled Orange/Seminole, 4-1, in their semifinal encounter. Pinellas overcame losses on both singles courts by sweeping through all three doubles positions, all in straight sets. Four of the five courts contested by SMD and Orange/Seminole went the distance, with SMD battling through for the victory on three of those.

In the championship tilt, all five courts were decided in straight sets. SMD claimed the first singles court, 6-1, 6-3, and the second, 6-3, 6-4. While Pinellas won the top doubles position, SMD came away with the second (6-0, 7-6) and the third (7-5, 6-4) for the 4-1 team triumph and the sectional championship.
