Sunday marked the second day of the 2018 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships, held at the USTA National Campus. Champions will be crowned on Monday.

Three teams managed to navigate their way through flight play unscathed to advance to the 7.0 semifinals. Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) delivered the most comprehensive performance, winning all nine courts they contested while dropping just one set and 43 games in Flight 3. Hillsborough (Hunters Green Sports Center) also faced little resistance in Flight 2, claiming eight individual matchups behind the loss of two sets and 62 games. Broward (City of Oakland Park) bested Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) on Sunday to finish atop Flight 1.

Flight 4 provided much more balance. SMD (Coral Gables/Salvadore Park) and Pinellas (Seminole Lake Family Tennis) ended Sunday tied with a 3-1 overall record. That left the flight’s semifinal representative to be determined by the tiebreaking procedure; with eight court victories compared to seven, SMD grabbed the final four spot over Pinellas.

The semifinal duals will pit Broward against Hillsborough and Orange/Seminole versus SMD.

It was a chaotic second day of play for the 9.0 division. Only Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) rounded out flight play undefeated, going 3-0 in Flight 3. But even they were challenged, actually finishing with more sets and games lost than second-place SMD (Coral Pine Park). Hillsborough (Harbour Island Athletic Club) lost their lone dual on Sunday, but that still put them in a tie for first in Flight 2 with Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center). The difference was the head-to-head result between the two, which was a sweep in favor of Hillsborough.

Broward (City of Oakland Park) rebounded from a loss on Saturday to win twice Sunday, placing them in a three-way tie in Flight 1. Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park) was eliminated thanks to winning fewer courts than Broward and Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club). Broward then took the flight via their victory over Pinellas.

The 9.0 semifinals were also played on Sunday. Broward ended Marion’s undefeated run by taking a tightly-contested 2-1 affair. Marion won the second doubles court 7-6, 7-5, but Broward clawed their way to a pair of triumphs in match tiebreaks for the team victory. They prevailed 6-1, 3-6, 1-0 on court one and staged an impressive 4-6, 6-2, 1-0 comeback at line three.

Hillsborough also moved into the finals by taking down wild card entrant SMD by a 2-1 margin. The top spot went to Hillsborough in straight sets (6-3, 6-4) while the other two courts were extended to match tiebreaks. SMD stayed alive by rallying at line two (3-6, 6-0, 1-0), but in the end Hillsborough succeeded in securing a place in the finals with a 3-6, 6-3, 1-0 win at the third position.

For Monday’s slate of matches, the 7.0 semifinals will get underway at 8:30 a.m. while the 9.0 championship dual is slated for a 9 a.m. start time.
