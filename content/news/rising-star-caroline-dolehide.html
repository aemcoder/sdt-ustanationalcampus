#NationalCampus

Alyssa Simonin | June 27, 2018

style
article-hero

Catch 19-year-old #USTennis rising star and Hindsdale, Illinois native, Caroline Dolehide, training and building her game at the USTA #NationalCampus in Orlando, FL. She’s currently ranked 113 in the world, thanks to a stellar first half of the 2018 season. Caroline gave No. 1 Simona Halep a run for her money by stealing a set at the BNP Paribas Open in Indian Wells this past March. Then in May, she played deep into the field at the French Open, only to fall to fellow American—Madison Keys.

Learn more about this up-and-comer as she trains at the USTA National Campus.
