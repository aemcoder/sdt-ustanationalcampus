USTA FL Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships

Sunshine State League Play Continues

January 21, 2021

style
article-hero

USTA Florida wraps up its first month of league play at the USTA National Campus this weekend when the Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships take to the courts. Matches are set to begin Friday, Jan. 22, with the two sectional champions to be determined Sunday, Jan. 24.

Teams will be competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

A total of 11 squads make up this year’s 7.0 competition. They have been divided into three flights for the round-robin portion of the weekend, with three in Flight 1 along with four in Flights 2 and 3. Following the completion of play Saturday afternoon, the trio of flight winners plus the top second-place team will advance to the Sunday’s divisional semifinals.

Just six regions, all placed into a single flight, will be vying for the 9.0 crown. There will be no knockout stages within this division; instead, the squad with the best record at the end of five rounds of duals will take home the title.

Defending champions from the 2019 edition of the event include the 7.0 crew representing Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) and the 9.0 group from South Miami/Dade (Coral Pine Park – Pinecrest).
