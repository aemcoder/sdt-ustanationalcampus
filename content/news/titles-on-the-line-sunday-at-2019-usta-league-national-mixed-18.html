Titles on the Line Sunday

November 9, 2019

Rain pushed back the start of the second day of action at the 2019 USTA League National Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Eventually the courts dried and match play got underway, leading the way to the divisional semifinalists being determined.

In 6.0 competition, Caribbean (Independent – Puerto Rico) and Southern (Burns Park Tennis – North Little Rock, Ark.) were the only two squads to finish up flight play a perfect 4-0. Caribbean turned in a thorough two days of play, winning 11 of the 12 courts they contested with 23 sets to their credit; Southern claimed nine individual matchups with 18 sets won.

Coming into Saturday atop the 6.0 standings, Missouri Valley (Oklahoma City Tennis Center) was able to offset a setback thanks recording three dual wins via the sweep. Their 10 court victories were the most among the one-loss clubs, putting them at the No. 3 seed. Finally, Pacific Northwest (Sunset Athletic Club – Portland, Ore.) emerged from the teams that entered day two at 1-1 overall, earning two team wins to vault up into the fourth and final semifinal spot.

Sunday’s divisional semifinals will pit Caribbean against Pacific Northwest while Southern will be across the net from Missouri Valley.

For the highly competitive 8.0 division, six sectional representatives brought 2-0 records into the second day of action. However, that number would be cut in half as play concluded. The No. 1 seed came down to Southern (St. Georges Independent School – Collierville, Tenn.) and Texas (Memorial Park Tennis Center – Houston), as both ended with nine individual matchups under their belt on the strength of 19 sets won. Ultimately, with one fewer set lost and a higher percentage of games won, Southern was awarded the first position.

Also navigating their way through to Sunday without a loss was the squad from Middle States (Mt Lebanon Tennis Center – Pittsburgh), checking in just behind Southern and Texas with eight court victories. Meanwhile, in a bit of a stunning development, Hawaii (Stevenson Middle School – Honolulu) battled back from losing to Texas on Friday to steal the last semifinal berth. Winning both duals on Saturday, Hawaii slowly moved up the standings over the course of the day, eventually edging out previously unbeaten Northern California.

For the semifinal matchups, Southern is set to go up against Hawaii while Texas will face Middle States.

The 10.0 Eastern squad (Carefree Racquet & Health Club – North Merrick, N.Y.) will enter the semifinal stage as the heavy favorite after sitting in the top slot of the divisional standings the past two days; Eastern won 11 of the 12 courts they contested, letting just two set slip away. The only other club to finish 4-0 was Northern California (Memorial Park – Albany), staking claim to the No. 2 seed.

Two other teams also came into day two undefeated, but their fortunes quickly changed, opening the door for a pair of one-loss contenders. Finding a way to finish in the third slot was Intermountain (Gates Tennis Center – Denver) despite losing their first dual of the Championships, while Texas (Oak Creek Tennis Center – Carrollton) made the most of their second chance as well, checking in at fourth overall thank to three consecutive dual wins.

Both semifinals will see a squad with one defeat look to topple a foe with a sterling record so far, as Eastern clashes with Texas and Northern California meets Intermountain.

All semifinal duals will get underway at 7:30 a.m. with the divisional finals and third-place playoffs to follow at approximately 10:30 a.m.
