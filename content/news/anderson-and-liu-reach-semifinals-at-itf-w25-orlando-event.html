Anderson and Liu Reach Semis

February 19, 2021

style
article-hero

Friday featured the singles quarterfinal round along with the doubles semifinals at the ITF W25 Orlando event, hosted by the USTA National Campus.

The semifinal in the top half of the singles draw will be an all-American matchup between Robin Anderson and Claire Liu. Both players dropped a mere three games en route to securing their quarterfinal victory, as Liu upset No. 6 seed Anhelina Kalinina (Ukraine) 6-2, 6-1 while Anderson dismissed Russian qualifier Amina Anshba 6-1, 6-2.

In the other semifinal, the lone seeded competitor remaining – No. 3 Renata Zarazua (Mexico) – will be across the net from Great Britain’s Katie Swan. Zarazua toughed out the opening frame by a 6-4 margin against eighth-seeded Maria Camila Osorio Serrano (Colombia) before cruising to the finish line for a 6-4, 6-1 triumph. For Swan, she was in control from the start in her 6-1, 6-1 result over American qualifier Katie Volynets.

While Osorio Serrano was eliminated in singles, she did bounce back later in the day to team up with Conny Perrin for a tightly-contested 7-5, 6-3 semifinal win against Serbian Jovana Jovic and Slovakian Rebecca Sramkova. Their opponents in the championship match will be American Emina Bektas and Great Britain’s Tara Moore after they defeated Gabriela Talaba (Romania) and Marcela Zacarias (Mexico) 6-4, 6-3.

The doubles final is scheduled for 10 a.m. with the two singles semifinals to follow. The ITF W25 Orlando event is closed to the public. For more information, including draws and daily schedule of play, click here.
