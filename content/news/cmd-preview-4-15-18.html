Fan Guide to MatchDay on April 15

UCF and USF close out the MatchDay season.

Brian Ormiston | April 10, 2018

style
article-hero

The ”War on I-4” closes out the 2018 College MatchDay season, with the UCF Knights and South Florida Bulls engaging in a men’s rivalry match on Sunday, April 15, at 5:30 p.m. ET. The contest will air live nationally on Tennis Channel.

Before making your way to Lake Nona, here is everything you need to know about attending the match:

Matchup

-UCF vs. South Florida (men)

Date and Time

-Sunday, April 15

-First serve: 5:30 p.m.

 

Promotion

-Fans who purchase a College MatchDay program will get a free T-shirt (first come, first served).

 

Pre-match Festivities

-Outdoor festivities are cancelled due to the weather.

-Fans coming to the campus are encouraged to stop by the Welcome Center before the match.

 

Location

-USTA National Campus (Collegiate Complex)

-Address: 10000 USTA Blvd., Orlando, FL 32827

-Phone: 844-872-8667

Tickets

-Admission is free.

-Reserve complimentary tickets by visiting Ticketmaster.com and searching College MatchDay. -Or click here: 2018 College MatchDay Complimentary Tickets.

Parking

-Parking is free and available on a first-come, first-served basis.

RV Parking

-Fans with RVs are directed to park in the back row of the main parking lot.

Bus Parking

-The bus lane is located near the front of the main parking lot.

Match Format

-For just the second time at College MatchDay, singles will kick off the action. Each of the six singles matches will be worth one team point.

-If the match is tied 3-3, three doubles matches will be played (one six-game set). The first team to win two of the three matches earns the final team point.

-The first school to secure four overall team points wins the match.

Match Preview

Sunday’s contest will be the final regular-season match of 2018 for both schools before they travel to Dallas for the American Athletic Conference Championships.

UCF racked up five straight victories from March 14-23, defeating the likes of Minnesota, Indiana, Miami (Fla.), Arkansas and Princeton. However, the Knights went 1-2 on their most recent road trip, and own a 10-8 overall record.

Although South Florida claimed a conference title in 2017, the team has a 7-15 record for the 2018 campaign. The Bulls are coming off a 6-1 loss at No. 21 Tulane, where Peter Bertran earned the team’s lone point.

Last year, UCF topped South Florida in the regular season, 4-2, only to lose to the Bulls in the AAC Championship finals, 4-1. Both matches were played at the USTA National Campus.

Tickets are complimentary and available on site but fans are encouraged to reserve their tickets at Ticketmaster.com.

For those who can’t make it, College MatchDay will be aired live on Tennis Channel.
