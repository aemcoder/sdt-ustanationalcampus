Alyssa Simonin | August 6, 2019

style
article-hero

Resident USTA National Campus player, Caroline Dolehide claims gold and silver medals at the 2019 Pan American Games in Lima, Peru. She captures gold with fellow American, Usue Arconada, in women's doubles against the team from Paraguay. One day later Caroline battled for women's singles gold, but fell to Nadia Podoroska from Argentina, to ultimately take home the silver.
