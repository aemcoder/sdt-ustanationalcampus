Georgia Takes Home Title

March 31, 2019

style
article-hero

Sunday marked the final day of competition for the 2019 USTA Tennis on Campus Spring Invitational, hosted by the USTA National Campus. Highlighting the day’s action was the Gold Bracket championship match, where the Georgia Bulldogs capped off their impressive weekend by defeating the Boston University Terriers, 29-19.

All dual matches were contested using the World TeamTennis (WTT) format, consisting of five no-ad sets – men’s singles, men’s doubles, women’s singles, women’s doubles, and mixed doubles.

Georgia came into the day having not dropped more than 11 games in any of their team matches. They quickly found themselves with a much tougher battle against Cal Poly. However, the Bulldogs were still the victors, moving into the championship round by a margin of 27-20. The gritty Boston University squad came up with the goods in crucial moments once again, this time at the semifinal stage, holding off Pittsburgh 24-18.

In what turned out to be a back-and-forth affair, Georgia eventually pulled away from Boston University to take home the team crown. It was the first Spring Invitational championship for the Bulldogs, who are the eighth school to claim the title.  

Taking home third-place overall was Cal Poly thanks to a tightly-contested 26-25 triumph over Pittsburgh. Other teams earning the top spot in their respective bracket were MIT (Silver), Washington State (Bronze), Portland (Copper), and College of Charleston (Titanium).

TOC Spring Invitational – Bracket Finals Results

Gold: Georgia def. Boston University 29-19

Silver: MIT def. Cincinnati

Bronze: Washington State def. Nevada 20-18

Copper: Portland def. Wisconsin 30-8

Titanium: College of Charleston def. Winona State 28-16
