GET READY FOR CAMP NO SNOW!

Shake off your holiday break cabin fever, grab your racquets, and join your coaches for the 2020 Camp No Snow!

FAMILY ZONE:

Pricing: $40.00 per day

Monday, December 21st - Wednesday, December 23rd

Monday, December 29th  -Wednesday, December 30th

All camps are offered from 8:30 a.m. -  10:30 a.m.

Family Zone Camps are offered in both Red Ball and Orange Ball options.

REGISTER HERE

YOUTH DEVELOPMENT:

Pricing: $60/day

Monday, December 21st - Wednesday, December 23rd

Monday, December 29th  -Wednesday, December 30th   

All camps are offered from 10:30 a.m. -  1:30 p.m.

Youth Development Camps are offered in Campus Youth 1, Campus Youth 2, Campus Youth Green Performance, Campus Youth Varsity, and Campus Youth Performance.

REGISTER HERE

ADULT PROGRAMS: 

Programs available December 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 28th, 29th and 30th!

Holiday programs offered in Cardio Tennis, Singles Drills and Doubles Drills! Both morning and evening programs are available.

REGISTER HERE*

*Adult Drop-In Programs open seven days in advance

RAIN: In the event of rain and courts become unplayable, camp will be canceled and players will not be able to stay on campus. Classes will remain at a four-players to one-coach ratio.

We hope to see you on the courts as we wrap up the year!
