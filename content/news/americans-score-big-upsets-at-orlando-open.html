Wednesday’s action at the Orlando Open was punctuated by more upsets as Americans Stefan Kozlov, Ulises Blanch and Michael Redlicki knocked off a trio of seeded players.

Kozlov scored the biggest upset of the day knocking off the tournament’s No. 2 seed, fellow American Mitchell Krueger, in three sets. 

Blanch downed the tournament’s No. 3 seed Thiemo de Bakker of the Netherlands, while Redlicki, who defeated fellow American and eighth-seeded JC Aragone, joined Sam Rifficeas American wild card recipients to reach the third round. No. 11 seed Kevin King was the lone American seeded player to advance on Wednesday.

The tournament, which is a USTA Pro Circuit ATP Challenger event, is the first USTA Pro Circuit ATP Challenger event of the 2019 season and is the highest level of tennis on display to date at the USTA National Campus, with a prize-money pool of more than $54,000.

Play continues Thursday with the start of the third round of singles matches as well as the doubles quarterfinals. Play begins at 10 a.m. Admission to the Orlando Open is free.

ABOUT THE USTA PRO CIRCUIT:

With more than 100 tournaments throughout the country and prize money ranging from $15,000 to upwards of $160,000, the USTA Pro Circuit is the largest developmental tennis circuit in the world and the pathway to the US Open and tour-level competition for aspiring players. Now in its 40th year, the USTA Pro Circuit has been a launching pad for many of today’s top pros, including Andy Murray, Maria Sharapova, John Isner, Sloane Stephens, Kei Nishikori, Victoria Azarenka, Caroline Wozniacki and Garbine Muguruza.
