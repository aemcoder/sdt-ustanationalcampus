USTA Florida 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+

Sectional Championships

August 10, 2017

style
article-hero

Adult teams will be back in action this weekend, as the penultimate USTA Florida Adult event is set to get underway Friday. Men’s and women’s teams from the 3.0, 4.0 and 5.0+ levels will all be in action over the tournament’s three days.

A total of 62 teams will be competing this weekend, with the women’s 3.0 division featuring the most at 17. Both the men’s and women’s 4.0 divisions are composed of 16 teams, while the men’s 3.0 and 5.0+ have just five teams and the women’s 5.0+ only three.

Round robin action will kick things off Friday morning, running through the end of Saturday’s schedule. For the divisions with four flights (women’s 3.0, women’s 4.0, and men’s 4.0) there will be a semifinal round on Sunday morning to decide which two squads will battle for the title. The remaining divisions (men’s 3.0, women’s 5.0+ and men’s 5.0+) have one flight each, meaning a champion will be crowned following the completion of flight play.

The men’s 3.0 will take place on the Team USA hard courts while all other matches are to be contested on the Har-Tru courts. Each team dual will consist of five individual matchups, three of which are doubles and the other two singles. In order to claim a team win, three of the five matchups must be secured. All matches will consist of two sets with a tiebreak at 6-6 and a match tiebreak in lieu of a full third set.
