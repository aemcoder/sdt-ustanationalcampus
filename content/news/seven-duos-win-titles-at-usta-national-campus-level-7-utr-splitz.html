Seven Duos Crowned Champions

December 23, 2019

article-hero

On the weekend before Christmas, the USTA National Campus served as the host site to the National Campus Level 7 UTR Splitz junior event.

The tournament featured the "Splitz" format, in which players pair up to compete as a team in dual matches. All dual matches began with a doubles match consisting of one set, counting for one point, followed by the players splitting up for two separate singles matches, also counting for one point apiece. The team winning at least two of the matches was awarded the dual victory.

In the boys' 12s-14s UTR 1.00-3.78 draw, the second-seeded pairing of Eric Echeverria and Cristian Ramirez of Doral swept No. 1 seeds Juan Pablo Jimenez and Kamran Sadikov from Kissimmee. Echevarria and Ramirez had just slipped by Armaan Lakhani (Orlando) and Pedro Valpereiro (Windermere) in the semifinals by a 2-1 margin, but were much sharper in the championship matchup.

The boys' 12s-16s UTR 2.73-5.42 draw went to top seeds Andre Bart (Tuxedo Park, N.Y.) and Jordan Lee (Orlando). Living up to their top billing, Bart and Lee were pushed by Ivan and Michael Rybak of Palm Coast before prevailing 2-1 in the semifinal round; they followed that up with an impressive 3-0 sweep of No. 2 seeds Pranav Kumar and Evan Sheffield from Orlando with the title on the line.

Staying with the theme of surviving tough semifinal tests, No. 1 seeds Leo Kwetkus (Ormond Beach) and Duane Headley (Deland) came through for the win in the boys' 14s-18s UTR 2.12-4.55 draw. Starting off with a quick 3-0 sweep, Kwetkus and Headley found themselves locked in a battle with Austin Martin (Spokane Valley, Wash.) and Sebastian Sanders (Libby, Mont.) in their next dual. Eventually emerging from that with a 2-1 triumph, the top-seeded tandem took the crown with a 3-0 win over Christian Morales and Cameron Phillips from Spokane Valley, Wash.

The seeded teams cruised to the championship match of the boys' 14s-18s UTR 5.41-9.03 draw, where No. 1 Aidan Bart (Tuxedo Park, N.Y.) and Siddharth Moturi (Orlando) bested No. 2 Bernardo Aguiar (Winter Garden) and Timothy Pleasant (Orlando) by a 3-0 score line.

For the girls' 14s-16s UTR 1.00-2.25 draw, the lone seeded pair of Kaia James and Mia Muniz lived up to their billing by winning all three of their round-robin duals to earn the title. They turned in two sweeps after slipping past Kailee Alteneder and Mackenzie Mortensen of Spokane Valley, Wash. by a 2-1 margin in their first match on the weekend.

No. 1 seeds Chloe Gooding and Holland Harris, hailing from Orlando, did not have an easy path en route to the girls' 14s-18s UTR 1.77-3.93 title, but they found a way to turn in consecutive 2-1 results after a bye in the opening round. Meanwhile, the girls' 14s-18s UTR 4.37-7.63 draw went to No. 2 seeds Asadah Maat (Orlando) and Madison Molock (Ocala), who were the most dominant team over the course of the weekend thanks to winning all three duals via a 3-0 sweep.
