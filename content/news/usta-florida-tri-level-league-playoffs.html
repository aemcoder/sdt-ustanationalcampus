USTA Florida Tri-Level League Playoffs

Berths into Tri-Level Nationals on the Line

January 29, 2019

style
article-hero

Taking place at the USTA National Campus for the first time, the USTA Florida Tri-Level League Playoffs are set to get underway the morning of Saturday, Feb. 2 and wrap up on Monday, Feb. 4.

Teams winning their respective divisional playoff will earn the right to represent the Florida Section at the 2019 Tri-Level Championships, held at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden over the final weekend of the 2019 BNP Paribas Open (March 15-17).

Both the men’s and women’s 3.5 champions will be crowned Sunday afternoon, as there are only two men’s and four women’s squads entered into the event. Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) and Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center) will square off in a best-of-three battle for the men’s title while those same two facilities will be joined by Duval (San Jose Country Club) and Escambia (Independent) on the women’s side.

Other divisions slated to conclude throughout the day Sunday are the 4.0 men, 4.5 men, and 5.0 women. Five teams make up the lone flight in the men’s 4.0 competition, with the team finishing atop the standings claiming the sectional title. The eight men’s 4.5 squads have been divided into two flights, with the pair of flight winners going head-to-head in Sunday afternoon’s championship dual.  Meanwhile, representatives from Broward (City of Oakland Park), Marion (Glenview Champions Tennis Club), and Sarasota (Esplanade Golf and Country Club) will clash in the three-team, double round-robin flight with the women’s 5.0 crown up for grabs.

Extending play into Monday are both the 4.0 and 4.5 women’s divisions. Teams advancing to the 4.0 semifinals will have to win twice on Monday to earn the sectional title, with the semifinals scheduled for 9 a.m. and the final to follow; the 11 teams have been divided into three flights, meaning the trio of flight winners plus a wild card entrant will compete on Monday. In 4.5 action, 12 squads placed into a total of three separate flights will compete in round-robin duals before moving into the knockout stages Sunday afternoon (semifinals) and Monday morning (final).

At last year’s USTA Florida Tri-Level Playoffs, victorious teams included the Escambia (Independent) women and Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA) men at the 4.0 level along with the women of Orange/Seminole (Winter Park – Parks & Rec/Azalea Center) and the men from Duval (Independent) in the 4.5 competition.
