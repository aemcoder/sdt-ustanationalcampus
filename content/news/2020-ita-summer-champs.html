A pair of ITA Summer Circuit events were held at the USTA National Campus in July, marking the first events that the facility has hosted since closing in March. The fields were open to all players and predominantly featured juniors and collegians.

Take a look back at the final results below, and continue to check USTANationalCampus.com for more information on future events and programming. 

2020 ITA Summer Circuit Final Matches at the USTA National Campus 

July 11-13 

Men's A Draw - Ozak Colak def. Jelani Sarr

Men's B Draw - Aiden Little def. Isaac Smith

Women's A Draw - Gianna Pielet def. Madison Sieg

Women's B Draw - Jada Hill def. Sasha Kiselova

July 18-20 

Men's Draw (Only one draw) - Jelani Sarr def. Jake Fellows

Women's A Draw - Sonya Macavei def. Kate Sharabura

Women's B Draw - Caitlin Neal def. Sophia Marchetta
