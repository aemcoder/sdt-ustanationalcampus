2020 USTA FL Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships

Sunshine State Play Continues

October 2, 2020

style
article-hero

A busy fall of USTA Florida Adult League action at the USTA National Campus continues this weekend with the Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships. Matches will get underway Saturday, Oct. 3, and conclude Monday, Oct. 5. First serve is slated for 7:30 a.m. on each of the three days of the event.

Teams will be representing regions from across the state in a weekend of mixed doubles play, squaring off in dual matches made up of three courts. In order to claim the dual victory, a team must win at least two of those courts.

The 7.0 division features 15 teams equally divided into three flights. All flighted duals will take to the courts Saturday and Sunday with the goal to be the top finisher in the flight, thus automatically advancing to Monday’s semifinal round. Joining the trio of flight winners will be one wild card entrant, which will be awarded to the top second-place finisher.

A mere four regions will be vying for the 9.0 title – Broward, Duval, Miami/Dade, and Pinellas. Each team will go head-to-head against the other three twice over the course of the tournament in a double round-robin format, with the squad boasting the best overall record earning the trophy.

Both divisional winners will be crowned Monday afternoon, as the 7.0 competition will contest their semifinals and final while the 9.0 division will hold its final two rounds of play.

Last year’s Championships saw the 7.0 Collier club representing the Pelican Bay Community Park in Naples defeat Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center – Plantation) by a 2-1 margin while Broward’s 9.0 crew from the Midtown Athletic Club in Weston bested Sarasota (Bath and Racquet Club) by the same score line.
