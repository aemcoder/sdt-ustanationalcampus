A massive crowd filled the USTA National Campus’ Collegiate Center to watch a fun-filled evening of tennis, camaraderie and festivities on Feb. 19. With 1,417 in attendance, the Air Force men delivered a strong 4-0 victory over Army, before West Point returned the favor with a come-from-behind 4-3 win on the women’s side.
