USTA Florida 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+

Sectional Championships - Day Two

August 12, 2017

style
article-hero

Three section champions emerged from Saturday’s play at the USTA Florida Adult 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional Championships. That leaves three more divisions to be decided on Sunday after teams survived flight play to move on to the knockout stage.

The squads from Duval and Polk were far and away the top two teams in the only men’s 3.0 flight. The two fittingly met in the closing match of the division, with the winner taking the title and the advancing to the national championship. Polk took both singles positions in straight sets but Duval won doubles lines one and three, also in straight sets. The second doubles spot would be the decider, which went the way of Duval in a tense 3-6, 7-6, 1-0 contest.

All four semifinalists in the men’s 4.0 division went undefeated in round robin action. Hillsborough secured Flight 1 with a win over Duval, as both squads finished with nine individual court victories. Marion claimed Flight 2 after posting a 10-5 individual court record, while Broward was 12-3 in the 15 total lines they contested in Flight 3. North Miami was absolutely dominant in Flight 4, surrendering just one court, three sets and 72 games over their three dual matches. In the semifinals, Hillsborough is matched up with Marion while Broward will take on North Miami.

Hillsborough raced out ahead of the rest of the pack from the start in the men’s 5.0 division and never looked back. Knocking off each of their four opponents over the two days, Hillsborough finished with a team record of 4-0. That included losing a combined 69 games in the four dual contests, while every other team in the division lost over 100 games.

While some flights within the women’s 3.0 division came down to the tiebreaker procedure, Hillsborough battled through Flight 1 unscathed and SMD bested the competition in Flight 4 to book spots in the semifinals. The Hillsborough squad combined to lose 10 sets in three dual matches, while SMD dropped just 14 sets in their four contests. Both Flights 2 and 3 saw a trio of teams end the round robin stage with overall records of 2-1. Alachua claimed Flight 2 after posting 10 wins compared to five losses in their individual singles and doubles lines, while Broward pulled out Flight 3 with a 9-6 individual effort. Hillsborough and Alachua will play in one semifinal; Broward and SMD are set to face off in the other.

The women’s 4.0 division has been whittled down to four teams, as the semifinals are set for Sunday morning. Three of the four semifinalists (Duval, Broward and Polk) went 3-0 in round robin play. Duval breezed through Flight 1, winning 12 of the 15 individual courts they contested. Broward owned Flight 2 after taking 11 total courts, losing only 10 sets and 89 games. Polk emerged from the highly-competitive Flight 4 with a 10-5 match record despite losing 14 sets. The fourth semifinalist, Alachua, will represent Flight 3 after advancing based on having a better individual match score (9-6) than Sarasota/Manatee (8-7) and Marion (8-7). They will battle Polk and Duval will be across the net from Broward on Sunday.

Coming down to the last match of the division, Orange/Seminole/Lake stormed back to claim the women’s 5.0 title over Hillsborough. Having lost to Hillsborough on Friday, the team from Orange/Seminole/Lake needed a win in one of the last duals on court Saturday night. Orange/Seminole/Lake rolled through the lone singles line 6-2, 6-2 and the top doubles position 6-0, 6-4 to go along with Hillsborough winning the second doubles spot 6-2, 4-6, 1-0. That left both teams not only with a 3-1 dual match record, but also an 8-4 slate in individual courts played. Taking the tiebreaking procedure all the way down to sets lost, Orange/Seminole/Lake was named the winner by virtue of eight sets lost compared to nine by Hillsborough.
