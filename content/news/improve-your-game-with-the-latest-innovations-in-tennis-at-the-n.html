A SmartCourt is a system of high-performance full HD cameras installed around a tennis court and connected to the internet – this connection turns a court into a SmartCourt. PlaySight technology leverages on-court multiangle video and proprietary analytics and is powered by a cloud-based platform to enhance training and performance. Simply sign up for a PlaySight account (online, through the free PlaySight app, or on-court), step onto a SmartCourt, and you are ready to play. With its system of cameras and algorithms, each SmartCourt PRO (with five cameras and a courtside kiosk) records, streams and tracks everything that happens, delivering cutting-edge analysis and video tools to professional, collegiate and amateur athletes. Each SmartCourt LIVE (with one camera and an on-court QR code sign) live streams and records video in full HD for after-action review, analysis and sharing.
