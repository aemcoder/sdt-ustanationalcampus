2020 ITF W25 Orlando

Qualifying Complete, Main Draw Underway

November 12, 2020

style
article-hero

Wednesday proved to be another day plagued by rain at the 2020 ITF W25 Orlando event hosted by the USTA National Campus. Even still, the final eight qualifying matches were able to be completed and a handful of main draw singles contests got on court before play was halted.

Three of the qualifying matches pitted two Americans going head-to-head. Ashley Lahey outlasted Emina Bektas in a battle of collegiate standouts by a slim 1-6, 6-3, 10-6 margin while Hurricane Tyra Black held off Eleana Yu 7-6(1), 6-1. Additionally, top seed Alycia Parks defeated Ashlyn Krueger 6-4, 6-3. Rounding out the Americans securing their spot in the main draw was Jessica Failla thanks to her 6-3, 6-3 victory over Ariana Arseneault (Canada).

Other qualifiers included German Vivian Heisen, Emiliana Arango (Colombia), Victoria Rodriguez of Mexico, and Katarina Jokic (Serbia). American Jessica Livianu, who lost to Heisen, also made her way into the main draw as a lucky loser when top seed Anna Kalinskaya (Russia) withdrew.

Just two main draw singles matches finished on Wednesday. Robin Montgomery (USA) fought her way past American wild card Reese Brantmeier 7-6(6), 6-2 and Catherine Harrison (USA) upset No. 4 seed Ankita Raina 6-4, 7-5. 

In other contests stopped in the middle of play, Katie Volynets held a 6-0, 4-5 lead over fellow American Hanna Chang, Jamie Loeb (USA) was on her way to taking out eighth-seeded Gabriela Ce (Brazil) with a 6-1, 3-2 advantage, American Claire Liu was tied at 2-2 in the opening frame with Conny Perrin (Switzerland), and Argentina’s Paula Ormaechea was up a break at 4-2 against Panna Udvardy (Hungary).

Thursday’s slate of matches is set to being at 9 a.m. For more information on the ITF W25 Orlando, click here.
