September 28, 2019

style
article-hero

The Junior Davis Cup and Junior Fed Cup teams have enjoyed a home field advantage this week at the USTA National Campus.

The competition is being held in the United States for the first time since 1999 and will be held in Orlando through 2021.

Toby Kodat, Martin Damm and Dali Blanch represent the U.S. on the Junior Davis Cup team, while Katrina Scott, Connie Ma and Robin Montgomery are on the Junior Fed Cup team.

With an undefated record, the team has shared some big reactions after big points.
