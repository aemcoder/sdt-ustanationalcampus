Get-to-Know Amanda Anisimova

Alyssa Simonin | July 18, 2018

style
article-hero

In 2017 Amanda Anisimova took the US Open Girls’ Singles title, and then turned her sights to the professional rankings. This 16-year-old US Tennis phenom has been training at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, FL to prepare for her return this summer, with hopes to compete again at the US Open.

Amanda had magical run at Indian Wells earlier this year, where she took down Petra Kvitova for the first top-10 win of her career. She’s been sidelined from competition with a foot injury for the past few months, but still remains inside the top 200 at No. 155.

Learn more about Amanda as she makes her assent up the rankings.
