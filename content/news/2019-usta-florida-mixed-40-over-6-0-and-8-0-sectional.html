2019 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional

Summer League Play Kicks Off

May 30, 2019

style
article-hero

Kicking off a busy month of June at the USTA National Campus is the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships. First matches are set to take to the courts at 8 a.m. Saturday morning with divisional champions to be crowned Monday.

Each team dual will feature three courts of doubles, with a team needing to take at least two of those contests to claim victory in the dual. Each court will be the best two-of-three tiebreak sets with a match tiebreak in lieu of a full third.

For the 6.0 division, a total of seven teams have been divided into two flights. The trio of squads in Flight 1 will compete in a double round-robin format while the four teams in Flight 2 will face off with each other just one. The winners of Flights 1 and 2 will move on to Monday morning’s championship round.

In the 8.0 competition, 14 squads have been placed into one of three flights for the weekend. Flight 1 is composed of four teams to go along with Flights 2 and 3 consisting of five. The three flight winners will advance to the semifinals, scheduled for 8 a.m. on Monday, along with the top second-place finisher, which will earn the wild card spot in the final four.  

At last year’s Sectional Championships, Escambia (Independent) defeated Volusia (Florida Tennis Center – Daytona Beach) by a 2-1 margin for the 6.0 title while IRMSL (Buttonwood Tennis Club – Stuart) held off Sarasota (Payne Park) by the same score line in the 8.0 final.
