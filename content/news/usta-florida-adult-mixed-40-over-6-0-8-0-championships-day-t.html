Saturday’s action in the 2017 USTA Florida Adult Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Championships saw one team inch ever closer to clinching the 6.0 divisional title along with the 8.0 division be whittled down from 16 teams to just two.

With just one round of 6.0 dual matches left to be played, Volusia (4-0) remains the lone unbeaten squad. However, as two teams – Alachua and Leon – remain in the hunt with 3-1 records overall, the crown is still up for grabs heading into Sunday. Volusia and Leon are slated to play in the final pool play round, while Alachua will be up against Polk (0-4). Marion (1-3) and Duval (1-3) will also hit the courts to wrap the division up.

After the conclusion of pool play, three of the four teams advancing to the semifinals in the 8.0 division went undefeated. Flight 1 was claimed by Broward, which went 3-0 despite losing the same amount of sets (seven) as and more games (79 compared to 71) than second place SMD. Polk was much more dominant in Flight 2, only dropping two sets en route to a pristine nine wins to zero losses record in its individual matchups. Hillsborough ran away with Flight 3, surrendering five sets but managing to be the only team in the flight with a winning record; the three other teams finished 1-2.

Flight 4 came down to the wire, as three teams went 2-1 in pool play – Pinellas, Lee/Charlotte and Sarasota/Manatee. As a result of losing four courts and a total of 10 sets, Sarasota/Manatee was eliminated from a potential semifinal berth. The comparison between Pinellas and Lee/Charlotte came down to a tiebreaker, which went in favor of Pinellas thanks to their games lost total of 74, five less than Lee/Charlotte’s total of 79.

That set the stage for the afternoon semifinals, where Broward faced off against Polk and Hillsborough took on Pinellas. Both would end up being tense 2-1 encounters, with Broward and Pinellas surviving to battle for the title on Sunday.

Polk raced off to a blazing start versus Broward, winning easily by a margin of 6-2, 6-2 at line two while also claiming the first set at the top position. Broward had taken the first set on court three, but that along with court one would be pushed into decisive super tiebreaks, where Broward managed to pull both out. In the other semifinal, Pinellas turned in a 6-3, 6-3 decision at the third spot but had trouble coming up with that crucial second court win. Hillsborough fought to win court one 2-6, 6-3, 1-0, but it wasn’t enough thanks to Pinellas narrowly winning line two 6-4, 4-6, 1-0.
