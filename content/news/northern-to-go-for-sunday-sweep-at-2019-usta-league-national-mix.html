Day two of the 2019 USTA League National Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus, wrapped up quickly on Saturday, as the fields were whittled down to the final four in both divisions. Highlighting the action was both Northern squads advancing to the semifinals, giving the section a chance for a sweep of the titles on Sunday.

In the 7.0 competition, exactly four teams posted unscathed 4-0 records to book qualify for the semifinals. Florida (City of Oakland Park) was an absolute dominant force over the course of Friday and Saturday to earn the top seed, not dropping a single court of the 12 they contested while also collecting 24 sets compared to losing only four. Finishing right behind them for second was Hawaii (Independent – Oahu), with 11 individual matchups and 22 sets to their credit.

Southern (Burns Park Tennis – Little Rock, Ark.) produced a pair of sweeps in their two dual matches on Saturday to vault up into the third slot, totaling 10 court victories on the strength of 21 sets won. Finally, Northern (Bearpath Golf and Country Club – Eden Prairie, Minn.) was part of the final team match out on court Saturday afternoon, as they held off Pacific Northwest to finish a perfect 4-0 despite earning just eight individual victories.

For the 7.0 semifinals, Florida will go up against Northern while Hawaii will take on Southern.

Claiming the No. 1 seed for the 9.0 semifinals was the crew from Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Northern Va.) with a 4-0 team record that including taking 10 of the 12 courts their players contested. Meanwhile, Northern (Life Time Athletic – White Bear Lake, Minn.) and Northern California (St. Ignatius College Prep – San Francisco) also got through to Sunday without suffering a defeat. Northern secured the second seed with nine individual matchups and 19 sets under their belt; Northern California’s numbers were just slightly below at eight court wins on the strength of 18 sets.

The group from Pacific Northwest (Robinswood Tennis Center – Bellevue, Wash.) opened the day unbeaten, but things did not stay that way for long after being bested by Northern California in the morning. That put all the pressure on their afternoon dual with Eastern, in which they needed a sweep to have any hope of reaching the playoff rounds. Their players answered the call, emerging victorious at all three lines, pushing them just ahead of Southern California for the fourth and final seed.

In the 9.0 semifinals, Pacific Northwest will be aiming to topple Mid-Atlantic and Northern will be across the net from Northern California.
