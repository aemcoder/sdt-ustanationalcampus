He’s big guy with big slides. #USTennis up-and-comer Chris Eubanks, currently ranking in the top 200 in the world, calls the USTA National Campus home for working on his game. The 22 year-old Atlanta, GA native who stands at a soaring 6’ 7’’ definitely leaves his mark on the USTA Player Development red clay courts.

This two-time ACC Player of the Year from Georgia Tech is seeing his career heat up after capturing his first ATP Challenger title in Leon, Mexico this past April. He just recently made his first trip to Paris for the 2018 French Open.
