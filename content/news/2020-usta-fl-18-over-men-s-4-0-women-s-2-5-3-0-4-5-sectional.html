Final League Event of 2020

December 11, 2020

style
article-hero

The final tournament of the USTA Florida 2020 calendar is scheduled to get underway Saturday, Dec. 12, as the 2020 18 & Over Men’s 4.0 & Women’s 2.5/3.0/4.5 Sectional Championships will take place on the grounds of the USTA National Campus. A total of four team titles will be on the line at the three-day league event.

Teams will be competing in dual matches consisting of three matchups in total – one singles and two doubles; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

A total of 11 teams are slated to compete in the men’s 4.0 division at the event. Groups of four have been placed in Flights 1 and 2 with the remaining trio making up Flight 3. The flight duals will play out over Saturday and Sunday, leading into Monday’s knockout stages featuring the three flight winners plus the top second-place finisher.

The 3.0 women’s division is slated to be the largest, as 14 squads are making their way to Orlando. The first two days of the tournament will see those crews compete within their respective flights in order to advance to the divisional semifinals. The competition consists of three flights overall, meaning there will also be a wild card slot up for grabs for those not landing atop the standings in a flight.

For the 4.5 women, there will be just two days of matches. The eight teams have been divided equally into two flights, with the two flight champions moving on to play for the title Sunday afternoon. Meanwhile, the women’s 2.5 competition will be the only one kicking off its round-robin play on Sunday. With just five squads entered, the club with the best record following four rounds of duals will claim the sectional crown.

Bringing home titles at last season’s event were the 4.0 men representing Broward (Weston Tennis Center), the 2.5 Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) women, the 3.0 women’s team of Duval (San Jose Country Club – Jacksonville), and the 4.5 women’s squad from Broward (Lauderdale Tennis Club – Ft. Lauderdale).
