2022 Special Olympics Florida State Tennis Championships

Athletes Will be in Action May 6-7

March 30, 2022

style
article-hero

The USTA National Campus is once again set to host the Special Olympics Florida State Tennis Championships. The 2022 edition of the competition, featuring 190 athletes, will take place May 6 and 7. Events included in the Championships include singles, individual skills, and unified doubles.

Friday’s schedule will see match play take place from 12 p.m. to 5 p.m. with the Opening Ceremony slated to begin at 7:15 p.m. Matches will continue on Saturday, with first serve at 8 a.m.

The Championships will also bring 200 volunteers, 50 coaches, 50 family members, and 50 other delegates to the National Campus.

Publix serves as the premier Games sponsor. For more information about Special Olympics Florida, click here.
