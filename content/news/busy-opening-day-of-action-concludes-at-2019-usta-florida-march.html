Busy Opening Day of Action

The 2019 USTA Florida March Level 4 Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus, got underway Saturday, March 16. The tournament’s eight draw got through two round of play on the day, leaving the main draws at the quarterfinal stage.

Each division features a draw of 32 with a feed-in consolation through the round of 16. All matches will be two out of three tiebreak sets with a 10-point match tiebreak to be played in lieu of a full third set.

The boys’ 12s draw saw just one upset on the day, with No. 8 Brennon Chow (Miami) falling to Felix Puyplat (Ocean Ridge) 6-3, 3-6, 10-5 in the round of 16. The only other seed to drop a set was fifth seed Sammy Nieder (Miami Beach), who narrowly escaped an opening round upset by outlasting Jeremiah Braswell (Sarasota) 4-6, 6-2, 10-6.

A much different story played out in the boys’ 14s, where half of the seeded competitors suffered defeat on Saturday. Both No. 4 Tanner Povey (Indian Rocks Beach) and eighth-seeded Calvin Wellman (Boynton Beach) were taken out in the opening round at the hands of Roy Horovitz (Key Biscayne) and Daniel Schaffer, respectively. Joining them in the consolation draw to close out the day were fifth seed Nathan Blokhin (Plantation) after he was bested by Kaj Quirjins (Naples) 6-1, 7-6(3) and No. 7 Abhishek Thorat (Lithia) thanks to a solid effort by Mikel Anderson (Hillsboro Beach), propelling him to a 7-5, 6-4 victory.

The boys’ 16s competition experienced a major shakeup right from the start, with four of the eight seeds immediately dismissed to the consolation draw. The highest-rated player to stumble was No. 3 Andrew Pereverzev (Hallandale Beach), who lost 7-5, 6-3 to Alexandre Moutran (Reunion). Stumbling out of the gate as well was fifth-seeded Nicholas Olomu (Melbourne), No. 6 Daniel Green (Boca Raton), and eighth seed Daniel Blackman (Orlando). Olomu could not battle back against Renzo Fassioli (Miami), eventually going down 6-3, 2-6, 10-2, while Green was outplayed by Sebastian Rondon (Hallandale Beach) 6-3, 6-4; Blackman dropped a tough opening set to Myles Decoste (Stuart) 7-6(8) before being forced to retire.

In boys’ 18s play, the bottom half of the draw stayed completely intact with both third-seeded Andres Golindano (Plantation) and No. 8 Maks Silagy (Bradenton) were removed from the top half. The two suffered similar fates, letting a lead evaporate before just being edged out in the match tiebreak, Golindano in the second round at the hand of Adam Duan (Davie) and Silagy in his opening round encounter with Ryan Fung (Miami). Fourth seed Jonah Braswell (Sarasota) was pushed to the limit twice on Saturday, needing to survive match tiebreak scores of 11-9 and 10-6 to reach the quarterfinals.

Turning the attention to the girls’ side of the event, it was mostly smooth sailing for the seeds in the 12s draw, with just No. 3 Amber Beautelus (Miami) faltering 6-2, 6-2 to Sofiya Janos (Riverview). However, top seed Leila Javaheri (Longwood) and No. 2 Aida Oviedo (Port Saint Lucie) could be on a collision court after surrendering a combined five games to being play.

All four seeded individuals in the top half of the girls’ 14s draw found themselves in some trouble Saturday. When all was said and done, only No. 1 Lexington Reed (Orlando) navigated her way to the quarterfinals, which was no easy feat thanks to the play of Olivia Bustos (Doral); Reed survived to claim a marathon 6-7(2), 6-2, 11-9 victory. The bottom half nearly held true to form, but fifth-seeded Mary Boyce Deatherage (Maitalnd) came up on the short end of a 2-6, 6-3, 10-4 score line versus Isabella Pampulov (Delray Beach).

The six girls’ 16s seed moving through to the quarterfinals did so fairly comfortably, all producing straight set efforts Saturday’s two round of play. The other two who were not as fortunate were No. 6 Emma Fernald (Saint Petersburg) and seventh seed Anna Babayan (Bradenton), both victims of first round upsets.

Saturday was mostly comfortable for the eight seeds in the girls’ 18s as well, with just No. 7 Isabella Montana (Cutler Bay) heading into Sunday in the consolation draw. The others played their way into the quarterfinals with relative ease, combining to lose a mere two sets in total.

Matches continue at 8 a.m. Sunday morning with the main draw quarterfinals.
