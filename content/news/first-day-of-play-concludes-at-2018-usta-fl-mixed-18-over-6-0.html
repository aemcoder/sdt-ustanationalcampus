Play kicked off at the 2018 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus, Saturday morning. Action ran through the afternoon, with all teams getting two dual matches under their belt.

Polk (Lake Wales Country Club) has taken over control of Flight 1 in the 6.0 competition, recording a team win over both Alachua (Westside Park Gainesville Rec Department) and Hillsborough (Grand Hampton). Another defeat of Alachua would send Polk to the championship dual. In Flight 2, Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) and Volusia (Florida Tennis Center) sit atop the standings at 2-0. The winner of their matchup tomorrow looks to be in position to advance, while Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) remains a factor after going 1-1 on Saturday.

For the 8.0 division, both Okaloosa (Bluewater Bay Tennis Center) in Flight 1 and Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park) in Flight 2 are one team victory shy of locking up a spot in the semifinals. Sarasota (Payne Park) has dominated Flight 3 so far, but Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) also brings a 2-0 record into Sunday. In Flight 4, Hillsborough (Hunters Green Sports Center) was the only team to win twice on the day, while a trio of squads is just behind with one win and one loss.

It’s Hillsborough (Macfarlane Park) who also has a firm grasp on the only flight of the 10.0 competition. To open play, they swept through Alachua (Haile Plantation Country Club) and also bested Pinellas (Clearwater). All three of those teams will square off again Sunday.

Matches will take to the courts starting at 8:30 a.m.
