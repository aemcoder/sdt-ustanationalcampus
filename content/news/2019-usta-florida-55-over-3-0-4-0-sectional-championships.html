2019 USTA Florida 55 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships

Sunshine State League Play Back in Action

May 1, 2019

style
article-hero

The lone USTA Florida Adult League event held at the USTA National Campus for the month of May is set to take to the courts this weekend, with the section’s best squads in the 55 & Over 3.0/4.0 divisions slated to be in action. First matches will get underway the morning of Saturday, May 4 and run through Monday, May 6.

Teams will compete in round-robin dual matches over the course of the first two days of the event in flight play. Each dual match will be made up of three individual doubles contests, with a squad needing to win at least two of those courts for the dual victory.

In the women’s 3.0 division, a total of 12 teams have been equally divided into three flights. Moving into the divisional semifinals will be the three flight winners plus the top second-place finisher from all of the flights as a wild card. Those semifinal matchups are scheduled for Sunday at 2 p.m.  

Mirroring the women’s 3.0 layout is the men’s 4.0 division, where a trio of flights will each see four teams battling for top position and an automatic berth into the semifinals. A wild card entrant will round out the semifinalists, with those duals also to be contested at 2 p.m. on Sunday. 

Looking at the women’s 4.0 competition, Flight 1 has four teams while Flights 2 and 3 each contain five squads overall. The division will see flight duals played out over Saturday and Sunday, leading to Monday morning’s semifinals at 8:30 a.m.  

In the 2018 Sectional Championships, the women representing Duval (Southside Tennis Complex – Jacksonville) claimed the 3.0 title. For the 4.0 competitions, Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) took home the women’s crown while Hillsborough (River Hills Country Club – Valrico) prevailed on the men’s side.
