Heavy Rain Cuts Saturday Short

While a majority of dual matches were completed, heavy rainfall shut down the opening day of the 2018 USTA Florida Adult 55 & Over 3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships early Saturday afternoon.

In the 3.5 men’s competition, just one contest – SMD (Coral Pine) vs. Alachua (JTC) – went unfinished. That was in Flight 2, where Hillsborough (Temple Terrace) sits atop the standings at 2-0, but SMD can also reach that mark by defeating Alachua. All duals were completed in Flight 1, with Broward (WTC) the only undefeated squad remaining in the four-team flight.

All teams in the 3.5 women’s division played one match on Saturday with the exception of Marion (SKY) in Flight 1, who will go into day two still 0-0. Sarasota (BNR Encore) bested IRMSL (Magnolia Lakes) to grab the inside track in that flight. Flights 2 and 3 each have a pair of squads at 1-0 and a pair at 0-1 so far. Sarasota (Sarasota National) is joined by Pinellas (Countryside) at the top of Flight 2; Alachua (DB) and Collier (Naples Beach Club) lead in Flight 3.

Hillsborough (Palma Ceia) looks to be the heavy favorite for the 4.5 men’s title heading into Sunday. With two duals under their belt, Hillsborough is 2-0 after winning all six individual courts contested and dropping only three sets. Duval (Deercreek Country Club) is the only other squad without a loss in the division, having played just one team match so far.

All six women’s 4.5 teams got two duals in the books on Saturday. After the dust settled, it appears the divisional crown with come down to the head-to-head battle between Alachua (Azalea Park) and Hillsborough (Cheval). Both went 2-0 on the opening day, with Alachua the more dominant of the two by virtue of surrendering just one set in six individual matchups.

Play is set to resume Sunday morning at 8:00 a.m.

The 2017 3.5/4.5 Sectional Championships showed strong competition. In the 3.5 women’s division, Collier County defeated Escambia County 3-0. Duval County defeated Collier County 2-1 to claim the 3.5 men’s title. In the 4.5 divisions, both champions went undefeated, as the Orange County women posted a 3-0 record while the Broward men finished 4-0.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
