Four Crowns on the Line Monday

December 8, 2019

style
article-hero

Flight play concluded on Sunday at the 2019 UST A Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. The event now moves into the business end of play with the divisional semifinal and championship rounds.

Both Broward (Bonaventure Town Center – Ft. Lauderdale) and Escambia (Independent) capped off undefeated runs through flight play in the men’s 6.5 division, with Broward claiming five court victories and 10 sets in Flight 1 and Escambia collecting nine individual matchups and 19 sets in Flight 2. They will now square off at 9:00 a.m on Monday with the sectional title on the line.

The 6.5 women’s competition was the lone division to play out its semifinal round on Sunday. Duval (Jewish Community Alliance – Jacksonville), SMD (Kendall Tennis Training Center – Miami), and Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) all navigated their way to the top of their respective flight unscathed. However, Flight 4 ended in a three-way tie, which eventually broke in the favor of SMD (Don Shula’s Athletic Club – Hialeah) thanks to their players coming through in seven of the nine matchups they contested.

In the semifinals, which ended in similar 2-1 final results, SMD (Kendall Tennis Training Center) held off Duval while Broward downed SMD (Don Shula’s Athletic Club).

Sunday also saw the squads book their spots in the men’s and women’s 8.5 semifinals. On the men’s side, Broward (Weston Tennis Center) out of Flight 2 and Sarasota (Lakewood National – Bradenton) form Flight 3 will bring unbeaten records into Monday. Joining them will be Flight 1 winner Hillsborough (Sandra W Freedman Tennis Complex – Tampa), who virtually had a semifinal berth locked up before the day began, and wild card entrant Duval (Boone Park Tennis Courts - Jacksonville), the second-place finisher in Flight 2. The semifinals will pit Duval against Sarasota while Broward will be across the net from Hillsborough.

The 8.5 women’s squads still in the hunt for the crown include undefeated Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) and Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club); the two crews posted almost identical numbers, with Orange/Seminole earning seven courts and 15 sets to keep pace with Hillsborough’s seven individual matchups and 16 sets. A tie atop the Flight 3 standings worked out in the favor of both Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Center – Tarpon Springs) and Escambia (Independent). After the two teams finished with 3-1 overall records, Pinellas claimed the automatic berth into the final four while Escambia got in via the wild card slot. Setting the stage for Monday, Orange/Seminole has drawn Pinellas and Hillsborough will clash with Escambia.
