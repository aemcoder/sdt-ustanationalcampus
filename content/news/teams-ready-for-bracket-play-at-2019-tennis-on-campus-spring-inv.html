Teams Ready for Bracket Play

Day one of the 2019 Tennis on Campus Spring Invitational, hosted by the USTA National Campus, saw each of the 48 teams complete three round robin matches over the course of Friday.

All dual matches are contested using the World TeamTennis (WTT) format, consisting of five no-ad sets – men’s singles, men’s doubles, women’s singles, women’s doubles, and mixed doubles; mixed doubles is always played last.

The 48 teams were divided into 12 four-team pools for the round robin portion of the weekend. Teams were then placed into brackets based on the overall pool play results. The Gold Bracket features the top 16 teams, while the Silver Bracket is composed of teams 17-24. Teams that finished in the 25-32 slots will compete in the Bronze Bracket, with the squads taking spots 33-40 going into the Copper Bracket. Rounding out bracket play will be teams 41-48 in the Titanium Bracket.

Teams winning their respective pool to lock up a spot in the Gold Bracket were Oregon (Pool A), UCLA (Pool B), Georgia (Pool C), Cornell (Pool D), defending champion Texas (Pool E), Cal Poly (Pool F), Pittsburgh (Pool G), Boston University (Pool H), Wash U (Pool I), Minnesota (Pool J), and Tennessee (Pool L). Rounding out the Gold Bracket are second-place finishers Arkansas, Butler, Rowan, UC Davis, and UCF.

Bracket play matches will take to the courts starting at 9 a.m. on Saturday. Sunday will kick off at 8 a.m., including the Gold Bracket semifinals, with the championship match slated for approximately noon.
