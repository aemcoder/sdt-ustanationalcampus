Saturday Kicked Off Match Play

April 18, 2021

All five divisions were in action on Saturday as the USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Championships kicked off its match play at the USTA National Campus.

The divisions in action this weekend are the 6.0, 7.0 and 8.0 women along with the 7.0 and 8.0 men. Teams are competing in dual matches that consist of three doubles contests; they must win at least two of those three courts to claim the overall victory.

Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) defeated both Duval (Southside Tennis Complex – Jacksonville) and Leon (Forestmeadows Park & Athletic Center – Tallahassee) by a 2-1 margin to sit atop the women’s 6.0 standings. They are another dual win over second-place Duval, which bested winless Leon, on Sunday away from clinching the divisional title.

In women’s 7.0 action, Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club – Tarpon Springs) took control of Flight 1 as the lone unbeaten while all four squads in Flight 2 went 1-1 on the day. Pinellas prevailed in five of the six individual matchups their players competed in, dropping just three sets in total. IRMSL (The Boulevard Tennis Club – Vero Beach) goes into day two as the technical frontrunner of Flight 2 thanks to owning nine set victories while the other three teams collected seven.

Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) took the early lead in the six-team women’s 8.0 competition with two dual triumphs on Saturday. Four teams are currently tied for second with one win and one loss, meaning there is still lots of potential movement that could take place over the next two days of play.

With two rounds of matches left in the men’s 7.0 division, it appears the title will be decided when Collier (Club at Longshore Lake – Naples) and Marion (Glenview Champions Tennis Club – The Villages) square off on Sunday. Both ended the opening day with a 2-0 record on the strength of five court victories and 11 sets earned. Duval (Baymeadows Tennis Center – Jacksonville) split their two duals on Saturday, leaving them mathematically in the hunt as well.

The two flights of the men’s 8.0 division have played out in very similar fashions so far. Pinellas (McMullen Tennis Complex – Clearwater) and Duval (Magnolia Point Golf & Country Club – Green Cove Springs) are one team win away from taking Flight 1 and Flight 2, respectively. Furthermore, with 2-0 records, they are both slated to face the only winless squad in their flight.

Sunday’s schedule of play gets underway at 7:30 a.m. For more information on the USTA Florida Adult 65 & Over Sectional Championships, click here.
