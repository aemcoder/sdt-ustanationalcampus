2019 USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Championships

Marion 7.0, Broward 8.0 Men Take Titles

April 15, 2019

style
article-hero

The men’s 7.0 and 8.0 divisional champions were crowned on Monday to conclude the 2019 USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Championships at the National Campus. Coming away with team titles was Marion (Glenview Champions Tennis Club – The Villages) in the 7.0 competition and Broward (Parkland Tennis Center) in 8.0 play.

They join the 6.0 Collier (The Players Club and Spa – Naples), 7.0 Duval (Boone Park - Jacksonville), and 8.0 Pinellas (City of Clearwater) women’s squads as sectional champions from the event.

Marion fell short of turning in a perfect 5-0 performance in the 7.0 division after being defeated by Collier (Club at Longshore Lake – Naples) Monday morning. That left the two squads with similar 4-1 overall records, meaning the winner would be decided by the tiebreaking procedure. In the end, Marion’s comprehensive efforts over the course of the three days paid off. Thanks to owning a total of 11 court victories, compared to 10 for Collier, Marion claimed the trophy.

Captained by Michael James, the full Marion roster includes John Ulrich, Robert Anglace, Robert Kluemper, David O’Steen, James Robertson, Tim Puckett, Juno Guevara, David Barnes, James VanEmburgh, Carlos Munoz, and Paul Whitehead.

In the men’s 8.0 semifinals, Broward (Parkland Tennis Center) bested Okaloosa/Walton (TOPS’L Beach and Racquet Resort – Miramar Beach) 2-1 and Collier (Lighthouse Bay – Bonita Springs) swept Pinellas (City of Clearwater) 3-0. Broward claimed court one 6-0, 7-5 and toughed out a 6-3, 7-5 decisions on court two for the win; Collier breezed through line one 6-4, 6-0 and line three 6-1, 6-0 to go along with a lengthy 5-7, 7-6, 1-0 effort at the second spot to complete the sweep.

The championship dual would go down to the wire, where Broward outlasted Collier, 2-1. A quick 6-0, 6-2 win by Russell Latham and John Dolle at line one got Collier off on the right foot, but Broward earned the opening sets on the other two courts. Collier managed to hang tough, taking close second sets at each position to force decisive match tiebreaks. At the second spot, Kevin McCarthy and William Dellinger came through 7-6, 4-6, 1-0 for Broward while Johnny Cisneros and Malcolm Waldron III prevailed 6-2, 5-7, 1-0 at line three.

Captain Les Liese’s complete Broward squad consists of Waldron III, Dellinger, McCarthy, Cisneros, Howard Weinstein, Carlos Rios, Charles Fox, and Arnold Resnick.
