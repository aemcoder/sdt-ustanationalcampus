7.0 Semis, 9.0 Final Set for Sunday

A lengthy rain delay on Saturday did not prevent the completion of round robin action in the USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships.

Rounding out play in the 7.0 division, four teams remained in the hunt for the sectional crown. Hillsborough (4-0) emerged from Flight 1 after taking down the rest of the competition, clinching its spot in the semifinals behind 10 individual court wins. Leon also had little trouble navigating its way through Flight 2, posting a perfect 4-0 record that included a mere five sets lost.

Flight 3 went to Bay, which did manage to go undefeated even though the team as a whole lost 104 games and a total of nine sets. Polk was an absolutely dominant force in Flight 4, producing a sterling 12-for-12 effort in its individual matchups, surrendering just two sets and giving up a grand total of 60 games.

The 7.0 semifinals will be contested at 8:30 am Sunday morning, as Hillsborough will face Leon and Bay is slated to go up against Polk.

The 9.0 sectional championship will be decided when Pinellas and Escambia meet in Sunday’s final. Pinellas (4-0) bested the competition in Flight 1 in commanding fashion, taking 11 courts while only losing one. Escambia emerged from Flight 2 unscathed, but it did not come easily. Losing a compiled 97 games, Escambia also dropped nine sets and pulled out three of its four dual matches by a 2-1 margin. The two squads will do battle at 9:00 am.
