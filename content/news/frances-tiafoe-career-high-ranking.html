USTA National Campus resident star, Frances Tiafoe achieves a career-high ranking of No. 52 after reaching the quarterfinals at the Fever-Tree Championships in London. He then secures his deepest advancement at Wimbledon on Thursday, with a 4-6, 6-3, 6-4, 6-2 victory over Frenchman J. Benneteau. How far will he go on grass?
