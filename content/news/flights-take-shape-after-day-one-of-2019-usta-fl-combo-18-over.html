Flights Take Shape After Day One

December 7, 2019

style
article-hero

Saturday marked the opening day of the 2019 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. All squads took part in two dual matches over the course of the day as part of the battle to advance to Monday’s championship rounds.

The 6.5 men’s final is shaping up to be a battle between Broward (Bonaventure Town Center – Ft. Lauderdale) and Escambia (Independent). Broward is just one dual win away from clinching Flight 1 following a 2-0 effort on the strength of five court victories, while Escambia has a little more work to do with a pair of matches on the schedule for Sunday in Flight 2 after picking up four individual matchups.

In the 6.5 women’s competition, a quartet of squads got through Saturday undefeated and stands one team win away from Monday’s semifinal round. SMD (Kendall Tennis Training Center – Miami) and Duval (Jewish Community Alliance – Jacksonville) were dominant in Flight 2 and Flight 1, respectively. Both claimed five of the six courts their players contested thanks to 11 sets earned, with SMD dropping just to sets and Duval giving away five. Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) sits atop Flight 3 with four individual matchups and nine sets won, the exact same numbers posted by Hillsborough (Arbor Greene Club – Tampa) in Flight 4.

No squad was more dominant on Saturday than Hillsborough (Sandra W Freedman Tennis Complex – Tampa) in the 8.5 men’s division. They took complete control of Flight one by sweeping all six courts in their two duals, impressively winning 12 of the 13 sets played. Broward (Weston Tennis Center) also appears to be on their way to winning Flight 2 after gathering up five court victories with 10 sets won, while a highly competitive Flight 3 belongs to Sarasota (Lakewood National – Bradenton) heading into Sunday.

For the women’s 8.5 flights, Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) and Escambia (Independent) looked dominant on the opening day; Orange/Seminole just needs to win their only match on Sunday to earn the semifinal bid from Flight 1 while Escambia still finds themselves to duals away from the semifinals in the five-team Flight 3. Meanwhile, Flight 2 will go to the victor between Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club) and Collier (Pelican Bay Community Park – Naples), while the loser will still be in contention for the wild card slot in the semifinals.

Matches continue Sunday starting at 8 a.m. For more information on the 2019 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 Sectional Championships, click here. Be sure to share your experience from the USTA National Campus using #NationalCampus.
