Men's 15k Futures Recap

March 7, 2017

The USTA Pro Circuit Men's 15k Futures event came to a conclusion this past weekend at the National Campus at Lake Nona in Orlando, Fla., crowning champions in both singles and doubles. 

The singles side of the action saw unseeded Dominik Keopfer of Germany edge No. 3 seed Tommy Paul of the U.S. in the final, 4-6, 6-3, 7-5, while the all-American team of Connor Smith and Rhyne Williams beat Jaume Pla Malfieto and Ramkumar Ramanathan, 6-4, 6-4, to claim the doubles title.

Ramanathan, the top seed in the singles draw, was knocked out early in the second round. 

Fans at the National Campus took in the final between Tommy Paul and Dominik Koepfer.
