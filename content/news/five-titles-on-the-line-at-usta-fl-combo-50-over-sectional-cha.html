Five Titles on the Line

February 4, 2021

article-hero

Sunshine State league action picks back up at the USTA National Campus, as a total of five titles will be on the line over the course of the three-day USTA Florida Combo 50 & Over Sectional Championships. Matches begin Friday, Feb. 5, at 7:30 a.m. and will run through the morning of Sunday, Feb. 7.

Teams will be competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Both 7.5 divisions along with the women’s 6.5 competition will get underway on the opening day of the event. The 7.5 men features five squads battling in one lone flight, while the 6.5 and 7.5 women are each made up of eight teams equally divided into two flights.

Just two crews are in the running to take home the men’s 6.5 and men’s 8.5 crowns; Marion and Alachua will square off in 6.5 action to go along with Broward and Orange/Seminole/Lake going head-to-head for the 8.5 title.

Defending champions include the Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) 6.5 men, the Escambia (Independent) 6.5 women, the Collier (Vineyards Golf & Country Club – Naples) 7.5 men, the Escambia (Independent) 7.0 women, and the South Miami/Dade (Don Shula’s Athletic Club – Hialeah) 8.5 men.
