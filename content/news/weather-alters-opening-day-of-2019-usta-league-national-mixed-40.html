Weather Alters Opening Day

November 15, 2019

style
article-hero

A rainy morning and chilly evening did not make for the most ideal of settings for the opening day of the 2019 USTA League National Mixed 40 & Over Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus, but in the end all matches originally scheduled to take to the courts on Friday were played and completed. In order for that to be the case, a shortened scoring format was utilized.

In the 6.0 division, just four squads will start day two without at least one loss. Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Fulton, Md.) put together a pair of convincing efforts to claim all six courts they contested with 12 sets won, while Pacific Northwest (Eastside Tennis Center – Kirkland, Wash.) also had a solid opening day after taking five individual matchups and 11 sets in total. Eastern (Centercourt of Marlboro – Morganville, N.J.) and Intermountain (Las Vegas) found the road to 2-0 much tougher, but they battled their way to currently sit within the coveted top four slots. However, Eastern and Intermountain are set to square off on Saturday, which will open the door for the eight teams with 1-1 records.

For the 7.0 competition, Texas (Independent – Plano) headlined the group of five squads making their way through Friday’s slate of matches without suffering a defeat. The Lone Star State crew came out on top in all six of their individual matchups after winning 12 sets. The other four 2-0 clubs, achieving that record with five court victories and 10 sets earned, were Southern (Southaven Tennis Center – Southaven, Miss.), Southern California (Alta Vista Racquet Center – Redondo Beach), Northern California (Independent – Santa Clara), and Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Columbia, Md.). Also staying alive for a potential semifinal spot were the seven teams with one dual win and one dual loss over the course of Friday.

Southern California (Fountain Valley Tennis Center) paced a group of six squads to a 2-0 finish at the end of the opening day of play by going a perfect 12-of-12 in sets played to sweep all six individual matchups. A quartet of teams checked in just behind them with five courts won; Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Fairfax, Va.), Middle States (Nassau Racquet & Tennis Club – Skillman, N.J.), and Intermountain (Las Vegas) all secured 11 sets while Florida (Longboat Key Tennis Center) managed to reel in 10.

Finally, Pacific Northwest (Tualatin Hills Tennis Center – Beaverton, Ore.) was challenged in both of their duals, but managed to come out on top 2-1 in both to remain unbeaten. A total of five crews split their matches on day one, leaving them with work to do but still in the hunt for a spot in the divisional semifinals.

Five of the 15 teams competing for the 9.0 title will head into Saturday without a loss. Both Southern (Memphis University School – Memphis, Tenn.) and Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Washington, D.C.) were dominant forces, not only going 2-0 but doing so without surrendering one individual matchup; Southern also did not give away a single set while Mid-Atlantic dropped just two.

Sitting behind them is Florida (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston), with five courts to their credit on the strength of 11 sets won, and Missouri Valley (Hanscom Tennis Center – Omaha, Neb.), also with five individual matchups claimed thanks to 10 sets won. Rounding out the unscathed squads is Pacific Northwest (Boeing Employees Tennis Club – Kent, Wash.) after just escaping defeat twice on the day; their players rounded up four courts and nine sets overall. An additional five clubs went 1-1 over the course of Friday, meaning two-thirds of the division is still in the running for the semifinals.
