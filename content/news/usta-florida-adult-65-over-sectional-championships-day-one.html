Day one of three is in the books for the USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Championships after a busy Saturday at the USTA National Campus. Every team in the event played a pair of matches to kick off the action.

In the dual for the women’s 6.0 crown, Duval (Southside Tennis Center) bested Collier (Pelican Marsh) twice on Saturday. Both matchups were 2-1 decisions in favor of Duval, meaning they need to defeat Collier just once on Sunday to clinch the divisional title.

Hillsborough (Temple Terrace) and Collier (Players Club) sit atop the lone flight of the men’s 7.0 competition after the opening day. Both squads won five of the six individual courts they contested. Hillsborough turned in the more dominant performance of the two, dropping a mere two sets and 35 games, while Collier lost five sets and 52 games.

The two flights composing the women’s 7.0 division present quite an opposite picture to one another heading into Sunday. Flight 1 leaders Hillsborough (Plant City) and Pinellas (Seminole Lakes Golden Aces) swept through their duals, surrendering a combined three sets. Those two squads will clash on day two to determine who moves on to the divisional championship. In Flight 2, all five teams posted a 1-1 record; Collier (World Tennis Center) currently owns the top spot thanks to taking four individual courts.

For the men’s 8.0 division, Hillsborough (Riverhills) is in good shape to advance to the divisional final after a solid opening day. They are the only undefeated team in Flight 1, with a trio of teams sitting at 1-1 overall.  Pinellas (Shipwatch) and Collier (Lighthouse Bay) look destined to determine with Flight 2 representative when they meet on Sunday. Both went 6-for-6 in individual matchups contested; Pinellas did not lose a single set while Collier gave away a slim 16 games.

Collier and Pinellas have far and away been the class of the women’s 8.0 division as well. Collier (Naples Grande) is the Flight 1 leader thanks to a duo of sweeps on Saturday, dropping just one set in the process. However, Pinellas (McMullen) have been even more impressive so far, claiming every set they contested with a mere 23 games lost.

Play will continue at 8 a.m. on Sunday. A full listing of the divisional flights and standings can be found here.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
