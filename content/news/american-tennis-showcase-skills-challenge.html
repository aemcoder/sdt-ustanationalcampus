Top American pros end their pre-season training block in front of the fans at the USTA National Campus. Team USA broke into two teams, Team Stars and Team Stripes, to test their skills at target hitting. 

 Madison Keys, Bjorn Fratangelo, Caroline Dolehide, Usue Arconada and Tenny Sandgren competed for Team Stars. While Mackie McDonald, Cici Bellis, Denis Kudla, Hailey Baptiste and Thai Kwiatkowski represented Team Stripes. See who took home the skills competition title. 

Follow Team USA into the 2020 season on social media with #TeamUSATennis.
