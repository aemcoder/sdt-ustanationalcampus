Lengthy Semis Highlight Saturday

February 20, 2021

While just three matches were on the schedule for Saturday, more than seven hours of tennis were played at the ITF W25 Orlando event.

In the day’s first semifinal, Great Britain’s Katie Swan upset third seed Renata Zarazua (Mexico) in a 7-5, 7-6(6) contest that lasted nearly three hours. After trading early breaks in the opening frame, the match stayed on serve until Swan secured the decisive break in the last game of the set.

Zarazua claimed a 4-2 advantage in the second before Swan leveled things at 4-4. The No. 3 seed had two break points for 5-4 but couldn’t convert; however, Zarazua did earn the right to serve for the set by breaking at 5-5, only to give the break back once again. In the tiebreak, Swan won four of the first five points yet still had to fight off a set point at 6-5; she would take the final three points to close out the victory.

The second semifinal eclipsed the three-hour mark, as Robin Anderson outlasted Claire Liu 6-3, 5-7, 7-5 in an all-American matchup. Both players struggled on serve consistently, with the first set alone featuring six double faults and five breaks. A string of three breaks in a row was ended by Anderson when she served out the set, and she appeared to be in firm control up 3-1 in the second. Even after Liu got back on serve for her first lead of the day at 4-3, it was Anderson who inched ahead with another break to serve for the match. With the finish line in sight, Anderson squandered two match points – the first on a double fault – and couldn’t regain momentum; Liu took full advantage to extend the match to a third frame.

A quick break of serve for each competitor began the decisive third, but this time it would be Liu pulling ahead by a seemingly comfortable 5-2 margin. She was two points from victory before Anderson held, which was followed by Liu being broken at love. With Anderson serving down 5-4, Liu got to her first and only match point of the contest, but once again Anderson dug deep for 5-5. The next game lasted a total of 14 points, ending with Liu dropping her serve one last time; Anderson faced some pressure at deuce while serving for the match, but managed to fight through her nerves for the win.

Anderson and Swan are slated to square off in the singles final at 11 a.m. Sunday.

The doubles final, which began the day’s matches, also split sets but still concluded in a tidy 82 minutes. Emina Bektas (United States) and Tara Moore (Great Britain) defeated Colombia’s Mario Camila Osorio Serrano and Switzerland’s Conny Perrin 7-5, 2-6, 10-5. The victory marked the second title for the duo already in 2021 and fourth overall as a team, with the other two coming in 2019.
