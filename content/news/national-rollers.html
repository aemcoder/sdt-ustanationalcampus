National Rollers is a wheelchair tennis program conducted by the USTA National Wheelchair Tennis staff and hosted at the USTA National Campus at Lake Nona in Orlando, FL. 

It is for any person who has a physical disability and would like to play wheelchair tennis. There are no limits to the program. All players of varying levels, disabilities and experience are welcome. Tennis wheelchairs and racquets will be provided.
