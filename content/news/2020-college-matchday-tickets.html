College MatchDay Tickets

Brian Ormiston | January 6, 2020

style
article-hero

College MatchDay is fast approaching, and fans can now secure their complimentary tickets for the 2020 season. The festivities will begin on Saturday, Feb. 1, when the men of Florida and Florida State resume their rivalry at 5 p.m. at the USTA National Campus.

Also on tap will be a classic matchup between the women of Notre Dame and USC on Saturday, Feb. 15, followed by Florida State and Ohio State colliding in a women's match on Sunday, March 1.

All College MatchDay events will air live or on tape delay on Tennis Channel. Gates open one hour before first serve, allowing fans to enjoy the family-friendly atmosphere, games, food, drinks and much more.

Visit CollegeMatchDay.com for more information.

Ticket Information

-Ticketmaster.com

-Fans must have an online ticket for admission to College MatchDay.

-Tickets are available online on a first-come, first-serve basis. Once the venue is at capacity, tickets will no longer be available.

-If inclement weather affects the match, it will be moved indoors and admission will be on a first-come, first-serve basis due to seating capacity.

-Parking is free.

2020 College MatchDay Schedule

-Saturday, Feb. 1 - 5 p.m. - Men - Florida vs. Florida State (Live on Tennis Channel Plus, Tape Delay on Tennis Channel on Feb. 2 at 9 a.m.)

-Saturday, Feb. 15 - 5 p.m. - Women - Notre Dame vs. USC (Live on Tennis Channel Plus, Tape Delay on Tennis Channel on Feb. 16 at 6 p.m.)

-Sunday, March 1 - 4 p.m. - Women - Florida State vs. Ohio State (Live on Tennis Channel)

VIPs, Alumni and Sponsors

-Groups that would like to get involved with College MatchDay can contact Brian Ormiston in the USTA Collegiate Department at brian.ormiston@usta.com.
