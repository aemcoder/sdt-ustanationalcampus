USTA FL 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships and 65 & Over Invitational

Busy Weekend of League Play

November 13, 2020

style
article-hero

Four straight days of USTA Florida Adult tournament play is set to get underway Saturday morning as the USTA National Campus hosts the 2020 USTA Florida 40 & Over Women’s 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships along with the 2020 USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Invitational.

The 40 & Over Women’s 3.0/4.0 Championships is slated to begin at 7:30 a.m. Saturday, Nov. 14 and run through the afternoon of Monday, Nov. 16. The 65 & Over Invitational will take to the courts starting at 9 a.m. Sunday, Nov. 15 with matches continuing through the evening of Tuesday, Nov. 17.

All 40 & Over duals will consist of one singles and two doubles contests; for the 65 & Over divisions, dual matches will be made up of three doubles matchups.

A total of 14 squads have entered the 40 & Over 3.0 field while the 4.0 competition is set to feature 13 crews. Both divisions feature three flights, meaning the trio of flight winners along with one wild card team (the second-place finisher with the best overall record) will advance to the knockout stages at the completion of all round-robin dual matches.

Four titles will be up for grabs at the 65 & Over Invitational. The 7.0 men’s division will see its three squads – Duval, Hillsborough, and Marion – square off in a double round-robin format to go along with the four women’s 8.0 clubs – Escambia, Hillsborough, Marion, and Pinellas – competing in a traditional round-robin format to decide the champion. For the men’s 8.0 division, seven teams have been divided into two flight, with the top finisher in each flight moving on to the final; the women’s 7.0 competition, with eight teams entered, will play out in the same format as the men’s 8.0 division.
