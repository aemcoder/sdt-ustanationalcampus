2018 USTA FL 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional Championships

First Serve Saturday Morning

August 9, 2018

style
article-hero

Following a month hiatus, USTA Florida League Section Championships action returns to the USTA National Campus with top Sunshine State men’s and women’s squads at the 18 & Over 3.0, 4.0, and 5.0+ levels looking to secure team titles.

The 3.0 men and 4.0 men will be competing on hard courts while the other four divisions will be on clay. Matches are slated to begin at 7:30 a.m. on Saturday morning, with all round robin flight dual matches being played out over the course of Saturday and Sunday.

For the 3.0 and 4.0 divisions, each dual is composed of five individual matchups – two singles and three doubles. In order to claim victory, a team must win at least three of those five courts. At the 5.0+ level, a dual consists of one line of singles and two lines of doubles, with a squad needing to take two out of the three.

At the completion of Sunday’s slate, both the 3.0 men and 5.0+ men will see a champion crowned, as those two competitions feature just one flight of four teams. The remaining divisions will transition into the semifinal and final rounds on Monday.

In last year’s sectional championships, Duval (Florida Yacht Club) went 4-0 to take home the men’s 3.0 title while the 3.0 women of Hillsborough (FishHawk Ranch) defeated Broward (Midtown) by a 4-1 margin. For the 4.0 finals, North Miami (Arthru Sydney Tennis Center) swept Hillsborough (FishHawk Ranch) 5-0 to earn the men’s crown; Broward (City of Oakland Park) bested Polk (Beerman Family Tennis Center) 4-1 on the women’s side. Hillsborough reeled in another team title thanks to the 5.0+ men from Plant City, who finished with a perfect 4-0 record. Orange/Seminole/Lake (Winter Park/Azalea Center) just edged out Hillsborough (Waterchase) in the women’s 5.0+ division, as the two ended with the exact same overall record and individual court wins, but Orange/Seminole/Lake losing one less set.
