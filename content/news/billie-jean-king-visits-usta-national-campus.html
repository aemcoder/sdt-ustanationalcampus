The USTA National Campus is no stranger to top American tennis talent gracing its’ courts, but the legendary Billie Jean King wanted to see the Home of American Tennis in action this week. She explored the 100 courts, watched as rising Americans honed their skills with USTA coaches, and even stopped for the occasional selfie with her adoring fans.
