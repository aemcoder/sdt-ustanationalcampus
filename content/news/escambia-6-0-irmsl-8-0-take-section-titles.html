Escambia 6.0, IRMSL 8.0 Take Section Titles

June 4, 2018

style
article-hero

The 2018 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships concluded Monday afternoon with a pair of squads claiming sectional titles. In the 6.0 division, Escambia (Wilson) emerged victorious, while IRMSL (Buttonwood) earned the crown in the 8.0 competition.

In the 6.0 championship dual, Escambia outlasted Volusia (FTC) for the 2-1 triumph. The squad from Escambia came through in the clutch throughout the tournament. On Monday, they found themselves locked in another pressure situation, and once again prevailed. Escambia recorded a 6-1, 6-4 result on the third court thanks to George Rials and Erin Rhone, but Volusia’s Brad Gifford and Alysia Webb took the top spot by the final of 6-2, 7-5. In the decisive second position, Escambia’s Nick Farkas and Jennifer Ghiorso quickly shook off the disappointment of not closing their matchup out in straight sets, hanging on to win 6-3, 5-7, 1-0.

The 8.0 division kicked Monday’s action off with its two semifinal encounters. IRMSL (Buttonwood) made quick work of Polk (Lakes Wales) on the strength of straight-set victories on courts one and three. Polk did take the matchup at the second position, making the final margin 2-1 in favor of IRMSL. SMD (Coral Oaks) looked to be in good shape to move on to the divisional final as well after winning the first sets at the top two spots versus Sarasota (PP Smashers). That momentum did not hold up for long, as Sarasota rallied back for second-set victories on both courts, in addition to finishing off court three 6-4, 6-2. That meant Sarasota needed just one of the two match tiebreaks that remained, which they got at the first position in a contest that ended 1-6, 6-4, 1-0. SMD was able to avoid the shutout thanks to a 6-4, 0-6, 1-0 result on court two.

IRMSL and Sarasota was a rematch from Flight 3. That went the way of IRMSL, 2-1, as they claimed the flight. Meanwhile, Sarasota reached the semifinals by virtue of being the wild card entrant. The dual would again end in a 2-1 final score line, highlighted by three match tiebreaks. All three Sarasota doubles tandems got off to hot starts, as the team held a set lead on each of the courts. However, the momentum did not hold up at even one of the positions. With IRMSL bouncing back across the board, the title essentially came down to the best two-of-three match tiebreaks. Sarasota’s Eric Abbott and Ann Lammers held strong to win at the top spot, 6-3, 2-6, 1-0. IRMSL answered back on court two thanks to a 4-6, 6-1, 1-0 victory by co-captain Patrick Galligan and Somhathai Immerman. With captain Larry Shull and partner Guadalupe Avinzano on the brink of defeat, the IRMSL duo battled back and eventually delivered for their team in the form of a 4-6, 6-4, 1-0 triumph.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
