US Open Wildcards Awarded to Resident Players

Alyssa Simonin | August 15, 2018

style
article-hero

Three #USTennis players who regularly train at the USTA #NationalCampus have received US Open Main-Draw Wildcards.

1. Noah Rubin: This NY native is ranked No. 139, and recently coming off his first top 10 win.

2. Amanda Anisimova: She’s making her comeback after being sidelined with an injury since early this spring. Last year, she won the girls’ singles US Open title, and now she’s ready for her first US Open main-draw appearance.

3. Whitney Osuigwe: After recently taking the Girls’ 18s National title in San Diego, she will also be making her US Open main-draw debut.

US Open Wildcard #NationalCampus Recipients
