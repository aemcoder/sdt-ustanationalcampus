2019 USTA FL "Bobby Curtis" Orange Ball 10 & Under Sectional

Allcott and Tong Capture Title

October 27, 2019

style
article-hero

A bright and humid Sunday was the setting for the completion of the 2019 USTA Florida “Bobby Curtis” Orange Ball 10 & Under Sectional Championship, hosted by the USTA National Campus. The day began with the completion of the quarterfinals and ended with the duo of Taylor Allcott (Tampa) and Haily Tong (Odessa) being crowned champions.

This year’s event utilized the new “Splitz” format, in which players are paired up to make teams of two to compete in team matches. Each player will play one set of singles and one set of doubles per match; the team that wins at least two sets claims the overall victory.

The tandem from the west coast of the Sunshine State opened up Sunday’s schedule of play by finishing off a sweep of Matthew Azizi (Plantation) and Olivia Diaz (Orlando) to ease into the final four. That set up what was a seemingly tough matchup against Central Florida pairing of Niko Saulovic (Saint Cloud) and Philip Sundsten (Orlando); however, Allcott and Tong recorded a third consecutive sweep.

Luka Kostic (Hallandale Beach) and Max Shilt (Sunny Isles Beach) represented the final, and most challenging, hurdle on the weekend. It was a championship tilt that lived up to the expectations of a clash between two teams yet to surrender a set. In the end, Allcott and Tong showed impressive toughness to battle their way to first place.

The result was the latest in a long line of successful efforts put forth by Allcott and Tong this fall, as they have both been commanding forces in 10 & Under competition across the state.

Allcott & Tong’s Path to the Title

Round of 16: def. Kyle Mang/Andrew Park Jr., 3-0

Quarterfinals: def. Matthew Azizi/Olivia Diaz, 3-0

Semifinals: def. Niko Saulovic/Philip Sundsten, 3-0

Final: def. Luka Kostic/Max Shilt, 2-1
