Fan Guide to MatchDay on March 3

Brian Ormiston and Christina Aguis | February 27, 2018

style
article-hero

The hometown UCF Knights and the Michigan Wolverines will collide in both men’s and women’s action for College MatchDay at the USTA National Campus on Saturday, March 3, at 12:30 p.m. ET.

Before making your way to Lake Nona, here is everything you need to know about attending the match:

Matchup

-UCF vs. Michigan (men and women)

Date and Time

-Saturday, March 3

-Gates open: 10:30 a.m.

-First serve: 12:30 p.m.

 

Location

-USTA National Campus (Collegiate Complex)

-Address: 10000 USTA Blvd., Orlando, FL 32827

-Phone: 844-872-8667

Tickets

-Admission is free.

-Reserve complimentary tickets by visiting Ticketmaster.com and searching College MatchDay.

-Or click here: 2018 College MatchDay Complimentary Tickets.

Parking

-Valet parking is available for $5. Fans who use this service will receive a free College MatchDay program.

-General parking is free and available on a first-come, first-serve basis.

RV Parking

-Fans with RVs are directed to park in the back row of the main parking lot.

Bus Parking

-The bus lane is located near the front of the main parking lot.

Pre-match Festivities

-Starting at 10:30 a.m., fans can experience a live DJ, tailgating games, face painting and much more, all for free.

-A free interactive kids’ clinic led by USTA teaching professionals will begin at 11 a.m.

-Giveaways will be distributed throughout the day.

-Event programs and merchandise from Fromuth Tennis will be available.

-Food and refreshments will be available for purchase.

Match Format

-Doubles will open the match. The first team to win two of the three matches earns one overall team point. Matches are one six-game set.

-Following doubles, six singles matches will take place. Each match is worth one team point.

-The first school to secure four team points wins the match.

Court Assignments

-The national anthem and team introductions will take place on Court 1 (the side facing the parking lot).

-The matches will be held on all 12 courts, with doubles taking place on Courts 1-6.

Match Preview

The UCF Knights and the Michigan Wolverines men’s and women’s tennis teams will compete at College MatchDay this Saturday at 12:30 p.m. at the USTA National Campus.

Gates open at 10:30 a.m. when fans and kids can come and enjoy pre-match festivities. The USTA will be providing games, giveaways, face painting and a kids’ clinic that will start at 11 a.m.

The last time that the UCF (6-2) and Michigan (4-5) women matched up was in 2011 at the UCF Tennis Complex, where the Wolverines dominated the Knights, 6-1. This year, the Wolverines return Brienne Minor, who won the 2017 NCAA Singles Championship.

This will be the first time that UCF (4-2) and Michigan (8-2) men will go head-to-head.

Tickets are complimentary and available on site but fans are encouraged to reserve their tickets at Ticketmaster.com.

For those who can’t make it, College MatchDay will be aired live on Tennis Channel.
