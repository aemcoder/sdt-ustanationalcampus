2018 USTA National Campus UTR Summer Jam Level 7

Tournament to Serve as UTR Pilot Event

June 29, 2018

style
article-hero

Junior play picks back up this weekend at the USTA National Campus with the UTR Summer Jam Level 7. Matches get underway the morning of Saturday, June 30 and run through Sunday, July 1. The event has been selected as one of approximately 20 USTA Florida sanctioned Level 6-7 junior tournaments in the second half of 2018 where Universal Tennis Ratings will be piloted.

UTR is an algorithmic-based ratings scale that includes a player’s last 30 matches, or 12 months of play. The rating considers the level of both opponents, as well as the score of the match, thus pulling in more match data than any other ratings tool to provide a universal scale for tennis players.

There are a total of five draws on the boys’ side and three on the girls’, along with a co-ed draw for the 10 & under division. The 14s age division features four boys’ draws and two girls’ draws; for the 18s draws, there is only one per gender.

The pilot program will better allow players to identify their level and to play in level-appropriate competitions. It will also allow players to maximize their number of competitive matches and help junior players better understand their potential prospects for player college tennis.

For this tournament, seeds and divisional flights have been determined based on UTR. All results will count for USTA ranking points and for UTR.
