USTA Annual Meetings

Georgiana Brown | March 24, 2017

style
article-hero

The USTA Annual Meetings commenced this week in Orlando. Beginning on Wednesday, March 22, the National Campus was proud to welcome and celebrate the arrival of USTA Board of Directors, Section Leadership, and Volunteers and guests to the new facility. For many this was their first chance to see and experience the new home of American tennis. With courts as far as the eye can see, USTA now has a place to bring together all their resources and deliver on the promise of growing the game at every level.

The Annual Meetings will continue through Monday, March 27th at the JW Marriott Orlando Grand Lakes.
