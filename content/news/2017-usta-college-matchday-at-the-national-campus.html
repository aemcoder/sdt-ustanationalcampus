Some of the best collegiate teams in the country will be coming to the USTA National Campus starting in January for College MatchDay. Fans will get to enjoy a pre-match experience with food, drinks, games, a free kids clinic and much more, all starting two hours prior to first serve. Make sure to save these dates:

Saturday, Jan. 21 – Alabama vs. Michigan – Men – 6 p.m.

Friday, Feb. 3 – North Carolina vs. Texas Tech – Women – 6 p.m.

Friday, Feb. 17 – Oklahoma State vs. Stanford – Women – 6 p.m.

Wednesday, March 8 – UCF vs. Penn State – Men – 6 p.m.

Friday, March 10 – Florida State vs. Florida – Men – 6 p.m.

Tuesday, March 14 – Florida vs. Ohio State – Women – 6 p.m.

Dates and times subject to change. More information to come, for any initial questions please contact collegiate@usta.com
