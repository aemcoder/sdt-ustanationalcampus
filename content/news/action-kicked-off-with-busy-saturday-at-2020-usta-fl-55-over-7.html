Action Kicked Off with Busy Saturday

October 24, 2020

style

Day one of the 2020 USTA Florida 55 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships was a busy one on the grounds of the USTA National Campus. Three of the divisions saw their teams compete in a pair of dual matches as the potential list of sectional title hopefuls began to dwindle.

A competitive day of men’s 7.0 play left Collier (Arthur Allen Tennis Center at Cambier Park – Naples) as the lone unbeaten at 2-0 overall. Meanwhile, four squads will bring a 1-1 record into Sunday, each of them splitting the six individual matchups their players took part in over the course of the day. With three more rounds of duals left on the schedule, little has been determined in the division.

In the 7.0 women’s competition, all three flights began to take shape as a trio of teams positioned themselves to advance to the semifinal round. Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park – Altamonte Springs) in Flight 1, IRSML (The Legacy Golf & Tennis – Port Saint Lucie) out of Flight 2, and Duval (Magnolia Point Golf & Country Club – Green Cove Springs) from Flight 3 are all just one win away from moving on to the knockout stages of the weekend. Additionally, there are six crews sitting at 1-1 looking to earn the lone wild card slot into the final four.

The four teams in the men’s 9.0 division took to the courts for just one dual on Saturday, with Hillsborough (Palma Ceia – Tampa) sweeping Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) and Sarasota (Palm Aire Country Club – Pompano Beach) holding off Pinellas (City of Clearwater). A different story is playing out on the women’s 9.0 side, where Sarasota (Longwood Athletic Club – Sarasota) and Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) remain unscathed while Orange/Seminole (Heathrow Country Club) stayed in the hunt by going 1-1 on the day.

Sunday’s slate of matches will get underway at 7:30 a.m. Both the men’s and women’s 9.0 champions will be crowned Sunday afternoon while the two 7.0 titles will be decided Monday morning.
