Kudla, Eubanks into Quarterfinals

November 18, 2020

article-hero

Wednesday’s schedule of play featured the bottom half of the singles draw in their second round matches plus the completion of the opening round of the doubles draw. Highlighting the day were straight-set triumphs by Americans No. 2 Denis Kudla and Christopher Eubanks.

Eubanks did not face a break point in his contest with Hiroki Moriya of Japan, winning 78% of his first serve points while breaking Moriya four times to ease his way to a 6-2, 6-1 victory. Kudla was also solid on serve but faced more of a challenge from American wild card Patrick Kypson. The two would end up separated by a mere seven points, as Kudla prevailed 6-4, 6-4. Eubanks and Kudla will clash in the final eight Friday.

Also advancing on the day was fourth-seeded Prajnesh Gunneswaran from India along with sixth seed Dmitry Popko (Kazakhstan). Gunneswaran found himself on the brink of elimination when Tung-Ling Wu (Taiwan) served for the match in the second set; however, he managed to put together a string of games to not only get back on serve, but ultimately force a decisive third frame. Gunneswaran would claim the first two games before Gunneswaran retired with the score standing at 5-7, 7-5, 2-0.

Popko’s 7-5, 1-6, 7-5 victory over 17-year-old American qualifier Zachary Svajda, which eclipsed the three-hour mark, was even more dramatic. A tight first set went to Popko 7-5 before Svajda mastered the wind to whip through the second 6-1. Popko built a seemingly comfortable 4-1 cushion in the final set, only to see Svajda make one last push to even things at 4-4. From there Popko would hold serve with ease while Svajda was made to work for every point on his serve, saving a match point at 4-5 but eventually wilting at 5-6.

The top two duos were part of Wednesday’s doubles slate. No. 1 seeds Hans Hach Verdugo and Miguel Angel Reyes-Varela, both representing Mexico, came through in the last contest of the day by holding off wild cards Bjorn Fratangelo (USA) and Kypson 5-7, 7-6(6), 10-6. It was just about as thin of a margin for the second-seeded Kazakhstan tandem of Andrey Golubev and Aleksandr Nedovyesov, who got the best of last week’s Atlantic Tire champions Teymuraz Gabashvili (Russia) and Dennis Novikov (USA) 6-7(4), 6-2, 10-5.

Other doubles action included Mitchell Krueger and Jackson Withrow winning their all-American battle versus No. 3 Robert Galloway and Nathaniel Lammons 6-7(1), 6-2, 10-6, Frenchmen Sadio Doumbia and Fabien Reboul dismissing the fourth-seeded pairing of Hunter Reese (USA) and Sem Verbeek (Netherlands) 6-4, 4-6, 10-7, and Daniel Elahi Galan (Colombia) teaming up with American Christian Harrison to defeat American wild cards Brandon Holt and Keegan Smith 7-6(5), 4-6, 10-5.
