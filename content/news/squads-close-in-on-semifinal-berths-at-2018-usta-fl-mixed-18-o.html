Squads Close in on Semifinal Berths

October 6, 2018

The opening day of the 2018 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships came to a conclusion in the late afternoon Saturday. With two matches in the books for all teams, the flights have begun to take shape as semifinals berths are close to being earned.

In the 7.0 division, Flight 1 will come down to the dual between Broward (City of Oakland Park) and Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) after those two squads went undefeated on day one. They both won four of the six individual matchups they contested, while Broward lost fewer sets (four versus five) and Leon gave away fewer games (45 to 48). Hillsborough (Hunters Green Sports Center) has already put away Flight 2 after a 2-0 effort that included zero sets lost and a mere 38 games given away.

Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) has virtually locked up Flight 3, but a sweep at the hands of Alachua on Sunday would give Alachua a chance to steal the flight. In the lone five-team flight, Duval (Fleming Island) sits in first at 2-0 with a trio of teams still in the mix after splitting their first two matches.

For the 9.0 competition, three teams are a victory away from advancing to the semifinals while the remaining squads are eyeing the wildcard slot. Orange/Seminole (Sanlando Park), Hillsborough (Harbour Island Athletic Club), and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) closed out Saturday with an overall record of 2-0. Hillsborough was the most dominant, claiming five court wins in the six they contested in Flight 2 with just five sets lost. Orange/Seminole and Marion came up victorious in four individual matchups each in Flight 1 and Flight 3, respectively.

In the race to move into the semifinals via the wild card spot, Flight 3 participant SMD (Coral Pine Park) holds the inside track. They are currently tied with Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club) of Flight 1, but Pinellas must defeat Orange/Seminole while SMD will be across the net from winless Escambia.

Sunday’s slate of matches will start at 8:00 a.m.
