Three Divisions in Action Friday

The USTA Florida Combo 50 & Over Sectional Championships kicked off play on Friday at the USTA National Campus. Three of the five divisions were in action on the opening day of the event.

Teams are competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

In the women’s 6.5 competition, Escambia (Roger Scott Tennis Center – Pensacola) has all but clinched a place in the championship match by recording two sweeps on Friday in Flight 2 action. They won all six individual matchups while giving away just two sets. Flight 1 is currently in the grips of unbeaten Alachua (Haile Plantation Country Club – Gainesville) with Orange/Seminole/Lake (Azalea Center – Winter Park) and IRMSL (The Boulevard Tennis Club – Vero Beach) remaining in the hunt.

For the lone men’s 7.5 flight, Pinellas (Seminole Lake Family Tennis) and Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) vaulted to the top of the standings thanks to 2-0 records heading into their Saturday showdown. The winner of that dual is likely to take home the sectional crown, but 1-1 Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) could still make waves on day two.

Two polar opposite stories have taken shape within the flights of the women’s 7.5 division. Pinellas (McMullen Tennis Complex – Clearwater) is threatening to run away with Flight 1 as the lone undefeated squad, having claimed five of the six courts their players contested on the day. Meanwhile, nothing has been decided after the opening day in Flight 2, where all four teams sit at 1-1 overall; furthermore, each team split their six individual matchups with seven sets won and seven lost.

Saturday’s schedule of play will get underway at 7:30 a.m., including the opening matches of the men’s 6.5 and men’s 8.5 divisions. For more information on the USTA Florida Combo 50 & Over Sectional Championships, click here.
