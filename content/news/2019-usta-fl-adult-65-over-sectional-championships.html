2019 USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Championships

FL Adult Play Back on Campus

April 10, 2019

style
article-hero

USTA Florida Adult play returns to the USTA National Campus this weekend, as the 65 & Over Sectional Championships will be held from Saturday, April 13 to Monday, April 15. Overall, five divisional crowns are up for grabs.

Each dual match will consist of three courts of doubles, with a team needing a win on at least two of those courts to take the dual.

Three squads will be competing for the women’s 6.0 title – Collier (The Players Club and Spa – Naples), Marion (Golden Ocala Golf and Equestrian Club), and Duval (Southside Tennis Complex – Jacksonville). The trio will battle it out in a double round-robin flight across Saturday and Sunday.

A total of eight women’s teams have been divided into two flights of four in 7.0 play. Collier (Village Walk – Naples), Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville), IRMSL (North River Shores Tennis Club – Stuart), and Duval (Boone Park – Jacksonville) make up Flight 1 while Sarasota (Lakewood Ranch Athletic), Marion (Citrus Hills Tennis Club – Hernando), Pinellas (East Lake Woodlands Country Club – Oldsmar), and Hillsborough (Plant City Recreation Parks) are in Flight 2. The top finisher in each flight will then face off for the divisional championship Sunday afternoon.

In men’s 7.0 action, the six squads have all been grouped into a single flight. Hillsborough (Temple Terrace Recreation), Collier (Club at Longshore Lake – Naples), Pinellas (Belleair Country Club), Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA – Jacksonville), Marion (Glenview Champions Tennis Club – The Villages), and Lee/Charlotte (Park Meadow Tennis Club – Fort Myers) are slated to take part in duals over the course of the three-day event.

For the women’s 8.0 division, the competition kicks off with two flights of four. Sarasota (Bath and Racquet Club – Sarasota), Pinellas (City of Clearwater), Marion (USTA National Campus – Orlando), and Escambia (Independent) will compete in Flight 1 along with Collier (Pelican Bay Community Park – Naples), IRMSL (Harbour Ridge Country Club – Palm City), Brevard (Independent), and Duval (Independent) fighting for position in Flight 2. The two flight winners will advance to the championship dual scheduled for Sunday afternoon.

The largest division on the weekend will be the 8.0 men’s, where a total of 11 squads have been split into three flights. The three-team Flight 1 is composed of Collier (Lighthouse Bay – Bonita Springs), Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center – Tallahassee), and Duval (Magnolia Point Golf & Country Club – Green Cove Springs). Flight 2 will see Pinellas (City of Clearwater), Polk (Lake Wales Country Club), Marion/Brevard (USTA National Campus – Orlando), and Okaloosa/Walton (TOPS’L Beach and Racquet Resort – Miramar Beach) in action. The four teams making up Flight 3 are Hillsborough (River Hills Country Club – Valrico), Broward (Parkland Tennis Center), Sarasota (Country Club of Sebring), and Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville). Moving on to the divisional semifinals will be the flight winners plus the top second-place squad, which will take to the courts at 8 a.m. Monday.

In last year’s Sectional Championships, Duval (Southside Tennis Center – Jacksonville) claimed the women’s 6.0 crown while the Pinellas (Seminole Lake Family Tennis – Seminole) women and Collier (The Players Club and Spa – Naples) men prevailed in the 7.0 division. Coming through in the 8.0 competition were the women of Pinellas (City of Clearwater) and the men from Collier (Lighthouse Bay – Bonita Springs).
