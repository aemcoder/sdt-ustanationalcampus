Historic Seasons for Lake Nona High Boys' and Girls' Tennis Teams

Lions Claim Pair of Runner-up Finishes

May 1, 2019

style
article-hero

Historic seasons for the Lake Nona High School boys’ and girls’ tennis teams came to a close on Tuesday with runner-up finishes at the Florida High School Athletic Association (FHSAA) 4A State Tennis Championships, held at Sanlando Park.

Both squads easily claimed the 4A District 9 titles, earning the right to host their regional semifinal and final contests. On the boys’ side, the Lions dispatched of Martin County 5-0 before holding off Vero Beach by a 4-2 margin. The girls had a much easier time advancing to the State Championships, turning in sweeps over Melbourne and Vero Beach.

With just eight teams remaining in the hunt for a state title, the LNHS boys’ ousted Buchholz by a 4-2 score line to move into the semifinal stage, where an impressive 4-0 effort over Wellington propelled them to the team final. Miami Palmetto proved too tough with the state championship on the line, besting the Lions, 4-0.

The Lake Nona girls took a similar path to the state title dual, surviving a 5-2 battle with Lake Mary in the quarterfinals and then quickly sweeping aside Winter Park, 4-0. The squad from Dr. Krop of Miami stopped the Lions in the championship, prevailing 4-0.
