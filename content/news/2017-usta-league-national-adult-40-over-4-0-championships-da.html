USTA League National Adult 40 & Over 4.0 Championships

Day One Complete; Flights Take Shape

October 20, 2017

style
article-hero

Action at the 2017 USTA League National Adult 40 & Over 4.0 Championships, hosted at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., kicked off on Friday. The day featured a full slate of flight play as teams look to position themselves for a run at advancing to the championship semifinals.

In the lone women’s flight featuring five teams, Broward (Florida) and SSH Green (Intermountain) went 2-0 to be co-leaders of Flight 1 going into Saturday. Broward was the more dominant of the two, losing just eight sets and 73 games. Johnson Ranch (Northern California) did drop a 3-2 decision to SSH Green but remains in the mix after picking up a team victory as well.

The remaining three flights feature two teams with a 1-0 dual record and two teams at 0-1 overall. San Diego (Southern California) and Southeast Michigan (Midwest) were dominant in Flight 2, each sweeping all five individual courts in their lone dual on Friday. Eastern Massachusetts (New England) also won via a sweep in Flight 3, while Puerto Rico (Caribbean) toughed out 3-2 victory. The same scenario is the case in Flight 4, where Dallas (Texas) polished off their opponent in sweeping fashion and Southern Oregon (Pacific Northwest) battled to pull out a tight victory.

On the men’s side, Flight 5 is of five teams. Both Washington, D.C. (Mid-Atlantic) and Dayton (Midwest) went 2-0 on the opening day, meaning the winner of their dual on Saturday will likely claim the flight. However, Puerto Rico (Caribbean) is also mathematically alive and could cause some chaos by winning both of their team matches on day two.

Flight 6 will go to the winner between Broward (Florida) and NOHO (Texas) thanks to those squads having defeated the other two teams in the flight already. NONO lost five less games but Broward gave away two less sets. Philadelphia (Middle States) only needs a dual win over the team from New England to clinch Flight 7, while Flight 8 will be decided by the match on Saturday between New Orleans (Southern) and RMTC Blue (Intermountain).

Saturday’s schedule is set to get underway at 7:00 am. By the end of the day, the men’s and women’s semifinals will be set. A full listing of the women’s flights and match schedule can be viewed here, and the men’s here.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
