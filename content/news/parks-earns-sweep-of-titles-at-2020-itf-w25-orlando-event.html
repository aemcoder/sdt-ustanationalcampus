2020 ITF W25 Orlando

Parks Earns Sweep of Titles

November 15, 2020

style
article-hero

American Alycia Parks began her week at the USTA National Campus by playing qualifying singles matches and closed out the 2020 ITF W25 Orlando event by claiming the singles and doubles titles on Sunday.

The victories mark the first two ITF titles of Parks’ career; prior to the event she had never reached a doubles final and was playing in her third singles final.

She was forced to dig herself out of an early hole in the singles championship tilt against fellow American Robin Montgomery, who secured the opening frame by a 6-3 margin. Parks managed to battle her way through a tight second set, 6-4, and kept the momentum to pull away in the decisive third six games to two.

Alycia Parks Path to the Title

Qualifying First Round – def. Erin Routliffe (New Zealand) 6-3, 6-2

Qualifying Second Round – def. Ashlyn Krueger (USA) 6-4, 6-3

First Round – def. Kyoka Okamura (Japan) 6-1, 6-3

Second Round – def. Paula Ormaechea (Argentina) 6-1, 6-2

Quarterfinal – def. No. 2 Katarzyna Kawa (Poland) 3-6, 6-3, ret.

Semifinal – def. Jamie Loeb (USA) 6-3, 6-2

Final – def. Robin Montgomery (USA) 3-6, 6-4, 6-2

Getting back on the court in the afternoon for the doubles final, Parks teamed up with Rasheeda McAdoo (USA) to outlast the second-seeded duo of Jamie Loeb and Erin Routliffe in an extended 4-6, 6-1, 11-9 contest.
