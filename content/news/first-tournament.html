Brian Ormiston | January 4, 2017

A total of 100 events will take place at the USTA National Campus in 2017. First up will be the USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 6.5/8.5 League Championships Jan. 6-8.

Action will take place on 32 hard courts. Parking and admission is free. Friday will get underway at 10 a.m., and Saturday and Sunday competition will start at 8 a.m.

While the tournament is going on, the Campus will remain open for court bookings throughout the weekend.

For more information, visit USTA Florida's Tournament Preview Page.

For participants, make sure to click on the USTA Florida Event Page.

The National Campus opened to the public on Jan. 2, and has already entertained countless numbers of players and guests. The facility opens at 7:30 a.m. each day and closes at 10 p.m. Monday-Saturday and 8 p.m. on Sunday. Reservations are currently being accepted for programming and court bookings.
