Saturday's Play Concludes

December 8, 2018

The opening day of play at the 2018 USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus, wrapped up Saturday evening. All six divisional competitions were in action on the day.

The men’s 5.5 title is still up for grabs after Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) and Alachua (Westside Park Gainesville) split their two duals on Saturday. Both matches ended in 2-1 results, with Marion dropping two fewer sets and 12 less games. The squads will meet twice on Sunday.

In the women’s 5.5 division, the three-team Flight 1 hangs in the balance heading into day two after each team posted a 1-1 overall record. Duval (Florida Yacht Club) sits atop the standings thanks to four court victories, one more than SMD (Coral Reef Tennis Center) and two better than Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center). Flight 2 is firmly within the grasp of Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center), which posted a 2-0 record on Saturday and just needs to beat winless Marion (Golden Ocala Golf and Equestrian Club) to reach the final.

Unbeaten efforts from Orange/Seminole (National Campus), Broward (Midtown Athletic Club), and Brevard (Fee Ave Courts) have them in prime position to book a spot in the men’s 7.5 semifinals. Orange/Seminole produced the best numbers, giving away a mere three sets and 44 games in Flight 1. Brevard soared to the top of Flight 3 while losing four sets and 51 games; Broward raced out ahead of the pack in Flight 2 with five sets and 50 games surrendered. With eight teams going 1-1 on Saturday, the wild card spot is far from being determined.

For the women’s 7.5 division, Orange/Seminole (Red Bug Lake Park) in Flight 1 and SMD (Coral Gables/Kerdyk Biltmore Tennis Center) from Flight 4 absolutely dominated the competition on the opening day of play. Both squads went 6-for-6 in their individual matchups while letting only a single set slip away. Collier (Greater Marco Family YMCA) also looked sharp in Flight 3, ending Saturday at 2-0 behind five court victories. Meanwhile, all four teams in Flight 2 went 1-1 and remain very much alive in the race to secure a semifinal berth. Hillsborough (Harbour Island Athletic Club) holds a slight edge via winning the most individual matchups so far.  

SMD (Doral Park) and Broward (Midtown Athletic Center), the only two squads competing in the men’s 9.5 division, played just once on Saturday. That dual went to SMD, two courts to one, putting them one win away from the sectional crown. On the women’s 9.5 side, where all six teams are battling in one flight, Hillsborough (Waterchase) and SMD (Coral Gables/Salvadore Park) hold the inside track to the crown with 2-0 records so far.
