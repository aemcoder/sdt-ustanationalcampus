Alvarez, Rus Capture Singles Titles

November 17, 2019

style
article-hero

The 2019 Oracle Pro Series Orlando came to a close on Sunday as the men's and women's singles finals were contested on the day. Peru's Nicolas Alvarez, a former collegiate standout at Duke, defeated Pedro Sakamoto (Brazil) 3-6, 6-2, 6-1 and Aranxta Rus (Netherlands) swept past Irina Fetecau of Romania 6-3, 6-2.

Marking the sixth of seven stops of the Oracle Pro Series this fall, the week featured men’s and women’s ITF World Tennis Tour $25k events.

Alvarez, seeded third in the singles draw, was facing off with doubles partner and No. 2 seed Sakamoto a day after the two teamed up to win the doubles crown. One break of serve was all Sakamoto needed to take the opening frame, serving it out on his fourth set point. Alvarez quickly responded by jumping out to a 2-0 lead, but would end up giving the break right back in the next game. 

The turning point of the match happened on Sakamoto's serve at 6-2, 1-2. Both players had chances to take the game, but it was Alvarez eventually doing so, this time not wasting his break advantage; he would race through the remainder of the set. The decisive third was all one-way traffic for Alvarez after he reeled off the first four games.

Alvarez earned his third title of the year with the effort, and for the first time in his career won the singles and doubles crowns at the same event. 

No. 1 seed Rus never trailed versus unseeded Fetecau, staking claim to the first two games right out of the gate. Fetecau did pull even at 2-2, but Rus then raised her level of play for 5-2 before serving out the frame at love. 

A 3-0, double break cushion in the second all but sealed the deal for Rus. Fetecau gathered herself for one final push to get one of the breaks back, but Rus emphatically answered by claiming eight of the next nine points. Just as she did in set one, she held at love to close out the victory.

Despite being the only WTA Top 100 player in the event, few could have predicted the run to the title for Rus after she dropped the opening frame to Gabriela Talaba (Romania) in her first match of the week. However, that would be the only set she would give away en route to her 10th title of 2019.
