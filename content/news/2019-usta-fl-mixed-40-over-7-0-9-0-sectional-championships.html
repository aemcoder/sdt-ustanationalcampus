2019 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional and Women's 2.5 Invitational

Action Gets Going Saturday Morning

June 21, 2019

style
article-hero

USTA Florida League play returns to the National Campus this weekend, as the 7.0 and 9.0 competitions of the Mixed 40 & Over division take to the courts starting Saturday. The first two days will feature round robin action; the 9.0 champion will be crowned Sunday afternoon while the 7.0 champion will be decided on Monday.

Each team dual will be composed of three courts of doubles, with a team needing to take at least two of those contests to claim victory in the dual. Each court will be the best two-of-three tiebreak sets with a match tiebreak in lieu of a full third.

A total of 20 teams are set to battle for the 7.0 title. To start out, the squads have been placed into four flights of five squads for the round robin matches. At the completion of Sunday’s slate, the four flight winners will advance to Monday’s semifinals. The championship dual will take place on Monday at 11 a.m.

Just eight 9.0 teams are in the mix for the sectional crown, making up two equally-divided flights. Moving on will be the top finisher in each flight only, as those two squads will square off in the final at 2 p.m. Sunday afternoon.

In last year’s Sectional Championships, Hillsborough (Hunters Green Sports Center – Tampa) bested Duval (Boone Park – Jacksonville) by a 2-1 score line in the 7.0 championship matchup while SMD (Coral Pine Park – Pinecrest) defeated Pinellas (Tarpon Tennis Club – Tarpon Springs) by the same margin for the 9.0 title.
