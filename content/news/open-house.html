The USTA is inviting everyone out to the National Campus for a special Open House from Jan. 3-5, allowing the Orlando community and tennis players from all over to get a taste of everything the Campus has to offer at absolutely no cost.

USTA National Campus Open House

Jan. 3-5, 2017

9:30-11:30 a.m. & 5-7 p.m.

-There also will be a daily 7 p.m. Q&A youth program meeting for parents.

-Call the USTA National Campus at (844) 872-8667 for more information.

New-To-Tennis Programs

During the Open House, there will be tennis programs available to try for the entire family:

Youth

-Beginning and intermediate (Red and Orange ball play)

-Mornings and after-school hours

-Match play Fridays

-Play-based learning programs

Adult

-"Play Tennis Fast" beginners program

-Tennis 101

Fitness-Focused

-Cardio Tennis and Live Ball program

-Youth cardio traininig

The USTA National Campus, which officially opens to the public Jan. 2, houses 100 courts of various surfaces. Program rates are $15 per person/per hour.
