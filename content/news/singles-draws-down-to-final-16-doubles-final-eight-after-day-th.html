Singles Draws Down to Final 16, Doubles Final Eight After Day Three

December 29, 2017

article-hero

Three days of play at the 2017 USTA National Winter Championships for the 16s and 18s, held at the USTA National Campus, are now complete. Heading into Saturday’s schedule, the singles draws have been whittled down to the round of 16 while the doubles draws are at the quarterfinal stage.

The quartet of No. 1 singles seeds continues to secure victories with relative ease. Boys’ 16s top seed Maxwell McKennon (Newport Beach, Calif.) had the closest match on the day, defeating Jameson Corsillo (Boca Raton, Fla.) 6-2, 6-4. Boys’ 18s No. 1 Carson Haskins (Ballwin, Mo.) handled Eric Hahn (Fullerton, Calif.) 6-3, 6-1, while top-ranked Chelsea Kung (Fort Worth, Texas) also surrendered just four games against Mackenzie Clark (College Park, Md.) in girls’ 18s action. The No. 1 seed in the girls’ 16s, Gianna Pielet (El Paso, Texas) moved on with a 6-1, 6-2 result over Rachel Arbitman (Hewlett, N.Y.).

A total of three unseeded players remain amongst the final 16 of the boys’ 16s draw, with two of them facing off – Zachary Smith (New Orleans, La.) and Luis Iriarte Bastidas (Weston, Fla.) Smith won his round of 32 contest quickly, dropping only one game, while Iriarte Bastidas rallied for a 3-6, 6-4, 6-2 triumph. The other unseeded player still in the hunt for the crown, Henry Lovett (Los Angeles, Calif.), will be up against No. 17 seed Daniel Milavsky (Needham, Mass.).

The boys’ 18s competition has four unseeded players still in the main draw, with two in the top half and two in the bottom half. Noah Schachter (Wesley Chapel, Fla.) and Alexander Kotzen (Short Hills, N.J.) will be across the net from each other on Saturday; Marcus McDaniel (Vacaville, Calif.) is set to clash with No. 3 seed Kevin Zhu (Pearland, Texas) and Andres Martin (Flowery Branch, Ga.) will take on eighth seeded A. Trice Pickens (Cary, N.C.). Additionally, No. 17 seed Siem Woldeab (La Mesa, Calif.) came up with the upset of the day on the boys’ side, knocking out second seed Bill Duo (Portage, Mich.) 6-4, 6-4.

While Pielet has had no trouble navigating through the girls’ 16s draw, exactly half of the remaining field is made up of unseeded participants. Pielet is the only top four seed left after No. 2 seed Julia Andreach (Rochester, N.Y.) came up just short in a back-and-forth match with Kiana Rizzolo (Jupiter, Fla.); Rizzolo claimed victory by a slim 2-6, 6-4, 7-6(5) margin.

The girls’ 18s draw continues to hold much closer to form, with all but three players left being seeded and no major upsets occurring on Friday. Crashing the party are Elizabeth Coleman (Midland, Mich.), Tia Mukherjee (Bloomfield Hills, Mich.) and Ava Hrastar (Duluth, Ga.). Coleman’s next opponent is No. 5 seed Reilly Tran (Dunn Loring, Va.), while Mukherjee will go up against seventh seed Anika Yarlagadda (West Bloomfield, Mich.) and Hrastar has drawn No. 16 Angelica Blake (Boca Raton, Fla.).

In doubles action, the boys’ draws continue to be unpredictable. Only two of the top eight remain in the boys’ 16s – second seeds Hunter Heck (Saint Paul, Minn.) and Alexander Petrov (Irvine, Calif.) along with No. 5 seeds Keshav Chopra (Marietta, Ga.) and Coy Simon (Charleston, S.C.). Those two duos play each other on Saturday. Day three was a wild one for the boys’ 18s doubles, as the Nos. 1, 3, 4 and 5 seeds all lost. The second-seeded tandem of Bradley Frye (Overland Park, Kan.) and Jared Pratt (Daniel Island, S.C.) barely stayed alive with a 6-1, 6-7(3), 10-4 victory.

The girls’ doubles draws tell a much different story. Though the top two girls’ 16s seeds have been tested, both have advanced to the quarterfinals. Top seeds Carmen Corley (Albuquerque, N.M.) and Pielet in addition to No. 2 seeds Winslow Huth (Brentwood, Tenn.) and Chidimma Okpara (Vienna, Va.) needed to pull out a match tiebreak to move on. All eight girls’ 16s quarterfinalists are seeded. For the girls’ 18s, first seeds Kolie Allen (Lombard, Ill.) and Ivana Corley (Albuquerque, N.M.) are safely through to the final eight, as are second seeds Briana Crowley (Battle Ground, Ind.) and Tran.
