Top 14-and-under players from across the Americas come to the USTA National Campus to compete for the 2nd annual America’s Cup. Teams from the South American Tennis Federation (COSAT), the Central American & Caribbean Tennis Confederation (COTECC), and Canada will compete with the #TeamUSATennis squad to see who reigns supreme.

Let’s meet the junior players who join together as Team USA to battle for the America’s Cup! On the girls’ side; Reese Brantmeier, Katja Wiersholm, Clervie Ngounoue, and Ashlyn Krueger round out the team for the red, white & blue. For the boys’, Ozan Colak, Kyle Kang, Adian Kim, and Jackson Armistead battle to keep the America’s Cup at home here at the Home of American Tennis. The team is coached by USTA Player Development Manager of Player ID & Development, Johnny Parkes and USTA National Coach, Lori Riffice.
