Three sectional titles are up for grabs this weekend at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., as the 2018 USTA Florida 55 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships get underway Saturday morning.

Teams will compete within their respective flights Saturday and Sunday in round robin dual match play, which consists of three doubles contests, with a team needing to win two of the three to claim the team wins.

The 3.0 women’s division consists of two flights, where each flight winner will secure their spot in the finals. The division finals are scheduled for first serve at 8:30 a.m. Monday morning.

The 4.0 women’s division consists of four flights, the winners of each flight securing a semifinal berth. The semifinals will be played Sunday at 2 p.m., with each semifinal winner earning their spot in the championship match.

The 4.0 men’s division features three flights, where the three flight winners and a wildcard team will earn their semifinal berths.

All division finals are slated for first serve on Monday at 8:30 a.m. A full listing of the 3.0 flights and schedule here, the 4.0 women flights and schedule here, and the 4.0 men flights and schedules here.

The 2017 Sectional Championships showed tough competition, as all three flight champions won 2-1. In the 3.0 women’s division, Duval County bested Marion County. In the 4.0 Divisions, the Broward County men defeated the South Miami-Dade men, and the Duval women bested the Broward County women.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
