The USTA League National Championships return to the USTA National Campus to host several title events throughout October and November.  Over 320,000 adult tennis players compete in USTA League competitions every year, making it the largest adult competitive tennis league in the United States.

Top teams in each of the 17 USTA Sections battle for National Championship titles in each of their respective divisions. 

Check-out all the action from the 18 & Over 5.0+ event. 

For more information on USTA League, please visit USTA.com/League or search #USTALeague on social media.
