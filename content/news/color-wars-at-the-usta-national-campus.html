Color Wars at the USTA National Campus

Alyssa Simonin | October 30, 2018

style
article-hero

Top USTA League players from the 17 USTA Sections have competed all year alongside their teammates to finally reach the National Championships. Teams from across the country suit up for battle in their assigned USTA Section colors and make their way to host sites from Las Vegas, Nevada to the USTA National Campus. 

The fun doesn't stop at Color Wars, players from all age brackets have a blast while competing at the USTA National Campus. These 55 & Over 7.0/9.0 teams certainly had a weekend they'll never forget. 

Follow along on social media for more USTA League Color War battles with #USTALeague, and all the action from the Home of American Tennis with #NationalCampus.
