MOST RECENT UPDATE: 7/31/2024

GENERAL PRESS RELEASE

There is an exciting development that will enhance your experience at the USTA National Campus. 

Beginning in June, we will be embarking on a reconstruction project in the Nemours Family Zone. This project is part of our ongoing commitment to provide you with the best facilities possible, tailored to meet your needs and preferences. The expected completion date for this project is mid-August.

The revamped Nemours Family Zone will feature 14 multi-racquet sport courts, designed to accommodate various racquet sports enthusiasts. Additionally, we will be introducing a small tournament administration building to streamline operations, a dedicated food and beverage area for your refreshment needs, and a large shade structure to ensure your comfort during your time at the facility.

During the construction period, all existing programs that are typically held in the Nemours Family Zone will be relocated to other areas of the campus. We assure you that every effort will be made to minimize disruptions to your schedule and ensure continuity of service. Additionally, we will keep you informed throughout the construction process with relevant updates.

We are incredibly excited about the possibilities that this new and vibrant area will bring to the USTA National Campus. It will provide a fun and inviting environment for individuals of all ages and skill levels, offering a wide range of racquet sport options to explore and enjoy.

We sincerely appreciate your understanding and cooperation during this period of transformation. We look forward to welcoming you to the enhanced Nemours Family Zone upon its completion, and we are confident that it will enrich your experience at the USTA National Campus.

Thank you for your continued support, and we cannot wait to share this exciting new space with you.

For any questions, please contact us at nationalcampusprograms@usta.com
