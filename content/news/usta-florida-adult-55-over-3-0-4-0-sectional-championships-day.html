2018 USTA FL 55 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships

Day One Schedule Complete Despite Early Rain Delay

May 5, 2018

style
article-hero

An ominous forecast produced just one minor rain delay on Saturday, as all matches on the schedule were completed on the opening day of the 2018 USTA Florida Adult 55 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus.

The day was extended deeper into the evening after the weather delay, but in the end all participating squads finished a pair of matches over the course of the day.

In the women’s 3.0 division, the Flights 1 and 2 took a similar shape, with two teams going undefeated, two going winless, and one earning a split. In Flight 1, both Alachua (300) and IRMSL (Magnolia Lakes) went 2-0 on Saturday, including four wins and two losses in individual courts contested and six total sets lost. Duval (Southside Tennis Center) and Collier (Marco YMCA) have the inside track in Flight 2, taking five of the six individual court matchups. Duval lost one less set and two more games in the comparison with Collier.

For the women’s 4.0 division, three teams soared to the top of their respective flights. Pinellas (SPTC) in Flight 2, Sarasota (LWR) in Flight 3, and Collier (Pelican Bay North) of Flight 4 all went 2-0 on Saturday behind five victories in six individual matchups played. Collier surrendered the fewest amount of sets (three) and Sarasota dropped the least amount of games (42). Flight 1 presents a totally different story after all four squads went 1-1 on the day. Sarasota (BNR) holds a slight advantage after losing six sets and 52 games, slotting them at the top of the flight, but SMD (Dante Fascell), Hillsborough (Apollo Beach) and Brevard (Rockledge) all remain in the hunt.

In the lone men’s competition on the weekend, two of the 4.0 flights have a clear favorite after the opening day while the other will come down to a head-to-head encounter on Sunday. Flight 1 will be decided when Broward (Parkland) and Marion (CCO) clash after the two went undefeated on day one. Broward was the most dominant team of any flight, taking all six courts with just one set and 43 games lost. The loser of that dual will still be in good shape to claim the wild card spot in the semifinals. Duval (Eagle Harbor) and Collier (The Strand) will look to cap off undefeated runs through Flight 2 and Flight 3, respectively, in their final match Sunday.

Play continues at 8:00 a.m. on Sunday. A full listing of the 3.0 flights and schedule here, the 4.0 women flights and schedule here, and the 4.0 men flights and schedules here.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
