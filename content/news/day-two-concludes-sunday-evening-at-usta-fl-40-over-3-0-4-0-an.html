USTA FL 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships and 65 & Over Invitational

Day Two Concludes Sunday Evening

November 15, 2020

style
article-hero

The 2020 USTA Florida 40 & Over Women’s 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships has moved into the semifinal and final rounds while the USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Invitational partially got underway Sunday at the USTA National Campus.

Four squads played their way into the 3.0 knockout stages, with three of them advancing without a loss. Broward was absolutely dominant in Flight 1, not only prevailing in all four of their duals but doing so without giving away one individual matchup; they surrendered a mere three sets. Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) and Miami-Dade (Kendall Tennis Training Center – Miami) came through Flight 2 and 3, respectively, unscathed with a record of 3-0. Joining that trio in the semifinals thanks to earning the wild card is Flight 1’s second-place crew Duval (Jacksonville Golf and Country Club).

Monday will see Broward clash with Miami-Dade and Orange/Seminole take on Duval.

It’s down to the championship tilt in the 4.0 division, where Hillsborough (FishHawk Ranch – Lithia) will square off with Pinellas (City of Clearwater). The day began with the remaining duals in flight play, resulting in both Pinellas in Flight 2 and Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) from Flight 3 going a perfect 3-0. Hillsborough actually finished tied atop Flight 1 with Miami-Dade (Dante Fascell Park – Miami) at 2-1 overall, but advanced to the semifinal round thanks to owning the head-to-head tiebreaker. Securing the wild card spot in the final four was Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala, the runner-up in Flight 2.

In the semifinals, Hillsborough held off Broward by a 2-1 margin after claiming both of the doubles courts; Christina Dorothy paired up with Julie Martin to take line one 6-4, 6-1 while teammates Jackie Caouette and Shannon Deck rallied 4-6, 6-3, 1-0 at the second position. Pinellas used the same formula to defeat Marion, losing the singles matchup but receiving a 6-4, 0-6, 1-0 victory from Jennifer Drukteinis and Jennifer Garlick on court one along with a 7-5, 6-4 effort from Kelly Hansen and Anne Pulley in the second spot.

Just one division in the 65 & Over Sectional Invitational kicked off play on Sunday, as the three squads in Flight 2 of the 8.0 competition stepped onto court for the first time. Collier (Lighthouse Bay – Bonita Springs) played twice, securing a sweep over Polk (Lake Wales Country Club) and coming up short versus Pinellas (City of Clearwater).
