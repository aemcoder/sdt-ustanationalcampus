Rising Wheelchair Tennis Stars Get to Work

Alyssa Simonin | November 26, 2018

style
article-hero

Up-and-coming American Wheelchair tennis stars hit the court together at the USTA #NationalCampus for a high performance training camp. Participating players included; Chris Herman - senior at the University of Florida, Casey Ratzlaff - Wichita State junior, Conner Stroud - Alabama freshman, and former University of Arizona athlete, Jason Keatseangsilp. 

Catch more rising American stars training at the USTA NationalCampus on social media with #NationalCampus.
