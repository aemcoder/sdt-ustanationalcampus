style
article-hero

Since opening day on Jan. 2, courts at the USTA National Campus have featured an incredible amount of activity. Thanks to perfect weather conditions during Week 2, collegiate programs also are now taking advantage of one of the most dynamic tennis facilities in the world.

The National Campus serves as the home for the University of Central Florida’s men’s and women’s tennis teams, and other schools have held practices at the collegiate complex as well. This weekend's UCF tournament includes New Mexico, Arizona and Texas Tech, while UCF hosts Iowa on Saturday at 12 p.m. to mark the first women’s match. Admission and parking are free.

This weekend will be highlighted by the debut of live streaming at the collegiate complex. All courts will be available on Playsight.com.

Live Streaming on PlaySight.com - Jan. 13-16

College Matches at the USTA National Campus – Jan. 13-16

Friday, Jan. 13 – Arizona vs. Texas Tech – Men – 4 p.m.

Saturday, Jan. 14 – Texas Tech vs. New Mexico – Men – 12 p.m.

Saturday, Jan. 14 – Arizona vs. New Mexico – Men – 6 p.m.

Saturday, Jan. 14 – UCF vs. Texas Tech – Men – 7 p.m.

Sunday, Jan. 15 – UCF vs. Iowa – Women – 12 p.m.

Sunday, Jan. 15 – UCF vs. New Mexico – Men – 2 p.m.

Monday, Jan. 16 – UCF vs. Arizona – Men – 12 p.m.

Times are subject to change in case of weather

Approximately 300 collegiate matches are scheduled at the USTA National Campus in 2017, with schools from all levels traveling to Orlando.

Spring Break Sports will run its tennis calendar from February-March, and the USTA’s College MatchDay series kicks off Saturday, Jan. 21, with Alabama and Michigan squaring off on the men’s side at 6 p.m. (Gates open at 4 p.m.). Fans can enjoy food, drinks, games and much more, while a free kids’ clinic will be offered at 5 p.m.

To RSVP for free tickets to each College MatchDay event, visit CollegeMatchDay2017.com.
