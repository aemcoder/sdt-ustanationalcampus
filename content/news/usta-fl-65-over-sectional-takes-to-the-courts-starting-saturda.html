Kicking off their 2018 league slate at the National Campus, USTA Florida brings the section’s best 65 & Over men’s and women’s teams to compete for Sunshine State supremacy this weekend. A total of five divisions, two men’s (7.0 and 8.0) along with three women’s (6.0, 7.0 and 8.0), will be in action.

The women’s 6.0 and men’s 7.0 feature just one flight, meaning the top finisher in the flight will claim the sectional title. The remaining divisions are composed of two flights with a championship match pitting the pair of flight winners against each other with the trophy on the line.

Play will begin at 8:00 a.m. on all three days of play. A full listing of the divisional teams and flights can be found here.

At the 2017 USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Championships, Duval (Southside Tennis Complex) took home the women’s 6.0 title while IRSLM (NRSTC) was crowned the women’s 7.0 champion and Collier (Hunters Ridge) emerged from the women’s 8.0 division. On the men’s side, Marion (Villages) won the 7.0 division and Hillsborough (Buckhorn) bested the competition for the 8.0 title.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
