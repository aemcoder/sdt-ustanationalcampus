Junior Match Play Set for July 28-31

Brian Ormiston | July 1, 2020

style
article-hero

The USTA will be hosting opportunities for juniors to get back on court as they look to raise their college recruiting profile. The USTA National Campus All-American Match Play events will take place July 28-31 at the Lake Nona facility, and registration will be free for UTR members and just $10 for non-UTR members.

For complete information, visit the UTR Registration Page.

To be eligible, players must be American juniors with high school graduation years 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024. Registration opens on July 8 at 10 a.m. ET, and is available on a first-come, first-serve basis. Players will receive two level-based UTR singles matches. 

In addition to match play, participants are invited to sign up for training sessions with USTA Teaching Pros. Each day during the week of July 27-31, sessions will take place from 1-3 p.m. and all players can sign up, even if they are not registered for match play that day. Sessions are $40 each day and players can email Tina.Lee@usta.com for more information and to sign up. 

USTA National Campus All-American Match Play Schedule

Tuesday, July 28 - Girls UTR 7.50 and Up

Wednesday, July 29 - Girls UTR 5.00 - 7.49

Thursday, July 30 - Boys UTR 8.50 at Up

Friday, July 31 - Boys UTR 6.00 - 8.49 

Daily Schedule 

1-3 p.m. - Additional Training Session with USTA Teaching Pros - Cost is $40 and email Tina.Lee@usta.com for more information and to sign up 

3-4 p.m. - Check-In and Warm-Ups

4-5:30 p.m. - Match 1

5:30-6:30 p.m. - Break

Not Before 6:30 p.m. - Match 2
