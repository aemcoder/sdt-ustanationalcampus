Opening Day Kicks Off

February 28, 2021

style
article-hero

Saturday kicked off play at the USTA Florida Combo 18 & Over 5.5/7.5/9.5 Sectional Championships. All divisional flights began to take shape as dual matches were completed over the course of the day.

Teams are competing in dual matches consisting of three courts of doubles contests; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) has full control of Flight 1 in the men’s 7.5 division, all but clinching their spot in the final already. Their tandems dominated the day, taking all six courts with just one set and 21 games lost. Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) holds the advantage in Flight 2 as the lone undefeated squad, but both Pinellas (Safety Harbor Resort and Spa) and Hillsborough (HCC Tennis Complex – Tampa) remain strongly in the mix.

Nothing has been decided in the men’s 9.5 competition, as only one round of duals was contested on Saturday. Both matches ended in 2-1 results, with Okaloosa/Walton/Bay (Rosemary Beach Racquet Club) besting Pinellas (Total Tennis LLC – Clearwater) and Broward (Parkland Tennis Center) defeating Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville).

Eyeing locking up a place in the women’s 5.5 championship dual are South Miami-Dade (Crandon Park Tennis Center – Key Biscayne) in Flight one and Alachua (Haile Plantation Country Club – Gainesville) from Flight 2. South Miami-Dade is a win over Hillsborough (FishHawk Ranch – Lithia) away from advancing; meanwhile for Alachua, they still have two matches left to contest, needing only to take down Escambia (Roger Scott Tennis Center – Pensacola) to clinch the flight.

Flight leaders heading into day two in the women’s 7.5 division include Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville), IRMSL (Buttonwood Tennis Club – Stuart), and Hillsborough (Tampa Yacht & Country Club). Alachua will be going up against Leon (Forestmeadows Park & Athletic Center – Tallahassee) with Flight 1 on the line, while IRMSL will be across the net from Orange/Seminole/Lake (Red Bug Lake Park – Casselberry), a squad with an equal number of individual matchup victories plus more sets and games won, in Flight 2 action. Hillsborough had the most complete performance Saturday, taking their pair of duals in Flight 3 on the strength of five courts and 11 sets earned.

In a very competitive women’s 9.5 division, Hillsborough (The Club at Cheval – Lutz) was the only team to escape Saturday without suffering a loss; they managed a perfect 2-0 record overall after five individual matchups and 10 sets collected. Day two could very well shake up the standings in a major way, as a trio of squads sitting at 1-1 is still very much alive to capture the title.
