You can now easily stay up to date with the newest tennis merchandise and technology thanks to the USTA National Campus.

The 64-acre facility, which boasts 100 courts of various surfaces and opens to the public starting January 2017, will also offer a state-of-the-art Pro Shop powered by Fromuth Tennis. The pro shop will feature the newest product releases from the top tennis brands like adidas, Nike, Wilson, HEAD and Babolat as well as unique National Campus memorabilia.

The 2,800-square-foot pro shop will also feature innovation through merchandising and technology, delivering an unmatched guest experience with every visit to the shop.

And if you happen to purchase a racquet at the pro shop and need to have it strung, the National Campus can help with that too. Located directly outside of the shop, in the Welcome Center lobby, is a top-notch stringing facility – appropriately called The Racquet Bar.

At The Racquet Bar, some of the best stringers in the industry will make each stringing a true customization experience. From deciding on the right string at the right tension for the right racquet, to making sure all of your racquets have identical weights and balances, this team is there for you.

The stringing team boasts an impressive resume, previously stringing for some of the top players on the ATP and WTA tours, at Grand Slam events as well as for the U.S. Davis Cup Team.

The ultimate goal of the both the Pro Shop and Racquet Bar is to elevate a player’s game while also enhancing their understanding of and appreciation for their equipment.
