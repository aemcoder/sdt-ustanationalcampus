Matches Continued Despite Heavy Rain and Wind

April 15, 2018

The second day of the USTA Florida 65 & Over Sectional Championships battled through heavy rain and wind gusts on Sunday to complete a majority of Sunday’s matches. Amongst the highlights on day two was Duval (Southside Tennis Center) clinching the women’s 6.0 divisional title. All other divisions will be decided on Monday.

After defeating Collier (Pelican Marsh) twice on Saturday, Duval needed only one more head-to-head victory over Collier to take home the crown in the division composed of just two teams. They came through in their first opportunity before Collier won the final meeting. Both teams dropped 14 sets over the four contests, while Duval actually lost three more games (114-111).

In the men’s 7.0 division, the lone flight has begun to take shape, but things are still far from being decided going into Monday. Marion (Villages), Collier (Players Club), and Hillsborough (Temple Terrace) all sit with an overall record of 3-1. Marion holds a slight advantage with nine court wins compared to eight for Collier and Hillsborough. For Monday’s matches, Hillsborough and Marion will face off while Collier goes up against last place Alachua (JTC).

The women’s 7.0 divisional final is set, as Pinellas (Seminole Lakes Golden Aces) emerged from Flight 1 and Collier (World Tennis Center) took Flight 2. Pinellas swept all three of their duals with ease, losing just one individual matchup and 64 games. Collier was challenged in their flight, finishing with a 3-1 team record behind 9 court victories. For the two days, Collier gave away eight sets.

In the men’s 8.0 division, Hillsborough (Riverhills) and Collier (Lighthouse Bay) have earned the right to battle for the sectional title. Both squads went undefeated in round robin action, picked up four wins. Collier put together a slightly more impressive performance over the course of the two days, taking 10 of the 12 individual matchups they contested, while Hillsborough claimed nine.

The most dominant team so far in the event has been the Pinellas (McMullen) 8.0 women. The squad not only swept all three head-to-head matchups to win Flight 2, but in doing so did not even surrender a single set. They will need to get through Collier (Naples Grande), who also claimed Flight 1 with relative ease behind four sweeps and 10 individual court wins.

The final day of play gets underway at 8:30 a.m. A full listing of the divisional flights and standings can be found here.

Established in 1980, USTA League has grown from 13,000 players in a few parts of the country in its first year to more than 900,000 players across the nation today, making it the world’s largest recreational tennis league.

USTA League was established to provide adult recreational players throughout the country with the opportunity to compete against players of similar ability levels. Players participate on teams in a league format, which is administered by the USTA through its 17 sections. The league groups players by using six National Tennis Rating Program (NTRP) levels, ranging from 2.5 (entry) to 5.0 (advanced). USTA League is open to any USTA member 18 years of age or older.
