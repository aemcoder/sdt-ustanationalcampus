2020 USTA FL 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships

Three More Titles on the Line

October 8, 2020

style
article-hero

Another trio of section titles are at stake this weekend as the USTA National Campus welcomes the USTA Florida 55 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships. The women’s 8.0 division will be contested Friday, Oct. 9, through Sunday, Oct. 11, while the women’s 6.0 and men’s 8.0 competitions will take to the courts starting Saturday, Oct.10, and concluding Monday, Oct. 12.

Teams will be representing regions from across the state in a weekend of doubles play, squaring off in dual matches made up of three courts. In order to claim the dual victory, a team must win at least two of those courts.

Kicking off the tournament are the eight women’s 8.0 squads, which have been divided into two flights of four. All round-robin matches are scheduled for Friday and Saturday, with the two crews finishing atop their respective flight moving on to Sunday’s championship tilt.

Getting underway a day later, the women’s 6.0 division is composed of nine teams and the men’s 8.0 competition is made up of a total of 10. Just as with the women’s 8.0 format, squads will begin the weekend in one of two flights with the goal of earning the best record, thus booking a place in the final. Duals within those flights are slated for Saturday and Sunday while the divisional championship matches will be contested Monday morning.
