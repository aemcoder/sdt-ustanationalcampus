Men’s 4.5 Division Underway

The 2020 USTA Florida 18 & Over Men’s 3.0/3.5/4.5 & Women’s 3.5/4.0 Sectional Championships kicked off Friday afternoon with the 10 squads in the men’s 4.5 division taking part in their first two rounds of flight play.

Teams are competing in dual matches consisting of three matchups in total – one singles and two doubles; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Flight 1 of the men’s 4.5 competition is likely to be won by the victor of Saturday’s head-to-head meeting between Alachua (D B Racquet Club – Gainesville) and Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) after the two squads went 2-0 to open the weekend. However, Escambia (Independent) sits at 1-1 and could still cause a shakeup in the standings. Meanwhile, Hillsborough (Sandra W Freedman Tennis Complex – Tampa) has full control of Flight 2 as the lone unbeaten; a trio of teams brings a 1-1 overall record into day two of the event.

Saturday’s schedule of play will get underway at 7:30 a.m. The men’s 3.5 and women’s 4.0 divisions are slated to begin on Saturday while first matches of the men’s 3.0 and women’s 3.5 competitions are set for Sunday.
