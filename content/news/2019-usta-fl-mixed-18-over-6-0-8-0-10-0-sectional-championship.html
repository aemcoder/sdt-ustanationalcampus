2019 USTA FL Mixed 18 & Over 6.0/8.0/10.0 Sectional Championships

Sunshine State League Play Continues

October 9, 2019

style
article-hero

Action in the USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over divisions wraps up this weekend at the USTA National Campus, as the 6.0, 8.0, and 10.0 sectional titles will be on the line. Opening dual matches of the event will get started at 8 a.m. Saturday, Oct. 12.

A dual consists of three doubles contests; a squad needs to take at least two of those courts in order to claim the overall victory.

The 6.0 division has been broken down into two flights, with four teams in Flight 1 and the other five in Flight 2. Round robin action will play out Saturday and Sunday to determine which two crews will advance to the championship dual scheduled for Monday morning at 9 a.m.

Four flights and 17 squads make up the weekend’s 8.0 division. As with the 6.0 competition, round robin duals will take to the courts throughout Saturday and Sunday. The four flight winners will earn the right to battle it out in Monday’s semifinals, slated for 8:30 a.m., while the championship match will get underway at approximately 11:30 a.m.

Only two teams are in the running for the 10.0 crown heading into the weekend. Brevard (Kiwi Tennis Club – Indian Harbour Beach) and Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando) will go head-to-head three times over the course of Saturday and Sunday; the champion could be determined as early as Saturday evening.

Last year’s championship squads included Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) in the 6.0 division, Okaloosa/Walton/Bay (Bluewater Bay Tennis Center – Niceville) for the 8.0 competition, and Hillsborough (Independent) in the 10.0 division.
