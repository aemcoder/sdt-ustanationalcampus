Friday marked the opening day of play at the 2021 NTRP 50 & Over National Singles Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. All players competed in two rounds of matches within their divisional group as they battle for playoff position.

On the men’s side, the 3.0 division features 17 players divided in Groups A-D, while the 3.5 competition consists of 33 individuals placed into Groups A-H. The largest men’s division, 36 players make up the 4.0 Groups A-I to go along with the 24 that will take part in the Groups A-F in the 4.5 competition. 

In the women’s divisions, both the 3.0 and 4.0 have 24 competitors entered, starting off in Groups A-F. The 3.5 competition is the largest for the women, where 30 players make up Groups A-H; a total of 16 players are slated to vie for the 4.5 division, which features Groups A-D.

Following the completion of group play, the competitors will be placed into a playoff draw based on their round-robin results. The playoff contests are set to begin Saturday afternoon with winners to be crowned over the course of the day Sunday.

All matches are the best-of-three short sets (first to four games) with no-ad scoring. A five-point tiebreaker is played at three games all; if the tiebreaker reaches 4-4, the next point wins and a racquet spin will determine which player serves on the decisive point.
