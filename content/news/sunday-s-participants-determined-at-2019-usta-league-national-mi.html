Sunday's Participants Determined

November 16, 2019

article-hero

Another chilly day in Orlando set the stage for the crucial second day of action at the 2019 USTA League National Mixed 40 & Over Championships. As play concluded for the day, the semifinal participants for the event’s four divisions were determined.

In the 6.0 division, three teams turned in perfect 4-0 efforts to easily advance to the semifinals. Pacific Northwest (Eastside Tennis Center – Kirkland, Wash.) started the day in the second slot overall, but turned in a pair of sweeps to vault up into the top spot overall. They ended the day with 11 court victories, the same number as Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Fulton, Md.), but Pacific Northwest won more sets and a higher percentage of games. Also checking in without a loss was Intermountain (Independent – Las Vegas) thank to nine individual matchups going their way, good enough for third. Eastern began the day at 2-0, but faltered in both duals on Saturday, opening the door for 3-1 New England (Cedardale Health and Fitness Club – Haverhill, Mass.) to steal the fourth and final semifinal berth.

Eastern will look to topple No. 1 Pacific Northwest while unbeaten squads Mid-Atlantic and Intermountain will square off in the semifinal round Sunday morning.

Texas (Independent – Plano) polished off their domination of flight play in the 7.0 competition by taking all 12 individual matchups, collecting 24 sets and giving away only five. Slotting in just behind them for second in the division was Southern (Southaven Tennis Center – Southaven, Miss.), also with a 4-0 record on the strength of 11 court victories. Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Columbia, Md.) locked up the third seed for Sunday’s semifinals after their players came through in nine of the 12 courts they contested. Southern California (Alta Vista Racquet Center – Redondo Beach) and Northern California (Independent – Santa Clara) were the other two clubs entering day two unscathed, and while they both stumbled on Saturday, Southern California’s nine individual matchups propelled them into the semifinals.

Sunday’s pairings will see Texas across the net from Southern California and Southern taking on Mid-Atlantic.

A trio of teams moved on to the playoff stages in 8.0 play with little resistance. Southern California (Fountain Valley Tennis Center) swept through all 12 of their individual matchups, doing so with an emphatic 24-2 record in sets played. Florida (Longboat Key Tennis Center) swept both of their foes on Saturday to finish undefeated with 11 court victories and 22 sets to their credit, while Middle States (Nassau Racquet & Tennis Club – Skillman, N.J.) came through with 10 courts and 21 sets claimed. Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Fairfax, Va.), 2-0 at the beginning of the day, managed to absorb a team loss and still finish fourth overall to reach the semifinals. They will be up against Southern California and Florida is slated to battle Middle States on Sunday.

The 9.0 competition was the lone division in which only two squads wrapped up flight play unbeaten. Leading the way was Southern (Memphis University School – Memphis, Tenn.), one of the most thorough teams on the weekend so far. Their players cruised in all 12 individual matchups over the course of Friday and Saturday, letting just a single set slip away. Earning the second seed was Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Washington, D.C.), who did slip up on one court but easily played their way to a 4-0 team record.

From there, it came down to four crews who checked in at 3-1, and it would ultimately be a pair of teams who had hopes of undefeated records moving on. Missouri Valley (Hanscom Tennis Center – Omaha, Neb.) managed to finish as the best one-loss squad for third overall thanks to nine court victories. Florida (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) just edged out Northern California, eight individual matchups to seven, for the fourth and final semifinal spot.

Sunday morning’s dual matches will pit Southern taking on Florida and Mid-Atlantic versus Missouri Valley.

All semifinals will get underway at 7:30 a.m. with the divisional finals and third-place playoffs scheduled for approximately 10:30 a.m.
