COLLEGE MATCHDAY IN MARCH

Brian Ormiston | March 1, 2017

style
article-hero

The Sunshine State will be well represented at College MatchDay on the USTA National Campus in March.

The action kicks off on Wednesday, March 8, when the hometown UCF Knights men’s team hosts Penn State. That will be followed by a matchup of in-state rivals Florida and Florida State (men) on March 10, with the No. 1-ranked Florida women wrapping things up March 14 against No. 3 Ohio State.

First serve for each match is at 6 p.m. ET, with festivities beginning at 4 p.m. Fans will be treated to live music, games, a free kids’ clinic, selfie pod, face painting and much more.

To RSVP for free tickets, visit CollegeMatchDay2017.com. If you cannot make it out to the campus, matches will air on ESPN3 through WatchESPN and the ESPN app.

UCF vs. Penn State (men) – March 8

WATCH: ESPN3

LIVE STATS: Tennis Ticker

UCF and PSU have some recent history. Their football teams squared off in 2013 and 2014, with the Knights stunning the Nittany Lions on their home field in 2013 only to watch as Penn State returned the favor with a last-second victory in Dublin, Ireland, a year later.

Now the two schools get together on the tennis court. UCF is led by 2014 National Coach of the Year John Roddick, who is in his first season in Orlando. Penn State head coach Jeff Zinn has been with the squad for six years and has been coaching tennis for 27 years overall.

The meeting between the Knights and the Nittany Lions will occur two days prior to one of the best rivalries in all of college sports.

No. 9 Florida vs. Florida State (men) – March 10

WATCH: ESPN3

LIVE STATS: Tennis Ticker

No. 9 Florida and Florida State will tangle on the men’s side on Friday, March 10, and more than 1,000 fans are expected to attend. UF has three wins over ranked teams through March 1 (USC, UCLA and Mississippi State). FSU, meanwhile, has accumulated a 9-3 record in 2017 and will have a big match against No. 20 Georgia Tech March 5 before coming to Orlando.

The Gators lead the all-time series, 56-20, with the Seminoles last posting a victory in the 2007 regular season.

Seminoles supporters will want to get to the National Campus early to take part in the FSU Meet-and-Greet with the team from 4:15 to 4:45 p.m.

No. 1 Florida vs. No. 3 Ohio State (women) – March 14

WATCH: ESPN3

LIVE STATS: Tennis Ticker

To conclude the inaugural season of College MatchDay at the National Campus, a potential NCAA Tournament women’s matchup is in store when No. 1 Florida meets No. 3 Ohio State on Tuesday, March 14.

The Gators claimed the ITA National Team Indoor Championship in February, defeating Michigan, Texas Tech, Georgia and North Carolina along the way. The reigning Big Ten champions, meantime, boast the No. 1-ranked player in the country in Francesca Di Lorenzo.

This will be the first women’s match between Florida and Ohio State since 1988 and the third overall. UF leads the series, 2-0.
