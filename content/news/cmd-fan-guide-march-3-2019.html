Fan Guide to College MatchDay, March 3

Complimentary tickets available on Ticketmaster.com

Brian Ormiston | February 26, 2019

style
article-hero

The Miami Hurricanes will make their College MatchDay debut when they rumble with the hometown UCF Knights in a men’s contest on Sunday, March 3, at 5 p.m. ET. The match will air live on Tennis Channel. The match is presented by the Central Florida Expressway Authority.

In-Depth with Miami and UCF 

Matchup

  • Miami (Fla.) vs. UCF - men.

Date and Time

  • Sunday, March 3

  • First serve: 5 p.m.

  • Gates open: 4 p.m.

Location

  • USTA National Campus (Collegiate Complex)

  • Address: 10000 USTA Blvd., Orlando, FL 32827

  • Phone: (844) 872-8667

Tickets

  • Click here to reserve tickets to College MatchDay. 

  • Seats are available on a first-come, first-serve basis, and standing-room viewing will be available once the seats are full.

Parking

  • Parking is free and is first-come, first-serve.

RV Parking

  • Fans with RVs are directed to park in the back row of the main parking lot.

Bus Parking

  • The bus lane is located near the front of the main parking lot.

Prematch Festivities

  • Starting at 4 p.m., fans can enjoy tailgating games, face painters, a caricature artist and much more, all for free.

  • A free, interactive College MatchDay kids’ clinic, powered by Net Generation, will begin at 4 p.m. There will be three 15-minute sessions held at the Collegiate Center.

  • There also will be giveaways throughout the night.

  • Merchandise, food and refreshments, including adult beverages, will be available for purchase.

Television

  • The match will air live on Tennis Channel.

Match Format

  • Doubles will open the match. The first team to win two of the three doubles matches earns one overall team point. Matches are one six-game set.

  • Following doubles, six singles matches take place (best of three sets). Each match is worth one team point.

  • The first school to secure four team points wins the match.

Courts

  • The national anthem and team introductions will take place on Collegiate Center Court 3 (side facing the parking lot), while all matches will take place on Courts 1-6.

Previous Meetings

  • Miami leads the all-time series with UCF, 15-3.

  • The Hurricanes won the first 14 meetings with the Knights, dating back to 1979. However, UCF has won three of the last four, all by 4-3 margins. That includes a Knights victory over the Hurricanes at the USTA National Campus in 2018.
