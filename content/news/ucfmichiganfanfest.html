Join us for UCF vs. Michigan Fan Fest This Saturday, March 5th!

This month, the National Campus is celebrating 5 Years of hosting collegiate tennis, including the hometown team of the UCF Knights.

Join us Saturday, March 5th for an all-day extravaganza as both the UCF and Michigan Men's and Women's teams battle it out at the National Campus!

The action-packed day will start at 11:30 a.m., with first serve of the women's match at 12:00 p.m. Afternoon festivities will begin at 2:30 p.m., with first serve of the men's match at 5:00 p.m. This is a free event, no tickets necessary Check out the full slate of the fan festival down below!

Food Trucks & Games

Our festivities will kick off at 11:30 a.m. with food trucks, inflatables, and games for the whole family to enjoy. Kids can also enjoy chalking on the walkway and write messages to players!

UCF Team Autographs

The UCF men's and women's teams will be signing autographs from 2:30-3:30 p.m. The teams will also be joined by UCF's very own Knightro and cheerleading squad!

Kid's Clinic

Families can join USTA National Campus Coaches and UCF Women's team from 3:30-4:30 for a Kid's Clinic!

Click here to register for the Kid's Clinic.
