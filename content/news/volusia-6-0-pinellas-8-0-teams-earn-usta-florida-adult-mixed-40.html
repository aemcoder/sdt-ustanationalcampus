Volusia 6.0, Pinellas 8.0 Earn Sectional Titles

June 4, 2017

style
article-hero

On the final day of play in the 2017 USTA Florida Adult Mixed 40 & Over Sectional Championships, two teams claimed titles and advanced on to compete for the national crown later this year.

Volusia had an opportunity to run away with the 6.0 division, entering Sunday with a 4-0 record. A team victory against Leon would have given them a perfect record and the sectional championship outright. However, Leon was able to take down Volusia, which coupled with Alachua’s victory over Polk, left three teams atop the standings at 4-1 – Volusia, Alachua and Leon.

That left the fate of the trio to be decided by the tiebreaker procedure. Leon fell out of the running due to losing more individual courts (six) than Volusia and Alachua (five) over the course of the tournament. In the end, Volusia’s 2-1 triumph over Alachua was the difference that awarded them the team title.

It was a tense clash in the final of the 8.0 division, featuring Broward and Pinellas. Pinellas managed to take all three first sets, including a 7-5 set at the first position that lasted well over an hour. After securing court three by the final score of 6-4, 6-4, they were just one individual match win away from the championship. However, Broward rallied back at line two with a 6-3 second set, sending that court into a decisive super tiebreak.

Simultaneously, the Pinellas pair secured the crucial break in the second set on court one for 5-3 and successfully served it out at love to clinch the sectional title for their squad.
