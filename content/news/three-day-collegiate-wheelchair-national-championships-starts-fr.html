Play Begins Friday at the Collegiate Center

April 19, 2018

 A total of 13 of the best collegiate wheelchair players from across the nation make their way to the USTA National Campus this weekend to take part in the 2018 USTA Collegiate Wheelchair Championships.

The three-day tournament features three draws of singles (Singles A, B, and C) along with two draws of doubles (Doubles Gold and Silver). All draws will use the round robin format, with Singles B consisting of five rounds and the others three rounds.

The Singles A and C draws are composed of four players. Competing in the A draw are Shelby Baron (Honolulu, Hawaii), Christopher Kelley (Grand Haven, Mich.), Chris Herman (Gulfport, Fla.) and Jose Estrada (San Diego, Calif.). Seeking a title in the C draw are Auburn Smith (Port Orange, Fla.), Avery Downing (Cordova, Tenn.), Sean Burns (Matthews, N.C.) and Piotr Pasik (East Lansing, Mich.).

Alexander Saporito (Prairieville, La.), Andrew Lee (Carlsbad, Calif.), Nathan Melnyk (Centerport, N.Y.), Lauren Haneke-Hopps (San Diego, Calif.) and Maude Jacques (Northport, Ala.) are the five competitors in the B draw.

For the doubles draws, trios of teams are set to battle. The pairs in the Gold draw are Burns/Downing, Herman/Melnyk and Haneke-Hopps/Saporito; Silver duos include Baron/Jacques, Kelley/Pasik and Lee/Estrada.

Singles action is slated to kick off all three days with doubles contests to follow. All matches will be played at the Collegiate Center. For more information on the 2018 USTA Collegiate Wheelchair Championships, click here.
