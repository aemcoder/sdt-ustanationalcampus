After Hours at the National Campus

A weather delay of nearly four hours kept matches on court well after midnight on the opening day of the 2018 USTA Florida 18 & Over 3.0/4.0/5.0+ Sectional Championships. But in the end, all of Saturday’s scheduled matches were completed.

In the lone flight of the men’s 3.0 competition, a pair of tightly-contested matches left Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) at 1-0 while Duval (Julington Creek Plantation) and Volusia (Minorca Tennis Center) will enter Sunday at 0-1. Leon holds a slight advantage overall thanks to dropping just five sets, while the other three lost six each.

In women’s 3.0 action, all four of the flights feature at least one team that posted a 2-0 record on day one. The two squads representing SMD, Dante Fascell Park and Crandon Park, have the inside track in Flight 1 and 3, respectively. Duval (Julington Creek Plantation) and Marion (Fort King Tennis Center) will square off with the top spot in Flight 2 on the line, while Escambia (Pensacola Athletic Center) or Duval (Florida Yacht Club) appear to be well on their way to locking up a semifinal spot out of Flight 4.

Broward (Weston Tennis Center), SMD (Ron Ehmann Tennis Center) and Escambia collected two team victories on Saturday in the men’s 4.0 division. Broward in Flight 1 and SMD from Flight 2 need just one more dual win to advance to the divisional semifinals; Escambia likely needs a pair of dual triumphs on the second day of play, as a trio of squads sits just behind Escambia at 1-1 in Flight 3. Sarasota (Longwood Athletic Club) and Pinellas (St. Petersburg Tennis Center) are tied atop Flight 4 at 2-0 with Duval (Florida Yacht Club) still in the mix after splitting their two matches.

The only team to clinch a semifinal berth on Saturday was the 4.0 women of Collier (Heritage Bay), as they were absolutely dominant in producing a pair of sweeps in Flight 4 behind the strength of surrendering just three sets and 63 games. Looking to join Collier in the semifinals are Escambia, South Palm (The Oaks) and Hillsborough (Westchase); all three of those teams ended Saturday with 2-0 overall records and need to win just once on Sunday.

With the men’s 5.0+ competition consisting of just four squads, no team has been eliminated from contention so far. Broward (Midtown Athletic Club) and Hillsborough (Northdale Golf and Tennis Club) were the two winners on Saturday, leaving them in good position. Meanwhile, Orange/Seminole (Orlando Tennis Center) and SMD (Doral Park) will have some work to do on Sunday in order to claim the section crown.

The matchup for the 5.0+ women’s championship is nearly set after a solid effort from both Orange/Seminole (National Campus) in Flight 1 and Duval (Florida Yacht Club) in Flight 2. The two teams won both of their duals easily, closing out day one with five individual court wins to just one defeat. Orange/Seminole locks up a spot in the final by defeating Pinellas (Clearwater) in Sunday’s rematch while Duval just needs to take care of 0-2 Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center).
