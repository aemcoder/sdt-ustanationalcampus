Teams Eye Semifinals After Day One

The USTA League National 65 & Over 7.0/9.0 Invitational, held at the USTA National Campus, kicked off play on Friday, as every team in the event got two dual matches under their belt.

After one day in the men’s 7.0 competition, just four squads remain without a loss. Managing to come out on top in both duals on Friday were Middle States (Community Park Tennis Complex – Princeton, N.J.), Southern (Clarke Oconee Tennis Association – Ga.), Southwest (Iron Oaks Tennis Club – Sun Lakes, Ariz.), and Florida (Glenview Champions Tennis Club – The Villages); Middle States, Southern, and Southwest both captured five of their six individual matchups while Florida collected four. With a games won percentage of 66%, Middle States currently sits atop the standings. None of the unbeaten crews scheduled to face off against each other on Saturday, but should one or more falter, a group of nine teams at 1-1 overall will be waiting to overtake them.

At least one of the women’s 7.0 semifinalists will have a loss, as three of the 16 squads played their way to a 2-0 start to the weekend. Eastern (Tennis Club of Hastings – Hastings-on-Hudson, N.Y.) turned in the best effort thanks to winnings 11 sets and five total courts. Coming in just behind them were Florida (Boone Park Tennis Courts – Jacksonville) and Southern (Independent – Lafayette, La.) with four individual victories. Additionally, 10 others split their duals on day one, leaving the large majority of teams still in the hunt.

Eastern (Independent – Westchester/Rockland, N.Y.), Middles States (Independent – Manasquan, N.J.), and Pacific Northwest (Independent – Yakima, Wash.) have all but secured a semifinal spot in the six-member men’s 9.0 division thanks to 2-0 performances on Friday. The focus will now shift to the trio currently sitting at 0-2 overall, as one squad from the group of Northern California (McInnis Park – San Rafael), Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Virginia Beach, Va.), and Southern (Independent – Selma, Ala.) will advance to Sunday.

The semifinal hopefuls in the women’s 9.0 competition have likely been whittled down to five. Leading the way is Pacific Northwest (Independent – Northern Ore.), as they not only went 2-0 but also a perfect 6-for-6 in their individual matchups. Checking in just behind them are undefeated Southern California (Westin Mission Hills – Rancho Mirage) and Southern (Independent – Birmingham, Ala.), each with five court victories, followed by unscathed New England (The Edge at South Burlington – South Burlington, Vt.). Hanging back with a 1-1 record and work left to do is Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Northern Va.).

Teams placing in the top four of their respective division will move on to Sunday’s playoff rounds. The semifinals are scheduled for 8:30 a.m. with the finals and third-place playoffs to follow at approximately 11:00 a.m.
