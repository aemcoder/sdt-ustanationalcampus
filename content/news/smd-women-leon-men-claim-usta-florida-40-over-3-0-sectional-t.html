USTA Florida Adult 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships

SMD, Leon Claim 3.0 Titles; Broward Sweeps 4.0

July 16, 2017

style
article-hero

Sunday produced four more USTA Florida sectional champions, this time from the 40 & Over 3.0 and 4.0 divisions. The SMD women’s and Leon men’s squads emerged victorious in the 3.0 division, while teams from Broward swept both the men’s and women’s titles in the 4.0 division.

SMD had little trouble in putting away Collier in the morning’s semifinal matchup, winning the match, 4-1, claiming both singles contests along with two of the three doubles matchups. Duval Eagle Harbor, which advanced to the semifinals via the wild card position, slipped past Escambia by a 3-2 margin in the other semi. The dual match came down to the second doubles spot, where Duval won 6-4, 4-6, [1-0].

In the women’s 3.0 final, SMD once again dominated the singles courts, dropping a combined six games over four sets. Duval staked claim to the top two doubles positions, but a 6-1, 6-4 victory from SMD at the third doubles spot was enough to clinch the team triumph.

Despite not playing a match on Sunday, the Leon men’s 3.0 team won the crown. The only match in that division featured Polk and Duval, with Polk needing to sweep all five courts to stay in the running for the division. While Polk did collect the team victory, it did so by losing on two courts, mathematically eliminating the team from contention and sending Leon on to the national championship later this year.

Both women’s 4.0 semifinals ended in tight 3-2 decisions, with Orange/Seminole and Broward moving on to play in the championship. Orange/Seminole struggled in the singles matches, losing both in straight sets, but managed to come out on top on all three doubles courts, including a 2-6, 6-0, [1-0] victory on court one. Broward defeated Volusia despite a retirement at the first singles spot, winning in straight sets at the second position while also taking doubles courts two and three.

The women’s 4.0 championship match came down to the first singles court after Broward won on court two and Orange/Seminole took two of the three doubles contests. Broward won the opening set in the decisive singles match before Orange/Seminole rallied in the second to take it in a tiebreak. However, it was Broward that would bounce back in the match tiebreak to secure the team title., 6-3, 6-7, [1-0].

While the women’s 4.0 semifinals were closely contested, the men’s 4.0 semifinals were both 5-0 sweeps. Broward won both singles courts without dropping a set against Leon. Despite being pushed to a pair of match tiebreaks in doubles, Broward managed to pull out all three of those contests as well. Meanwhile SMD swept aside Polk, going a perfect 10-for-10 in sets.

Broward and SMD proved to be a tense final. Each squad won a singles and doubles match in straight sets, putting all the focus on the number two doubles court. Broward eased through the first set 6-2 only to see SMD come back in the second 7-5. The sectional crown was decided by a match tiebreak, which went the way of Broward.
