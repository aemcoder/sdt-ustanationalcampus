Collegiate Series Features Big Matches

Brian Ormiston | March 6, 2019

style
article-hero

Starting on March 6, the USTA National Campus hosts five exciting matches as part of its Collegiate Series in 2019.

Penn State and Florida Gulf Coast kick it off in a men’s match with first serve set for March 6 at noon. The Penn State women’s team then takes the courts March 7 at noon vs. Virginia Commonwealth.

Those weekday matches lead into a top-10 women’s tilt with No. 4 Duke colliding with No. 8 Michigan on March 9 at noon. One day later, Atlantic Coast Conference rivals Florida State and Miami do battle on the men’s side at 5 p.m.

To wrap it up, Notre Dame and Iowa will get together in a women’s match on March 12 at 11 a.m.

The latter three matches will feature PlayFair using PlaySight technology.

The USTA National Campus Collegiate Series concludes on April 4 in one of the best rivalries in college sports as Florida A&M takes on Bethune-Cookman in a men’s and women’s feature at 6 p.m.
