Team USA, Chile Men Earn Day One Victories

January 30, 2018

style
article-hero

Two dual matches were contested on the opening day of the 2018 BNP Paribas Wheelchair World Team Cup Americas Qualifications, both of which were on the men's side. The United States and Chile were victorious, both by the final margin of 2-1. 

The Chilean men dominated singles play to defeat Ecuador. Jaime Sepulveda breezed through the opening set 6-1 before holding on in a tight second frame, 7-5, against Eduardo Lopez. At the second singles position, Chile's Alexander Cataldo dropped only one game versus Francisco Beltran. In the lone doubles contest, Beltran and Lopez swept aside Cataldo and Francisco Cayulef 6-0, 6-0.

Casey Ratzlaff got Team USA off to a hot start with his 6-0, 6-1 result over Colombia's Manuel Sanchez at the top singles spot. Eliecer Oquendo kept his team alive by battling past American Chris Herman 6-2, 6-7(3), 6-2. That turned all the focus onto the doubles, where Herman and Ratzlaff clashed with Oquendo and Sanchez. The Colombian duo took the first frame, 6-1, but Herman and Ratzlaff responded to win the second 6-4. In the decisive match tiebreak, the American pair staged a comeback from 5-1 down to clinch the team triumph by the slimmest of margins, 11 points to nine.
