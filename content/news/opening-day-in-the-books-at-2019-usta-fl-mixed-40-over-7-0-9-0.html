Opening Day in the Books

Saturday marked the opening day of action at the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships and Women’s 40 & Over 2.5 Invitational. Every squad played two dual matches over the course of the day.

Four teams secured the inside position to locking up a spot in Monday’s 7.0 semifinal round. Broward (Frank Veltri Tennis Center – Plantation) was the most dominant, posting a 2-0 record in Flight 1 thanks to five court victories and just one set lost. The remaining trio of flight leaders had a much more difficult road to get to the top of their respective flights. Alachua (Jonesville Tennis Center – Gainesville) in Flight 2, SMD (Crandon Park Tennis Center – Key Biscayne) from Flight 3, and Pinellas (Belleair Country Club) out of Flight 4 all came away with four individual wins compared with two losses and a total of six sets surrendered. The four semifinalists will be determined at the end of Sunday’s slate, which includes two more duals for all squads.

In the 9.0 division, Flight 1 will be determined when Sarasota (Bath and Racquet Club) and Pinellas (City of Clearwater) clash Sunday morning after both managed to come through with 2-0 efforts on day one. Sarasota racked up five court victories with three sets lost while Pinellas took four courts after letting five sets get away. Meanwhile, Flight 2 is in the firm control of Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) with one round of team matches remaining. Broward, on the strength of five individual victories, only needs to defeat third-place Escambia to move into the divisional final.

A similar scenario has played out in the Women’s 2.5 Invitational. Dominant forces Escambia (Independent) and Hillsborough (Cheval Athletic Club – Lutz) will go head-to-head with the top spot in Flight 1 on the line. The two had nearly equal performances on the day (five court wins), with Escambia giving away one less set (two) and two fewer games (34). Miami/Dade (Independent) looks to be well on their way to competing in the final, sitting at 2-0 in Flight 2 with just a matchup against 0-2 Alachua left to go.

Sunday’s schedule gets underway at 8 a.m. Both the 9.0 and Women’s 2.5 Invitational championship contests will take to the courts at 2 p.m.
