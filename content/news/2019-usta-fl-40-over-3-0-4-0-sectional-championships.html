2019 USTA FL 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships

July 18, 2019

style
article-hero

USTA Florida Adult tournament play returns to the USTA National Campus for the first of two consecutive weekends, starting with the 40 & Over 3.0/4.0 Sectional Championships. First matches are slated for 7:30 a.m. on Saturday, July 20 with the event to conclude on Monday, July 22.

Each dual match consists of two lines of singles and three lines of doubles, meaning a team needs to capture at least three of those matchups to secure the overall victory. 

A total of five squads have entered into the men’s 3.0 division this year; all five have been grouped into a singles round-robin flight. Dual matches will be contested on Saturday and Sunday only, with the divisional champion to be decided by Sunday evening based on head-to-head results.

The largest of the divisions for the weekend is the women’s 3.0, which features 17 teams divided up into four divisions overall. Flight play will be spread out over the first two days of the event before the four flight winners advanced to Monday morning’s semifinal round.

For the men’s 4.0 competition, 12 squads have been split equally into three flights, meaning a semifinal wild card slot will be available to the best of the flight runners-up. Those semifinal duals are scheduled for Sunday at 2:30 p.m. with the championship to take place Monday at 8:30 a.m. On the women’s side, the 16 total 4.0 teams were evenly distributed into four flights. Their semifinals and finals will be played alongside the men’s. 

At last year’s Sectional Championships, Leon (Forestmeadows Park and Athletic Center – Tallahassee) finished atop the lone flight to claim the 3.0 men’s title while Orange/Seminole (Star Island Resort – Kissimmee) outlasted Duval (Charles J. Williams YMCA - Jacksonville) in a 3-2 battle in the women’s championship dual. In the 4.0 division, the SMD (Tamiami Tennis Center) men bested Orange/Seminole (Villagewalk at Lake Nona) by a 4-1 margin and the Collier (Heritage Bay Golf Country Club – Naples) women held off Hillsborough (Westchase Community Association – Tampa) for a 3-2 victory.
