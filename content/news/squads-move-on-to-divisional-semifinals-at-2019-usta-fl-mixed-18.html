Squads Move on to Divisional Semifinals

October 6, 2019

Sunday got off to a slow start, and therefore a late finish, due to a morning rain delay at the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus. After two days of flight play the action now turns to the playoff stages, as the divisional semifinals and finals will be contested Monday.

Three of the four flights in the 7.0 competition were highly competitive over Saturday and Sunday. Only defending champion Broward (City of Oakland Park) eased their way into the semifinal round, locking up the top spot in Flight 4 with a 4-0 record on the strength of 11 individual matchups and 22 sets won. Even Orange/Seminole (USTA National Campus – Orlando), who came through in all four of their duals in Flight 2, actually ended up with the same number of court and set victories as runner-up Volusia (Racquet Tennis Center – New Smyrna Beach).

The representatives emerging from Flights 1 and 3 survived a loss on the weekend. Hillsborough (Hunters Green Sports Center – Tampa) battled their way through Flight 1 despite tying at 2-1 overall with Collier (Heritage Bay Golf Country Club – Naples) and Alachua (Westside Park – Gainesville). Their sweep of Sarasota (Lakewood National – Bradenton) not only got them back in the mix, but also propelled them to the top of the flight, as they owned more set wins than Alachua and a head-to-head defeat of Collier.

From Flight 3, South Palm Beach (Stonebridge Country Club – Boca Raton) was the only squad of the five to drop just one dual; they moved on despite turning in a 14-15 record in sets played. Looking ahead to the semifinal matchups, Hillsborough will be across the net from Orange/Seminole while South Palm Beach faces Broward.

The trio of 9.0 flight winners closed out the weekend unscathed. SMD (Coral Pine Park – Pinecrest) swept through the double round robin Flight 1 at 4-0 thanks to nine court victories compared to just three setbacks. Pinellas (Total Tennis LLC – Clearwater) and Sarasota (Lakewood National – Bradenton) also cruised through flight play to advance to Monday. Pinellas claimed Flight 2 with a 3-0 effort after prevailing on seven of the nine courts they contested, while Sarasota did not surrender a single court in dominating Flight 3. Monday morning’s divisional semifinals will pit SMD against Sarasota and Pinellas going up against wild card entrant Broward (City of Oakland Park), the second-place finisher in Flight 3.

All semifinals are scheduled for 8:30 a.m. with the championship duals slated to take to the courts at approximately 11:30 a.m. For more information on the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Sectional Championships, click here.
