Orlando Open Presented by Nemours

Brooksby, Harrison & Kudla into Semis

April 17, 2021

style
article-hero

A trio of Americans – No. 5 seed Denis Kudla, Jenson Brooksby, and wild card Christian Harrison – secured victories on Friday to advance to the semifinals of the ATP Challenger Orlando Open Presented by Nemours. Rounding out the final four competitors remaining in the singles draw is Roberto Cid Subervi (Dominican Republic).

Kudla, who was on the brink of defeat in the last round against qualifier Tim Van Rijthoven (Netherlands), stormed out of the gates in his quarterfinal contest with fellow American Michael Mmoh. However, Mmoh leveled the score at 5-5, only to see Kudla claim the final two games of the frame. From there, the fifth seed kept the momentum on his side for a 7-5, 6-3 victory.

Cid Subervi will be Kudla’s semifinal opponent after prevailing in a war of attrition with Brayden Schnur (Canada). The two former collegiate standouts battled for two hours, with Schnur unable to take advantage of his early lead and eventually having to retire with the score standing at 3-6, 6-2, 4-0 in Cid Subervi’s favor.

Wild card Harrison, who upset No. 3 seed Mackenzie McDonald in the opening round, scored his second big win of the week with a 7-6(3), 6-3 effort over Jack Sock (United States). Sock was betrayed by his serve on the day, hitting seven double faults and making just 58% of his first deliveries; conversely, Harrison put 70% of his first serves in play – winning 33 of those 43 points – while getting broken just once for the match.

Brooksby has been the most consistent throughout the tournament to date, still not being pushed past 6-4 in any set in his three contests. On Friday, he was up against countryman Zane Khan, a wild card who was fresh off an impressive defeat of the No. 2 seed. Ultimately, Brooksby would prove to be too strong, coming through 6-4, 6-3.

Saturday’s doubles final will be an all-American affair, as Harrison and Dennis Novikov will be across the net from Sock and Mitchell Krueger. In the semifinals Friday afternoon, Harrison and Novikov bested the pairing of JC Aragone (United States) and Luis David Martinez (Venezuela) by a 6-2, 6-3 score line; Krueger and Sock held off Americans Sekou Bangoura and Junior Ore 6-4, 6-3.

Saturday’s schedule of play is set for an 11 a.m. start with the Harrison versus Brooksby match, followed by Kudla taking on Cid Subervi. The doubles championship will round out the day’s action.
