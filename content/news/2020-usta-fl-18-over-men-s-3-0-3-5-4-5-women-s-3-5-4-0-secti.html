USTA FL 18 & Over Men's 3.0/3.5/4.5 & Women's 3.5/4.0 Sectional Championships

Five Team Titles at Stake

December 3, 2020

style
article-hero

Five more Sunshine State team titles are at stake this weekend when the USTA National Campus welcomes in the 2020 USTA Florida 18 & Over Men’s 3.0/3.5/4.5 & Women’s 3.5/4.0 Sectional Championships. Play is set to begin on the afternoon of Friday, Dec. 4, and run through Tuesday, Dec. 8.

Teams will be competing in dual matches consisting of three matchups in total – one singles and two doubles; at least two of those three courts must be won in order to claim the dual.

Kicking off the event will be the men’s 4.5 division, which features 10 squads divided equally into two round-robin flights. They will contest all flighted duals over the course of Friday and Saturday with the divisional championship to be played Sunday.

Both 3.5 competitions will take to the courts for the first time on Saturday. For the men, 11 teams have made the trip to Orlando, starting off the weekend in one of three flights; the trio of flight winners plus the top second-place finisher will advanced to the knockout stages scheduled for Monday. Meanwhile the women’s 3.5 is the lone division that will be in action on the last day of the tournament thanks to being made up of 14 total squads.

The other women’s competition, with the best 4.0 crews from across the state in action, will start play Sunday and crown its champion Monday. The men’s 3.0 division, using a double round-robin format with only three teams vying for the title, will also be on the courts those two days.

Champions from the 2019 edition included the 3.0 Leon (Tom Brown Park – Tallahassee) men, the 3.5 Broward (Dillon Tennis Center – Oakland Park) men, the 4.5 Broward (Midtown Athletic Club – Weston) men, the 3.5 Escambia (Independent) women, and the Orange/Seminole (Fort Gatlin Recreation Center – Orlando) 4.0 women.
