National Campus to Host Top 16s Boys and Girls

May 18, 2018

style
article-hero

Junior competition returns to the National Campus this weekend with the arrival of the 2018 USTA May National Level 2 Championships. Featuring some of the top players from across the country in the boys’ and girls’ 16s, the championship will play out over the course of four days, culminating in singles and doubles winners being crowned on Tuesday.

Both singles draws consists of 64 players, with players feeding into a consolation draw through the round of 16. For the doubles competitions, the two draws are made up of 32 duos and is single elimination with a third-place playoff match.

Orlando native Jack Anthrop enters the weekend as the boys’ top overall seed. Anthrop has played up a division in his last two events; however, he did make a run to the final of the boys’ 16s draw at a National Level 2 in February, upsetting the first and third seeds along the way. He has competed numerous times at the National Campus, with his last action coming in the 2017 USTA National Winter Championships.

Rounding out the top eight seeds for the boys are No. 2 Jacob Bickersteth, who claimed the consolation title at the 2017 USTA National Winter Championships; No, 3 Dillon Blake (Miami, Fla.); No. 4 Trinity Grear (Adelphi, Md.); No. 5 Bruno Kuzuhara (Coconut Creek, Fla.); No. 6 Nadim Motaghedi (Cerritos, Calif.); No. 7 Jeffrey Fradkin (New York); and No. 8 Eli Gordon (Highland Park, Ill.).

In the girls’ draw, Madison Sieg (Greenwich, Conn.) is the top seed. Sieg owns one title on the year, battling her way through the girls’ 16s draw at the Smith Stearns Junior Championship National Level 3 event in January; she also won the doubles crown as well there. Sieg has not spent much time on the courts of the National Campus, but did find great success at the 2017 USTA National Winter Championships, where she rebounded from an opening round loss to earn the consolation title as an unseeded competitor. She has received a bye in the first round this week.

The players seeded in the top eight on the girls’ side also include No. 2 Leyden Games (Newport Beach, Calif.), who recently won the singles draw of the Darling Memorial Tennis Center National Level 3 in Las Vegas; No. 3 Brooke Theis (Lake Forest, Ill.); No. 4 Ellie Pittman (Indianapolis); No. 5 Anushka Khune (Palo Alto, Calif.); No. 6 Sophia Fornaris (Pinecrest, Fla.); No. 7 Sara Snyder (San Juan, PR); and No. 8 Mya Byrd (Norfolk, Va.).

Due to the volume of activity taking place at the National Campus on Saturday, a portion of that day’s schedule will be contested at a nearby facility – Lake Cane Tennis Center.

Matches are scheduled to kick off Saturday morning at 8:00 a.m. in a day that will see the opening round of the singles main and consolation draws plus the doubles draw be completed.
