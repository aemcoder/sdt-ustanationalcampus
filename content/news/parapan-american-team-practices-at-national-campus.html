The Parapan American Team will have its final set of practices at the USTA National Campus before heading off to the Parapan American Games from August 24-30 in Lima, Peru.

The team will consist of Casey Ratzlaff, Chris Herman, Dana Mathewson, Emmy Kaiser, David Wagner and Bryan Barten. Jason Harnett and Paul Walker will coach the Parapan team.

"We're pretty satisfied with our teams," Harnett said.  "Overall, I think we're really strong."
