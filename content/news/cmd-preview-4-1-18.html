Fan Guide to MatchDay on April 1

Brian Ormiston | March 27, 2018

style
article-hero

Two of the premier women’s tennis programs in the country converge at the USTA National Campus for College MatchDay when Florida and Texas collide on Sunday, April 1, at 5 p.m. Special pre-match festivities will begin at 3 p.m.

Before making your way to Lake Nona, here is everything you need to know about attending the match:

Matchup

-Florida vs. Texas (women)

Date and Time

-Sunday, April 1

-Gates open: 3 p.m.

-First serve: 5 p.m.

Pre-match Festivities

-Starting at 3 p.m., the USTA National Campus will host the Spring Egg-Stravaganza. Families can experience a crafts table, relay races, tennis drills, face painting, music, games and so much more, all for free.

-Fans who purchase a program will receive a free College MatchDay t-shirt. Supply is limited.

-A free interactive kids’ clinic led by USTA teaching professionals will begin at 3:30 p.m.

-Giveaways will be distributed throughout the day.

-Food and refreshments will be available for purchase.

Location

-USTA National Campus (Collegiate Complex)

-Address: 10000 USTA Blvd., Orlando, FL 32827

-Phone: 844-872-8667

Tickets

-Admission is free.

-Reserve complimentary tickets by visiting Ticketmaster.com and searching College MatchDay.

-Or click here: 2018 College MatchDay Complimentary Tickets.

Parking

-Parking is free and available on a first-come, first-serve basis.

RV Parking

-Fans with RVs are directed to park in the back row of the main parking lot.

Bus Parking

-The bus lane is located near the front of the main parking lot.

Match Format

-For the first time at College MatchDay, singles will kick off the action. Each of the six singles matches will be worth one team point.

-If the match is tied 3-3, three doubles matches will be played (one six-game set). The first team to win two of the three matches earns the final team point.

-The first school to secure four overall team points wins the match.

Court Assignments

-The national anthem and team introductions will take place on Court 1 (the side facing the parking lot).

-The match will be held on the south side of the Collegiate Complex, Courts 1-6.

Match Preview

Last year’s NCAA National Champions, Florida has a busy week before facing Texas, hosting Arkansas and Missouri in SEC play. The Gators are 9-1 in their last 10 matches entering the week, but are 0-3 in neutral-site matches this year. One of those losses came at the hands of Florida State in the first College MatchDay event of 2018.

Anna Danilina and Florida native Josie Kuhlman have led the way for Florida, posting 23-8 and 18-5 records, respectively, in 2017-18.

The Longhorns have been rolling as of late, winning eight consecutive contests for a 13-4 record. That streak is highlighted by 4-0 wins over Ohio State, Michigan, Kansas State, Oklahoma and Oklahoma State.

The Italian sisters Anna and Bianca Turati have combined for a 40-4 record this season.

Florida leads the all-time series, 25-9, and UF won the previous meeting, 5-2, on Feb. 24, 2008, in Austin, Texas. The last time the Longhorns claimed a victory over the Gators was in the 2005 NCAA semifinals in Athens, Ga.

Both schools have sat inside the top 15 of the USTA College Tennis Top 25 for the majority of the season. New USTA rankings will be released on March 28.

Tickets are complimentary and available on site but fans are encouraged to reserve their tickets at Ticketmaster.com.

For those who can’t make it, College MatchDay will be aired live on Tennis Channel.
