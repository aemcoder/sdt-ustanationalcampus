2019 USTA League National Mixed 18 & Over 7.0/9.0 Championships

Focus Shifts to Mixed Divisions

October 30, 2019

style
article-hero

USTA League National Championships play switches over to the Mixed divisions this weekend at the USTA National Campus, with the 7.0 and 9.0 titles the next to be claimed. Matches are set to get underway at 7:30 a.m. on Friday, Nov. 1 and continue through the afternoon of Sunday, Nov. 3.

All 17 Sections have sent a 7.0 and 9.0 team to Orlando with the goal of bringing home a league national title. Across the first two days of action, each squad will compete in two dual matches per day. A dual consists of three courts of doubles contests, in which a team must win at least two of those courts to claim the overall victory. At the conclusion of play Saturday evening, the top four 7.0 and top four 9.0 clubs will officially secure their place in the playoff rounds, which will take to the courts Sunday.

The divisional semifinals are slated to get underway at 7:30 a.m. with the finals and third-place matches scheduled for approximately 10:30 a.m.

At last year’s Championships, the Southern California squad (Whittier Narrows Tennis Center – El Monte) went undefeated on the weekend in 7.0 action, clinching the title with a 3-0 sweep of Texas (Wagon Wheel Tennis Center – Coppell). The crew representing Mid-Atlantic (Independent – Northern Va.) claimed the 9.0 crown after narrowly qualifying for the semifinals, holding off Southern (Mountain Empire Tennis Association – Tenn.) by a 2-1 margin in the championship dual.
