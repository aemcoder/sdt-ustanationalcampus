Day One Beats the Rain

June 1, 2019

The opening day of play at the 2019 USTA Florida Mixed 40 & Over 6.0/8.0 Sectional Championships, held at the USTA National Campus, just beat out the rain. All teams completed two matches over the course of Saturday.

Alachua (Westside Park – Gainesville) needs just one more victory on Sunday to move into the championship dual in the 6.0 competition. They swept Escambia (Independent) and Hillsborough (Grand Hampton – Tampa) in Flight 1, while Escambia was also able to defeat Hillsborough. Meanwhile in Flight 2, Duval (Fleming Island Plantation) moved atop the standings with a pair of wins on Saturday. A triumph over Marion (Fort King Tennis Center – Ocala) in their lone dual on Sunday will advance them to Monday’s final.

For the 8.0 division, Marion (Golden Ocala Golf and Equestrian Club) is in the driver’s seat in Flight 1 thanks to a 2-0 effort on Saturday. Flights 2 and 3 are more up in the air, with two squads in each flight still without a loss; Flight 2 will likely come down to the clash between Sarasota (Longboat Key Tennis Center) and Broward (City of Fort Lauderdale) while Flight 3 should be determined when Duval (Southside Tennis Complex – Jacksonville) and Pinellas (East Lake Woodlands Country Club – Oldsmar) face off. All teams are competing for a spot in the divisional semifinals, which does include one wild card slot.
