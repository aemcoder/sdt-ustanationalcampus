The final day of group play highlighted action at the 2017 ITF Super-Seniors World Team Championships, hosted by the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla. Going into Wednesday’s schedule the tournament shifts gears, as playoff draws within the nine cups will get underway.

Coming into Tuesday with an unblemished record, it was mostly smooth sailing for Team USA once again. The United States will have representation in the championship playoff draws in eight of the nine cups, the most of any nation.

The Bitsy Grant Cup (Men’s 75) is the lone exception. Featuring four groups and two seeded teams within each group, the second-seeded Americans came up short against No. 6 seed Netherlands in Group B.

Only two cups, the Jack Crawford Cup (Men’s 70) and Gardnar Mulloy Cup (Men’s 80), saw all of their top seeds secure spots in the championship playoff. The Althea Gibson Cup (Women’s 70) produced the biggest surprises, as the United States took down top seed Great Britain in Group A while Australia got the best of No. 3 France in Group C.

Featuring 123 teams (72 men’s and 51 women’s) representing 31 nations, the 2017 ITF Super-Seniors World Team Championships are being held at the USTA National Campus in Orlando, Fla., from Oct. 8-13. The international tournament features nine cups for the first time in its history, as the Men’s 85 Team Cup is in its first year of existence.
